How it works

  1. Apply with the space name, website and a contact email — about a minute.
  2. We review the space and reply by email, usually within one business day.
  3. Add photos, prices and hours in the dashboard, then publish.

What we list

Coworking spaces designed by architects or design firms, or spaces that clearly care about design. Plans are billed monthly and can be changed or cancelled at any time, with no minimum term.

Already listed? Link to your page

Every space we list has an English page with photos, prices, hours, directions and the address in Japanese. Many visitors look for an English page before they come; you are welcome to link to yours from your website or booking page. Replace your-space with the address of your page:

<a href="https://cowkml.com/spaces/your-space.html">Read about us in English on COWORKMILL</a>

Each page shows the date we last checked it against your official website. If your prices, hours or day-pass rules change, tell us through the contact form and we will update the page.
