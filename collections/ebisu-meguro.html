Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in Ebisu, Meguro and Nakameguro without a membership?

Yes — 3 of the 10 spaces in this collection offer a day pass or hourly drop-in: co-ba ebisu (Drop-in ¥2,000/day + tax); MIDORI.so Nakameguro (¥3,300/day pop-in, or ¥33,000 for 11 days); SENQ Meguro (Drop-in ¥550/hour (+ ¥550 one-time joining fee), incl. tax). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Ebisu, Meguro and Nakameguro are open at weekends?

Business-Airport Ebisu list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Ebisu, Meguro and Nakameguro have an English website?

WORK STYLING Ebisu, NewWork Ebisu, The Executive Centre Meguro Arco Tower, MIDORI.so Nakameguro, WORK STYLING Meguro, WORK STYLING Nakameguro. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in Ebisu, Meguro and Nakameguro near?

The stations mentioned most often are Ebisu Station, Daikanyama Station, Meguro Station, Nakameguro Station. Every space page has the Japanese address to show a taxi driver and a Google Maps link.
