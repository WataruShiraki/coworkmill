Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 7 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

WORK STYLING Kasumigaseki Building, WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi), WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit, CIC Tokyo. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Shibuya Station, Toranomon Station, Roppongi Station, Tokyo Station, Hills Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
