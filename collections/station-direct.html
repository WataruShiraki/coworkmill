Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 10 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

LIFORK Otemachi, WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

LIFORK Otemachi, CIC Tokyo, WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Yaesu, WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit, WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building, WORK STYLING Nihonbashi 1-chome, WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit, WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi). Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Tokyo Station, Nihombashi Station, Otemachi Station, Shibuya Station, Toranomon Hills Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
