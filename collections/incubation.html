Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

Yes — one of the 9 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: Shinagawa Industry Support and Exchange Facility SHIP (One-time ¥550/hour incl. tax). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

Startup Hub Tokyo Marunouchi, NEXs Tokyo Community Space, Shinagawa Industry Support and Exchange Facility SHIP list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

SHIBUYA QWS, StartupSide Tokyo, Shinagawa Industry Support and Exchange Facility SHIP. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Takebashi Station, Suidobashi Station, Kyobashi Station, Osaki Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
