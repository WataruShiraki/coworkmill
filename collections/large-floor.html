Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

Yes — one of the 4 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: co-ba ebisu (Drop-in ¥2,000/day + tax). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

CIC Tokyo, SHIBUYA QWS. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Toranomon Hills Station, Daikanyama Station, Ebisu Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
