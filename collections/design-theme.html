Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 9 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

Business-Airport Hibiya, Business-Airport Shimbashi, Business-Airport Nihonbashi, Business-Airport Ebisu list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya, WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building, WORK STYLING Shiodome City Center 5F, WORK STYLING Otemachi. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Shinjuku Station, Tochomae Station, Otemachi Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
