Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in Aoyama and Omotesando, Tokyo without a membership?

Yes — one of the 10 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: SENQ Aoyama Namiki-dori (Drop-in ¥550/hour (+ ¥550 one-time joining fee), incl. tax). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Aoyama and Omotesando, Tokyo are open at weekends?

SENQ Aoyama, WORK STYLING Omotesando, H¹T Omotesando, CROSSCOOP Aoyama, fabbit Aoyama-itchome list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Aoyama and Omotesando, Tokyo have an English website?

MIDORI.so Aoyama, WORK STYLING Omotesando. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in Aoyama and Omotesando, Tokyo near?

The stations mentioned most often are Aoyama-itchome Station, Omotesando Station, Gaiemmae Station, Gaienmae Station, Nogizaka Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
