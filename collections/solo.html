Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

Yes — one of the 8 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: SENQ Meguro (Drop-in ¥550/hour (+ ¥550 one-time joining fee), incl. tax). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN, H¹T Shinjuku West Exit, BIRTH WORK Azabu-Juban, BIRTH WORK Toranomon list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

WORK STYLING Shinagawa. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Ikebukuro Station, Shinjuku Station, Ochanomizu Station, Shinagawa Station, Azabu-Juban Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
