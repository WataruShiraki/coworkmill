Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in Shibuya without a membership?

Yes — 2 of the 19 spaces in this collection offer a day pass or hourly drop-in: MIDORI.so Shibuya (¥3,300/day pop-in; free desk ¥44,000/month (+ ¥11,000…); .andwork Shibuya (¥1,100/hour / ¥3,300/day / ¥29,000–34,000/month). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Shibuya are open at weekends?

WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage, Business-Airport Shibuya Sakura Stage, CROSSCOOP Shibuya Nextsite, fabbit Shibuya Ekimae, Business-Airport Shibuya Nanpeidai, CROSSCOOP Shibuya list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Shibuya have an English website?

SHIBUYA QWS, WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage, MIDORI.so Shibuya, .andwork Shibuya, JustCo Shibuya Hikarie, NewWork Shibuya Goto Ikueikai Building The Flagship, The Executive Centre Cerulean Tower, WORK STYLING Shibuya. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in Shibuya near?

The stations mentioned most often are Shibuya Station, Omotesando Station, Daikanyama Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
