Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

Yes — 2 of the 7 spaces in this collection offer a day pass or hourly drop-in: co-ba akasaka (Drop-in ¥550 for 2 hours, ¥2,200/day, incl. tax); BIZcomfort Asakusa (Drop-in ¥440/hour, ¥1,650/day). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

LIFORK Otemachi, BIRTH WORK Azabu-Juban, BIZcomfort Asakusa list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

LIFORK Otemachi, CIC Tokyo, Global Business Hub Tokyo. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Otemachi Station, Toranomon Hills Station, Tameike-Sanno Station, Azabu-Juban Station, Asakusa Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
