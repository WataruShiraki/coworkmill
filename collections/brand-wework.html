Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 15 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Roppongi-itchome Station, Nishi-Shinjuku Station, Jimbocho Station, Kayabacho Station, Nihombashi Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
