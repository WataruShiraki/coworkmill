Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 11 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

The Executive Centre Cerulean Tower, The Executive Centre GranTokyo South Tower, The Executive Centre Jingumae Tower, The Executive Centre JP Tower, The Executive Centre Kyobashi Edogrand, The Executive Centre Meguro Arco Tower, The Executive Centre Roppongi Hills North Tower, The Executive Centre Sanno Park Tower and 3 more. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Tokyo Station, Shibuya Station, Kyobashi Station, Roppongi Station, Hamamatsucho Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
