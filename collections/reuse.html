Which coworking spaces in renovated old buildings in Tokyo have an English website?

MIDORI.so Nakameguro, MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

The stations mentioned most often are Otemachi Station, Daikanyama Station, Ebisu Station, Gaiemmae Station, Kayabacho Station. Every space page has the Japanese address to show a taxi driver and a Google Maps link.
