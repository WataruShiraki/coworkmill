Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

None of the 14 spaces in this collection list a day pass on their official websites; they are run as memberships or offices. See our day-pass list for spaces you can use for a day.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

NewWork Akihabara, NewWork Ikebukuro East Exit, NewWork Kinshicho 2nd, NewWork Oimachi list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

NewWork Akihabara, NewWork Daimon / Hamamatsucho, NewWork Ebisu, NewWork Ginza, NewWork Ikebukuro East Exit, NewWork Kinshicho 2nd, NewWork Oimachi, NewWork Shibuya Goto Ikueikai Building The Flagship and 6 more. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Ikebukuro Station, Akihabara Station, Daimon Station, Hamamatsucho Station, Ebisu Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
