Yes — one of the 13 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: BasisPoint Jimbocho (Drop-in from ¥330 per 30 min, with a daily cap). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Kanda, Akihabara, Ochanomizu and Jimbocho are open at weekends?

Business-Airport Kanda, NewWork Akihabara, BasisPoint Jimbocho list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Kanda, Akihabara, Ochanomizu and Jimbocho have an English website?
