Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in Shinjuku without a membership?

Yes — one of the 10 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: CASE Shinjuku. Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Shinjuku are open at weekends?

WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit, H¹T Shinjuku West Exit list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Shinjuku have an English website?

WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building, WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit, JustCo Shinjuku Miraina Tower. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in Shinjuku near?

The stations mentioned most often are Shinjuku Station, Shinjuku-sanchome Station, Tochomae Station, Nishi-Shinjuku Station, Takadanobaba Station. Every space page has the Japanese address to show a taxi driver and a Google Maps link.
