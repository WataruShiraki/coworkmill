Yes — 2 of the 10 spaces in this collection offer a day pass or hourly drop-in: THE HUB Shiodome (Rental office from ¥65,289/month / all-site plan…); BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginzaguchi (Drop-in from ¥330 per 30 min, with a daily cap). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in Ginza, Shimbashi and Shiodome are open at weekends?

WORK STYLING Ginza, fabbit Ginza, Business-Airport Shimbashi, H¹T Ginza, BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginzaguchi list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in Ginza, Shimbashi and Shiodome have an English website?

WORK STYLING Ginza, NewWork Ginza, WORK STYLING Shimbashi, WORK STYLING Shiodome City Center 5F. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

The stations mentioned most often are Shimbashi Station, Ginza Station, Ginza-itchome Station, Uchisaiwaicho Station, Daimon Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
