Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

MIDORI.so Kichijoji list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

MIDORI.so Nakameguro, MIDORI.so NAGATACHO, MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama, MIDORI.so Shibuya, MIDORI.so Aoyama, MIDORI.so Kichijoji, MIDORI.so Nihonbashi. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Nagatacho Station, Shibuya Station, Aoyama-itchome Station, Kichijoji Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
