Questions visitors ask

Can I use a coworking space in this collection without a membership?

Yes — one of the 7 spaces in this collection offers a day pass or hourly drop-in: MIDORI.so Kichijoji (¥1,100 for 2 hours or ¥2,200/day pop-in; free desk…). Check each page for booking and payment.

Which coworking spaces in this collection are open at weekends?

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit, WORK STYLING Ginza, H¹T Shinjuku West Exit, H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN, H¹T Roppongi, MIDORI.so Kichijoji, WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage list weekend hours on their official websites.

Which coworking spaces in this collection have an English website?

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit, WORK STYLING Ginza, MIDORI.so Kichijoji, WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage. Booking forms elsewhere are often in Japanese only.

Which stations are the coworking spaces in this collection near?

The stations mentioned most often are Tokyo Station, Ginza Station, Shinjuku Station, Ikebukuro Station, Roppongi Station. Every space page has directions by train and the address in Japanese.
