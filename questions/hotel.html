How does coworking inside a hotel work?

You rent the lobby or lounge by the hour or the day. .andwork Shibuya starts at ¥1,100 an hour and .andwork Azabujuban at ¥800 an hour. MIDORI.so Nihonbashi works as a workplace by day and a place to stay by night.

.andwork Shibuya →

A hotel lobby that becomes a workplace for workers from around the world

.andwork Shibuya is inside The Millennials Shibuya, with a coworking area, lounge, kitchen and phone booths.

Prices are ¥1,100 an hour, ¥3,300 a day, and ¥29,000–34,000 a month.

.andwork Azabu-Juban →

A 2nd-floor lounge and a top-floor bar

.andwork Azabujuban is inside THE LIVELY Tokyo Azabujuban, with a lounge on the 2nd floor and a bar on the top floor.

It is open 8:00–22:00, at ¥800 an hour, ¥2,500 a day or ¥34,000 a month.

MIDORI.so Nihonbashi →

Working and staying in the same building

MIDORI.so Nihonbashi has a café, bar and gallery on the ground floor, a lounge on the 2nd, and rooms for work and sleep on the 3rd and 4th.

Pop-in is ¥2,200 for a day or ¥8,800 for a night, with no joining or membership fee.
