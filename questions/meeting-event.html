How do meeting rooms and event spaces work?

Meeting rooms are usually booked by the hour, while event spaces are normally shared areas hired out exclusively for events. WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square has 70 meeting rooms, so you can choose the size you need.

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square →

70 meeting rooms and a lounge rearranged for events

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square has 70 meeting rooms, from small discussion rooms to larger meeting rooms.

The 42nd-floor lounge can be rearranged for events, and phone booths let you take online meetings away from your seat.

LIFORK Otemachi →

A 199.1 m² rental lounge for ¥44,000 an hour

LIFORK Otemachi's rental lounge is 199.1 m², holding 50 seated or 60 standing, with a 130-inch screen and wireless microphones.

It costs ¥44,000 per 60 minutes, minimum 60 minutes then 15-minute steps, and is used for seminars, mixers, award ceremonies, shoots and screenings.

co-ba ebisu →

PARK for 70 and CAFE for 40, half price for members

co-ba ebisu's ground floor has two event spaces, PARK and CAFE. PARK holds up to 70 and CAFE up to 40, and members rent them at half price.

They are shared areas day to day, hired out only for events — work and networking on the same floor.

SHIBUYA QWS →

A 200-person hall and three meeting rooms for all members

SHIBUYA QWS's SCRAMBLE HALL is an event space for about 200, looking down on the Scramble Crossing, with three screens, sound, lighting and a green room.

Besides three meeting rooms open to all members, there are bookable private rooms.

What is an event space?

A large space for seminars, mixers and presentations. At coworking spaces, lounges and shared areas are often hired out exclusively for events.

Capacity, screens and sound, member discounts and hourly rates are the points to compare.
