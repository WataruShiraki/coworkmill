Who chooses private rooms and fixed desks?

People who want the space as their company's base — for company registration, a lockable room or a desk where they can leave their things. A fixed desk at MIDORI.so Nakameguro is ¥60,500 a month.

co-ba ebisu →

Rooms for 8 to 20 on two-year fixed-term leases

co-ba ebisu's 2nd floor is for private offices. Single and twin rooms of about 31–34 m² hold about 8 people, and suites of about 48–72 m² hold 10–20.

Leases are two-year fixed-term, including management fees. 2nd-floor tenants can add the ground-floor coworking for ¥30,000 a month.

ARCH, Toranomon Hills →

A secure second office, and short-term rooms

ARCH's SMALL OFFICE is a secure second office, also suited to new businesses run jointly by several companies.

PROJECT ROOM is a room for small teams that can be used for short projects — rented only for the period of intensive work.

MIDORI.so Nakameguro →

Free desks at ¥42,350 and fixed desks at ¥60,500 a month

At MIDORI.so Nakameguro, free desks cost ¥42,350 a month and fixed desks ¥60,500, both with an ¥11,000 joining fee.

The second building holds private offices, so members move between the shared areas of the main building and closed rooms on the same site.

What is company registration?

Recording a company's address in the public register. Some coworking spaces let you use their address for registration with a fixed-desk or private-room contract.

Many places do not allow registration on free-desk plans, so people who need a company address choose a fixed desk or private room.
