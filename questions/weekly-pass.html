Q&A

Is there a weekly pass for travellers?

Some spaces sell passes by the week. .andwork Kyoto has 1- to 4-week plans with 24-hour access, and Zero-Ten Park in Fukuoka has a Digital Nomad Plan with English-speaking staff.

.andwork Kyoto →

1 to 4 weeks, open 24 hours

.andwork Kyoto sells weekly plans: ¥7,400 for 1 week, ¥14,300 for 2 weeks, ¥18,900 for 3 weeks and ¥23,500 for 4 weeks, with no sign-up fee.

Weekly members can use the space 24 hours a day, and lockers and mail handling are available as add-ons.

Zero-Ten Park Canal City Hakata-mae →

A Digital Nomad Plan in Fukuoka

Zero-Ten Park offers a Digital Nomad Plan, from short hourly use up to weekly plans for mid- and long-term stays.

The operator says English-speaking staff are on site, and plan users get free onboarding: information on places to stay, local communities, sightseeing and activities in Kyushu.
