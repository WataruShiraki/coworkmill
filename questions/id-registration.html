Q&A

Do I need ID or registration to use a day pass?

Usually a little. Some spaces ask you to register at reception and show photo ID on your first visit; others ask you to sign up online first. It is worth checking before you go.

Zero-Ten Park Fukuoka PARCO →

Photo ID and a form at reception

At Zero-Ten Park, first-time drop-in users show photo ID and fill in a form at reception, which takes about 5 minutes. The official FAQ names a driving licence or My Number card. From the second visit, the form alone takes about a minute.

Payment is cashless only — credit card, transit IC card, e-money or QR payment.

HOKKAIDO×Station01 →

Agree to the terms on your first visit

HOKKAIDO×Station01 needs no booking. First-time users complete a short registration to agree to the terms of use.

Payment is cashless; cash is not accepted.

.andwork Fukuoka →

Free online membership first

.andwork asks drop-in users to register as a free member on its website. There is no sign-up fee for hourly, daily or ticket plans.
