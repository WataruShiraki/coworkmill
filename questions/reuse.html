Q&A

Are there coworking spaces in old buildings?

Yes. MIDORI.so Nakameguro is two shared-office buildings made from an ivy-covered derelict house. Because the old structure was kept as it was renovated, it has textures and shadows that new buildings lack.

MIDORI.so Nakameguro →

An ivy-covered ruin turned into a main building and an annex

MIDORI.so Nakameguro was created by renovating an ivy-covered derelict house. The main building's 3rd floor holds the workspace, lounge, meeting rooms and gallery, and the annex holds private offices.

Because the old structure was kept, it has textures and shadows new buildings lack. It is open 24/7.

co-ba ebisu →

By contrast, a new building opened in November 2019

co-ba ebisu opened in November 2019 on the 1st and 2nd floors of JP noie Ebisu-Nishi, with 1,573.42 m² over two floors and high-ceilinged shared areas.

Its design theme is “WORK ASSEMBLE”. In a new building, the interior theme is what gives a place its character.

co-lab Shibuya Cast →

On the lower floors of a 17-storey building

co-lab Shibuya Cast occupies the 1st and 2nd floors of Shibuya Cast, a 17-storey building designed around the idea of “harmony in diversity”.

Next door is the café Are, loosely linking inside and outside. More than whether a building is old or new, how it meets the street shapes the impression of a place.

What is renovation?

Keeping an old building's structure while redoing its interior and services. MIDORI.so Nakameguro renovated an ivy-covered derelict house this way.

Because it keeps character a new build cannot have, coworking is one of the strongest uses for people who own old buildings.
