What do I need for my first visit?

It depends on the space. Some let you walk in and sign up at reception, some ask you to register in an app before you arrive, and monthly plans usually need photo ID.

Coworking Space MONO →

Walk in and sign at reception

MONO in Aomi needs no booking or registration for drop-in: you check in at reception, and on your first visit you sign the terms of use.

Drop-in runs on weekdays from 10:00 to 18:00, for 2 hours or a full day.

RoomUs Yokohama →

Register online before you go

RoomUs Yokohama is unstaffed, so you register online before your first visit.

It charges ¥100 per 20 minutes and is open 7:00–23:00 every day.

tefu lounge Shimokitazawa →

Sign up in the LINE app

The (tefu) lounge is used through the LINE app: you register with a credit card, choose a plan and check in, and you can book before you arrive.

Lounge users get free specialty coffee.

co-ba akasaka →

Book first — no walk-ins

co-ba akasaka takes drop-in only through the STATION WORK booking site and does not accept same-day visitors without a booking.

A day is ¥2,200, on weekdays from 10:00 to 19:00.

Business-Airport Tokyo →

Photo ID for monthly plans

Business-Airport asks for photo ID — a driving licence, passport or My Number card — when you sign up, and you can start the same day.

Its plans run for at least two months.
