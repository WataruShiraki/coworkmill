Which coworking spaces are open at weekends?

Some keep the same hours at weekends as on weekdays. WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit and WORK STYLING Ginza are open 8:00–22:00 at weekends too, and MIDORI.so Kichijoji 8:00–23:00 every day.

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit →

8:00 to 22:00 at weekends too

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit is on the 2nd floor of Pacific Century Place Marunouchi.

It is open 8:00–22:00 on weekdays and weekends, three minutes on foot from Tokyo Station.

WORK STYLING Ginza →

One minute from Ginza Station, until 22:00 on weekends and holidays

WORK STYLING Ginza is on the 9th floor of Ginza Glasse.

It is open 8:00–22:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays, one minute on foot from Ginza Station on the Tokyo Metro.

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit →

7:00 to 22:00 every day

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit is open 7:00–22:00 every day.

It is on the 9th floor of the Nishi-Shinjuku Showa Building, two minutes from the West Exit of Shinjuku Station.

MIDORI.so Kichijoji →

The 8th floor of Kichijoji PARCO, until 23:00 every day

MIDORI.so Kichijoji is a coworking space on the 8th floor of Kichijoji PARCO.

It is open 8:00–23:00 every day, with a workspace, meeting rooms, a kitchen, a lounge and booths.
