Which coworking spaces open early in the morning?

Many open at 7:00. Coworking at most Business Airport sites opens at 7:00 on weekdays, H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN at 7:00 every day, and drop-in at .andwork Shibuya runs 7:00–24:00.

Business-Airport Kudanshita →

Kudan Kaikan Terrace, from 7:00 on weekdays

Business Airport Kudanshita is on the ground floor of Kudan Kaikan Terrace, facing the greenery and water of the palace moat.

The coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays, and the serviced offices 24 hours.

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN →

7:00 to 22:30 every day

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN is open 7:00–22:30 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

It is three minutes on foot from the North Gate of JR Ikebukuro Station.

.andwork Shibuya →

Drop-in from 7:00 to midnight

.andwork Shibuya turns the lobby of The Millennials Shibuya into a workplace.

Drop-in runs 7:00–24:00, and weekly and monthly members have 24-hour access. It costs ¥1,100 an hour or ¥3,300 a day.
