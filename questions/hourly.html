Which coworking spaces can I use by the hour?

Some offer hourly drop-in. DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO is ¥660 an hour on weekdays, .andwork Azabujuban ¥800 an hour and .andwork Shibuya ¥1,100 an hour.

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO →

¥660 an hour on weekdays

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO costs ¥660 an hour on weekdays, or ¥3,300 a day.

It describes itself as a place where people, companies and ideas from Tokyo and all over Japan cross diagonally.

.andwork Azabu-Juban →

¥800 an hour, with a 10-visit pass

.andwork Azabujuban costs ¥800 an hour or ¥2,500 a day. A 10-visit pass is ¥17,500.

It is open 8:00–22:00.

.andwork Shibuya →

¥1,100 an hour, drop-in until midnight

.andwork Shibuya costs ¥1,100 an hour or ¥3,300 a day.

Drop-in runs from 7:00 until midnight.

MIDORI.so Nihonbashi →

Daytime pop-in at ¥2,200 a day

Daytime pop-in at MIDORI.so Nihonbashi is ¥2,200 a day, with no joining or membership fee.

At night you can stay in the same building for ¥8,800.
