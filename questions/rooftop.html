Q&A

Which coworking spaces have a rooftop or terrace?

Some have a rooftop garden or farm, others a terrace on a high floor. SENQ Roppongi has a 9th-floor sky terrace, MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama a rooftop farm and THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho a rooftop terrace.

SENQ Roppongi →

A 9th-floor sky terrace in Shin-Roppongi Building

SENQ Roppongi's 9th floor has about 70 coworking seats, meeting rooms, a café corner and a sky terrace.

Its theme is CHANGE THE THEORY. Private rooms are available 24/7.

MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama →

A farm on the roof of a Showa-era building

MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama fills seven floors of a Showa-era building with a café, print workshop, gallery and workspace.

On the roof is a garden used as a farm.

THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho →

Fully private rooms and a rooftop terrace

THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho is a boldly renovated SOHO building. Every room is fully private, and there is a rooftop terrace.

There are three meeting rooms for 4, and private rental offices start at ¥75,350 a month.
