What does a community manager do?

They are staff who connect members with each other and with outsiders, and act as the contact point for events and advice. Their role differs from a reception desk that manages seats. At Business Airport, they are described as partners who work alongside members.

Business-Airport Aoyama →

Community managers who work alongside members

At Business Airport Aoyama, community managers work alongside members. Staff are on hand during reception hours, and security is in place.

Members of any site can use the coworking at every site. Whether there is someone whose job is introducing people changes how a shared office feels.

ARCH, Toranomon Hills →

Chief Incubation Officers who advise and connect

At ARCH, two Chief Incubation Officers give advice and connect people. The title differs, but the role is close to a community manager's.

Mentoring, networking and training are the pillars of the service, with weekly mixers, round-table gatherings and pitch events held inside.

SHIBUYA QWS →

QWS Commons, who support members as experts

SHIBUYA QWS has a membership category, QWS Commons, for experts who support members. They share the floor with project members, who join in teams of three or more.

CROSS PARK, at the centre of the facility, is where people cross paths — the shape of the space itself helps connect people, not only the staff.

What is a community manager?

Staff at a coworking space or shared office who connect members with each other and with outsiders. They also plan events and act as a contact point for advice.

Titles vary. More and more places appoint people whose job is advice and introductions, like ARCH's Chief Incubation Officers.
