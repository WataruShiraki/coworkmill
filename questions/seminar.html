Which coworking spaces can host seminars or large meetings?

Some have large meeting rooms or event spaces. Global Business Hub Tokyo has an event space for up to 200, WORK STYLING Ebisu a conference room for up to 150, and X-NIHONBASHI TOWER about 120 seats in theatre style.

Global Business Hub Tokyo →

An event space for up to 200

Global Business Hub Tokyo is on the 3rd floor of Otemachi Financial City Grand Cube.

It has 50 furnished offices, 14 meeting rooms and an event space for up to 200.

WORK STYLING Ebisu →

A conference room for 13 to 150

WORK STYLING Ebisu has a conference room for 13 to 150 people.

It is on the 8th floor of the Fujiwara Building, one minute on foot from Ebisu Station on the Tokyo Metro.

X-NIHONBASHI TOWER →

About 120 seats in theatre style

X-NIHONBASHI TOWER is a co-creation hub for the space business on the 7th floor of Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower.

At 307 m², it seats about 120 in theatre style or holds about 150 standing.

CROSSCOOP Shimbashi →

A seminar room for 72

CROSSCOOP Shimbashi has a seminar room for 72 and a lounge of about 96 seats.

Private offices are for 10 to 100 people, and shared facilities are available 24 hours.
