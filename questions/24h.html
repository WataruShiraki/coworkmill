Q&A

Are there coworking spaces open 24 hours?

Yes. co-lab Shibuya Cast, LIFORK Otemachi, MIDORI.so Nakameguro and co-ba ebisu's ground floor are open 24/7. Late-night and holiday access relies on keyless systems such as smart locks.

co-lab Shibuya Cast →

A 24/7 shared office, running since 2003

co-lab Shibuya Cast is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year — every room alike, from booths for 1–2 to rooms for 12–22.

The 2nd floor has a window-side shared space and a kitchen, so there is somewhere to take a break outside your room, day or night.

LIFORK Otemachi →

A smart lock for access when reception is closed

LIFORK Otemachi's shared offices are open 24/7 (except building closure days). Members enter and leave with a smart lock.

With no key to carry, you can come and go late at night or on holidays by yourself.

MIDORI.so Nakameguro →

An old building, open 24/7

MIDORI.so Nakameguro is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with a kitchen, lockers and free drinks.

Free desks cost ¥42,350 a month and fixed desks ¥60,500. With no time limits, you choose your contract by seat type.

co-ba ebisu →

A 24-hour plan, and a nights-and-weekends plan

On co-ba ebisu's ground floor, a free-address membership usable 24 hours every day is ¥30,000 a month.

Night & Weekend, for weekday evenings and weekends only, is ¥12,000 a month, and Early Bird ¥6,500 — the price changes with the hours you use.

What is a smart lock?

An electronic lock opened with a smartphone or card instead of a physical key. LIFORK Otemachi uses one for member access.

For 24-hour spaces, managing access when no one is at reception is the key issue. Not having to hand out keys greatly reduces the work of running the place.
