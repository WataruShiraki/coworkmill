How much does coworking cost per month?

Plans for shared seats range from the ¥10,000s to the ¥60,000s a month. Free desks at DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO start at ¥18,700 a month, and at MIDORI.so Nakameguro cost ¥42,350.

MIDORI.so Nakameguro →

Free desk ¥42,350, fixed desk ¥60,500 a month

At MIDORI.so Nakameguro, a free desk is ¥42,350 a month and a fixed desk ¥60,500, with an ¥11,000 joining fee.

It is a two-building shared office made from an ivy-covered derelict house.

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO →

Free desks from ¥18,700 a month

At DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO, free desks start at ¥18,700 a month. Drop-in is ¥3,300 a day, or ¥660 an hour on weekdays.

It is on the 4th floor of the Kyobashi MID Building, about a minute on foot from Takaracho Station.
