Q&A

Coworking in Japan, answered

Short answers to the questions people ask most. Every answer is written from the official information of the spaces we list.

What is the difference between a coworking space and a shared office?

Coworking means sharing open seats; a shared office means renting a lockable room. In practice, many places offer both — co-ba ebisu has coworking on the ground floor and private offices on the 2nd.

Are there places I can use without becoming a member?

Yes. Day-rate drop-in, rooms by the half-hour and guided tours are all ways in. Drop-in at co-ba ebisu is ¥2,000 a day, on weekdays from 10:00 to 19:00.

Are there coworking spaces open 24 hours?

Yes. co-lab Shibuya Cast, LIFORK Otemachi, MIDORI.so Nakameguro and co-ba ebisu's ground floor are open 24/7. Late-night and holiday access relies on keyless systems such as smart locks.

What does a community manager do?

They are staff who connect members with each other and with outsiders, and act as the contact point for events and advice. Their role differs from a reception desk that manages seats. At Business Airport, they are described as partners who work alongside members.

How do meeting rooms and event spaces work?

Meeting rooms are usually booked by the hour, while event spaces are normally shared areas hired out exclusively for events. WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square has 70 meeting rooms, so you can choose the size you need.

Who chooses private rooms and fixed desks?

People who want the space as their company's base — for company registration, a lockable room or a desk where they can leave their things. A fixed desk at MIDORI.so Nakameguro is ¥60,500 a month.

Where are coworking spaces linked directly to a station?

There is LIFORK Otemachi, linked to Otemachi Station, and WeWork and SHIBUYA QWS in Shibuya Scramble Square, linked to Shibuya Station. LIFORK Otemachi connects directly to Exits C8, C11 and C12 of Otemachi Station, and is five minutes on foot from JR Tokyo Station.

Are there coworking spaces in old buildings?

Yes. MIDORI.so Nakameguro is two shared-office buildings made from an ivy-covered derelict house. Because the old structure was kept as it was renovated, it has textures and shadows that new buildings lack.

Which coworking spaces have private rooms for focused solo work?

Some places line up rooms for one. H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN and H¹T Shinjuku West Exit each have 18 single rooms, and SENQ Meguro has 26 solo booths.

Which coworking spaces are open at weekends?

Some keep the same hours at weekends as on weekdays. WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit and WORK STYLING Ginza are open 8:00–22:00 at weekends too, and MIDORI.so Kichijoji 8:00–23:00 every day.

Which coworking spaces open early in the morning?

Many open at 7:00. Coworking at most Business Airport sites opens at 7:00 on weekdays, H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN at 7:00 every day, and drop-in at .andwork Shibuya runs 7:00–24:00.

How does coworking inside a hotel work?

You rent the lobby or lounge by the hour or the day. .andwork Shibuya starts at ¥1,100 an hour and .andwork Azabujuban at ¥800 an hour. MIDORI.so Nihonbashi works as a workplace by day and a place to stay by night.

How much does coworking cost per month?

Plans for shared seats range from the ¥10,000s to the ¥60,000s a month. Free desks at DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO start at ¥18,700 a month, and at MIDORI.so Nakameguro cost ¥42,350.

Which coworking spaces can I use by the hour?

Some offer hourly drop-in. DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO is ¥660 an hour on weekdays, .andwork Azabujuban ¥800 an hour and .andwork Shibuya ¥1,100 an hour.

Which coworking spaces have a rooftop or terrace?

Some have a rooftop garden or farm, others a terrace on a high floor. SENQ Roppongi has a 9th-floor sky terrace, MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama a rooftop farm and THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho a rooftop terrace.

Which coworking spaces have themed interiors?

Some bring local history or a story into their interiors. WORK STYLING's Hibiya site is “ZEN MODERN” and its Shinjuku Mitsui Building site “RETRO FUTURE”; Business Airport uses a theatre motif in Hibiya and the railway in Shimbashi.

Which coworking spaces are on high floors with a view?

Some occupy the upper floors of skyscrapers. WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square is on floors 37–42 and 45, WORK STYLING Kasumigaseki Building on the 36th, and Business Airport Shinagawa on the 28th.

Which coworking spaces can host seminars or large meetings?

Some have large meeting rooms or event spaces. Global Business Hub Tokyo has an event space for up to 200, WORK STYLING Ebisu a conference room for up to 150, and X-NIHONBASHI TOWER about 120 seats in theatre style.

How do I pay at a coworking space in Japan?

Most drop-in spaces take cards, and several take no cash at all. SENQ's drop-in accepts credit cards, transit IC cards, Apple Pay, iD and QUICPay but not cash, and NAKANO HAKO is cashless only.

Which coworking spaces have English-speaking staff or an English website?

CIC Tokyo says it has bilingual staff, and StartupSide Tokyo has staff who speak English, Chinese and Malay. Operators such as The Executive Centre, JustCo and MIDORI.so publish English websites, while THE HUB states that its contracts require Japanese.

What do I need for my first visit?

It depends on the space. Some let you walk in and sign up at reception, some ask you to register in an app before you arrive, and monthly plans usually need photo ID.
