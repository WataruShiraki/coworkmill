Q&A

Are there places I can use without becoming a member?

Yes. Day-rate drop-in, rooms by the half-hour and guided tours are all ways in. Drop-in at co-ba ebisu is ¥2,000 a day, on weekdays from 10:00 to 19:00.

co-ba ebisu →

Sit down for a day for ¥2,000

Drop-in at co-ba ebisu is ¥2,000 a day, on weekdays from 10:00 to 19:00. Even non-members can try a day first.

The ground-floor coworking has Wi-Fi, power and phone booths — a way to get the feel of the place before signing a monthly contract.

MIDORI.so Nakameguro →

Pop-in at ¥3,300 a day, or ¥33,000 for 11 days

Pop-in at MIDORI.so Nakameguro costs ¥3,300 a day. An 11-day pack is ¥33,000, which suits people who come two or three days a week.

The main building's gallery is also rented out for exhibitions and shoots, so people other than members find their way in.

LIFORK Otemachi →

Style rooms by the half-hour

LIFORK Otemachi has ten Style Rooms, B to L: a room for 4 costs ¥1,650 per 30 minutes, and G, for 16, ¥4,400 per 30 minutes.

They are available 9:00–21:00, from a minimum of 30 minutes and then in 15-minute steps — a way to rent a room without membership.

100BANCH →

A 40-minute tour from 17:00 on weekdays

100BANCH runs a facility tour of about 40 minutes from 17:00 on weekdays, for ¥2,200 per person including a book.

With a way to see inside without membership, it is a good way to get a feel for a coworking space while you are looking.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space for a day or a few hours without a monthly contract. Some places use other names, such as “pop-in”.

Prices of around ¥2,000–3,300 a day are typical. Before joining, you can check for yourself how busy and how noisy it gets.
