Where can I cowork on a day pass outside Tokyo?

In Kyoto, Osaka, Fukuoka, Sapporo and Naha there are spaces you can walk into for a day without a membership — several of them inside hotels, from about ¥2,000 to ¥2,200 a day.

.andwork Kyoto →

Kyoto: a hotel lounge near Kawaramachi

.andwork Kyoto, inside The Millennials Kyoto, is a 5-minute walk from Kawaramachi Station. Drop-in is ¥700 an hour or ¥2,000 a day, from 8:00 to 22:00.

Every desk in the coworking area has an Aeron chair, and there is a self-service kitchen.

.andwork Osaka →

Osaka: 1 minute from Sakaisuji-Hommachi

.andwork Osaka, inside THE LIVELY Osaka Honmachi, is a 1-minute walk from Sakaisuji-Hommachi Station. It costs ¥700 an hour or ¥2,000 a day, from 8:00 to 22:00.

Zero-Ten Park Canal City Hakata-mae →

Fukuoka: no booking, ¥2,200 a day

Zero-Ten Park Canal City Hakata-mae is right outside Kushida-jinja-mae Station. Drop-in needs no booking: ¥660 an hour, capped at ¥2,200 a day, and an All Day Pass runs until 9:00 the next morning.

In Fukuoka, .andwork Fukuoka in Nakasu and Zero-Ten Park Fukuoka PARCO in Tenjin are other options.

HOKKAIDO×Station01 →

Sapporo: 2 minutes from the station

HOKKAIDO×Station01 is on the 6th floor of JR GOGO SAPPORO, 2 minutes from Sapporo Station. Drop-in is ¥605 an hour or ¥2,200 a day, on weekdays from 9:00 to 20:00.

.andwork Naha →

Naha: 10 minutes from the airport

.andwork Naha, inside ESTINATE HOTEL Naha, is 10 minutes by car from Naha Airport. It costs ¥500 an hour or ¥2,000 a day, from 7:00 to 22:00, and every seat has power.
