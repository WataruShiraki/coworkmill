Which coworking spaces have private rooms for focused solo work?

Some places line up rooms for one. H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN and H¹T Shinjuku West Exit each have 18 single rooms, and SENQ Meguro has 26 solo booths.

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN →

18 rooms for one person

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN is on the 10th floor of the Ikebukuro Ito Building, with 18 single rooms and meeting rooms for 4 and 6.

It is open 7:00–22:30 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit →

18 private rooms, boxes and 7 meeting rooms

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit is a 9th floor with 18 single rooms, boxes and 7 meeting rooms, plus an open space for 17.

It is open 7:00–22:00 every day.

WORK STYLING Shinagawa →

“A nest for working, built for the best solo work”

WORK STYLING Shinagawa's concept is “a nest for working, where you can do the finest solo work”.

It is on the 5th and 6th floors of NBF Shinagawa Tower, linked to JR Shinagawa Station by a pedestrian deck.

SENQ Meguro →

26 solo booths and a home-like lounge

SENQ Meguro is an 8th-floor space with 26 solo booths and 13 rooms. Its concept is “working while living”.

The coworking is open 9:00–22:00 on weekdays, and the rooms 24 hours.
