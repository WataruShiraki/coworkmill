Office · Tokorozawa, Saitama · Publishing & entertainment

KADOKAWA — Tokorozawa Campus: a village on a 9,000-square-metre floor

In Tokorozawa Sakura Town, KADOKAWA built a single floor of about 9,000 square metres for some 1,000 people. The concept — diversity made by terrain — won the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry Award, the top prize of the 34th Nikkei New Office Awards.

Office · Chiba · Fashion e-commerce

ZOZO — a headquarters that fits like clothing

ZOZO, which runs the fashion marketplace ZOZOTOWN, opened its headquarters in December 2020 on Gakuen-dōri near Chiba University, designed by Hiroshi Nakamura & NAP with Takenaka Corporation. The boldest decision: no café, no gym, no nursery inside — the town does that.

Office · Roppongi, Tokyo · Marketplace app

Mercari — “CultureHub”: why gather at all, in the age of AI

In December 2025 Japan’s largest resale marketplace moved two floors up in Roppongi Hills and named the new headquarters CultureHub: an office built to answer one question — when remote work is normal, what is worth coming in for?

Office · Shinagawa, Tokyo · Technology

Microsoft Japan — Shinagawa: an office rebuilt to empower hybrid work

Microsoft Japan finished rebuilding its Shinagawa headquarters on 30 June 2022. A company that dropped core hours in 2016 and trialled a four-day week in 2019 turned its own office into a showcase for hybrid work.

Office · Takeshiba, Tokyo · Telecom

SoftBank — “Smart & Fun!”: a headquarters run by a thousand sensors

In September 2020 SoftBank began moving into a new headquarters on the Tokyo waterfront at Takeshiba. WeWork wrote the design concept; SoftBank added 700 works of art and more than 1,000 IoT devices.

Office · Shibuya, Tokyo · Entertainment & communication

MIXI — Shibuya Scramble Square: nine floors “for communication”

The maker of Monster Strike and the family photo app FamilyAlbum moved into floors 28 to 36 of Shibuya Scramble Square in March 2020. A company whose business is communication named its office concept exactly that — and in 2024 rebuilt parts of it for deep focus and rest.
