Nishi-Umeda, Osaka

WORK STYLING Nishi-Umeda — booth seats on a green floor, one minute from Nishi-Umeda and Kitashinchi stations

WORK STYLING Nishi-Umeda is on the 9th floor of the Sakurabashi Plaza Building, a one-minute walk from Nishi-Umeda Station on the Osaka Metro Yotsubashi Line and JR Kitashinchi Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Right by Nishi-Umeda and Kitashinchi

It is one minute on foot from Nishi-Umeda and Kitashinchi stations, and seven from JR Osaka Station.

In the photos, table seats and partitioned booths are set out on green carpet.

What is a booth seat?

A single seat separated from its neighbours by panels or walls.

WORK STYLING Nishi-Umeda has rows of numbered booth seats.
