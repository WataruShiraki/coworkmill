Shibuya, Tokyo

fabbit Shibuya Ekimae — a rental office with TKP meeting rooms on the same floor, on the 5th floor of the Shibuya Higashiguchi Building

fabbit Shibuya Ekimae is on the 5th floor of the Shibuya Higashiguchi Building, a two-minute walk from Shibuya Station. It is listed on the official website of the rental-office brand CROSSCOOP as one of its sites. It has fully private rooms for 6 to about 18 people, a coworking space and TKP meeting rooms on the same floor. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Close to the station, with meetings handled in one place

The website presents it as a property close to the station, two minutes from Shibuya Station, suited to opening a branch or setting up a project base.

Its feature is TKP meeting rooms on the same floor — large meetings, seminars and client meetings can all be handled in one facility.

01 · Entrance and coworking space

The entrance on the 5th floor

The entrance is on the 5th floor of the Shibuya Higashiguchi Building.

The coworking space is a shared area where private-room tenants can work outside their rooms.

02 · Private rooms

For 6 to about 18 people

Rooms for 6 to about 18 people are all fully private, and company registration is possible.

Offices have security systems and high-speed Wi-Fi.

03 · TKP meeting rooms and the building

Meeting rooms on the same floor

TKP meeting rooms on the same floor handle large meetings and seminars.

It is on the east side of Shibuya Station, a two-minute walk away.

What is fabbit?

The name of this facility. The operating brand CROSSCOOP's website writes it as “fabbit Shibuya Ekimae”.

In CROSSCOOP's list, it is one of the sites presented under this name, along with Ginza, Kyobashi and Aoyama-itchome.
