Shinjuku, Tokyo

JustCo Shinjuku Miraina Tower — coworking on the 18th floor of JR Shinjuku Miraina Tower, directly connected to Shinjuku Station

JustCo Shinjuku Miraina Tower is on the 18th floor of JR Shinjuku Miraina Tower, directly connected to the Miraina Tower gate of Shinjuku Station. The building houses NEWoMan Shinjuku and adjoins Takashimaya Times Square, and an underground passage links it to Shinjuku-sanchome Station. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace above the station

The website presents the centre as an exceptionally convenient location on the 18th floor of JR Shinjuku Miraina Tower, directly connected to Shinjuku Station.

The building contains NEWoMan Shinjuku for shopping and dining, and an underground passage leads to Shinjuku-sanchome Station on the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi and Fukutoshin lines and the Toei Shinjuku Line.

01 · Main lounge and pantry

Shared space on the 18th floor

The main lounge where members gather, and a well-stocked pantry.

There is also a reading area for quiet reading and writing.

02 · Hot desks and private offices

Shared seats and team rooms

Coworking hot desks, and private offices for 1–3 up to 50 or more.

Phone booths, lockers and printers are available.

03 · Meeting rooms

Set up for online meetings

Meeting rooms are equipped with online meeting systems.

There is also an event space.

What is a pantry?

A small kitchen-like area for drinks and snacks.

JustCo Shinjuku Miraina Tower lists a well-stocked pantry among its facilities — also a place where members bump into each other.
