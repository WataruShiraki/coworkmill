Akasaka, Tokyo

THE HUB Akasaka — all-private offices at BIZMARKS Akasaka, with a waiting area and tenants' lounge on the ground floor

THE HUB Akasaka is in BIZMARKS Akasaka, a four-storey building renovated in November 2020, four minutes from exit 5 of Akasaka Station on the Chiyoda Line and five from exit 11 of Tameike-Sanno Station. The ground floor has a waiting area for visitors, meeting rooms and a tenants-only lounge, and every office, for 1 to 5 people, is fully private. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Shared spaces gathered on the ground floor

The website says: “The ground floor has a waiting area for visitors, meeting rooms and a tenants-only lounge. Offices for 1–5 people are all private, with furniture and internet included.”

Places to welcome and to gather are on the ground floor, with private rooms above — a whole four-storey building used as one site.

01 · Ground-floor lounge and waiting space

A tenants' lounge and a waiting area for visitors

The ground floor has a tenants-only lounge and a waiting area for visitors, with free drinks.

Coworking costs ¥16,500 a month for the all-site plan, ¥9,900 for the single-site plan and ¥3,960 for the weekends-and-holidays plan.

02 · Meeting rooms and booths

Two meeting rooms for six

There are two meeting rooms for six.

Booth-type private rooms are for calls and online meetings.

03 · Private offices

For 1–5 people, all private

Offices for 1 to 5 people are all private, from ¥78,430 a month.

Move in with furniture and internet already in place.

What is renovation?

Updating an existing building's uses and performance. BIZMARKS Akasaka, home to THE HUB Akasaka, was renovated in November 2020.

Buildings brought back to life rather than rebuilt create places to work in the quieter parts of the city.
