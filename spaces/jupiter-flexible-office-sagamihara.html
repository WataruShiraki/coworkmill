Jupiter Flexible Office — 24-hour coworking in Sagamihara

Jupiter Flexible Office is in Yokoyama, Chuo Ward, Sagamihara, a 15-minute walk from Kamimizo Station on the JR Sagami Line. The ground-floor coworking space has café seats, counter seats, business-meeting seats, individual booths and central seats, plus six lockable fully private rooms. It is open 24 hours. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A ground-floor coworking space where members connect

Jupiter's ground floor is presented as a coworking space where members can connect. With many kinds of seat, you can choose by task or mood.

There is free stationery and cables, a magazine corner and a water server, plus a snack corner stocked with treats brought in by members.

01 · Café seats

Café seats with orange light and paintings

This is the corner where you can eat and drink. The official website describes “warm orange lighting and paintings that evoke nature”. It works for lunch or a tea break too.

02 · Counter and central seats

Seats looking out, and seats under a large painting

The counter seats, looking out through large windows, are popular.

The central seats are marked by a large painting and look out over the whole space.

03 · Meeting seats and individual booths

Seats for meetings, and seats to focus alone

The business-meeting seats are roomy four-person seats, suited to quick business talks.

The individual booths are popular single seats that feel open yet let you focus alone.

04 · Private rooms and prices

Six lockable fully private rooms

There are six lockable fully private rooms; drop-in use costs ¥880 for the first hour, then ¥220 per 15 minutes.

The full monthly membership is ¥13,200 for 24-hour access. There are also weekday-morning-only, evening-only and weekend/holiday-only plans.

What is a fully private room?

A private room closed off by walls and a door, separating your space from other users. It suits web meetings and calls.

Jupiter has six lockable fully private rooms with Wi-Fi and power.
