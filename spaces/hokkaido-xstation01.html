Sapporo, Hokkaido

HOKKAIDO×Station01 — coworking on the 6th floor of JR GOGO SAPPORO, 2 min from Sapporo

Two minutes' walk from Sapporo Station, HOKKAIDO×Station01 (xStation01) is on the 6th floor of JR GOGO SAPPORO. It is run by JR East Marketing & Communications, an advertising company in the JR East group, as a regional business incubation space. The community lounge is open for drop-in with no booking: ¥605 an hour or ¥2,200 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Connecting places and people

xStation01 is run by JR East Marketing & Communications for people working on Hokkaido's local challenges. It has an event space, a coworking space and offices.

The whole facility covers 709 m², of which the community lounge is 289.20 m². It holds up to 90 people.

01 · Community lounge

A lounge with free seating

The free-seating community lounge is open to drop-in users. Seats cannot be booked — take any free seat when you arrive.

There are fast Wi-Fi, power, a printer and free drinks. Payment is cashless.

02 · Single booths

Booths for calls and online meetings

The lounge has single-person booths — good for meetings and calls you would not take in a café.

The official site says that when it is busy, use is limited to about two hours.

03 · Kitchen and events

A kitchen and events for up to 90

The lounge has a kitchen. Light, odour-free snacks are allowed at the window seats.

The lounge also hosts events for up to 90 people.

04 · Meeting rooms

Three meeting rooms and a seminar room

Three meeting rooms — two for 6 people and one for 10 — are open to anyone.

A seminar room, partitioned off from the lounge, seats 50 theatre-style.

What is an incubation space?

“Incubation” means hatching: a place that helps new businesses and companies grow.

xStation01 calls itself a regional business incubation space that connects people who want to solve local challenges in Hokkaido.
