Shimbashi, Tokyo

H¹T Shimbashi Ginzaguchi — a corporate shared office with 12 single rooms open until 23:00 on weekdays, on the 8th floor of the SNT Building

H¹T Shimbashi Ginzaguchi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 8th floor of the SNT Building, a one-minute walk from the Shimbashi-crossing gate of Shimbashi Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, and three minutes from the Ginza Exit of JR Shimbashi Station. Seating is 12 single-person rooms and one meeting room for 6, open 8:00–23:00 on weekdays and 10:00–20:00 on weekends and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms in Shimbashi, open late

H¹T Shimbashi Ginzaguchi stands out for its long weekday hours, until 23:00. With 12 single-person rooms, it suits evening work or tidying up after meetings.

The official website describes it as a satellite-type corporate shared office run by Nomura Real Estate, with no initial or base fee, usable for remote work, as a base for sales staff, and for seminars, training and interviews.

03 · Four-line Shimbashi Station

One minute from the Ginza Line

It is one minute from the Ginza Line's Shimbashi-crossing gate, three from JR's Ginza Exit and the Yurikamome's Exit 1B, and four from the Oedo Line.

It is open until 23:00 on weekdays and 10:00–20:00 on weekends and holidays.

What is a satellite shared office?

A small workplace away from head office, for working while out or near home.
