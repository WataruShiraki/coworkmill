Ueno, Tokyo

NewWork Ueno — a 37-seat corporate-membership satellite office on the 2nd floor of VORT Ueno, 3 minutes from Ueno Station

NewWork Ueno is on the 2nd floor of VORT Ueno, a three-minute walk from the Asakusa and Iriya exits of JR Ueno Station. It has 37 open and booth seats, meeting rooms for six and four, and five enclosed booths for one. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working near a major terminal

NewWork aims to support new ways of working, promote work-life balance, raise productivity and reduce commuting stress, the website says.

Three minutes from the Asakusa and Iriya exits of JR Ueno Station, NewWork Ueno lets people who commute through Ueno work without going all the way to the office.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 37-seat workspace

There are 37 seats of two types, open and booth.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for six and four

One meeting room for six and one for four, by booking.

Choose a room by headcount.

03 · Enclosed seated booths

Five booths for one

There are five enclosed seated booths for one.

Use them for online meetings and calls.

What is work-life balance?

The balance between work and the rest of life — the idea of making room for both working hours and time for family and yourself.

NewWork lists promoting work-life balance among its aims.
