WORK STYLING Yokohama West Exit 21F — a 21st-floor office of wooden shelves, five minutes from the West Exit of Yokohama Station

WORK STYLING Yokohama West Exit 21F is on the 21st floor of the Yokohama Tenri Building, a five-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Yokohama Station, and also five minutes from Yokohama Station on the Keikyu Main Line and Yokohama Municipal Subway. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The 21st floor of the Yokohama Tenri Building

It is five minutes on foot from the West Exit of JR Yokohama Station, and also five minutes from Yokohama Station on the Keikyu Main Line and Yokohama Municipal Subway.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see wooden shelves and desks and counter seats with white partitions.

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

What is a diner-style seat?

Seats with sofas facing each other, like a booth in a family restaurant.
