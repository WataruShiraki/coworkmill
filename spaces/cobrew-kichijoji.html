Kichijoji, Tokyo

CoBREW KICHIJOJI — a “place for co-creation” with 50+ seats on two floors, five minutes from Kichijoji Station

CoBREW is on the ground and 2nd floors of Wellbe's Kichijoji in Kichijoji-Honcho, a five-minute walk from Kichijoji Station. Split into a working space and an open space across two floors, it has more than 50 seats in all, with private rooms, fixed desks, web-meeting booths and two meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

More people who want to work in this town

CoBREW's motto is “more people who want to work in this town”, and it aims to create a “place for co-creation” where people connect and new value is born.

It runs a community that brings together freelancers, start-ups and local companies, and holds consultations and regular study sessions. Some evenings, it becomes a community bar for members.

01 · 1F free address

Seats spreading either side of the entrance

The ground floor is a free-address area, with seats spreading out to the left and right of the entrance.

Drop-in is available on weekdays from 9:30 to 18:00, at ¥660 per hour.

02 · 2F fixed desks and booths

A place to focus, up the stairs

As well as free-address seats, the 2nd floor has fixed desks and web-meeting booths.

Fixed desks cost ¥47,300 a month.

03 · Private rooms and meeting rooms

Private rooms and two meeting rooms

The 2nd floor also has private rooms and meeting rooms A and B.

Private rooms are ¥66,000 a month. Projectors, monitors and whiteboards are available.

What is free address?

A system with no assigned seats: you choose any free seat to work.

CoBREW has free-address areas on both floors, available on the ¥33,000-a-month plan.
