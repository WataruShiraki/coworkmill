Jimbocho, Tokyo

WeWork Jimbocho — a small site with hand-drawn art from the book town, 2 minutes from Jimbocho Station

WeWork Jimbocho is a two-minute walk from Jimbocho Station on the subway. Seven stations are within reach, and the area combines educational and cultural institutions with a busy shopping district. The website highlights the homely community of a small site and hand-drawn art inspired by the book town. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Art born from the book town

The shared areas have hand-drawn art created with inspiration from Jimbocho, famous as Tokyo's book town. Art is woven into the lounges and private offices too, the website says.

Small as it is, it packs WeWork's functionality and comfort into one site.

01 · Shared areas

A lounge with hand-drawn art

Hand-drawn art inspired by Jimbocho brightens the shared areas.

A small site means a homely community.

02 · Private offices

Offices with an artistic touch

Art is also brought into the private offices to create spaces where it is easy to focus, the website says.

Ceilings are 2,900 mm high.

03 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Five meeting rooms

There are five meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

With seven stations nearby, getting around central Tokyo is quick.

What is a parents' room?

A private room where members with children can nurse, change nappies or take a break.

Many WeWork sites have one — part of making it easier to work while raising children.
