Ebisu, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Ebisu — a shared office with a conference room for up to 150, on the 8th floor of the Fujiwara Building

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Ebisu is on the 8th floor of the Fujiwara Building in Ebisu-nishi 1-chome. It is one minute from Ebisu Station on the Tokyo Metro, four from JR Ebisu Station and seven from Daikanyama Station on the Tokyu line. Besides meeting rooms for up to 12, it has a conference room for 13 to 150 people. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An Ebisu base where up to 150 can gather

WORK STYLING Ebisu has a row of meeting rooms for up to 12 plus a conference room for 13 to 150 people — a base that can host large events such as briefings and company gatherings.

It is on the 8th floor of the Fujiwara Building in Ebisu-nishi 1-chome: one minute from the Tokyo Metro station, four from JR Ebisu and seven on foot from Daikanyama.

01 · Entrance and open space

Seats you can take without booking

From the 8th-floor entrance you step into the open space.

The open space at your home site needs no booking.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people. The website shows six photos of them — this site has many.

Choose a room to suit the number of attendees.

03 · Conference room

From 13 to 150 people

The conference room holds 13 to 150 people.

Use it for training, briefings or all-hands meetings.

04 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for focus

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused work.

They also give you a quiet place to prepare between large events.

What is a conference room?

At WORK STYLING, a large meeting room for 13 or more people. The one in Ebisu holds 13 to 150.

Use meeting rooms for up to 12 for everyday meetings and the conference room for big gatherings.
