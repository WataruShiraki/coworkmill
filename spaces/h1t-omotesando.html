Omotesando, Tokyo

H¹T Omotesando — a corporate shared office with 15 private rooms and a meeting room for 10, on the 4th floor of Prefas Omotesando

Nomura Real Estate's H¹T Omotesando is on the 4th floor of Prefas Omotesando, a two-minute walk from exit A2 of Omotesando Station (Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hanzomon lines). H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fees. Built around 15 single-person rooms, this site also has an open space, boxes and meeting rooms for 2–10, and is open 8:30–21:00 on weekends and holidays too. Introduction

An Omotesando base built around private rooms

H¹T Omotesando centres on 15 single rooms, plus 7 open-space seats, 2 single boxes and 3 meeting rooms — sized for 2, 3 and 10.

H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base between sales visits, and for seminars, training and interviews, the website says. The Omotesando site also opens on weekends and holidays.

01 · Open space and boxes

Seats for short stays

The open space seats 7, and there are 2 single boxes.

Use them in short gaps before your next appointment to check email.

02 · Rooms

15 single-person rooms

The 15 single rooms are the heart of this site.

With doors, they suit online meetings and focused work.

03 · Meeting rooms

Three rooms, for 2, 3 and 10

There are three meeting rooms, for 2, 3 and 10 people.

The room for 10 is big enough for small training sessions and briefings.

What does “no initial or base fees” mean?

H¹T's pricing approach as a corporate shared office: no up-front cost when signing up and no base charge when you don't use it.

For companies using several sites, the advantage is paying only for what is used.
