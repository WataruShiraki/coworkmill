Ochanomizu, Tokyo

H¹T Ochanomizu THE GARDEN — a private-room shared office on the 2nd floor of the Ochanomizu Hotaka Building, one minute from the station

Nomura Real Estate's H¹T Ochanomizu THE GARDEN is on the 2nd floor of the Ochanomizu Hotaka Building, a one-minute walk from the Hijiribashi exit of JR Ochanomizu Station. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fees. This small site consists only of 11 single-person rooms and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays. Introduction

A small, rooms-only site one minute from the station

H¹T Ochanomizu THE GARDEN is made up only of 11 single rooms. With no open space or meeting rooms, it focuses entirely on places to concentrate alone.

It is on the 2nd floor of the Ochanomizu Hotaka Building, one minute from the Hijiribashi exit of JR Ochanomizu Station. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work and as a base between sales visits.

01 · Rooms

11 single-person rooms

All seats are single rooms — 11 of them.

With doors, they work for online meetings too.

02 · Uses

A base between appointments

The website says it can be used for remote work, as a base between sales visits and for interviews.

Just a minute from the station, it is easy to drop in even for a short gap before your next appointment.

03 · Hours

8:00–21:00 on weekdays

It is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays and closed on weekends and holidays.

From before the start of the working day into the evening, it fits around weekday work.

What is an H¹T room?

One of H¹T's seat types: a private room for one person.

H¹T also has open space and semi-private “boxes”. Ochanomizu THE GARDEN consists of rooms only.
