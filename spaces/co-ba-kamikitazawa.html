co-coono KAMIKITAZAWA WORK LOUNGE — a work lounge attached to renovated rental housing, 6 minutes from Kamikitazawa Station

co-coono KAMIKITAZAWA WORK LOUNGE is a workplace attached to “co-coono KAMIKITAZAWA”, renovated rental housing in Kamitakaido, Suginami-ku, a six-minute walk from Kamikitazawa Station on the Keio Line. Part of the co-ba network, it has a work lounge, meeting space, meeting room, phone booth and event space, open every day from 6:00 to 24:00. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace attached to homes

The website describes it as “a workplace attached to the renovated rental housing ‘co-coono KAMIKITAZAWA’”.

It aims for “an environment where you can value time alone, take inspiration from meeting others and weave the future together”, the website says.

01 · Work lounge

A shared space open from 6:00

The work lounge is open from 6:00 to 24:00.

Monthly membership is free address at ¥13,200 a month (including tax). Lockers and bicycle parking are available as options.

02 · Meeting space and meeting room

Places to talk and to meet

There is a meeting space and a meeting room for discussions.

A phone booth lets you take calls and online meetings separately.

What is an attached work lounge?

A shared workspace added to a building such as housing. co-coono KAMIKITAZAWA WORK LOUNGE is attached to renovated rental housing.

Having a place to work close to home makes switching between home and work easier.
