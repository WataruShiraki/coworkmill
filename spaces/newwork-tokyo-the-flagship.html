Marunouchi, Tokyo

NewWork Tokyo The Flagship — a corporate-membership satellite office on the 11th floor of Marunouchi Trust Tower N, one minute from Tokyo Station

NewWork Tokyo The Flagship is on the 11th floor of Marunouchi Trust Tower N, a one-minute walk from Tokyo Station. It is a large site with 95 open and booth seats, three meeting rooms for 8, a premium meeting room for 6, and wide four-person and single-person private booths. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The flagship in front of Tokyo Station

“The Flagship” marks this as a flagship location: 95 seats, four meeting rooms and 22 private booths right by Tokyo Station.

The official website describes NewWork as a satellite shared office aiming to support new ways of working, work–life balance, higher productivity and less commuting stress.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 95-seat workspace

There are 95 seats of two kinds: open and booth.

There is a printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Three rooms for 8 and a premium room

There are three meeting rooms for 8 and one premium meeting room for 6, all booked in advance.

Close to Tokyo Station, they also work for meetings with outside guests.

03 · Private booths

Wide booths for 4 and single booths

There are two wide private booths for 4 and twenty for one, all booked in advance.

That is enough to secure a private booth even on days full of online meetings.

What does “The Flagship” mean?

A flagship is the leading ship of a fleet — and, by extension, a company's main store. At NewWork, the sites in the Shibuya Gotoh Ikueikai Building and Shinjuku-sanchome also carry the name.

Tokyo The Flagship is on the 11th floor of Marunouchi Trust Tower N, a one-minute walk from Tokyo Station.
