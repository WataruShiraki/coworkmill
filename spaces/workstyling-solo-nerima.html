Nerima, Tokyo

WORK STYLING SOLO Nerima — single rooms and a café space, one minute from Nerima Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Nerima is on the 8th floor of BPR Place Nerima, a one-minute walk from Nerima Station on the Seibu and Toei subway lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms by the station

It is one minute on foot from Nerima Station.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
