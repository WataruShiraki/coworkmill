Kudanshita, Tokyo

Business-Airport Kudanshita — a workplace carrying on history, on the ground floor of Kudan Kaikan Terrace overlooking the moat

Business-Airport Kudanshita, run by Tokyu Land, is on the ground floor of Kudan Kaikan Terrace, in front of Kudanshita Station. Overlooking the lush greenery and water of the Imperial Palace moat, it is a workplace that inherits and makes use of history while answering new ways of working. Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays, and the serviced offices 24 hours. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Innovation and creativity, in the heart of Tokyo

The website's slogan is “Innovation and creativity in the heart of Tokyo”. In front of Kudanshita Station, facing the moat, it looks out over rich greenery and water — a workplace that carries history forward while meeting the new needs of the times.

Business-Airport is Tokyu Land's shared-office brand, named after an airport where diverse people gather and depart. Kudanshita is the site set on the ground floor of a historic building.

01 · Coworking

7:00–22:00 on weekdays

Coworking is available 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00). It is closed on weekends and holidays.

Address membership is ¥82,500 a month, Master membership ¥38,500 and Private membership ¥14,300.

02 · Serviced offices

Private rooms for 1–15 people

Serviced-office ROOMs for 1–15 people start at ¥176,000 a month; DESKs for 1–2 start at ¥104,500.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting room

Meeting room A for 8

Meeting room A seats 8 and costs ¥3,300 an hour.

Hold your meetings with a view of the moat's greenery and water.

What is a serviced office?

A private office with furniture, equipment and reception staff already in place, rented by the month. Business-Airport calls them ROOM and DESK.

The advantage is having shared coworking and a closed private room in the same building. As the team grows, it can keep working in the same place.
