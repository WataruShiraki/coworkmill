Gotanda, Tokyo

co-lab Gotanda with JPRE — a shared office for creators, with a sauna, on the 2nd floor of the Gotanda JP Building

co-lab Gotanda with JPRE is on the 2nd floor of the Gotanda JP Building. Its motto is “When makers gather, the city changes”. Along with rooms, ateliers, studios and booths, it has nine meeting rooms, eleven online booths, an event space — and even a sauna. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

When makers gather, the city changes

The motto of co-lab Gotanda with JPRE is “When makers gather, the city changes”. As a collaborative shared office just for creators, it is building a place in Gotanda where makers come together.

It stands out for its range of room types: from rooms for up to 36 to ateliers, studios and booths for 1–4, so teams can choose by size.

01 · Reception and open space

The shared areas

Past reception, the open space and shared areas open up.

Flex seats start at ¥22,000 a month.

02 · Meeting rooms and project rooms

Nine meeting rooms

There are nine meeting rooms, and the official website also shows project rooms.

Eleven online booths are available for web meetings.

03 · Studios

Studios for 16

There are seven studios for 16 and six ateliers for 8–16.

There are also ten booths for 1–4 people, from ¥64,000 a month.

04 · Sauna

A sauna in the workplace

There is a sauna inside the facility.

It is a place to warm up and reset your mind between tasks.

What is an atelier?

Originally a word for a painter's or craftsperson's workshop. At co-lab Gotanda with JPRE, it is the name of a room type for 8–16 people.

Giving distinct names to rooms, ateliers, studios and booths is a touch that suits a space made for creators.
