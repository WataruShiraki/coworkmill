Musashikosugi, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING LaLa Terrace Musashikosugi — a base on the 4th floor of a shopping complex, one minute from Musashikosugi Station

WORK STYLING LaLa Terrace Musashikosugi is on the 4th floor of the LaLa Terrace Musashikosugi shopping complex, a one-minute walk from Musashikosugi Station on the Tokyu Toyoko and Meguro lines. It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

A workplace inside a shopping building

It sits on the 4th floor of LaLa Terrace Musashikosugi, one minute from the Tokyu station, four minutes from the JR Nambu Line and six from the Yokosuka Line.

The official website lists this site as available for company registration. There is also a plan for unlimited use of a single site, so it can serve as a home base close to where you live.

01 · Entrance

Through reception to the workspace

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

It is open on weekdays from 8:00 to 21:00.

02 · Open space

Seats of different shapes

The open space mixes sofa seats, desks and other kinds of seating.

It suits both solo work and short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

There are meeting rooms you can choose by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

Each has a desk, chair and monitor for when you need to focus.

What is company registration?

Filing a company's address and other details with the Legal Affairs Bureau so they become part of the public record.

Some shared offices let you register a company at their address. The official website lists this site as available for registration.
