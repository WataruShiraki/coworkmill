Meguro, Tokyo

SENQ Meguro — a home-like workplace built around “working while living”, on the 8th floor of Meguro Center Building

SENQ Meguro is on the 8th floor of the Meguro Center Building. Built around the concept of “working while living”, its appeal is a relaxed, home-like atmosphere, the website says. It has a lounge, a café area, 13 rooms, 26 solo booths, a meeting room and meeting booths. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working while living

SENQ Meguro's concept is “working while living”, and its appeal is a home-like, relaxed atmosphere, the website says.

On the 8th floor of the Meguro Center Building are a lounge and café area, private rooms and solo booths — a base where relaxation and focus share one floor.

01 · Lounge and café area

Shared spaces as relaxed as home

The lounge and café area are shared spaces with a home-like, relaxed feel.

Coworking is ¥22,000 a month, available 9:00–22:00 on weekdays.

02 · Solo booths and rooms

26 solo booths and 13 rooms

There are 26 solo booths from ¥95,700 a month and 13 rooms from 5.40 m² to 19.45 m², from ¥165,000 a month. Rooms are available 24 hours.

From a seat to shut yourself away in to a room for a small team, it is all on one floor.

03 · Meeting room and meeting booths

A room for ten and booths for four

There is one meeting room for ten and three meeting booths for four.

Short meetings in the booths, longer ones in the meeting room.

What is a solo booth?

A small, partitioned seat for one person. SENQ Meguro has 26 of them.

A relaxed lounge and solo booths to retreat into: having both creates the rhythm of “working while living”.
