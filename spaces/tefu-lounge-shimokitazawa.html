Shimokitazawa, Tokyo

tefu lounge Shimokitazawa — a workplace with a café lounge, booths and rooms in a complex right at the station

tefu lounge Shimokitazawa is right outside the southwest ticket gate of Shimokitazawa Station on the Odakyu Line. The official website describes it as “a complex centred on a lounge you can use however you like — for work, study, reading, tea or conversation”. Along with the lounge, it lists booths, rooms, a studio and a cinema. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A lounge to use your own way

The official website describes tefu as “a sharing service for furniture, space and time” — rather than owning spaces and things and consuming their value, the idea is to share them and pass them on to the future.

It is zero minutes on foot from the southwest gate of Shimokitazawa Station. Lounge plans range from easy hourly use to monthly plans you can use as an office, plus a 24-hour add-on.

01 · Lounge

A lounge with a café

The official website describes the lounge as “a café lounge you can use however you like — for work, study, reading, tea or conversation”.

There is power and Wi-Fi, and specialty coffee is free-flow.

02 · 3rd floor

Lounge, booths and meeting rooms

According to the official website, the 3rd floor has a lounge area, a booth area and several meeting rooms.

Booths are individual seats available 24 hours, and meeting rooms hold up to 8.

03 · 4th floor

Rooms, terrace and kitchen

The 4th floor has a room area for small companies and a shared terrace and kitchen.

The same building also has a studio and a cinema, so there are plenty of ways to spend time between work.

What is tefu?

The name of a sharing service for furniture, space and time. Its official website carries the phrase “Share.”

Through vintage-furniture sharing and space management, it aims to support “living true to yourself while sharing many kinds of value”.
