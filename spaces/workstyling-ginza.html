Ginza, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Ginza — a shared office open at weekends, on the 9th floor of Ginza Glasse, one minute from Ginza Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Ginza is on the 9th floor of Ginza Glasse, a one-minute walk from Ginza Station on the Tokyo Metro. It is open from 8:00 to 22:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays alike, with an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

In the middle of Ginza, at weekends too

WORK STYLING Ginza is on the 9th floor of Ginza Glasse, a minute from Ginza Station and three from JR Yurakucho Station.

Its distinguishing feature is opening from 8:00 to 22:00 not only on weekdays but on weekends and holidays. In Ginza, where people gather to shop and eat, you can slip in some work time at the weekend.

01 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space can be used without booking.

There is a drinks corner, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

One minute from Ginza Station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for calls and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls and online meetings.

Separate phone booths are also available.

What is a phone booth?

A small enclosed seat for making calls or joining online meetings alone. Your voice doesn't carry, and the open space stays quiet.

WORK STYLING Ginza has phone booths in addition to single-person rooms: booths for short calls, rooms for longer meetings.
