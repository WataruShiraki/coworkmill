Mitaka, Tokyo

Coworking Space Breath — a glass-fronted ground floor you can bring children to, five minutes from the North Exit of Mitaka Station

Coworking Space Breath is on a glass-fronted ground floor in Nakacho, Musashino, a five-minute walk from the North Exit of Mitaka Station. It has a lounge, working desks, a raised tatami-style area (koagari) and café seats, plus a childminding service (paid). Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Making time to work, with your children along

Breath is a place for coworking and events. Seats for people working and a childminding service sit side by side on the same ground floor.

Part of the lounge can be entered with a pushchair, and there is a changing table and educational toys. It is reachable from Mitaka Station, or on foot from Kichijoji Station along Nakamichi-dori.

01 · Exterior and lounge

A glass-fronted ground floor

The bright, glass-fronted exterior lets you see inside from the street.

Part of the lounge can be entered with a pushchair.

02 · Working desks

Laptop seats and wall-facing focus desks

There are seats for laptop work and wall-facing desks for focus.

A copier and a drinks corner are also available.

03 · Childminding space

A service to watch over children

The childminding service costs ¥1,500 for one hour and ¥2,800 for two hours.

The childminding space is stocked with educational toys.

What is koagari?

A raised area of tatami or wooden flooring, one step above the main floor, where you sit.

The official website says Breath has a koagari space. Somewhere you can take off your shoes and sit is easy to use with children.
