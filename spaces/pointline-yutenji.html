Yutenji, Tokyo

Pointline YUTENJI — a work lounge with books chosen by a bookshop, one minute from Yutenji Station

Pointline YUTENJI is a six-storey creative office in Yutenji 2-chome, a one-minute walk from Yutenji Station on the Tokyu Toyoko Line. Floors 3–6 hold 20 office units, and the 6th floor has a meeting room, a work lounge and a rooftop terrace. Free desks are inside the work lounge, where you can read books selected by a bookshop. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

People grow, the town grows

Pointline YUTENJI is an office developed by Tokyu Corporation, with planning, production and management by Real Gate Inc. Its motto is “people grow, the town grows”.

The ground floor holds a café, a drugstore and a supermarket, and the 2nd floor a nursery. Places for work and for daily life sit close together in one building.

01 · Work lounge

A lounge with books chosen by a bookshop

In the 6th-floor work lounge, you can read books selected by a bookshop. The book selection and Yutenji map were curated by the bookshop SUNNY BOY BOOKS.

It can be used for meetings and work, with Wi-Fi.

02 · Entrance

A paper collage of the neighbourhood

The ground-floor entrance lobby is decorated with a paper collage inspired by the scenery around Yutenji, and with design furniture.

03 · Meeting room and terrace

A meeting room for 8 and a planted rooftop terrace

The 6th-floor meeting room holds up to 8 and has Wi-Fi.

The rooftop terrace, with planting and a wooden deck, looks out over Tokyo. Wi-Fi is available there too.

04 · Free desks

Free desks inside the lounge

The free desks are inside the 6th-floor work lounge. The plan without company registration is ¥25,000 a month (¥27,500 incl. tax), without a company name display or dedicated mailbox. The plan with company registration is listed as full.

Equipment includes power outlets, Wi-Fi, a multifunction printer and a shredder.

What is a free desk?

A contract with no fixed seat, where you pick any free seat to use.

At Pointline YUTENJI, free desks are inside the 6th-floor work lounge and available by the month.
