Akabane, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Akabane — an 8th floor with yellow box seats, three minutes from Akabane Station

WORK STYLING Akabane is on the 8th floor of Pal Road 3, a three-minute walk from JR Akabane Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Three minutes on foot from the West Exit of Akabane Station

It is three minutes on foot from JR Akabane Station.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see box seats enclosed by yellow sofas and a long counter by the window.

01 · Open space

Many ways to sit

The open space can be used without booking.

Seating includes box seats enclosed by yellow sofas and a long counter by the window.

02 · Other seats

Sofa box seats and hanging lights

You will also find sofa box seats and hanging lights.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with yellow-green walls.

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is a box seat?

Facing seats enclosed on three sides by high-backed sofas.
