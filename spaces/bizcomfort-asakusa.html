Asakusa, Tokyo

BIZcomfort Asakusa — 24-hour coworking 2 min from Asakusa Station, near Senso-ji

Two minutes' walk from Asakusa Station on the Tsukuba Express, BIZcomfort Asakusa fills floors 1 to 7 of the Imon Asakusa Sushiya-dori Building in Taito City, a short walk from Kaminarimon. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day. Drop-in costs ¥440 an hour or ¥1,650 a day on weekdays 9:00–18:00, with no registration (the official site currently shows drop-in as paused, so check before you go). Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A renovated building that tells Asakusa's story of change

BIZcomfort renovated a whole building two minutes from the station and says the design expresses the transitions in Asakusa's history.

Booths are split by use: café booths where you can eat, talk and take online meetings, work booths where calls are limited to 10 minutes, and silent booths for focused work.

01 · Free seating

Free desks and booths

There are 63 free-seating desks.

Café booths allow food, calls and online meetings; work booths allow calls of up to 10 minutes; silent booths allow no talking or calls.

02 · Reception

The entrance

The entrance is on Sushiya-dori. A concierge offers optional services.

03 · Focus seats

Partitioned desks

Rows of partitioned desks for focused work.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space for the day without becoming a member.

At BIZcomfort Asakusa, drop-in is ¥440 an hour or ¥1,650 a day with no advance registration. Seats cannot be booked.
