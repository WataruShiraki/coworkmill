Shibuya, Tokyo

JustCo Shibuya Hikarie — coworking and private offices on the 33rd floor of Hikarie, directly connected to Shibuya Station

JustCo Shibuya Hikarie is on the 33rd floor of Shibuya Hikarie, a complex directly connected to Shibuya Station. According to the website, the offices look out over Shibuya, Shinjuku, Shinagawa and Roppongi, the greenery of Yoyogi Park and Meiji Jingu, and on clear days all the way to Mt Fuji. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Tokyo from the 33rd floor

The website presents the centre as a premium rental office on the 33rd floor of Shibuya Hikarie, directly connected to Shibuya Station.

From the windows: the cityscapes of Shibuya, Shinjuku, Shinagawa and Roppongi, the greenery of Yoyogi Park and Meiji Jingu — and on clear days, Mt Fuji.

01 · Main lounge

A shared space on the 33rd floor

The main lounge is where members gather, with a well-stocked pantry and a reading area.

Resident community staff support the centre.

02 · Hot desks and private offices

From a single seat to a team room

Coworking hot desks, and private offices ranging from small rooms for 1–3 to large ones for 50 or more.

Phone booths, lockers and printers are available.

03 · Meeting rooms

Set up for online meetings

Meeting rooms are equipped with online meeting systems.

There is also an event space for internal and external events.

What is a phone booth?

A small private booth you enter alone for calls and online meetings.

Common in coworking spaces as a place where people working in an open lounge can talk without worrying about others — JustCo Shibuya Hikarie has them too.
