Bakuro-yokoyama, Tokyo

MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama — coworking, a studio and a gallery on seven floors of a Showa-era building in Nihonbashi Yokoyamacho

MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama is in Nihonbashi Yokoyamacho, in the Bakuro-yokoyama district between the Kanda and Sumida rivers. It uses seven floors of a Showa-era building: a coffee shop and an upcycling studio on the ground floor, workspaces on floors 2–4, a gallery on the 7th and a permaculture garden on the roof. Three nearby stations are each a two-minute walk away. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working, making and showing, layered in a Showa-era building

MIDORI.so is a coworking community exploring “the future of work in a society that values the individual”. The Bakuro-yokoyama site sits in a district of wholesalers between the Kanda and Sumida rivers, using seven floors of a building from the Showa era.

There is a coffee shop and an upcycling studio on the ground floor, workspaces and a lounge on floors 2–4, private rooms on the 5th and 6th, a gallery on the 7th and a permaculture garden on the roof — places to work, make and show, stacked in one building.

01 · 2F workspace and lounge

A parlour-like lounge and kitchen

The second floor has a workspace, lounge and kitchen. Workspaces and meeting rooms continue on the 3rd and 4th floors.

A free desk is ¥42,350 a month and a fixed desk ¥60,500 (both with an ¥11,000 sign-up fee). Pop-in is ¥2,200 a day or ¥22,000 for an 11-day pack.

02 · 1F studio

An upcycling studio with Risograph printing

The ground-floor studio has Risograph, inkjet and painting equipment, so ideas from the workspace upstairs can be made real downstairs.

There is also a coffee shop on the ground floor — the doorway through which neighbours come into the building.

03 · 7F gallery

An exhibition space from ¥33,000 a day

The 7th floor is a gallery. The basic rate is ¥33,000 a day; creators can exhibit for a week for ¥110,000.

Bakuro-yokoyama, Higashi-Nihonbashi and Bakurocho stations are each a two-minute walk away.

What is permaculture?

An approach to designing lasting ways of living and growing food by following natural systems. MIDORI.so Bakuroyokoyama has a permaculture garden on its roof.

Few coworking spaces have a garden on the roof. People who work at desks can spend time with their hands in the soil without leaving the building.
