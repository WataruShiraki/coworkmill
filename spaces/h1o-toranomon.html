Toranomon, Tokyo

H¹O Toranomon — a lounge overlooking Kasumigaseki, in a building directly linked to the station

H¹O Toranomon, a service office run by Nomura Real Estate, is in Tokyo Toranomon Global Square, directly linked to Toranomon Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, one minute on foot. It stands out for the Connective Lounge, exclusively for tenant companies, and the Personal Lounge, with single sofas lined up along floor-to-ceiling windows. Interior design of the 5th-floor lounge and common areas was supervised by Draft. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A glass lounge opening onto a street corner

The official website describes it as “in the front row of the Toranomon area”. The building was developed together with Toranomon Station, and a level path leads from the ticket gates to the B1 entrance.

Making use of a site with corners on all four sides, it was designed with floor-to-ceiling glass and 3,000 mm ceilings for an open feel — the highest ceiling height in the H¹O series.

01 · Connective Lounge

For meetings and light meals

A shared lounge exclusively for tenant companies, usable for meetings, light meals and more. It can also host meetings with visitors and events.

02 · Personal Lounge

Single sofas by the windows

Single sofas line the floor-to-ceiling windows, with a panoramic view over the Kasumigaseki area. The official website describes it as “a refined space inspired by a first-class lounge”.

03 · Corridors and meeting rooms

Corridors with moving images, and six meeting rooms

Projection mapping showing more than ten seasonal animations is used at points along the shared corridors.

There are six meeting rooms, some with camera-equipped displays and AI speakers with 360° cameras.

04 · Entrance

A double-height entrance

The entrance is double-height, with flapper gates. On the 4th floor is a Conference room holding up to 117 people.

What does “directly linked to the station” mean?

You can walk from the station gates or underground passages into the building without going outside.

H¹O Toranomon's building was developed together with Toranomon Station, and the official website notes that thanks to the direct underground passage, you can arrive without an umbrella even on rainy days.
