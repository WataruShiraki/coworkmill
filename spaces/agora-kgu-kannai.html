Kannai, Kanagawa

AGORA KGU KANNAI — coworking on the Kanto Gakuin University Kannai campus

AGORA KGU KANNAI, a coworking space and shared office, is on the 4th floor of Kanto Gakuin University's Yokohama-Kannai campus, a two-minute walk from Kannai Station. It has a free space, single and double private rooms, meeting rooms and a web-meeting room. Non-members can drop in on weekdays from 10:00 to 17:00. Introduction

A workplace on the 4th floor of a university campus

AGORA KGU KANNAI is a coworking space and shared office on the 4th floor of Kanto Gakuin University's Yokohama-Kannai campus, run by AGORA Inc.

Services include company registration, start-up support, mail handling, a copier, lockers and meeting rooms.

01 · Free space

Shared seats open to drop-ins

Non-members can drop into the free space on weekdays from 10:00 to 17:00, from ¥500 an hour or ¥1,500 a day (incl. tax).

Monthly memberships are Full-time (8:00–22:00) and Night & Holiday (weekdays 18:00–22:00, weekends and holidays 8:00–22:00).

02 · Private rooms

Single and double rooms

Single rooms come in corridor-side and window-side types; double rooms are fully private with their own air conditioning. Both are furnished, allow company registration and include up to 15 hours of free meeting-room use a month.

03 · Shared facilities

Meeting rooms and a web-meeting room

Meeting rooms are for members only, at ¥1,000 an hour. There is also a web-meeting room, a business meeting area and a drinks booth.

Options include company registration (¥5,000/month), a mailbox (¥5,000/month) and a locker (¥2,000/month).

What is a Night & Holiday plan?

A monthly plan for weekday evenings plus weekends and holidays, often aimed at office workers preparing a side business or start-up.

At AGORA KGU KANNAI, it lets you use the free space on weekdays 18:00–22:00 and on weekends and holidays 8:00–22:00.
