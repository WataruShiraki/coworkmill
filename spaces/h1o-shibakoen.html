Shibakoen, Tokyo

H¹O Shibakoen — a lounge of wood and greenery in a hybrid timber building

H¹O Shibakoen, a service office run by Nomura Real Estate, faces Shiba Park, a three-minute walk from Onarimon Station on the Toei Mita Line. Like H¹O Aoyama, it uses a hybrid timber structure as an environmentally considerate office. The lounge uses wood and greenery throughout, with interior design supervised by Line Inc. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A timber office facing Shiba Park

The official website says: “Six lines from four stations. An environmentally considerate service office with a hybrid timber structure, right in front of Shiba Park.”

It is in an area where a town-planning vision centred on Shiba Park has been drawn up, and is presented as a place that carries on the area's culture and history.

01 · Lounge

A lounge of wood and greenery

The lounge uses wood and greenery throughout, described as a comfortable, relaxing space.

Wi-Fi is available in the shared lounge and meeting rooms.

02 · Rooftop terrace

A rooftop terrace for working outdoors

The rooftop terrace has desks, chairs, power outlets and Wi-Fi, so you can work outdoors. Hours of use are limited.

03 · Meeting rooms and private offices

Meeting rooms and offices in different designs

There are many meeting rooms in different designs, plus private rooms for web meetings.

Every private office has its own air conditioning, and walls that can't be seen through from the shared corridor.

What is a hybrid timber structure?

A building structure that combines wood with other materials, such as steel or reinforced concrete, to support the building.

H¹O Shibakoen is presented as an environmentally friendly office using this structure.
