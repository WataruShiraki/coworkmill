Gotanda, Tokyo

WeWork TK Ikedayama — interiors evoking Showa-era nostalgia, 3 minutes from Gotanda Station

WeWork TK Ikedayama is on the 2nd floor of the TK Ikedayama Building, a three-minute walk from Gotanda Station, served by three lines. Its interior is built around Showa-era nostalgia, portraying a neighbourhood that is becoming more international while keeping its familiar old scenery. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Showa-era nostalgia

The interior concept is “Showa nostalgia” — expressing Gotanda, a district growing more international while keeping the familiar scenes of the past, the website says.

Gotanda is home to many leading venture and startup companies, and the website describes new business opportunities arising from exchanges across industries and companies.

01 · Shared areas

A space with a nostalgic feel

The shared areas have interiors that recall the Showa era.

Ceilings are 2,700 mm high.

02 · Meeting rooms

Eight meeting rooms

There are eight meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

In a district full of exchange, it is a convenient base for meetings.

What is the Showa era?

Japan's imperial era from 1926 to 1989, often remembered with nostalgia for the post-war decades of growth and their street scenes.

WeWork TK Ikedayama takes “Showa nostalgia” as its interior concept, reflecting Gotanda's lingering old-time scenery.
