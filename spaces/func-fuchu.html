Fuchu, Tokyo

Share Office func — designed and run by architects, six minutes from Fuchu Station, “growing the joy of work with the town”

Share Office func is on the 2nd floor of the Tama Building, a six-minute walk from Fuchu Station on the Keio Line. Designed and run by Our Design, a first-class architects' office based in Fuchu, it has coworking with 120 cm-wide sit-stand desks at every seat, six private rooms, and a meeting and event space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Growing the joy of work with the town

The name func carries three meanings: fun (FUN), creative (Creative) and bringing a new function to the town (FUNCtion).

The official website lists three values: the individual, coworking and connection. Lose yourself in a lockable private room, collaborate at the big table, and connect with the area at members' meet-ups.

01 · Coworking space

Sit-stand desks at every seat

Every seat in the coworking space has a 120 cm-wide sit-stand desk.

The all-day plan is ¥14,300 a month, and there are also weekday morning, afternoon and weekend/holiday plans.

02 · Rental offices

Six private rooms

There are six private rooms in all, from 5.2 m² and usable by one person.

They start at ¥39,600 a month and can also be used as a business address.

03 · Meeting and event space

A place to connect with the town

The meeting room doubles as an event space.

Members' meet-ups and events with invited guests are held here.

What is a sit-stand desk?

A desk whose top can be raised and lowered, so you can work sitting or standing.

Every seat in func's coworking space has one.
