Kyobashi, Tokyo

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO — coworking linking Tokyo with the rest of Japan, on the 4th floor of Kyobashi MID Building

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO is on the 4th floor of the Kyobashi MID Building, about a minute's walk from Takaracho Station on the Toei Asakusa Line. It is a coworking space where Tokyo meets regions across Japan, and where people, companies (technologies), ideas and information intersect. It has free desks, booths, small offices, meeting rooms and an event space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Where Tokyo meets the rest of Japan

DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO aims to be a place where Tokyo and regions across Japan — and people, companies (technologies), ideas and information — meet. The word “diagonal” suggests connections that cut across the usual vertical and horizontal lines.

On the 4th floor of the Kyobashi MID Building, within walking distance of Tokyo Station, it is a gateway where people arriving from the regions meet people working in Tokyo.

01 · Drop-in and free desk

Use it from one hour

Drop-in is ¥3,300 a day, or from ¥660 an hour on weekdays.

Free desks start at ¥18,700 a month.

02 · Booths and small offices

Seats for one, rooms for teams

Booths start at ¥48,400 a month and small offices at ¥148,500.

You can move from a free desk to a booth to a small office as your team grows.

03 · Meeting rooms and event space

Meeting rooms from 30 minutes, and a place for events

Meeting rooms start at ¥2,200 per 30 minutes, and the event space at ¥55,000 an hour.

It is the stage for events linking regions across Japan with Tokyo.

Why “diagonal”?

The English word that gives DIAGONAL RUN TOKYO its name.

Against vertical organisations and horizontal industries, it cuts across at an angle — a name for a place that links Tokyo and the regions, people, companies and ideas diagonally.
