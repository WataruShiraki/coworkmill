Ikebukuro, Tokyo

H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN — a corporate shared office with 18 private rooms, on the 10th floor of the Ikebukuro Ito Building

Nomura Real Estate's H¹T Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN is on the 10th floor of the Ikebukuro Ito Building, a three-minute walk from the North Gate of JR Ikebukuro Station. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fees. This site has 18 single-person rooms and 2 meeting rooms, and is open 7:00–22:30 on weekdays, weekends and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A corporate shared office built around private rooms

H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. With no initial or base fees, it can be used for remote work, as a base between sales visits, and for seminars, training and interviews, the website says.

Ikebukuro East Exit THE GARDEN consists of 18 single rooms and 2 meeting rooms. Its big feature is opening every day, weekends and holidays included, from 7:00 to 22:30.

01 · Rooms

18 single-person rooms

All seats are single rooms — 18 of them.

With doors, they let you take online meetings and calls without worrying about others.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for four and six

Two meeting rooms seat 4 and 6.

Use them for small interviews and internal meetings.

03 · Access

Walkable from three lines

Three minutes from the North Gate of JR Ikebukuro Station, four from the South Passage East Gate of the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line and five from the B1F gate of the Seibu Ikebukuro Line.

Open from 7:00, it suits the time before work or before heading out on visits.

What is a satellite office?

A small workplace set up away from the head office.

H¹T has many sites, mainly in the Tokyo area, as a “satellite shared office” that companies use for their staff.
