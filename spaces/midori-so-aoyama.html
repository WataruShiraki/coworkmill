Aoyama, Tokyo

MIDORI.so Aoyama — a workspace and bar without fixed seats, built for meeting people, on the 2nd and 3rd floors in Minami-Aoyama 1-chome

MIDORI.so Aoyama is in Minami-Aoyama 1-chome, a four-minute walk from Aoyama-itchome Station. With no fixed desks, it is a workspace that rethinks work by focusing on meeting people. The 2nd floor has a listening lounge and a bar, the roof has a garden overlooking Aoba Park, and the ground floor houses Ohizumi Factory's BROOKS. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Rethinking work through meeting people

MIDORI.so Aoyama is a workspace without fixed desks. The website describes it as a place to rethink “working” by focusing on “meeting”.

From 9:00 to 18:00 it is a salon; from 18:00 to 22:00, a bar. The same room turns from a place of work into a place for conversation as the day goes on. The sound system in the 2nd-floor listening lounge is by KOMATSU ONKYO.

01 · 2F workspace

A floor with no fixed seats

The 2nd floor is a workspace without fixed seats; choose where to sit according to your plans for the day.

A free desk is ¥44,000 a month (sign-up fee ¥11,000).

02 · Listening lounge and bar

A room built for sound, and a bar at night

The 2nd-floor listening lounge is for listening to music on a KOMATSU ONKYO sound system.

From 18:00 it becomes a bar. The lounge can be hired: 89.0 m², up to 30 seated or 45 standing.

What is a listening lounge?

A room acoustically tuned for listening to music well. At MIDORI.so Aoyama it is on the 2nd floor, with sound by KOMATSU ONKYO.

Putting a room for music in a workplace values something other than efficiency. Here, the reason to gather is created by sound, not desks.
