Tachikawa, Tokyo

Tamashin Regional/Future Co-creation Centre me:rise Tachikawa — a regional and future co-creation centre in the renovated former head office of Tama Shinkin Bank

me:rise Tachikawa, the Tamashin Regional/Future Co-creation Centre, is in TAMA MIRAI SQUARE, about a four-minute walk from the North Exit of Tachikawa Station. It is a community hub run by Tama Shinkin Bank, opened in February 2023 by renovating the bank's former head-office building. The ground floor has reception, a café counter and a challenge space; the 2nd floor has an advice counter, private rooms, booth seats, meeting rooms and incubation rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Creating the region's future together

The official website presents me:rise Tachikawa as the “Tamashin Regional/Future Co-creation Centre”, a community hub run by Tama Shinkin Bank.

Through start-up support for entrepreneurs and local companies, business support and community building, it aims to create new industries in the Tama region.

01 · Free space

Eat, take calls and talk at every seat

The free space is open seating where eating, phone calls and conversation are allowed at every seat.

Besides memberships from ¥7,800 a month, there is one-off use at ¥500 per hour.

02 · Booth seats and private rooms

Seats to focus, and rooms with a key

Booth seats are semi-private and suit people who want to concentrate.

Lockable private rooms can serve as a business base.

03 · Meeting rooms and web-meeting booths

Places for meetings

The 2nd floor has meeting rooms and web-meeting booths.

Meeting rooms can be rented for ¥1,000 per hour, with discounts for members.

04 · Advice and incubation

With a credit union by your side

At the 2nd-floor advice counter, you can talk to bank staff about starting or running a business.

Incubation rooms have 24-hour access and are aimed at people in the early stages of starting a business.

What is a challenge space?

A place where you can try selling or showcasing a new product or service for a set period.

On the ground floor of me:rise Tachikawa is a challenge space that can be rented in two-week blocks.
