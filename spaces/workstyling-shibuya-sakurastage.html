WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage — Mitsui Fudosan's shared office with an open space for up to 108, on the 2nd floor of SAKURA Side

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shibuya Sakura Stage is on the 2nd floor of the SAKURA Side of Shibuya Sakura Stage. It is a WORK STYLING site, separate from other operators' shared offices in the same complex. It has a shared open space for up to 108 people and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A shared space for up to 108 people

The website says: “In addition to a shared open space for up to 108 people, secure private rooms and meeting rooms are available as options.” It is a base with a large shared space, one minute's walk from Shibuya Station.

Listed on the website as open on weekends and holidays, it is open 10:00–18:00 on those days. Along with single-person rooms and meeting rooms for up to 12, there are rental offices for teams.

01 · Entrance and open space

A shared space for up to 108

The entrance leads into an open space that holds up to 108 people.

The open space at your home site needs no booking. The photos show many kinds of seating.

02 · Meeting rooms and private rooms

Meeting rooms for up to 12, and rooms for one

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused time.

03 · Rental offices

Rooms that become a team's base

Rental offices can be taken as a team's base.

There is also a lounge for a break outside your room.

Which WORK STYLING sites open at weekends?

The WORK STYLING website marks sites that are open on weekends and holidays. Shibuya Sakura Stage is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays.

It is in unit 201 on the 2nd floor of the SAKURA Side. Look for that unit number so you don't confuse it with other workplaces in the same complex.
