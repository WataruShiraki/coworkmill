Gotanda, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Gotanda — on the 5th floor of A-PLACE Gotanda Ekimae, one minute from Gotanda Station

WORK STYLING Gotanda is on the 5th floor of A-PLACE Gotanda Ekimae, a one-minute walk from Gotanda Station (JR, Toei Subway and Tokyu lines). It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

Generous space by a station where three lines meet

Gotanda is served by JR, the Toei Subway and Tokyu lines. This site is on the 5th floor of a building right in front of the station.

As the many published photos of its open space suggest, it devotes a lot of floor area to shared space.

01 · Entrance

From reception to the work areas

There is a reception at the entrance with crew to help.

Last entry is 20:00.

02 · Open space

Plenty of floor for shared seating

The open space mixes seat shapes so you can choose how to use it.

Switch between working alone and talking with others on the same floor.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms can be chosen by headcount.

With Wi-Fi and a multifunction printer, they suit meetings with handouts.

04 · Private rooms

Single rooms and phone booths

There are single-person rooms.

Phone booths, a drinks and café corner and equipment to borrow are also available.

What is a pay-as-you-go plan?

A contract where you pay only for the time you use, with no fixed monthly cost.

WORK STYLING offers one in 10-minute units; meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.
