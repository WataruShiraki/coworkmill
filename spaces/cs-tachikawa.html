Tachikawa, Tokyo

Cs TACHIKAWA — 24/7 coworking and three meeting rooms for 12, five minutes from the South Exit of Tachikawa Station

Cs TACHIKAWA is on the 2nd floor of the Sunny Building in Nishikicho, a five-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Tachikawa Station. It has a coworking space that monthly members can use 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, private workspaces and three rental meeting rooms each seating 12. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Opening up a place to work in Tachikawa

Cs TACHIKAWA is a shared office that can serve as a company office, a remote-work spot, a base for a new business or sales office, or a study room for qualifications.

Power, Wi-Fi, desks, chairs, a business-card scanner and a water server are free. It is also registered with the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's incubation-facility scheme, INCU Tokyo.

01 · Private-room area and booths

Seats for focusing alone

There is a private-room area and Booth S; private workspaces start at ¥29,480 a month.

Address use and a virtual office (from ¥6,600 a month) are also available.

02 · Rental meeting rooms

Three rooms for 12

There are three rental meeting rooms, each seating 12, and all three can be combined.

Meeting rooms start at ¥2,200 per hour, or ¥1,100 per hour for monthly members.

What is a virtual office?

A service that gives you a business address and mail handling without renting an actual desk.

Cs TACHIKAWA offers a virtual office that allows company registration from ¥6,600 a month.
