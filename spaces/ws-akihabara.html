Akihabara, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Akihabara Chuo — one minute from the central gate of Akihabara Station, with large meeting rooms too

WORK STYLING Akihabara Chuo is on the 3rd floor of the Akihabara Fukoku Seimei Building (formerly Akihabara Center Place Building), a one-minute walk from the central gate of JR Akihabara Station. It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Rooms of every size by a station where three lines meet

Akihabara is served by JR lines, the Tsukuba Express and the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line. This site is in a building one minute from the central gate.

With single rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12, meeting rooms for 13 to 80 and an open space, you can choose rooms by headcount.

01 · Entrance

From reception to the work areas

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

It is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays.

02 · Open space

A mix of seat shapes

The open space has sofa seats, desks and other seat shapes.

Use it for solo work or short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms can be chosen by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

With a desk, chair and monitor, they are for when you need to focus.

What is a pay-as-you-go plan?

A contract where you pay only for the time you use, with no fixed monthly cost.

WORK STYLING offers one in 10-minute units; meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.
