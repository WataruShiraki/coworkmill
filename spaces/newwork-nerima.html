Nerima, Tokyo

NewWork Nerima — a corporate-membership satellite office one minute from Nerima Station, open weekends and holidays

NewWork Nerima is on the 4th floor of the Nerima CR Building, a one-minute walk from Nerima Station. It has 28 booth seats, a meeting room for 8, two wide private booths for 4 and five single private booths, and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A weekend-open site at the ward's central station

One minute from Nerima Station at the heart of Nerima City, it is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

Along with 28 booth seats, there are two wide private booths that seat 4, covering solo work through to small meetings.

01 · Booth seats

A 28-seat workspace

There are 28 booth-style seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Two wide booths for 4 and five single booths

There are two wide private booths for 4 and five single private booths.

It works as a place near home where you can shut yourself away for online meetings.

03 · Meeting room

One room for 8

There is one meeting room for 8.

Because it is open at weekends, it also suits meetings with people who find it hard to get together on weekdays.

What is corporate membership?

A system where a company signs up as a member and its employees use the facility. It differs from drop-in, where an individual uses a space for a single day.

NewWork's official website states that, because it is corporate-membership only, one-off use (drop-in) is not available.
