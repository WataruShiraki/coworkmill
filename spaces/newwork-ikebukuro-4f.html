Ikebukuro, Tokyo

NewWork Ikebukuro 4F — a satellite office with seven private booths and two meeting rooms on the 4th floor of the Oak Ikebukuro Building

NewWork Ikebukuro 4F is on the 4th floor of the Oak Ikebukuro Building, a four-minute walk from the East Exit of Ikebukuro Station. It has 24 booth seats, meeting rooms for 8 and 6, and seven single-person seated booths, with lockable booths and monitors available. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A floor centred on meeting rooms and private booths

NewWork Ikebukuro 4F has a different character from the 7th floor of the same building. Its 24 seats are all booths, with meeting rooms for 8 and 6 and seven single-person seated booths.

With lockable booths and monitors, it suits online meetings and somewhat longer discussions. There is no printer on this floor; members use the one on the 7th.

01 · Booth seats

A 24-seat workspace

All 24 seats are booth seats.

There is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths, along with lockable booths and monitors.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 8 and 6

There is one meeting room for 8 and one for 6, both booked in advance.

They can handle larger meetings.

03 · Seated private booths

Seven single-person booths

Seven single-person seated booths are available by booking.

To keep using one beyond 120 minutes, you make a new booking.

What is a seated private booth?

A small single-person room closed off by a door, where you sit to work. It suits online meetings and phone calls.

NewWork Ikebukuro 4F has seven of them.
