Komagome, Tokyo

H¹T Komagome — a corporate shared office with 13 single rooms, open 7:30–22:30 every day, on the 6th floor of the Komagome TR Building

H¹T Komagome, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 6th floor of the Komagome TR Building, a two-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Komagome Station and the South Gate of Komagome Station on the Namboku Line. Its seats are 13 single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms by Komagome Station

H¹T Komagome is open 7:30–22:30 every day. Its seats are 13 single-person rooms.

The official website describes it as a satellite-type corporate shared office run by Nomura Real Estate, usable for remote work or as a base while out.
