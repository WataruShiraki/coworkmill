Kyodo, Tokyo

H¹T Kyodo — a shared office of single private rooms only, 2 minutes from Kyodo Station's North Exit

H¹T Kyodo is on the 4th floor of the Kyodo Fukoku Seimei Building, a two-minute walk from the North Exit of Kyodo Station on the Odakyu Line. Its only seats are ten single rooms — a base for people who want to work quietly in a private room. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A quiet base of private rooms only

H¹T Kyodo consists of ten single rooms (there is no multifunction printer) — a place to concentrate on your own work.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.
