Kumamoto, Kumamoto

Q-Works KUMAMOTO (Kumamoto) — a workspace under the elevated tracks, open weekends and holidays, two minutes from Kumamoto Station

Q-Works KUMAMOTO is under the elevated tracks of JR Kumamoto Station in Kasuga, Nishi Ward, Kumamoto, a two-minute walk from the station. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workspace under the station tracks

It is under the elevated tracks of the station, two minutes on foot from JR Kumamoto Station.

The open space needs no booking — just use any free seat. It is open on weekends and holidays too.
