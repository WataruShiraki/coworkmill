Shibuya, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Shibuya — a 30-seat coworking lounge halfway up Miyamasuzaka, on floors 5–7 of the Hulic Shibuya 1-chome Building

Halfway up Miyamasuzaka, the slope that runs from Shibuya Station's East Exit to Aoyama-dori, CROSSCOOP Shibuya occupies the 5th to 7th floors of the Hulic Shibuya 1-chome Building. The website describes a place where “the energy of the city centre coexists with a calm presence”. It has a 30-seat coworking lounge, a refresh lounge, six meeting rooms, five web booths and private rooms for 3 to 25. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Energy and calm, halfway up Miyamasuzaka

CROSSCOOP Shibuya is halfway up Miyamasuzaka, the slope from Shibuya Station's East Exit to Aoyama-dori. The website describes it as a place where “the energy of the city centre coexists with a calm presence”.

Within walking distance of both Shibuya and Omotesando stations, it uses three floors, the 5th to the 7th.

01 · Meeting rooms and web booths

Six meeting rooms and five booths

There are six meeting rooms for 4 to 10 people and five web booths.

Use them for client meetings and online calls.

02 · Private rooms

For 3 to 25 people

Private rooms hold 3 to 25 people.

There are spacious window-side rooms too, so you can choose by team size.

What is Miyamasuzaka?

The slope climbing from Shibuya Station's East Exit up to Aoyama-dori. CROSSCOOP Shibuya is halfway up it.

A little away from the bustle in front of the station, it is where things start to feel calmer.
