Ginza, Tokyo

H¹T Ginza — a corporate shared office on the 4th floor of the Ginza Center Building, one minute from Ginza Station and open weekends and holidays

H¹T Ginza, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 4th floor of the Ginza Center Building, a one-minute walk from Exit A7 of Ginza Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza, Marunouchi and Hibiya lines. The official website describes it as “a corporate shared office one minute from Ginza Station, with no initial or base fee”. It keeps the same hours, 7:00 to 22:00, on weekdays, weekends and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A Ginza site with the same hours at weekends

H¹T Ginza is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays alike. With hours that never change by day, it is easy to plan around.

The open space is generous at 18 seats, with three single-person boxes, eleven single-person rooms and four meeting rooms for 2 to 8. The official website says it can be used for remote work, as a base for sales staff and for interviews.

01 · Open space

Eighteen shared seats

The open space seats 18.

02 · Boxes and rooms

14 single-person spots

Four rooms for 2 to 8

There is one meeting room each for 2, 6, 7 and 8 people.

They work for meetings with clients in Ginza and team discussions.

What is H¹T?

A satellite shared office for companies run by Nomura Real Estate. The official website states there is no initial fee and no base fee.

The Ginza site is on the 4th floor of the Ginza Center Building.
