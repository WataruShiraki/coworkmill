WORK STYLING Nagoya — a window counter and lounge seats, four minutes from Nagoya Station

WORK STYLING Nagoya is in the Nagoya Mitsui Building North Tower, a two-minute walk from Kintetsu Nagoya Station and four minutes from JR Nagoya Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

5 photos · © Mitsui Fudosan (WORK STYLING “Nagoya”) · Tap any photo to view it full size

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

West of Nagoya Station

It is two minutes on foot from Kintetsu Nagoya Station, three from Nagoya Station on the subway and Meitetsu, and four from JR Nagoya Station.

The open space can be used without booking.

01 · Open space

A lounge with a red carpet

In a dim space with an exposed ceiling are sofa and table seats.

The floor is laid with red carpet.

02 · Window seats

A counter looking down over the city

Counter seats line the large windows.

From high up, you can see the city of Nagoya.

03 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with tall chairs

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with tall chairs and a monitor.

04 · Private rooms

Private rooms with monitors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: a private room with a monitor and desk.

What is a lounge?

A shared area with sofas and the like, where you can relax.

At WORK STYLING Nagoya, sofa seats sit on a red carpet.
