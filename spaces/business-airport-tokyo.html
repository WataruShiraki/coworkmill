Business-Airport Tokyo — where Tokyo's way of working changes, on the 3rd floor of Nippon Life Marunouchi Garden Tower

Introduction

Tokyo's work style changes here

Business-Airport Tokyo's slogan is “Tokyo's work style changes here.” It is on the 3rd floor of Nippon Life Marunouchi Garden Tower in Marunouchi 1-chome, close to Tokyo Station.

Business-Airport is Tokyu Land's brand, named after an airport where diverse people gather and depart. Members of any site can use the coworking areas of every site.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Sundays and holidays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Sundays and holidays.

Address membership is ¥99,000 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM and BOOTH

Private ROOMs start at ¥396,000 a month and BOOTHs at ¥154,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting room

A room for six

The meeting room seats six and costs ¥2,750 an hour.

Next to Tokyo Station, it is an easy place to meet people travelling in from afar.

What is Address membership?

A Business-Airport coworking membership that lets you use the site as your company's registered address. At the Tokyo site it costs ¥99,000 a month.

Its value is being able to put a Marunouchi address on your business card.
