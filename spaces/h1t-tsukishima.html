Tsukishima, Tokyo

H¹T Tsukishima — a corporate shared office with 26 single rooms, open 7:00–22:30 every day, on the ground floor of the Tsukishima Medical Station

H¹T Tsukishima, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the ground floor of the Tsukishima Medical Station building, a two-to-three-minute walk from Exit 4 of Tsukishima Station on the Yurakucho and Toei Oedo lines. Its seats are 26 single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms by Tsukishima Station

H¹T Tsukishima is open 7:00–22:30 every day. Its seats are 26 single-person rooms.

The official website describes it as a satellite-type corporate shared office run by Nomura Real Estate, usable for remote work or as a base while out.

What is a satellite shared office?

A small workplace away from head office, for working while out or near home.
