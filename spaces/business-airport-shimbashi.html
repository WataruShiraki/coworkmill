Shimbashi, Tokyo

Business-Airport Shimbashi — a workplace full of motifs from Shimbashi, birthplace of the railway, on floors 6–8 of Shimbashi Place

Business-Airport Shimbashi, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 6th to 8th floors of Shimbashi Place in Shimbashi 1-chome. Shimbashi, birthplace of Japan's railways, has always led the times, and the website describes a workplace full of motifs that recall the district. There are four meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Leading the times from the birthplace of the railway

The website writes: “Shimbashi, birthplace of the railway, has always been ahead of its time. Business-Airport Shimbashi is full of motifs that evoke the district.”

A brand named after an airport brings the memory of a railway town into its interior. As places of departure, the two overlap.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM and BOOTH

Private ROOMs start at ¥184,800 a month and BOOTHs at ¥136,400.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Four rooms across three floors

The meeting rooms are 7A (8 people, ¥3,300/hour), 7B (6 people, ¥2,750/hour), 6A (4 people, ¥2,200/hour) and 8A (8 people, ¥3,300/hour).

Spread across three floors, the site lets you choose a room by the size of your meeting.

Why is Shimbashi the birthplace of the railway?

Shimbashi was the site of Japan's first railway station. Business-Airport Shimbashi brings motifs evoking that history into its interior.

When a district's history becomes the starting point for an interior, the result is a space that could only exist in that place.
