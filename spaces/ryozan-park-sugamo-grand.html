RYOZAN PARK Sugamo GRAND — coworking in wood, stone and art, with offices, a shared kitchen and a gallery

RYOZAN PARK Sugamo GRAND is in the Grand Toho Building near JR Sugamo Station. The official website calls it “an office that stimulates the five senses and sparks interaction”. The ground floor is a coworking space, and the 3rd and 4th floors are private offices. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Stimulating the senses, sparking interaction

The official website describes it as a workspace “built from natural materials such as wood and stone, decorated with greenery and artworks, that stimulates ideas and creativity”.

With a professional shared kitchen and a gallery, it can be used for food-related projects, art exhibitions and much more.

01 · 1F coworking space

Natural materials and greenery

Single-site full-time coworking is ¥16,500 a month, available 6:00–24:00 every day.

There are two standing desks and two reclining booths, so you can change posture as you work.

02 · Booths and meeting room

Places for calls and meetings

There are two Framery phone booths and two meeting booths for 4, both free.

The meeting room is bookable, seats 8 and costs ¥550 per hour.

03 · Private offices and shared kitchen

Offices on 3F–4F and food facilities

The 3rd and 4th floors are private offices.

The professional shared kitchen is licensed for restaurant, confectionery and deli production, from ¥2,200 per hour.

What is a shared kitchen?

A commercial kitchen that several people or businesses share in time slots. The kitchen at RYOZAN PARK Sugamo GRAND is licensed for restaurant, confectionery and deli production.

It lets you start developing and testing food products before opening a shop of your own.
