Shinagawa, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shinagawa — “a nest for working, made for superb solo work”, on the 5th and 6th floors of NBF Shinagawa Tower

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shinagawa is on the 5th (reception) and 6th floors of NBF Shinagawa Tower. Its concept is “a nest for working, made for superb solo work”, with a wide variety of distinctive spaces for working alone. The building connects to Shinagawa Station by a pedestrian deck, so you can walk there without getting wet. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A nest for working

The concept of WORK STYLING Shinagawa is “a nest for working, made for superb solo work”. The website says it offers a wide variety of distinctive solo spaces: places that feel open yet calm, places to relax while working, places for deep focus and places for two.

NBF Shinagawa Tower is linked to Shinagawa Station by a pedestrian deck, so you can get there without getting wet. Reception is on the 5th floor, and the site uses the 6th floor too.

01 · Entrance

Reception on the 5th floor

Reception is on the 5th floor, at a wooden counter where crew help you get started.

From Shinagawa Station, the pedestrian deck keeps you dry on the way.

02 · Open space

Many kinds of solo seats

The open space has seats with different characters: open, relaxed, focused and for two.

Choose where to sit according to your work or mood that day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12, and a large meeting room

There are meeting rooms for up to 12 and a larger room for big groups.

Choose a room to suit the number of attendees.

04 · Private rooms and rental offices

Rooms for one and rooms for teams

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused time.

Rental offices can be taken as a team base.

What is solo work?

Work you push forward alone, with concentration. WORK STYLING Shinagawa's concept is “a nest for working, made for superb solo work”.

Even working alone, thinking, writing and talking each suit a different seat. Shinagawa provides a variety of seats to match.
