Toranomon, Tokyo

CIC Tokyo — an innovation center of about 6,000 m² with more than 325 companies, on floors 15–16 of Toranomon Hills Business Tower

CIC Tokyo is on the 15th and 16th floors of Toranomon Hills Business Tower, directly connected to Toranomon Hills Station on the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line. It is the first Asian site of CIC, the innovation center founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in 1999. About 6,000 m² in total, it is home to more than 325 companies and individuals from Japan and abroad. Open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, it has 163 private offices, 100 coworking seats, meeting rooms and 24 phone booths. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A launch base for innovation, built for entrepreneurs

CIC Tokyo calls itself “a launch base for innovation connected to the world”. It supports startups as they grow globally while building a dense community of innovators, investors and companies from around the world. Set in the heart of Toranomon, its roughly 6,000 m² make it one of the largest in Japan, according to the official website.

There are 163 private offices for 1–46 people, 100 coworking seats, meeting rooms and 24 phone booths. Meeting rooms, soundproof phone booths, kitchens, 24-hour building access, bilingual staff, a registrable business address, a wellness area and a game room are all included in the monthly fee. Members can also visit CIC sites around the world as guests at no extra cost.

01 · Venture Café and the 16F kitchen

A 300-person event space and two kitchens linked by a grand staircase

The community kitchen also works as part of an event space for up to 300 people. Every Thursday from 16:00, Venture Café Tokyo hosts its Thursday Gathering.

The 16th-floor kitchen is linked to the 15th-floor kitchen by a grand staircase. It is also used as part of the event space and, the website says, sparks new encounters between members.

02 · Meeting rooms and living room

Meeting rooms each designed differently, and a living room of carefully chosen furniture

Meeting rooms seat 1 to 16 people, and each has its own design. They can be used without limit, and window-side rooms look out over Toranomon.

The living room is furnished with carefully selected pieces — for work, reading or lunch.

03 · Wellness area and game room

From a nursing room and showers to table tennis

The wellness area has a nursing room, a massage room, a nap room and showers, meant to support members' wellbeing and fit around their lives.

The game room has video games, a table-tennis table and board games — a place to refresh, build teams and meet other members.

What is Venture Café?

A sister organisation of CIC that runs events connecting entrepreneurs and supporters. At CIC Tokyo it hosts the Thursday Gathering every Thursday from 16:00.

A place to work and a weekly gathering on the same floor — that combination is why CIC is called a community rather than an office.
