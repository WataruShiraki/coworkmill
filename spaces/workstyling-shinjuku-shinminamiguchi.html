Shinjuku, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shinjuku New South Exit — on the 9th floor of the KI Shinjuku Building, one minute from Shinjuku Station on the Keio and Toei Shinjuku lines

WORK STYLING Shinjuku New South Exit is on the 9th floor of the KI Shinjuku Building, a one-minute walk from Shinjuku Station on the Keio Line, Keio New Line and Toei Shinjuku Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 9th floor in Yoyogi 2-chome, south of Shinjuku Station

WORK STYLING Shinjuku New South Exit is one minute on foot from Shinjuku Station on the Keio, Keio New and Toei Shinjuku lines, and three minutes from JR Shinjuku Station.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, grey walls and black lighting rails are paired with green plants for a calm space.

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

Some rooms have two monitors.

What is a single-person room?

A small room with a door, used by one person.

It suits calls, online meetings and focused work. At WORK STYLING it can be used in 10-minute units.
