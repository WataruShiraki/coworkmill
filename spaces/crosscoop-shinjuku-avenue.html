Shinjuku, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Shinjuku AVENUE — bright private rooms with big windows on floors 4–7, and a 7th-floor lounge open 24 hours

CROSSCOOP Shinjuku AVENUE sits between Shinjuku-sanchome and Shinjuku-gyoemmae stations, three minutes' walk from either. It uses floors 4 to 7, and the website says it has many bright private rooms with large windows. The 7th-floor lounge is free to use 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and there are eight meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Bright private rooms between two stations

CROSSCOOP Shinjuku AVENUE sits between Shinjuku-sanchome and Shinjuku-gyoemmae stations, so you can commute from either.

The website highlights fully private rooms with face-recognition security and the large number of bright rooms with big windows.

01 · 7th-floor lounge

A lounge open 24/7

The lounge on the 7th floor is free to use 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Both wired LAN and Wi-Fi are available.

02 · Meeting rooms

Seven on the 6th floor, one on the 4th

There are eight meeting rooms in all — seven on the 6th floor and one on the 4th — including rooms for 6 and 10.

The Telecube for web meetings can be used without booking.

03 · Rooms with windows

For 1 to 24 people

Private rooms range from 1 to 24 people, in many sizes across the floors.

Many are bright rooms with large windows.

What is a Telecube?

A small box-shaped private booth for web meetings and calls. At CROSSCOOP Shinjuku AVENUE it is free and needs no booking.

It gives you a place outside your room where you can talk without worrying about being overheard.
