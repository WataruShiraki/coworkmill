Shinjuku, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building — a “RETRO FUTURE” shared office on the 11th floor of the Shinjuku Mitsui Building

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building is on the 11th floor of the Shinjuku Mitsui Building in Nishi-Shinjuku. Its design concept is “RETRO FUTURE”. It is a one-minute walk from Tochomae Station on the Toei Oedo Line and six from JR Shinjuku Station, with an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12, single-person rooms and rental offices. Introduction

RETRO FUTURE

The design concept of WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building is “RETRO FUTURE”. The website describes it as “crossing past and future, returning to the origin while building the power to leap forward”, aiming for “a refined space design that never gets old and that everyone can love”.

It is on the 11th floor of the Shinjuku Mitsui Building in Nishi-Shinjuku. SHARE is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays, FLEX gives 24-hour access, and company registration is supported.

Rooms for up to 12, and rooms for one

Meeting rooms hold up to 12; the website shows six photos of them.

Rooms that become a team's base

Rental offices can be taken as a team's base. The website shows nine photos, and there is a range of room types.
