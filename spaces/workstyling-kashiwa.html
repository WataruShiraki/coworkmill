Kashiwa, Chiba

WORK STYLING Kashiwa — an 8th floor lined with plywood walls, one minute from the East Exit of Kashiwa Station

WORK STYLING Kashiwa is on the 8th floor of the Sakura Kashiwa Building, a one-minute walk from the East Exit of Kashiwa Station on both the JR Joban Line and Tobu Noda Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

One minute from the East Exit of Kashiwa Station

It is one minute on foot from the East Exit of Kashiwa Station, on both the JR Joban Line and Tobu Noda Line.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see counter seats along a plywood wall and partitioned counter seats by the window.

01 · Open space

Many ways to sit

The open space can be used without booking.

Seating includes counter seats along a plywood wall and partitioned counter seats by the window.

02 · Other seats

Seats enclosed by plywood

You will also find seats enclosed by plywood.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with black walls.

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is plywood?

Board made by gluing many thin layers of wood together. Its wood grain stays visible.

At WORK STYLING Kashiwa, plywood is used for the walls that enclose the seats.
