Shibuya, Tokyo

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square — floors 37–42 and 45 above Shibuya Station, with 70 meeting rooms

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square is in the East Tower of Shibuya Scramble Square, directly connected to Shibuya Station. It uses floors 37–42 and 45 of the 47-storey building (with 7 basement levels) and has 70 meeting rooms. Ceilings are 3,000–3,200 mm high. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A high-rise workplace right above the station

Shibuya Scramble Square's East Tower opened in November 2019. The 47-storey building connects directly to Shibuya Station, served by the JR Yamanote Line, Tokyu Toyoko and Den-en-toshi lines, Tokyo Metro Hanzomon and Fukutoshin lines, and the Keio Inokashira Line. WeWork occupies floors 37–42 and 45.

There are four plans: Private Office, Private Access, Dedicated Desk and All Access Plus. Reception is open 8:30–20:00 on weekdays. There are phone booths, a parent-and-child room and a café.

01 · 42F lounge

A long shared lounge along the windows

The 42nd-floor lounge runs along the windows, so you can work while looking down over Shibuya. It suits both solo work and quick chats.

With ceilings of 3,000–3,200 mm and high-rise windows, it rarely feels crowded.

02 · Meeting rooms

70 rooms across the building

There are 70 meeting rooms, from small huddle rooms to larger conference rooms.

Phone booths let you take online meetings away from your desk.

03 · Kitchen and events

A kitchen, and a lounge set up for events

The 42nd floor has a kitchen, and the lounge can be rearranged for events.

With a café on site, switching between work and breaks happens on the same floor.

What is All Access?

A WeWork plan with no fixed desk or room: you use the shared spaces of multiple locations. The official site lists it as “All Access Plus”.

It suits people who do not work from one place. Make Shibuya your base and still sit down on the same terms in other parts of the city.
