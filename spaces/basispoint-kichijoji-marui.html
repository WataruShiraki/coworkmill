Kichijoji, Tokyo

BasisPoint Kichijoji Marui — a drop-in coworking space on the 4th floor of Kichijoji Marui, one minute from the station

BasisPoint Kichijoji Marui is on the 4th floor of the Kichijoji Marui department store, a one-minute walk from the Park Exit gate of JR Kichijoji Station and also one minute from Kichijoji Station on the Keio Inokashira Line. The official website calls it “a coworking space you can drop into and use right now”. Because it is inside a shopping complex, it follows Kichijoji Marui's hours: 10:30–20:00 every day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace inside a shopping complex in a shopping town

Kichijoji Marui is a coworking space on the 4th floor of a station-front shopping complex. Once you register as a member (free), you can drop in without booking.

There are three kinds of seat: an open space with tables and sofas, single booth seats partitioned on both sides, and semi-private BOX seats with monitors for 1–4 people. Water, coffee, tea and blankets are free.

01 · Entrance

The entrance on Marui's 4th floor

The entrance is on the 4th floor of Kichijoji Marui.

In line with Marui, it is open 10:30–20:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

02 · Open space

Tables and sofas

Open seats cost ¥330 for the first 30 minutes, with a daily cap of ¥3,300. Monthly plans start at ¥27,500 (All day).

Sofa seats are an extra ¥1,980 per hour, and every seat has Wi-Fi and power.

03 · Booth seats

Single seats partitioned on both sides

Single booth seats are partitioned on both sides and cost ¥625 per 30 minutes.

They suit reading or writing without distractions.

04 · BOX seats

Semi-private, with monitors

BOX seats are semi-private spaces with monitors for 1–4 people, at ¥1,980 per hour.

They suit small meetings and discussions around a screen.

What is drop-in?

Using a seat just for the day without a monthly contract.

BasisPoint Kichijoji Marui starts at ¥330 for the first 30 minutes, with a daily cap of ¥3,300.
