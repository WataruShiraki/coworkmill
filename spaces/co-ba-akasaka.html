Akasaka, Tokyo

co-ba akasaka — 24-hour coworking with roomy desks and chairs for focus, one minute from Tameike-Sanno Station

co-ba akasaka is on the 2nd floor of the Yoshikawa Building in Akasaka 2-chome, a one-minute walk from Tameike-Sanno Station. Calling itself “a calm space for focus, Akasaka's startup base”, it offers a quiet environment and generously sized desks and chairs. Open 24 hours, it has free-address seats, fixed desks, private rooms, meeting rooms, phone booths and an event space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A calm space for focus — Akasaka's startup base

co-ba akasaka calls itself “a calm space for focus, Akasaka's startup base”. With a quiet environment and roomy desks and chairs, you can claim your own spot and get to work, the website says.

co-ba is a network of “shared workplaces for every kind of challenge”, and the Ebisu site is one of them. Akasaka, close to government offices and large companies, presents itself as a base for startups.

01 · Free address and fixed desks

Roomy desks and chairs

Free-address membership is ¥16,500 a month and a fixed desk ¥42,790 a month.

In a quiet setting you get generously sized desks and chairs, 24 hours a day.

02 · Private room

12 m² for 4–6 people

The private room is 12 m² and holds 4–6 people, at ¥143,000 a month.

A small team can have its own room on the same floor as the coworking space.

03 · Meeting rooms and event space

Places to talk and to gather

There is a meeting space, a meeting room, phone booths and an event space.

A one-minute walk from exits 9 and 11 of Tameike-Sanno Station, it is easy to invite people for meetings.

What is free address?

A setup with no assigned seat: you use whichever seat is free. At co-ba akasaka it costs ¥16,500 a month.

It is cheaper than a fixed desk, and you can change where you sit depending on your mood that day.
