Shinagawa, Tokyo

LIBPORT Shinagawa — coworking overlooking a bamboo grove, with hanging-chair nap spots and a café next door

LIBPORT Shinagawa is on the 2nd floor of the W Building in Konan 1-chome, a five-minute walk from the Konan Exit of Shinagawa Station. Across two floors, West and East, are a spacious coworking area, 20 private rooms and meeting rooms. The official website describes it as “a comfortable space near Sony's head office, overlooking a bamboo grove”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A place to refresh heart, body and mind

LIBPORT's official website describes the brand as “a place to refresh your heart, body and mind” — somewhere to switch gears when work gets stuck.

Shinagawa is described as offering “a range of plans, from a spacious coworking area to project rooms of up to 20 tsubo (about 66 m²)”.

01 · Coworking

A spacious workspace

The coworking area spreads across the West and East floors.

Drop-in starts at ¥550 per hour.

02 · Café and bamboo grove

A café next door and bamboo outside the window

A café adjoins the floor.

The official website describes a comfortable space overlooking a bamboo grove.

03 · Naps and meetings

Hanging chairs and meeting rooms

A nap space with hanging chairs lets you rest before getting back to work.

There are also meeting rooms, phone booths and a multifunction printer.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space by the hour for just one day, without becoming a member.

At LIBPORT Shinagawa, drop-in is available on weekdays from 9:00 to 23:00 (last entry 17:30).
