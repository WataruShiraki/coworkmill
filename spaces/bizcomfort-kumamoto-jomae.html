Hanabatacho, Kumamoto

BIZcomfort Kumamoto Jomae — coworking near Kumamoto Castle, 1 minute from the tram

One minute's walk from Hanabatacho tram stop and two minutes from Kumamoto-jo/Shiyakusho-mae tram stop, BIZcomfort Kumamoto Jomae is on the ground and 2nd floors of the JR Kumamoto Hanabatacho Building in Chuo Ward. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 you can drop in without registering. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

By Kumamoto Castle, and handy on a business trip

BIZcomfort describes this as a coworking space in Kumamoto, one minute from Hanabatacho tram stop and open 24 hours a day, every day.

There is a concierge on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00, and the website notes that, seven minutes from Sakuramachi Bus Terminal, it works as a drop-in place to get things done on a trip. There are meeting rooms with large monitors and private rooms for online meetings.

01 · Free seating

Free seating on a green floor

Seats with partitions line a large floor with an exposed ceiling.

02 · Café booths

Café booths among plants

Bench seats in front of plants and a long table; café booths allow food, calls and online meetings.

03 · Private offices

Rental offices

04 · Meeting room

Meeting room

The meeting room with large windows is paid and must be booked.

What is a drop-in?

Using the space for just one day without becoming a member.

At BIZcomfort Kumamoto Jomae you can drop in on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 for ¥440 per hour or ¥1,650 per day. Reservations are not taken.
