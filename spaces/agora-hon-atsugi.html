Hon-Atsugi, Kanagawa

AGORA Hon-atsugi — coworking inside Mylord, directly linked to Hon-Atsugi Station

AGORA Hon-atsugi is a coworking space on the 6th floor of Hon-Atsugi Mylord 1, the station building directly linked to Hon-Atsugi Station on the Odakyu Odawara Line. It has a free space, single private rooms and single booths, with drop-in available on weekdays from 10:00 to 17:00. It is also a base for Kanagawa Prefecture's HATSU entrepreneur support programme. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A start-up base inside a station building

AGORA Hon-atsugi is a coworking space run by AGORA Inc. Its official website says it is a base for Kanagawa Prefecture's HATSU entrepreneur support programme, providing start-up support.

It is on the 6th floor of a shopping building directly linked to Hon-Atsugi Station; follow the signs from the North Exit.

01 · Free space

Shared seats open to drop-ins

The free space is open to drop-ins on weekdays from 10:00 to 17:00, at ¥500 an hour, ¥1,000 for three hours or ¥1,500 a day (incl. tax).

Monthly membership is ¥14,000, and the Night & Holiday plan (weekdays 17:00–22:00 plus weekends and holidays) is ¥8,900.

02 · Private rooms and booths

Single rooms and single booths

Single rooms and single booths are also available for drop-in, at ¥750 an hour, ¥1,250 for three hours or ¥1,750 a day.

Monthly rates are ¥29,800 for a single room and ¥24,000 for a single booth.

03 · Options

Company registration and a mailbox

Options include company registration (¥5,000/month), an online school with start-up consultations (¥2,980/month), a mailbox (¥5,000/month) and a locker (¥2,000/month).

What does “directly linked to the station” mean?

You can walk from the station gates or concourse into the building without going outside.

AGORA Hon-atsugi is on the 6th floor of Hon-Atsugi Mylord 1, which is directly linked to Hon-Atsugi Station, so you can get there without stepping outside.
