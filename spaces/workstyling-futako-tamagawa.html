Futako-Tamagawa, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Futako-Tamagawa — a shared office inside a shopping centre, on the ground floor of Keyaki Court at Tamagawa Takashimaya S.C.

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Futako-Tamagawa is on the ground floor of Keyaki Court at Tamagawa Takashimaya Shopping Center, a five-minute walk from Futako-Tamagawa Station on the Tokyu line. Its feature is being inside a shopping complex rather than a city-centre office building. It is open on weekends and holidays too, with an open space, meeting rooms and single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working inside a shopping centre

WORK STYLING Futako-Tamagawa is on the ground floor of Keyaki Court at Tamagawa Takashimaya Shopping Center. The website describes the centre as a large complex by the West Exit of Futako-Tamagawa Station that opened in 1969 as Japan's first suburban shopping centre.

Unlike the city-centre sites, it is a shared office close to where people live, open 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays too.

01 · Entrance and open space

Entered from the ground floor of a shopping complex

The entrance is on the ground floor of Keyaki Court.

The open space needs no booking, and drinks such as coffee and tea are provided.

02 · Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms in 10-minute units

Meeting rooms hold 2 to 12 people, by booking, in 10-minute units.

They are handy for meetings between people who live nearby.

03 · Private rooms

A row of single-person rooms

Single-person rooms are booked in 10-minute units, and the official gallery shows many of them.

You can come in just for an online meeting in the middle of working from home.

What is a suburban shopping centre?

A large retail complex built near residential areas away from the city centre.

According to the website, Tamagawa Takashimaya Shopping Center opened in 1969 as Japan's first suburban shopping centre. WORK STYLING Futako-Tamagawa is inside it.
