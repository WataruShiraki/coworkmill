Yodoyabashi, Osaka

WORK STYLING Yodoyabashi — long tables and a round-table meeting room, one minute from Yodoyabashi Station

WORK STYLING Yodoyabashi is on the 2nd floor of Yodoyabashi odona, a one-minute walk from Exit 10 of Yodoyabashi Station on the Midosuji Line and Keihan Main Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

One minute from Yodoyabashi Station

It is one minute on foot from Exit 10 of Yodoyabashi Station on the Midosuji Line and Keihan Main Line.

The open space can be used without booking.

01 · Open space

Seats at long tables

Long tables are arranged around large plants.

The chairs come in different colours, such as yellow and blue.

02 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with a round table

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with green walls and a round table.

03 · Private rooms

A passage of doors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: a passage lined with private-room doors.

What is an open space?

A shared area where you can use any free seat without booking.

At WORK STYLING Yodoyabashi, long tables are set around plants.
