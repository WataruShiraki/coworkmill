Tenjin, Fukuoka

Zero-Ten Park Fukuoka PARCO — coworking on the 5th floor of PARCO, linked to Tenjin

Zero-Ten Park (formerly The Company) Fukuoka PARCO is on the 5th floor of the Fukuoka PARCO annex, directly linked to Tenjin Station on the subway and Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) Station. The official site says it was the first shared office and coworking space in Japan to open inside a fashion building. Drop-in needs no booking: ¥660 an hour or ¥2,200 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Japan's first coworking space inside a shopping complex

Fukuoka PARCO is full of culture, food, homeware and beauty shops — and the whole 5th floor of its annex is Zero-Ten Park.

The same floor has a café, a radio station, a talent agency and lesson studios. The operator describes a bright, lively atmosphere that only a fashion building can give.

01 · Coworking space

A bright, lively floor

Drop-in needs no advance booking — register at reception on the day. The operator recommends it as a place to work at weekends and on holidays too.

It costs ¥660 an hour; three hours or more is capped at the ¥2,200 day rate. Phone booths are free, even for drop-in users.

02 · Meeting room and offices

Meeting rooms and private offices

Meeting rooms for 6–8 people have a monitor and a high-quality camera.

There are also semi-private offices open to the ceiling, and fully private offices.

03 · The building

Fukuoka PARCO, linked to Tenjin Station

It is directly linked to both the Fukuoka City Subway and the Nishitetsu Tenjin Omuta Line — an easy place to meet people.

What does “directly linked to the station” mean?

You can walk from the station gates or underground passages into the building without going outside.

Fukuoka PARCO is directly linked to both the subway's Tenjin Station and Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) Station, so you arrive dry even on rainy days.
