Sendai, Miyagi

WORK STYLING Sendai — yellow-green walls and desks with lamps, one minute from Sendai Station

WORK STYLING Sendai is on the 15th floor of Sendai Mark One, a one-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Sendai Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office that, according to the official website, operates without on-site staff. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

One minute from the West Exit of Sendai Station

It is one minute on foot from the West Exit of JR Sendai Station and from North Exit 8 of the subway's Sendai Station.

According to the official website, it is an unstaffed site with no crew.

01 · Open space

Seats by yellow-green walls

Against yellow-green walls are seats with white sofas and black tables.

Counter seats also line the windows.

02 · Partitioned seats

Desks with lamps

Single desks are divided by panels, each with its own desk lamp.

On the wall is a decoration shaped from white tubes.

03 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with a monitor

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with a white table and monitor.

04 · Private rooms

Private rooms with lamps

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: a private room with a desk and lamp.

What is an unstaffed site?

A site run without reception staff (crew) on hand.

The official website describes WORK STYLING Sendai as an unstaffed site with no crew.
