Kyobashi, Tokyo

City Lab Tokyo — an open-innovation base for sustainable urban development, directly linked to Kyobashi Station

City Lab Tokyo is on the 6th floor of Tokyo Square Garden, directly linked to Exit 3 of Kyobashi Station. Opened in 2018, it is a participatory platform aiming to create business for sustainable urban development. Its roughly 300 m² space uses natural materials in the interior with the environment in mind. Through running coworking and event spaces, it fosters connections between people working towards sustainable cities. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An Open Innovation Platform for sustainable cities and society

The official website describes it as “an Open Innovation Platform for building sustainable cities and society”.

Opened in Kyobashi in 2018, it builds networks and collaboration towards sustainable cities by running coworking and event spaces and planning a range of programmes.

01 · Salon

A space built with natural materials

The roughly 300 m² space uses natural materials in its interior, with the environment in mind.

It adapts to solo work, workshops and standing meetings.

02 · Meeting room

A room that also hosts seminars

The meeting room and salon each have a projector, wireless microphone and Blu-ray player.

With the meeting room and salon combined, it seats up to about 120 people.

What is a sustainable city?

A city that keeps environmental, economic and social needs in balance so people can go on living there well into the future.

City Lab Tokyo works on building networks and encouraging collaboration towards sustainable cities.
