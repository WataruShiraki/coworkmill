Sapporo, Hokkaido

WORK STYLING Sapporo — on the 6th floor of Sapporo Mitsui JP Building, two minutes from Sapporo subway station

WORK STYLING Sapporo is on the 6th floor of the Sapporo Mitsui JP Building, a two-minute walk from Exit 11 of Sapporo Station on the Sapporo Municipal Subway Namboku Line and five minutes from Exit 2 of Odori Station on the Tozai Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Two minutes on foot from the subway's Sapporo Station

It is two minutes on foot from Exit 11 of Sapporo Station on the Namboku subway line and five from Exit 2 of Odori Station on the Tozai Line. JR Sapporo Station is ten minutes away.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see tall wooden tables in front of white curtains and seats at a large wooden table.

02 · Other seats

Desks with wooden partitions

Rooms for meetings

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is a high table?

A table at a height for standing or for tall chairs.
