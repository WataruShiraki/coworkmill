Ikebukuro, Tokyo

NewWork Ikebukuro East Exit — a satellite office with 15 private booths, open weekends, on the 9th floor of the Kikumura 91 Building

NewWork Ikebukuro East Exit is on the 9th floor of the Kikumura 91 Building, three minutes from the East Exit of JR Ikebukuro Station and one minute from exit 23 of the Tokyo Metro station. It has 40 booth seats, two meeting rooms for six and 15 enclosed booths for one, and opens on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site with 15 private booths

What sets NewWork Ikebukuro East Exit apart is its 15 enclosed booths for one. With 40 booth seats, it provides plenty of places to work alone.

One minute from Tokyo Metro exit 23, it keeps rainy-day walks short and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.

01 · Booth seats

A 40-seat workspace

There are 40 booth seats.

A multifunction printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths are available.

02 · Enclosed seated booths

15 booths for one

There are 15 enclosed seated booths for one, used by booking.

Focus on your work alone for a good stretch of time.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for six

Two meeting rooms for six are available by booking.

Use them for meetings with clients in Ikebukuro.

What is drop-in?

Using a space for just the day, without becoming a member.

NewWork is corporate-membership only, so drop-in is not available: a company joins and its employees use the sites.
