Miyazaki, Miyazaki

ATOMica Miyazaki — on the 8th floor of Miyazaki Nanairo East, open 10:00–18:00 every day

ATOMica Miyazaki is on the 8th floor of Miyazaki Nanairo East Building in Tachibana-dori-nishi, Miyazaki, a ten-minute walk from JR Miyazaki Station. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.
