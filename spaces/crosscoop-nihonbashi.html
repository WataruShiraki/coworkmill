Nihonbashi, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Nihonbashi — a 43-seat lounge and nine phone booths on the 2nd and 3rd floors of Nihonbashi 3-chome Square

CROSSCOOP Nihonbashi is on the 2nd and 3rd floors of Nihonbashi 3-chome Square, two minutes from Nihombashi Station and seven from Tokyo Station. The website calls it “a newly built, high-grade rental office”. It has a lounge of about 43 seats, a coworking space, a refresh lounge, four meeting rooms, nine phone booths and private rooms for 10, 20 and more. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A newly built, high-grade office

The website introduces CROSSCOOP Nihonbashi as “a newly built, high-grade rental office with company registration, fully private rooms, Wi-Fi, a lounge and meeting rooms”.

It uses the 2nd and 3rd floors of Nihonbashi 3-chome Square, and the shared facilities are open 24 hours. Nihombashi, Tokyo, Kyobashi and Mitsukoshimae stations are all within walking distance.

01 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Four meeting rooms and nine booths

There are four meeting rooms and nine phone booths.

Client meetings and online calls can all be handled on the floor.

02 · Private rooms

Rooms for 10, 20 and more

Private rooms include an 11-person room of 24.3 m² and a 20-person room.

All are fully private, and company registration is possible.

What is a refresh lounge?

A shared space for taking breaks between work. CROSSCOOP Nihonbashi has one, separate from the lounge and coworking area.

Keeping seats for work and seats for rest apart makes it easier to switch your mind.
