Seijogakuen-mae, Tokyo

WORK STYLING SOLO Seijogakuen-mae — single rooms and a café space, one minute from Seijogakuen-mae Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Seijogakuen-mae is on the 4th floor of the SMBC Seijo Building, a one-minute walk from Seijogakuen-mae Station on the Odakyu Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A one-minute walk from Seijogakuen-mae Station on the Odakyu Line.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
