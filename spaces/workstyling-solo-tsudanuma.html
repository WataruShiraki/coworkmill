Tsudanuma, Chiba

WORK STYLING SOLO Tsudanuma — an orange café space and private rooms, three minutes from Tsudanuma Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Tsudanuma is on the 8th floor of the Shizen Center Building Tsudanuma, a three-minute walk from JR Tsudanuma Station and five minutes from Shin-Tsudanuma Station on the Shin-Keisei Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A three-minute walk from JR Tsudanuma Station and five minutes from Shin-Tsudanuma Station on the Shin-Keisei Line.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
