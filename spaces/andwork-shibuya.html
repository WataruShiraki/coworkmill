.andwork Shibuya — hybrid coworking in the lounge of The Millennials Shibuya hotel

About a six-minute walk from Shibuya Station, .andwork Shibuya is inside The Millennials Shibuya hotel in Jinnan. The hotel lounge doubles as the workspace — a “hotel hybrid” coworking space where travellers from around the world and local workers share the same room. Drop-in is available from 7:00 to 24:00. Introduction

A global community of workers from around the world

.andwork describes itself as a workplace where striking interior design, good music and conversations with guests and workers from all over the world help you get more creative. The idea is to bring the lift of being on a trip into an ordinary working day.

In Shibuya, the space is inside The Millennials Shibuya. Hotel guests and local members mix in an international lounge, and every evening a happy hour with free beer gets new conversations going.

01 · Kitchen

A self-service kitchen, open around the clock

The self-service kitchen next to the lounge has all the basic cooking tools, so you can make your own meals.

A free coffee machine serves coffee, tea and cocoa 24 hours a day.

02 · Phone booths

Private booths, free to use

The phone booths are free to use — for online meetings and calls without worrying about the people around you.

They can be booked from My Page. Monthly membership costs ¥29,000–34,000 and gives 24-hour access.

What is “hotel hybrid” coworking?

A coworking space set inside a hotel, so the hotel's facilities and the workspace are used together. .andwork calls this its signature style.

Working in a lounge full of travellers brings the feeling of being on a trip into everyday life — a way of putting the extraordinary into the workplace.
