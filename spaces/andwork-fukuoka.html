Nakasu-Kawabata, Fukuoka

.andwork Fukuoka — a double-height lounge at THE LIVELY Fukuoka Hakata, 1 min from Nakasu-Kawabata

A one-minute walk from Nakasu-Kawabata Station on the Airport Line, .andwork Fukuoka is inside THE LIVELY Fukuoka Hakata hotel in Nakasu. It has a double-height lobby bar, a lounge, a coworking space on the 2nd floor and an all-day dining restaurant. Drop-in runs from 8:00 to 22:00 and costs ¥600 an hour or ¥2,000 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace that sparks ideas, leading Fukuoka's out-of-the-ordinary experiences

An open, double-height lobby bar and lounge, a coworking space and an all-day dining restaurant.

A café-bar, a relaxed lobby lounge and a working lounge with fast Wi-Fi and power — with live sound and video installations creating a lively atmosphere for everyone who passes through.

01 · Lobby bar

A double-height lobby bar

Past the entrance opens a double-height lobby bar serving café sweets and drinks.

At the evening happy hour, draught beer is free.

02 · Coworking space

LIVERALLY on the 2nd floor

LIVERALLY on the 2nd floor is the coworking space, with tables with power and the hotel's fast Wi-Fi.

The official site says the Wi-Fi tops 100 Mbps both up and down. There is a free coffee machine.

03 · Restaurant

An 80-seat all-day dining restaurant

An 80-seat all-day dining restaurant with a Japanese-Italian theme.

It works for a meal alone or with a group.

04 · The building

1 minute from Nakasu-Kawabata Station

It is a one-minute walk from Nakasu-Kawabata Station on the Airport Line, and 20 minutes by taxi from Fukuoka Airport.

What does double-height mean?

A space where the floor above has been left out, so one room rises through two storeys.

The lobby bar at .andwork Fukuoka is double-height, with a high, open ceiling.
