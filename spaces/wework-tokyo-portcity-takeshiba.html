Takeshiba, Tokyo

WeWork Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba — linked by deck to Hamamatsucho Station, in a “New York × Japanese” space

WeWork Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba is on the 10th floor of Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba, the building at the centre of the Takeshiba smart city project. Pedestrian decks connect it to JR Hamamatsucho Station and Takeshiba Station on the Yurikamome line, so you can arrive without getting wet. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A “New York × Japanese” design

The official website describes a space themed “New York × Japanese”, designed so you can feel nature and art around you.

The building aims for top-level environmental performance in Japan, and its common areas are presented as open, with garden views.

01 · Common area

A space with nature and art

The common area follows the “New York × Japanese” theme.

Ceilings are on the high side, from 2,900 mm to 3,200 mm.

02 · Meeting rooms

14 meeting rooms

There are 14 meeting rooms.

The official website also mentions phone booths and a parents' room.

What is a smart city?

Urban development that uses digital technology and data to make living and working in a district more convenient.

In Takeshiba, this effort is centred on Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba.
