Kanda, Tokyo

BIRTH WORK Kanda — a “grow-with-you” flexible office on the 4th, 6th and 7th floors of the Kanda Takagi Building

BIRTH WORK Kanda is in the Kanda Takagi Building in Kanda-Nishikicho 1-chome. It aims “to promote company growth as Japan's first ‘growth-type free-working office’”, with private rooms for 3–8 people on the 4th and 7th floors and a workspace with a view on the 6th. It can be entered 24 hours a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A growth-type free-working office

BIRTH WORK Kanda aims “to promote company growth as Japan's first ‘growth-type free-working office’”. From a workspace for one to private rooms for 3–8 people, companies can move rooms within the same building as they grow.

BIRTH is Takagi Building's brand, with sites in Azabu-Juban and Toranomon as well. In Kanda it uses three floors: the 4th, 6th and 7th.

01 · 6F workspace

Window seats with a view, and sofa seats

The 6th floor is the workspace, with “window seats with a good view and comfortable sofa seats”, the website says.

It can be booked out for events of about 30 people. Free working costs ¥27,500 a month and the BIRTHERs plan ¥38,500.

02 · 4F private rooms

Four bright rooms facing the windows

The 4th floor has four private rooms for 3–8 people, all bright offices facing the windows.

Private rooms cost ¥104,500 to ¥231,000 a month.

03 · 7F private rooms and lounge

Three private rooms and a lounge-style shared area

The 7th floor has three private rooms for 3–8 people, and the shared area is set up as a lounge.

Teams working in private rooms can take a break in the shared lounge.

What is a growth-type free-working office?

BIRTH WORK Kanda's own term: start at a workspace on your own, then move into private rooms in the same building as the company grows.

Growing without moving is a big advantage for a small company.
