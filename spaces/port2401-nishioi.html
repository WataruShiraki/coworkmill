Nishi-Oi, Tokyo

PORT2401 (Shinagawa City Nishi-Oi Startup Support Center) — Shinagawa City's start-up support coworking, one minute from Nishi-Oi Station

PORT2401, the Shinagawa City Nishi-Oi Startup Support Center, is on the 2nd floor of J Tower Nishi-Oi East Tower, a one-minute walk from Nishi-Oi Station on the JR Yokosuka Line. For entrepreneurs from before founding to under three years in business, it offers a coworking space, multipurpose space, meeting rooms, a kitchen and more. Drop-in costs ¥2,000 a day for general users. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Your start-up voyage begins here

PORT2401 is a Shinagawa City facility that supports entrepreneurs — from before founding to under three years in business — from launch to growth.

The official website says it aims to create an environment where you can get advice, connect, try things out and learn. It is run by Noot Ltd. on commission from Shinagawa City.

01 · Coworking

A workspace surrounded by plants

The coworking space is for focused work. The official website describes it as surrounded by plants and designed to help you concentrate.

Monthly membership is ¥8,000 (general) or ¥5,000 (students). Drop-in is ¥2,000 a day (general) or ¥1,300 (students).

02 · Multipurpose space

For networking and events

The multipurpose space is normally used for networking and work, and can be rented as an event venue at ¥2,000 per hour for private use.

03 · Meeting rooms and kitchen

Rooms for meetings and breaks

Meeting rooms are bookable, with rooms for 6 and 8 that can be combined for more than ten people, at ¥300 per hour.

The kitchen space is free to use for coffee breaks and lunch.

04 · Pop-up and café

A place to try things, and a place to meet

In the pop-up space, members can sell their products or display information about their services.

The café space is used for quick meetings between members and outside guests, and as a waiting area for visitors. A community manager is stationed at reception.

What is a startup support center?

A facility set up by a local government or similar body for people thinking of starting a business or who have just started one. Besides workspace, it offers advice and networking.

PORT2401 is run by Shinagawa City, and puts spaces for trying things out and learning on the same floor as its coworking.
