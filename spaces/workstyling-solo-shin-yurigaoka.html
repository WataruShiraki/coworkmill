WORK STYLING SOLO Shin-Yurigaoka — single rooms and a café space, one minute from Shin-Yurigaoka Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Shin-Yurigaoka is on the 3rd floor of the Shin-Yurigaoka City Building, a one-minute walk from Shin-Yurigaoka Station on the Odakyu Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A one-minute walk from Shin-Yurigaoka Station on the Odakyu Line.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
