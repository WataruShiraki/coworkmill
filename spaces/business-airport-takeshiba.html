Takeshiba, Tokyo

Business-Airport Takeshiba — a “water airport” in a smart city, on the 8th floor of Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba Office Tower

Business-Airport Takeshiba, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 8th floor of Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba Office Tower. The website calls it “a water airport floating on Tokyo Bay”: in Takeshiba, a district gaining attention as a new hub for international business, it aims to be a space where people versed in cutting-edge technology inspire one another. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A water airport floating on Tokyo Bay

The website calls Business-Airport Takeshiba “a water airport floating on Tokyo Bay, where new ways of working and communicating are born”. Takeshiba is drawing attention as a new base for international business.

It is a space where engineers versed in the latest technology, gathered in a smart city, can inspire each other. To reach the 8th floor, take the dedicated “Creation Port (8–11F)” elevator from the 6th floor.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM and BOOTH

Private ROOMs start at ¥176,000 a month, BOOTHs at ¥110,000 and partitioned BOOTHs at ¥385,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Takeshiba

Inside a smart city

Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba was developed as a smart city where engineers gather.

This site's role is to give them a shared place where they can spark ideas in each other.

What is a smart city?

A way of building cities that uses information technology to improve how people live and work. Tokyo Portcity Takeshiba was built on this idea.

Put a workplace in a district full of engineers, and the person at the next desk is more likely to work in your field.
