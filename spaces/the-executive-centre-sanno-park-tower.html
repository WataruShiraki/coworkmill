Nagatacho, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Sanno Park Tower — a quiet 3rd floor directly connected to Tameike-Sanno and Kokkai-gijido-mae stations

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 3rd floor of Sanno Park Tower, directly connected to Tameike-Sanno and Kokkai-gijido-mae stations, close to the National Diet Building and the Prime Minister's Office. The website describes a refined, minimal fit-out and a quiet, calm environment. Everything below is based on the official website.

LayoutPrivate offices / coworking (hot desks, dedicated desks) / 3 meeting rooms / event space

Introduction

A quiet workplace in the political district

The website describes the centre as being at the political heart of Chiyoda-ku, near the National Diet Building and the Prime Minister's Office.

The interior is “elegant minimalism”, with meeting rooms in various formats and a quiet environment set apart from its surroundings.

01 · Coworking and private offices

Shared seats and rooms for teams

There are coworking seats and private offices priced on request.

Hot desks and dedicated desks start at ¥54,000 a month; day passes from ¥5,000 a day.

02 · Meeting rooms and function room

Meeting rooms in different formats

There are three meeting rooms in different formats, plus a function room usable as an event space.

Outside the windows are the streets of Akasaka and Nagatacho.

What is a function room?

A multipurpose room for briefings, small events and more.

The centre at Sanno Park Tower has a function room usable as an event space.
