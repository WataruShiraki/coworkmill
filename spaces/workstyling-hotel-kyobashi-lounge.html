Kyobashi, Tokyo

Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyobashi Lounge — a hotel lounge open 15:00–22:00, one minute from the Yaesu side of Tokyo Station

The 2nd-floor lounge of Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyobashi, a one-minute walk from entrance 24 of the Yaesu underground mall at Tokyo Station and two minutes from Kyobashi Station on the Tokyo Metro, can be used as a WORK STYLING site. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working in a hotel lounge

The hotel's 2nd-floor lounge can be used from 15:00 to 22:00, and is open at weekends too.

In the photos you can see a large table, sofa seats and plants.

What is a lounge?

A large room in a hotel, airport or similar place where people relax.

At Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyobashi, the 2nd-floor lounge can be used as a place to work.
