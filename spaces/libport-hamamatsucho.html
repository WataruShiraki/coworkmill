Hamamatsucho, Tokyo

LIBPORT Hamamatsucho — a 24-hour workspace 20 seconds from the station, with a view of the monorail

LIBPORT Hamamatsucho is a one-minute walk from Hamamatsucho Station — “20 seconds from the station”, in the official website's words. In a building with one basement level and seven floors above ground, it has 12 free-address seats, 28 private rooms, three web-meeting booths, a phone box and two meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

20 seconds from the station, 24 hours a day

The official website describes LIBPORT Hamamatsucho as “a workspace 20 seconds from the station where you can work freely, available 24 hours”.

The brand idea is “a place to refresh your heart, body and mind”. Hamamatsucho has a viewing space overlooking the monorail and a lounge exclusively for Premium Room users.

01 · Free address

Twelve coworking seats

Coworking members can use the 12 free-address seats.

Drop-in starts at ¥550 per hour, on weekdays from 9:00 to 23:00.

02 · View and lounge

A space overlooking the monorail

The official website features a viewing space overlooking the monorail.

Premium Room users have their own lounge, and there is also a nap space.

03 · Private rooms and meeting rooms

28 private rooms and booths

There are 28 private rooms, plus three web-meeting booths and a phone box.

There are two meeting rooms for 6, and storage rooms are available too.

What is address membership?

One of LIBPORT's price plans.

The official website lists it alongside coworking membership, room membership and drop-in (¥35,200 a month at Hamamatsucho).
