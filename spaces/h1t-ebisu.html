Ebisu, Tokyo

H¹T Ebisu — a corporate shared office on the 8th floor of the EBS Building, one minute from the West Exit of Ebisu Station

H¹T Ebisu, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 8th floor of the EBS Building, a one-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Ebisu Station and from Exit 2 of Ebisu Station on the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line. It has an open space for 21, 18 single-person rooms, eight meeting rooms for 2 to 8, and boxes for 2 and 4. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site with plenty of places for small groups

H¹T Ebisu has eight meeting rooms in finely graded sizes: for 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8. There are also boxes for 2 and 4, making it easy to find somewhere for two or more people to talk.

For solo work there is an open space for 21, two single-person booths and 18 single-person rooms. It is open until 20:00 on Sundays too.

01 · Open space

Twenty-one shared seats

The open space seats 21.

One minute from the West Exit of Ebisu Station, it is easy to drop in while out.

02 · Booths and boxes

From one person to four

There are two single-person booths, three boxes for 2 and one box for 4.

They suit short discussions in small groups.

03 · Rooms

18 single-person rooms

There are 18 single-person rooms.

Even on days full of online meetings, you can work calmly in a private room.

04 · Meeting rooms

Eight rooms of different sizes

The meeting rooms are three for 2, one each for 3, 5 and 7, and two for 8.

You can pick a room that matches your headcount exactly.

What is the EBS Building?

The building in Ebisu-Nishi that houses H¹T Ebisu.

H¹T Ebisu is on its 8th floor, a one-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Ebisu Station.
