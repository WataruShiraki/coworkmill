Akasaka, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Akasaka-mitsuke — a shared office on the 4th floor of the Akasaka Nakagawa Building, three minutes from Akasaka Station

WORK STYLING Akasaka-mitsuke is on the 4th floor of the Akasaka Nakagawa Building, a three-minute walk from Akasaka Station on the Tokyo Metro Chiyoda Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

From three stations: Akasaka-mitsuke, Akasaka and Tameike-sanno

WORK STYLING Akasaka-mitsuke is four minutes on foot from Akasaka-mitsuke Station on the Ginza and Marunouchi lines and three from Akasaka Station on the Chiyoda Line. Tameike-sanno Station on the Ginza and Namboku lines is eight minutes away.

The open space can be used without booking. Table seats, sofa booths and partitioned desks let you choose how you sit.

01 · Open space

Tables and booths

The open space can be used without booking.

There are seats at wooden tables and booths enclosed by high-backed sofas.

02 · Partitioned seats

Desks for focusing alone

There are desks divided by partitions.

With no side view of others, they suit solo work.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12.

With monitors, they also work for online meetings.

04 · Entrance

Reception at a wooden counter

At the entrance is a reception with a wooden counter.

Beyond the glass partition lie the work areas.

What is a booth seat?

A seat enclosed on three sides by high-backed sofas or panels.

Although it sits in an open area, it screens you from view. There are some in the open space at WORK STYLING Akasaka-mitsuke.
