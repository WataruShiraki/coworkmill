Yaesu, Tokyo

xBridge-Yaesu — a hub for people who support start-ups, three minutes from the Yaesu North Exit of Tokyo Station

xBridge-Yaesu, run by Tokyo Tatemono, faces Sakura-dori, a three-minute walk from the Yaesu North Exit of JR Tokyo Station. It is a base for “ecosystem builders” — large companies, VCs and others who support start-ups. By day it is a coworking and office space; by night, an event and community space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Not just an office, but a strategic base

The official website presents xBridge-Yaesu as “a new hub created together with the ‘ecosystem builders’ who support start-ups' growth”.

It says the space was designed as a base for start-ups and projects backed by large companies and VCs, where ideas and possibilities cross and grow. One line reads: “This is not just an office, but a strategic base for bringing next-generation innovation into society.”

01 · Entrance

An entrance facing Sakura-dori

The entrance faces Sakura-dori, within walking distance of both Tokyo and Nihombashi stations.

02 · Office space

44 seats in all

Contracts are per seat, with 44 seats in the office space. Internet, printers and other equipment are free to use.

Each seat comes with one locker, and there is a fridge, microwave and water server.

03 · Meeting room and booths

A meeting room and booths, free to book

There is one meeting room for 6 and three booths for web meetings and the like. Both are booked in advance and free to use.

04 · In the evening

An event and community space at night

In the evening, it is rented out as an event and community space for ¥20,000 per hour, with discounts for tenants. A projector, screen and sound system are available.

What is an ecosystem builder?

A company or organisation that helps build the environment (ecosystem) in which start-ups grow, such as corporate venture capital arms of large companies and VCs.

xBridge-Yaesu is aimed at companies and organisations working as ecosystem builders, and at people seeking to create innovation through start-up support.
