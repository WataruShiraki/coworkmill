Marunouchi, Tokyo

Business-Airport Marunouchi — a refined workplace with reassurance and calm, on the 6th floor of the Kishimoto Building

Business-Airport Marunouchi, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 6th floor of the Kishimoto Building in Marunouchi 2-chome. The website describes a refined workplace combining reassurance, status, calm and trust — a space where morale naturally rises. There are three meeting rooms and an online meeting room, and the serviced offices are open 24 hours. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Reassurance, status, calm and trust

Business-Airport Marunouchi calls itself “a refined workplace combining reassurance, status, calm and trust”. A space that naturally lifts morale, the website says, promises the next leap for your business.

It is on the 6th floor of the Kishimoto Building, a long-established building in Marunouchi, with a shared workspace, private rooms, booths and fixed desks.

01 · Shared workspace

7:00–22:00 on weekdays

The shared workspace is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and closed on weekends and holidays.

Address membership is ¥99,000 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM, BOOTH and DESK

Private ROOMs start at ¥214,500 a month, BOOTHs at ¥159,500, and a fixed DESK is ¥132,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for eight and one for six

Meeting rooms 1 and 2 seat eight at ¥3,300 an hour; meeting room 3 seats six at ¥2,750. The one-person online meeting room costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

A calm setting for meetings built on trust.

Why “status”?

The word for social standing and credibility. Business-Airport Marunouchi names it as one of the qualities of a refined workplace.

A Marunouchi address and calm interiors also convey trust to the people who come to visit you.
