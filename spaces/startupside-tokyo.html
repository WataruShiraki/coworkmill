Suidobashi, Tokyo

StartupSide Tokyo — an entrepreneur support centre on two floors in Suidobashi, with 50 coworking seats and 8 private rooms

StartupSide Tokyo is on the 8th and 9th floors of VORT Suidobashi III, a five-minute walk from Suidobashi Station. An incubation facility that aims to raise the “start-up quality” of entrepreneurs, it has 50 coworking seats for networking and events on the 9th floor, and eight private rooms for 2–6, meeting rooms and online booths on the 8th. It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and community managers and incubation managers support entrepreneurs. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Raising start-up quality

The official website states its aim of raising entrepreneurs' “start-up quality”, which it defines as the rates of starting, continuing and scaling a business.

Community managers and incubation managers provide ongoing support for members' business growth.

01 · 9F coworking

A floor for networking and events

The 9th floor holds reception and the coworking space. There is room for networking and events, with a desk-work area at the back.

It is set up as a place to meet people who create innovation together.

02 · 9F work area

Desk seats at the back

At the back of the coworking space is a desk-work area for focused work.

Wi-Fi and power are free, and printers and lockers are available.

03 · 8F private rooms and booths

Including rooms with windows

The 8th floor has private rooms, meeting rooms and online booths. Private rooms include ones for 3 and for 5 (with a window).

Private-room members can also use the 9th-floor coworking space free of charge.

What is an incubation manager?

A support person who helps entrepreneurs' businesses grow — giving advice and connecting them with experts and support schemes.

At StartupSide Tokyo, community managers and incubation managers support entrepreneurs.
