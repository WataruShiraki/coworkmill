Musashikosugi, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING Musashikosugi North Exit — green booths and meeting rooms, two minutes from Musashikosugi Station

WORK STYLING Musashikosugi North Exit is in Musashikosugi Tower Place, a two-minute walk from Musashikosugi Station on the Tokyu and JR lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Two minutes from Musashikosugi Station

It is two minutes on foot from Musashikosugi Station on the Tokyu and JR lines.

The open space can be used without booking.

01 · Open space

Colour-blocked carpet

The carpet is divided into blocks of green and yellow.

There are seats at white tables under round lights.

02 · Partitioned seats

Desks with wood-grain partitions

There are also single desks divided by wood-grain panels.

Each seat is numbered.

03 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with purple walls

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with purple walls and a monitor.

04 · Private rooms

A corridor of doors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: a long corridor lined with private-room doors.

What is a booth seat?

A seat enclosed by walls or sofas, making it feel half like a private room.

WORK STYLING Musashikosugi North Exit has booths enclosed by green sofas.
