Hakata, Fukuoka

WORK STYLING Hakata — greenery from the ceiling and a long counter, one minute from the subway's Hakata Station

WORK STYLING Hakata is on the ground floor of the Daihaku Center Building, a one-minute walk from the subway's Hakata Station and five minutes from JR Hakata Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A ground floor in front of Hakata Station

What is a private room?

A room you can use alone with the door closed.
