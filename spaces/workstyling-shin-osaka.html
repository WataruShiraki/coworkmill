Shin-Osaka, Osaka

WORK STYLING Shin-Osaka — sofa seats among greenery and private rooms, two minutes from Shin-Osaka Station

WORK STYLING Shin-Osaka is in the Nissay Shin-Osaka Building, a two-minute walk from Shin-Osaka Station on the Midosuji subway line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office is on the 8th and 13th floors. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

Within walking distance of the Shinkansen station

It is two minutes on foot from Shin-Osaka Station on the Midosuji subway line and ten from JR Shin-Osaka Station.

It is open until 21:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 18:00 on Saturdays.

01 · Open space

Seats surrounded by plants

The open space can be used without booking.

Red sofa seats are set out with plants between them.

02 · Other seats

Bright seats by the window

By the windows there are also seats at white tables.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with a monitor

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with a whiteboard and monitor.

04 · Private rooms

A private room with two monitors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: a private room with two monitors.

What does “greenery” mean here?

Indoor foliage plants placed around a room.

At WORK STYLING Shin-Osaka, plants are set between the sofa seats.
