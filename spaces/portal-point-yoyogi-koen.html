Yoyogi-Koen, Tokyo

PORTAL POINT Yoyogi-Koen — “URBAN NATURAL” — a work lounge and rooftop terrace where you feel the breeze from Yoyogi Park

PORTAL POINT Yoyogi-Koen is a one-minute walk from Yoyogi-Hachiman Station on the Odakyu Line and two minutes from Yoyogi-Koen Station on the Tokyo Metro Chiyoda Line. Completed in October 2020, the seven-storey building has free desks on the 4th floor, small offices with balconies on floors 4–7, and shops on the ground floor. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

URBAN NATURAL

The official website says that, under the concept “URBAN NATURAL”, the building — where you can feel the breeze from Yoyogi Park — livens up the whole area.

All 17 small offices have balconies, and throughout the building you can feel close to fresh air and greenery.

01 · Work lounge

For meetings and for work

The official website describes an open work lounge that can be used for meetings or as a workspace.

Free desks are on the 4th floor, with plans that do or do not include company registration.

02 · Terraces

A rooftop and garden overlooking Yoyogi Park

There is a rooftop terrace overlooking Yoyogi Park, and a garden terrace.

The official website calls them “perfect for recharging”.

03 · Offices and meeting rooms

Private offices with balconies

Floors 4–7 have 17 small offices of 29.40–55.97 m², all with balconies.

Visitors can be met in the hourly meeting rooms.

What does “URBAN NATURAL” mean?

The concept phrase of PORTAL POINT Yoyogi-Koen.

The official website says the aim is a building where you feel the breeze from Yoyogi Park, livening up the whole area.
