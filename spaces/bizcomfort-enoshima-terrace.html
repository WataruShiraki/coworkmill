Enoshima, Kanagawa

BIZcomfort Enoshima TERRACE — 24-hour coworking with a terrace near Enoshima

Six minutes' walk from Katase-Enoshima Station on the Odakyu Enoshima Line, BIZcomfort Enoshima TERRACE is on the 3rd floor of the Lead Sea Katase-Enoshima Building in Fujisawa, right in front of the Enoshima Aquarium. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and the 1-minute plan lets you pay only for the minutes you use. Everything below is based on the official website.

Seats24 free-seating desks, 5 semi-private booths, 20 private offices

Introduction

Slow working by the sea

BIZcomfort describes Enoshima as a sightseeing spot that is easy to reach and full of nature, and says the interior was designed to bring that calm nature inside.

The café booths use a large roof and a wide terrace; you can eat, talk and take online meetings there.

01 · Terrace

Café booths on the terrace

Café booths allow food, calls and online meetings.

There is a covered terrace with seating.

02 · Focus seats

Work booths

Green work booths inspired by Enoshima's nature. Calls of up to 10 minutes; no online meetings.

03 · Private offices

Rental offices

There are 20 lockable private offices.

What is the 1-minute plan?

A pay-as-you-go plan charged by the minute.

At BIZcomfort Enoshima TERRACE it is ¥6.6 a minute, and once registered you can use any BIZcomfort coworking space in Japan.
