Nakameguro, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Nakameguro — a shared office on the 8th floor of Nakameguro GS Daiichi Building, 2 minutes from the station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Nakameguro is on the 8th floor of the Nakameguro GS Daiichi Building, a two-minute walk from the East Exit of Nakameguro Station (Tokyu Toyoko and Tokyo Metro Hibiya lines). It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, available 8:00–21:00 on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

On the 8th floor in Nakameguro

WORK STYLING Nakameguro is on the 8th floor of the Nakameguro GS Daiichi Building, two minutes from the East Exit of Nakameguro Station, served by the Tokyu Toyoko and Tokyo Metro Hibiya lines.

The official site shows seven photos of the open space, a sign of how much the site invests in shared seating. The open space at your home site needs no booking.
