Aoyama, Tokyo

Business-Airport Aoyama — a shared office opened by Tokyu Land in 2013, 3 minutes from Gaiemmae Station

Business-Airport Aoyama is on the 4th and 6th floors of the Spline Aoyama Tokyu Building, a three-minute walk from Gaiemmae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line. Business-Airport is Tokyu Land's brand of shared and rental offices, and Aoyama opened in March 2013. The official website calls it “a stylish workspace that sparks free thinking, in true Aoyama style”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An “airport” where diverse people gather and take off

The name Business-Airport comes from international airports: a place where diverse people gather and depart. It expresses the hope that new businesses will take off from here and spread around the world. Run by Tokyu Land, the brand has 20 sites, including Marunouchi, Hibiya, Shinjuku, Shibuya, Yokohama and Osaka.

Staff are on site during reception hours and security is thorough. Community managers support members, and members of any site can use the coworking areas of every site. Aoyama aims to be “a stylish workspace that sparks free thinking, in true Aoyama style”.

01 · Coworking

Open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays

The coworking area is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on Sundays and holidays. Reception is staffed 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.

Window seats look out over Aoyama. Because it opens early, you can spend an hour here before heading to the office.

02 · Meeting rooms

Five rooms, an online meeting room and an event space for 60

There are five meeting rooms — A, B, A+B, C and D — plus an online meeting room.

The event space holds up to 60 people, and there is a lounge and a break area.

03 · Private rooms

ROOM and BOOTH

Private spaces come in two types, ROOM and BOOTH, suited to using the site as a company base.

The brand-wide price list shows a serviced-office ROOM at ¥132,000 a month, a BOOTH at ¥110,000 and a DESK at ¥104,500.

04 · Location

3 minutes from exit 1a of Gaiemmae Station

It is on the 4th and 6th floors of the Spline Aoyama Tokyu Building, a three-minute walk from exit 1a of Gaiemmae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line.

Opened in March 2013, it is one of the brand's earliest sites.

What is a community manager?

A staff member who connects members with each other and with people outside, and who is the go-to person for events and questions. Business-Airport presents them as “partners who run alongside you”.

Their role differs from reception staff. Whether there is someone who manages seats or someone who introduces people changes how comfortable a shared office feels.
