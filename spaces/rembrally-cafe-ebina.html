Ebina, Kanagawa

Rembrally Cafe — a timed self-service café in a hotel annex

Rembrally Cafe is on the ground floor of the annex of Rembrandt Hotel Ebina, about an eight-minute walk from Ebina Station on the Odakyu and Sotetsu lines. It is a timed self-service café designed for remote work, workations and coworking. There are counter seats by the windows, sofa seats and a Camp Base area for breaks, plus about 500 manga and a free drink bar. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A “third place” with the added value only a hotel can offer

Rembrally Cafe is presented as a “third place” that is neither home nor work. It is a timed self-service café designed for remote work, workations and coworking.

There are no staff on site. You enter, leave and pay through the smartphone cashless service “Threes”. To pay in cash, apply at the hotel front desk.

01 · Counter seats

The window counter, for when you need to focus

The counter seats by the window suit focused solo work. They are the only seats with USB ports.

There are 30 seats in all, with free Wi-Fi and power outlets.

02 · Sofa seats

Sofa seats for working at ease

Sofa seats are for when you want to work in a relaxed way.

03 · Camp Base and manga

A Camp Base area for breaks and naps

The Camp Base area is a place to rest or nap between work. It is not counted in the 30 seats.

There are about 500 popular manga, including new releases, which you can read in a hanging chair.

04 · Drinks and prices

A free drink bar and timed pricing

There is a free drink bar with a range of hot and iced drinks, plus a mini snack bar. You can bring your own food and drink, and the hotel's takeaway menu and delivery are also available.

Cashless use costs ¥250 for the first 30 minutes, then ¥125 per 15 minutes, up to ¥1,500 a day (incl. tax).

What is a third place?

A third place to be that is neither home (the first place) nor work (the second place).

Rembrally Cafe offers a new option for working in Ebina, as a “third place” with the added value of a hotel.
