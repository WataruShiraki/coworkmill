Shinjuku, Tokyo

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit — a corporate shared office with many seat types and meeting rooms, on the 9th floor of the Nishi-Shinjuku Showa Building

Nomura Real Estate's H¹T Shinjuku West Exit is on the 9th floor of the Nishi-Shinjuku Showa Building, a two-minute walk from the West Exit of Shinjuku Station (JR, Odakyu and Keio lines). H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fees. This site stands out for its many seat types — open space, boxes, single-person rooms and seven meeting rooms — and is open 7:00–22:00 every day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A Shinjuku base where you choose your seat

H¹T Shinjuku West Exit has 17 open-space seats, boxes for one and for four, 18 single rooms and seven meeting rooms for 2–6 people. Within one site, you choose the seat type to suit the work.

The website presents it as a satellite shared office suited to people doing sales rounds mainly in Tokyo's 23 wards. Two minutes from the West Exit of Shinjuku Station, it is open every day from 7:00 to 22:00.

01 · Open space

17 shared seats

The open space seats 17.

Use it to check email or prepare documents in short gaps.

02 · Boxes and rooms

Semi-private and private

There are four single boxes and one box for four.

The 18 single rooms have doors and suit online meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Seven rooms for 2 to 6

There are seven meeting rooms: three for 2, one for 4 and three for 6.

Even when interviews and small meetings overlap, it is easy to get a room.

What is an H¹T box?

One of H¹T's seat types, between the open space and a private room. H¹T Shinjuku West Exit has boxes for one and for four.

Use them when you want to focus alone, or for a short talk with a few people.
