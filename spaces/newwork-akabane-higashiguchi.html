Akabane, Tokyo

NewWork Akabane East Exit — a 53-seat corporate-membership satellite office on the 6th floor of the Akabane Minami Building, two minutes from the East Exit

NewWork Akabane East Exit is on the 6th floor of the Akabane Minami Building, a two-minute walk from the East Exit of Akabane Station. It has 53 open and booth seats, meeting rooms for 6 and 4, and four single private booths — the large number of seats is its hallmark. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A seat-rich site at Tokyo's northern gateway

Two minutes from the East Exit of Akabane Station, this site has 53 seats. Built around open and booth seats, it is easy to find somewhere to sit.

There are two meeting rooms, for 6 and 4, and four single private booths, all booked in advance, with locks and monitors.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 53-seat workspace

There are 53 seats of two kinds: open and booth.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Four single booths

There are four single private booths, booked in advance.

You can move into a booth just for the length of an online meeting.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 6 and 4

There is one meeting room each for 6 and 4, booked in advance.

You can choose a room by headcount.

What is Akabane?

The name of a JR station in Kita City and the neighbourhood around it, served by the Saikyo, Keihin-Tohoku and Shonan-Shinjuku lines among others.
