Omiya, Saitama

WORK STYLING Omiya East Exit — a 4th floor with green sofa seats, two minutes from Omiya Station

WORK STYLING Omiya East Exit is on the 4th floor of the Omiya Nakacho Center Building, a two-minute walk from Omiya Station on the JR, Tobu and New Shuttle lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Two minutes on foot from Omiya Station's East Exit

It is two minutes on foot from Omiya Station on the JR, Tobu and New Shuttle lines.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see counter seats with wooden pillars and green chairs and seats at wooden tables with orange chairs.

01 · Open space

Many ways to sit

The open space can be used without booking.

Seating includes counter seats with wooden pillars and green chairs and seats at wooden tables with orange chairs.

02 · Other seats

A long table between green sofas

You will also find a long table between green sofas.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with a monitor.

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is a sofa seat?

Seats with high-backed sofas set facing each other.

At WORK STYLING Omiya East Exit, a long table sits between green sofas.
