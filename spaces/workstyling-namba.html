Namba, Osaka

WORK STYLING Namba — wooden-partition seats and sofas, one minute from Namba Station

WORK STYLING Namba is on the 5th floor of the Taiyo Life Namba Building, a one-minute walk from Namba Station on Osaka Metro and Osaka-Namba Station on the Kintetsu and Hanshin lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

One minute from Namba Station

It is one minute on foot from Namba Station on Osaka Metro and from Osaka-Namba Station on the Kintetsu and Hanshin lines.

The open space can be used without booking.

01 · Open space

Seats under black lights

Seats with wooden partitions line up beneath black pendant lights.

It is a bright area by the windows.

02 · Sofa seats

Grey sofas

There are also grey sofa and table seats.

They are screened by blind-like partitions.

03 · Meeting rooms

A white meeting room

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with navy chairs and a monitor.

04 · Private rooms

Private rooms with black doors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: private rooms with black-framed doors.

What is a pendant light?

A light that hangs from the ceiling on a cord.

At WORK STYLING Namba, black pendant lights hang above the seats.
