Shibuya, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shibuya — a shared office on the 3rd floor of Shibuya Park Building, 4 minutes from Shibuya Station's East Exit

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shibuya is on the 3rd floor of the Shibuya Park Building in Shibuya 3-chome, a four-minute walk from the East Exit of Shibuya Station. It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, available 8:00–21:00 on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Pay as you go, on the east side of Shibuya Station

WORK STYLING Shibuya is on the 3rd floor of the Shibuya Park Building, four minutes from the East Exit — a short walk east from Shibuya Station, where JR, Keio Inokashira, Tokyu and Tokyo Metro lines meet.

On SHARE, the pay-as-you-go plan, you pay only for the time you use. Meeting rooms and private rooms are booked separately.

01 · Entrance and open space

Seats you can take without booking

Through the entrance is the open space.

The open space at your home site needs no booking, with a drinks and café corner and equipment to borrow.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Close to Shibuya Station, they work well for meeting people from outside.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for one, by booking

Single-person rooms are used by booking.

They suit online meetings and calls you'd rather take away from others.

What is SHARE?

WORK STYLING's pay-as-you-go plan: pay only for the time you use and work from sites across the network.

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately and booked. It suits using a site near a station like Shibuya only when you need it.
