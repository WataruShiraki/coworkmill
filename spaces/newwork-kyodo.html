Kyodo, Tokyo

NewWork Kyodo — a corporate-membership satellite office on the Odakyu Line, on the 5th floor of the Kyodo Fukoku Life Building

NewWork Kyodo is on the 5th floor of the Kyodo Fukoku Life Building, a two-minute walk from Kyodo Station on the Odakyu Line. It is a small site with 18 booth seats, a meeting room for 6 and five single private booths, in front of a station in a Setagaya residential area. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

In front of a residential station on the Odakyu Line

Two minutes from Kyodo Station, this small 18-seat site is built around booth seats and private booths.

The meeting room and private booths are booked in advance, and the official website notes that to keep using one beyond 120 minutes, you must book again.

01 · Booth seats

An 18-seat workspace

There are 18 booth-style seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Five single booths

There are five single private booths, booked in advance.

With locks and monitors, they suit online meetings.

03 · Meeting room

One room for 6

There is one meeting room for 6, booked in advance.

It is just the right size for meetings among members who live nearby.

What is Kyodo?

The name of an Odakyu Line station in Setagaya City, and of the neighbourhood around it.

NewWork Kyodo is on the 5th floor of the Kyodo Fukoku Life Building, two minutes on foot from the station.
