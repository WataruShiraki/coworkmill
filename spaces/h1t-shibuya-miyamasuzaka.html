Shibuya, Tokyo

H¹T Shibuya Miyamasuzaka — a corporate shared office with 14 private rooms and 5 meeting rooms on the 8th floor of Wako Miyamasuzaka

H¹T Shibuya Miyamasuzaka (formerly H¹T Shibuya), run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 8th floor of Wako Miyamasuzaka on the Miyamasuzaka slope, a three-minute walk from Shibuya Station on the Ginza, Hanzomon and Fukutoshin lines and the Tokyu lines. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fee. This site has 14 single-person rooms and five meeting rooms for 2 to 6, and is open 7:30–20:00 on weekdays, Saturdays and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A Shibuya site with plenty of meeting rooms

H¹T Shibuya Miyamasuzaka has five meeting rooms — two for 2, two for 4 and one for 6 — so you can choose a room by headcount.

H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies, and the official website describes it as usable for many purposes: remote work, a base for sales staff out on the road, seminars, training and interviews.

01 · Open space

Ten shared seats

The open space seats 10.

It is handy for checking email or polishing documents before or after meetings in Shibuya.

02 · Rooms

14 single-person rooms

There are 14 single-person rooms.

With a door to close, you can take online meetings and calls without worrying about those around you.

03 · Meeting rooms

Five rooms for 2 to 6

The meeting rooms are two for 2, two for 4 and one for 6.

They cover everything from client interviews to team meetings.

What is Miyamasuzaka?

The name of the slope that rises from the east side of Shibuya Station towards Aoyama.

H¹T Shibuya Miyamasuzaka is on the 8th floor of Wako Miyamasuzaka on this slope, and was previously called “H¹T Shibuya”.
