Kojimachi, Tokyo

H¹T Kojimachi — a shared office with four meeting rooms, one minute from Kojimachi Station

H¹T Kojimachi is on the 3rd floor of the MK Kojimachi Building, a one-minute walk from exit 4 of Kojimachi Station on the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line. With seven single boxes and eight single rooms, plus one meeting room for two and three for six, it is strong on meetings. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Introduction

Three meeting rooms for six, a minute from the station

H¹T Kojimachi has open seats, single and four-person boxes, eight single rooms and four meeting rooms — from focused solo seats to places for meetings.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.
