Kanda, Tokyo

Business-Airport Kanda — a workplace sprinkling new trends over a retro style, on the 7th floor of oak Kanda Kajicho

Business-Airport Kanda, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 7th floor of oak Kanda Kajicho in Kanda-Kajicho 3-chome. The website describes a workplace that blends new trends seamlessly into a retro design style, giving a curious sense of calm and comfort. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

New trends in a retro style

The website describes “a workplace that sprinkles new trends naturally over a retro design style, with a curious calm and comfort”.

Kanda is a district where old-established trade and new companies mix. The interior expresses that character by blending old and new.

01 · Coworking

From 8:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

Coworking is open 8:00–20:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM and BOOTH

Private ROOMs start at ¥275,000 a month and BOOTHs at ¥110,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting room

A room for eight

The meeting room seats eight at ¥3,300 an hour; the one-person online meeting room is ¥550 per 30 minutes.

Meet in a calm, retro setting.

What does retro mean here?

Design that evokes the styles and mood of the past and enjoys their flavour. Business-Airport Kanda sprinkles new trends over a retro style.

Bringing an old-time atmosphere into a new building creates a sense of calm.
