Kamiyacho, Tokyo

WeWork Kamiyacho Trust Tower — one of the largest WeWork sites in Japan, on floors 21–24, directly connected to Kamiyacho Station

WeWork Kamiyacho Trust Tower is on the 21st to 24th floors of Kamiyacho Trust Tower, the core of Tokyo World Gate, completed in 2020. The website calls it one of the largest sites in Japan, and highlights art and interiors built around the past and present of Toranomon. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Toranomon, past and present

The interior concept is “Toranomon, past and present”. In an open space that takes in Japanese elements, style and playfulness coexist, the website says.

It is directly connected to Kamiyacho Station on the subway and within walking distance of Toranomon, Toranomon Hills and Roppongi-itchome stations. Several other WeWork sites are nearby, and members move between them often.

01 · Shared areas

Japanese touches and playfulness

The shared areas are open and take in Japanese elements.

Ceilings of 2,900–3,000 mm let you feel the spaciousness of the high floors.

02 · Meeting rooms

56 meeting rooms

There are 56 meeting rooms — a very large number for one site.

Phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market are also available.

Why does ceiling height matter?

The distance from floor to ceiling. In office buildings, around 2,700 mm is a common benchmark.

At WeWork Kamiyacho Trust Tower, ceilings are 2,900–3,000 mm, so the same floor area feels more spacious.
