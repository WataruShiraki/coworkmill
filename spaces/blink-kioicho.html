Kioicho, Tokyo

BLINK Kioicho — lounge seats and private offices in Tokyo Garden Terrace Kioicho, directly linked to Nagatacho Station

BLINK Kioicho is on the 2nd floor of Tokyo Garden Terrace Kioicho, directly linked to Nagatacho Station on the Namboku, Yurakucho and Hanzomon lines. The official website calls it “a place that makes new ways of working possible”. It has lounge seats including semi-private ones, private offices for 2–8 and meeting rooms for 4–6. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A place that makes new ways of working possible

The official website describes BLINK Kioicho as “a place that makes new ways of working possible”. Directly linked to Nagatacho Station, it is convenient for people on the move.

BLINK's second site after Roppongi, it has auto-adjusting lights, auto-locking doors and security cameras.

01 · Lounge seats

Including semi-private seats

The lounge is lined with seats, including semi-private ones.

With a stable internet connection and a multifunction printer, you can get work done on the spot.

02 · Private offices

Rooms for 2 to 8

Private offices come in rooms for 2 to 8.

According to the official website, they offer 22-hour access.

03 · Meeting rooms

For meetings of 4 to 6

There are meeting rooms for 4 to 6.

They handle small team meetings and visitors.

What is Tokyo Garden Terrace Kioicho?

A mixed-use complex in Kioicho, Chiyoda City.

BLINK Kioicho is on its 2nd floor; the official website gives it as directly linked to Nagatacho Station and two minutes on foot from Akasaka-mitsuke Station.
