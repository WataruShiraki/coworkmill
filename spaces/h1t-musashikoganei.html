Musashi-Koganei, Tokyo

H¹T Musashi-Koganei — a shared office with 18 private rooms, 3 minutes from Musashi-Koganei Station

H¹T Musashi-Koganei is on the 2nd floor of Sokora Musashi-Koganei Cross, about a three-minute walk from the central gate of JR Musashi-Koganei Station. It has 18 single rooms, 5 single boxes and 6 open-space seats. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A large base with 18 private rooms

H¹T Musashi-Koganei has 6 open seats, 5 single boxes and 18 single rooms — a place to concentrate on your own work.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.
