Shibuya, Tokyo

SHIBUYA QWS — a 2,600 m² members' space on the 15th floor of Shibuya Scramble Square, built around asking questions

SHIBUYA QWS (pronounced “Shibuya Cues”) is on the 15th floor of Shibuya Scramble Square's East Tower. The 2,600 m² floor holds PROJECT BASE with movable tables, the busy CROSS PARK, and SCRAMBLE HALL for 200 people. The official website describes it as a members' facility where diverse people cross paths and grow seeds of social value. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Question with Sensibility — start with a question

The name QWS comes from “Question with Sensibility”. Ask the question before the answer, and let diverse people cross paths to grow seeds of social value — that is what this members' facility is for. Its academic partners include the University of Tokyo, Tokyo University of Science, Keio University, Waseda University, Tokyo Metropolitan University, Tokyo University of the Arts and Meiji University.

There are four kinds of members: QWS Project Members (teams of three or more), Corporate & Public Members for companies and local governments, QWS Commons who support as experts, and individual QWS Members. Through QWS Challenge, teams that pass a selection can use the project base for free.

01 · Scramble Hall

A 200-person hall overlooking the Scramble Crossing

SCRAMBLE HALL is an event space for about 200 people, looking down on Shibuya's famous Scramble Crossing. It has screens on three walls, sound, lighting and a green room.

The SALON has comfortable sofas and private rooms, used with restaurant service for business talks and for members to get to know each other.

02 · Playground and café

A running-track floor and a colourful café

PLAYGROUND is made for workshops. It has easy-to-move desks and chairs and screens that double as partitions, and its floor uses the same material as an athletics track.

CAFE HInT serves a colourful café menu. Besides three meeting rooms open to all members, there are private rooms by reservation.

What is QWS Challenge?

A selection programme run by SHIBUYA QWS. Projects that are chosen can use the project base free of charge.

Paying members and selected teams share the same floor. With one entrance for money and another for questions, the mix of people widens.
