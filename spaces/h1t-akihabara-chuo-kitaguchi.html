Akihabara, Tokyo

H¹T Akihabara Chuo Kitaguchi — a corporate shared office with 17 single rooms and a meeting room for 8, on the 2nd floor of the Hasegawa Building

H¹T Akihabara Chuo Kitaguchi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 2nd floor of the Hasegawa Building in Kanda-Matsunagacho, a three-minute walk from the Central Gate North Exit of JR Akihabara Station. It is one minute from Exit A3 of the Tsukuba Express station and two from Exit 2 on the Hibiya Line. Seating is 17 single-person rooms and one meeting room for 8, and it is open 7:00–22:00 every day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An Akihabara site built around private rooms

Seating at H¹T Akihabara Chuo Kitaguchi centres on 17 single-person rooms. No open space or boxes appear in the official seat list, making it suited to calm work behind a closed door.

The official website describes it as a corporate shared office with no initial or base fee, usable for many purposes: remote work, a base for sales staff, seminars, training and interviews.

03 · Close to three lines

JR, Hibiya Line and Tsukuba Express

It is three minutes from JR Akihabara's Central Gate North Exit, two from Exit 2 on the Hibiya Line and one from Exit A3 on the Tsukuba Express.

Open 7:00–22:00 every day, it is easy to stop by early in the morning or at weekends.

What is the Central Gate North Exit?

One of the ticket gates at JR Akihabara Station, which also gives H¹T Akihabara Chuo Kitaguchi its name.

The official website gives it as three minutes on foot from this gate.
