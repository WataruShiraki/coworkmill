Toranomon, Tokyo

WeWork KDX Toranomon 1-chome — a lounge joined to an outdoor terrace, three minutes from Toranomon Station

WeWork KDX Toranomon 1-chome is in the KDX Toranomon 1-chome Building, a three-minute walk from Toranomon and Toranomon Hills stations on the Tokyo Metro and five minutes from Kasumigaseki Station. Uchisaiwaicho Station and JR Shimbashi Station are also within walking distance. Everything below is based on the official website.

BuildingCompleted October 2013 / 11 floors above ground, 1 below, 2 rooftop levelsFloorCeiling height 2,800 mm / 24 meeting rooms / garden terrace, phone booths, parents' room, honesty market

Introduction

A lounge and terrace in one

The official website describes the main 11th floor as a seamless space where the lounge flows onto an outdoor terrace, comfortable and full of creativity.

The building has enhanced energy-saving performance. It sits close to Shimbashi and Kasumigaseki, next to an area where many large companies are based.

01 · Workspace

24 meeting rooms

There are 24 meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

The ceiling height is 2,800 mm.

What is a garden terrace?

An outdoor terrace with planting.

Here the 11th-floor lounge and the outdoor terrace are joined, so you can move between indoors and outdoors as you work.
