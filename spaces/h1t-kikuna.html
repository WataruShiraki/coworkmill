Kikuna, Kanagawa

H¹T Kikuna — a corporate shared office with 12 single rooms, open 7:00–22:30 every day, on the ground floor of the Amenity 115 Building

H¹T Kikuna, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the ground floor of the Amenity 115 Building, a two-minute walk from the East Gate of Kikuna Station on the Tokyu Toyoko Line and four minutes from the East Exit of JR Kikuna Station. Its seats are 12 single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms by Kikuna Station

H¹T Kikuna is open 7:00–22:30 every day. Its seats are 12 single-person rooms.

The official website describes it as a satellite-type corporate shared office run by Nomura Real Estate, usable for remote work or as a base while out.
