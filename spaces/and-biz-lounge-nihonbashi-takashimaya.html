&BIZ lounge Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building — meeting rooms for 2–12 and a lounge, directly linked to Nihombashi Station

&BIZ conference / &BIZ lounge Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building is on the 10th floor of the Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building, directly linked to Nihombashi Station on the Ginza, Tozai and Asakusa lines. It is listed as a site on the WORK STYLING official website. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site with both meeting rooms and a lounge

Directly linked to Ginza, Tozai & Asakusa lines Nihombashi Station.

There are bookable meeting rooms for 2–12 people, usable in 10-minute units, and conference rooms for 30–78 people. Every room has curtains.

01 · Lounge

The lounge

A lounge with table seats, long desks and sofa seats.

02 · Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms and conference rooms

There are meeting rooms for small groups and conference rooms for large ones.

03 · Entrance

Entrance

The entrance on the 10th floor.

What is a conference room?

A large meeting room used for big meetings and briefings.
