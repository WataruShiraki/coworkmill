Meguro, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Meguro Arco Tower — a 7th floor overlooking the Meguro River, with wood finishes and a lounge

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 7th floor of Arco Tower in Meguro, about 31,645 sq ft in size. The website describes views over the Meguro River, lined with more than 800 cherry trees, and wood finishes that reflect the surrounding nature. Everything below is based on the official website.

SizeAbout 31,645 sq ft (per the official site)LayoutPrivate offices / coworking (hot desks, dedicated desks) / 5 meeting rooms / event space / café

Introduction

An interior that reflects the Meguro River's greenery

According to the website, the centre's design mirrors its natural surroundings and pays respect to a redevelopment that values the local environment.

Its features are wood finishes inspired by the environment, vintage furniture by Timothy Oulton and a large lounge looking out over the city. The Meguro River flows outside the windows.

01 · Lounge and reception

A lounge looking out over the city

The shared space the website calls “a large lounge overlooking the city”, and reception.

The lounge is also used as an event space.

02 · Coworking and private offices

Seats with height-adjustable desks

Coworking seats and private offices priced on request, with height-adjustable desks.

Hot desks and dedicated desks start at ¥54,000 a month; day passes from ¥5,000 a day.

03 · Meeting rooms and the view

Five meeting rooms and the Meguro River

There are five meeting rooms, including a boardroom suited to board meetings.

The windows look out on the Meguro River and the city.

What is a boardroom?

A meeting room around a large table, used for board meetings and the like.
