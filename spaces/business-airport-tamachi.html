Tamachi, Tokyo

Business-Airport Tamachi — a workplace carrying on the dignified office tradition, on the 2nd floor of Tamachi Square

Business-Airport Tamachi, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 2nd floor of Tamachi Square in Shiba 5-chome. It holds that the joy of working with a sense of fulfilment opens up the future, and aims for a workplace where calm and function coexist, carrying on the dignified office that has long symbolised business status. Introduction

Carrying on the dignified office

The website describes Business-Airport Tamachi, in the heart of the city, as a workplace that inherits the form of the dignified office that has symbolised business status, where calm and functionality coexist — because the joy of working with fulfilment opens the future.

Rather than chasing the new, it carries on a respected tradition. It is a base for people who value calm.

01 · Coworking

7:00–22:00 on weekdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00).

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

A single ROOM for ¥132,000 a month

A single-person ROOM costs ¥132,000 a month; multi-person ROOMs start at ¥308,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for six and eight

Meeting room A seats six at ¥2,750 an hour; room B seats eight at ¥3,300.

The one-person online meeting room costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

What does a “dignified” office mean?

The Japanese word kakushiki refers to established forms and manners befitting one's standing. Business-Airport Tamachi says it carries on the form of the dignified office.

Choosing forms long valued over the latest novelty — a choice for people who seek calm.
