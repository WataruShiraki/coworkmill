Marunouchi, Tokyo

The Collective GranTokyo South Tower — luxury coworking from JustCo's top-tier brand, directly connected to Tokyo Station

The Collective GranTokyo South Tower is on the 9th floor of GranTokyo South Tower, a complex directly connected to Tokyo Station. It is luxury coworking and shared offices from “The Collective”, JustCo's top-tier brand. It offers Herman Miller Aeron chairs and height-adjustable desks, TWG tea and % Arabica coffee, daily breakfast and a dedicated concierge service. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A premium experience, meticulous care

The website presents it as luxury coworking and shared offices under “The Collective”, JustCo's top brand, with the headline “A premium experience, meticulous care”.

JustCo GranTokyo South Tower is on the 11th floor of the same building; here on the 9th, breakfast, drinks hours and a dedicated concierge offer a step-up experience.

01 · Shared desks

Aeron chairs and height-adjustable desks

Shared desks come with Herman Miller Aeron chairs and height-adjustable desks.

Switch between sitting and standing as you work.

02 · Pantry and coffee

TWG tea and % Arabica coffee

The pantry stocks TWG tea and % Arabica coffee. There is breakfast every day and a drinks hour.

A dedicated concierge service handles visitors and arrangements.

03 · Meeting rooms

Multipurpose rooms in small, medium and large

There are multipurpose meeting rooms in small, medium and large sizes, plus a private meeting room and tea area.

Connected to the Yaesu South exit of Tokyo Station, it suits meetings with people arriving by shinkansen.

04 · Private offices

Rooms for your team

You can rent team spaces with independent access control.

From small rooms to suites for whole company teams.

What is a concierge?

A person who takes on users' requests and arrangements — a role best known from hotels.

The Collective GranTokyo South Tower offers a dedicated concierge service.
