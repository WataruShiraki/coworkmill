Shibuya, Tokyo

Business-Airport Shibuya Fukuras — a high-rise workplace on the 17th floor of Shibuya Fukuras, in front of the station

Business-Airport Shibuya Fukuras, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 17th floor of Shibuya Fukuras in front of Shibuya Station. It has private rooms, coworking, meeting rooms and an event space for 20, and the serviced offices are open 24 hours. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

On the 17th floor, in front of Shibuya Station

Business-Airport Shibuya Fukuras is on the 17th floor of Shibuya Fukuras, right in front of Shibuya Station.

Business-Airport is Tokyu Land's brand, named after an airport where diverse people gather and depart. In Shibuya it also has the Shibuya Sakura Stage and Shibuya Nanpeidai sites.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Sundays and holidays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Sundays and holidays. It is closed on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥99,000 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

Rooms for one, and for 2–5 people

Private rooms for one cost ¥198,000 a month; rooms for 2–5 start at ¥363,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms and event space

Two rooms for six, and an event space for 20

Meeting rooms A and B seat six at ¥2,750 an hour; the one-person online meeting room is ¥550 per 30 minutes.

The event space holds 20 people: ¥33,000 an hour for members and ¥49,500 for non-members.

What is Master membership?

A Business-Airport membership that lets you use the coworking areas of every site, for ¥38,500 a month.

With three sites in Shibuya alone, you can pick the one that fits your schedule that day.
