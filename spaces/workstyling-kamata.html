Kamata, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Kamata — a shared office on the 2nd floor of the Kamata Tsukimura Building, two minutes from Kamata Station

WORK STYLING Kamata is on the 2nd floor of the Kamata Tsukimura Building, a two-minute walk from Kamata Station on the JR and Tokyu lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 2nd floor two minutes from Kamata Station

WORK STYLING Kamata is two minutes on foot from Kamata Station on the JR and Tokyu lines, and eight from Keikyu Kamata Station.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, the interior plays cheerfully with colour and pattern: check-patterned sofas, yellow lights and walls split into coloured triangles.

01 · Open space

Check-patterned sofas

The open space can be used without booking.

There are check-patterned sofa seats and a long table under pendant lights.

02 · Partitioned seats

Desks for focusing alone

There are desks divided by wooden partitions.

By the windows there are also partitioned counter seats.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms in different colours

Meeting rooms hold up to 12.

From walls split into coloured triangles to wood-grain walls, each room has its own look.

04 · Private rooms

Single rooms with yellow walls

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

Each has a desk, monitor and desk lamp.

What is a pendant light?

A light that hangs from the ceiling on a cord or chain.

It focuses light where you work and creates a cosy spot around the table. They are used in the open space at WORK STYLING Kamata.
