Daikanyama, Tokyo

co-lab Daikanyama — a shared office for creators on floors 4–6 of SodaCCo, with a terrace and rooftop

Walk along Hachiman-dori from Daikanyama Station to near Namikibashi, and co-lab Daikanyama occupies the 4th to 6th floors of SodaCCo. With 15 rooms, 16 booths, 9 desks, flexible seats, five meeting rooms, a terrace and a rooftop, it is a shared office for creators. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Creative education for children

co-lab Daikanyama's mission is “creative education for children, drawing on the collective knowledge of creators and the local community”.

The official website describes a cycle: developing educational programmes and products for the next generation to nurture children's creativity, while children's imagination and curiosity bring fresh perspectives to the creators' own work.

01 · SodaCCo and reception

Starting at the 4th-floor reception

The building is SodaCCo, and reception is on the 4th floor.

In front of reception is a shared area where members run into each other.

02 · 4th-floor desks and booths

Seats for one, seats for two

Desks are for one person from ¥35,000 a month; booths for 1–2 people start at ¥60,000 a month. Flex seats start at ¥15,000 a month.

The 4th floor also has a large meeting room.

03 · 6th-floor rooms and rooftop

Rooms with a terrace, and a rooftop

There are 15 rooms for 3–25 people. On the 6th floor, some rooms face a terrace.

The building also has a rooftop where you can spend time in the open air.

What is Flex?

One of co-lab's membership types: a single-person seat with no fixed place. At co-lab Daikanyama it starts at ¥15,000 a month.

Seat types step up from Flex to desks, booths and rooms.
