Gaiemmae, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Aoyama — private rooms and a shared-office floor on the 2nd, 4th and 5th floors of Landwork Aoyama Building, 2 minutes from Gaiemmae

CROSSCOOP Aoyama is in the Landwork Aoyama Building (formerly Hulic Gaiemmae Building), a two-minute walk from exit 3 of Gaiemmae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line. The 2nd floor has private rooms and meeting rooms, the 4th private rooms and a shared office, and the 5th private rooms. It can be used from one person, and there are five meeting rooms for 4 to 10. Introduction

Each floor with its own role

CROSSCOOP Aoyama uses three floors of the Landwork Aoyama Building, each with its own role: the 2nd for private rooms and meeting rooms, the 4th for private rooms and a shared office, and the 5th for private rooms.

The website says it “can be used from one person, with fully private rooms for 3 to 20”. Reception is staffed 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.

01 · Entrance and reception

Staffed reception on weekdays

Beyond the entrance is reception, staffed 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.

There is a waiting area for visitors.

02 · Meeting rooms and meeting area

Five meeting rooms

There are five meeting rooms: two for 4, two for 6 and one for 10.

Meeting-room fees come with a free allowance of 3 to 5 hours a day.

03 · Private rooms

For 1 to 20 people

Rooms range from single-person rooms to fully private rooms for 3 to 20.

Utilities and air-conditioning are included in the monthly rent, and company registration is possible.

What is a shared office?

An office where one floor is shared by several companies or individuals. At CROSSCOOP Aoyama, the 4th floor combines private rooms and a shared office.

People who don't need a whole room can still work in the same building.
