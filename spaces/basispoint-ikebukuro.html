Ikebukuro, Tokyo

BasisPoint Ikebukuro — a coworking space on the 4th floor of the Fukano Building where every seat allows conversation, one minute from the East Exit

BasisPoint Ikebukuro is on the 4th floor of the Fukano Building, a one-minute walk from the East Exit of JR Ikebukuro Station. The official website says its “refined space like a hotel lounge” allows conversation and web meetings at every seat. An open space with tables, a counter and sofas is joined by BOX seats, private booths and a meeting room for 10. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A lounge where you can talk at every seat

The Ikebukuro site clearly states that talking and web meetings are fine at every seat. With no seats where you must keep quiet, it suits people who make lots of calls or hold meetings.

Small extras are covered too: free drinks (coffee, tea, water), blankets and phone chargers.

01 · Open space

Tables, counter and sofas

Open seats cost ¥330 for the first 30 minutes, with a daily cap of ¥3,300.

Sofa seats are available at ¥1,980 per hour.

02 · BOX seats and private booths

Semi-private and fully private

BOX seats with monitors are semi-private spaces for 1–4 people. The private booth is a fully enclosed room for one, at ¥600 per 30 minutes.

You can switch seats depending on whether you need to talk or to focus.

03 · Meeting room

A room for 10

The meeting room holds 10 people and measures 13.94 m². It costs ¥1,980 per 30 minutes (minimum one hour).

One minute from the station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

What is a BOX seat?

A semi-private seat enclosed by partitions. At BasisPoint Ikebukuro, BOX seats have monitors and fit 1–4 people.

Less closed-off than a private room, but calmer for conversation than an open seat — somewhere in between.
