Roppongi, Tokyo

BLINK Roppongi — coworking that feels “just like being abroad”, with a ground-floor café lounge and workspaces from B1F to 4F

BLINK Roppongi is in Moto-Azabu 3-chome, a two-minute walk from the WEST WALK entrance of Roppongi Hills. The official website calls it “a coworking space that feels just like being abroad”. Across five floors, from the basement to the 4th floor, are a café lounge, open desks, private offices and meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Coworking that feels just like being abroad

The official website says you can enjoy proper coffee in an international café, and that more than 200 events a year are held, covering business, lifestyle, music, art and more.

It is dog-friendly too — a place to work in a relaxed, unbuttoned atmosphere.

01 · 1F café lounge

A café open to non-members too

The ground floor holds the café lounge, event space and open desks. The official website says “members, of course, and non-members too can use it freely”.

There is a food menu as well as drinks.

02 · B1F and 2F

Fixed desks and a thinking room

The basement has private offices and fixed desks available 24 hours, with meeting rooms and phone booths.

The 2nd floor has meeting rooms for 4–6, open desks and a thinking room for giving your mind a rest.

03 · 3F and 4F

Private offices

The 3rd and 4th floors hold private offices that companies can use as a 24-hour base. They are fully private, from rooms for 2 to rooms for 16.

The kitchen in the 4th-floor pantry is free to use.

What is the thinking room?

The name of a room on the 2nd floor of BLINK Roppongi.

The official website says “when you want to refresh your mind, you can spend some unhurried time in the thinking room”.
