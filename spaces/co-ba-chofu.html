Chofu, Tokyo

co-ba CHOFU — a “work-centred community” with conversation-friendly tables, on the 2nd floor of the Kotobuki Building, one minute from Chofu Station

co-ba CHOFU is on the 2nd floor of the Kotobuki Building, a one-minute walk from Chofu Station on the Keio Line. It opened in September 2014 and moved and renewed in March 2021. It calls itself “a work-centred community”, with work tables arranged to make conversation easy, valuing “room to not overwork” and connections between people. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A work-centred community

co-ba CHOFU calls itself “a work-centred community”. It opened in September 2014 and moved and renewed in March 2021.

Work tables are arranged so people can talk easily. It aims to be a place that achieves both “room to not overwork” and connections between people, the website says.

01 · Work tables

Seats arranged for conversation

Seating is free address, with work tables arranged for easy conversation.

Standard membership is ¥14,300 a month, usable every day from 7:00 to 23:59. There are also weekday-morning-only and evenings-and-weekends memberships.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space for the day without becoming a member. At co-ba CHOFU, drop-in is available 10:00–16:00 on weekdays for ¥110 per 15 minutes or ¥2,200 a day.

It is also a way to get a feel for the place and the people before joining.
