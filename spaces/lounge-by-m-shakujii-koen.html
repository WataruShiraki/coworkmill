Shakujii-koen, Tokyo

LOUNGE by m Shakujii-koen — “a moment of bliss in a refined space”

LOUNGE by m Shakujii-koen is a coworking space on the 2nd floor of Blue Stone in Shakujiimachi 2-chome, Nerima. There are wide window-side tables, individual workspaces and seats that can be closed off with curtains. You enter and leave by tapping an IC card key, and there is a drink bar. Introduction

A moment of bliss in a refined space

LOUNGE by m has sites in Oizumi-Gakuen and Shakujii-koen in Nerima City. Its official website carries the line “a moment of bliss in a refined space”.

Coworking membership is available at the Shakujii-koen site; the Oizumi-Gakuen site is described as a “learning café”.

01 · Workspace

A wide window-side table and individual seats

By the windows is a workspace with a wide table, plus individual workspaces. The official website describes the whole site as “a spacious place”.

02 · Curtained seats

Draw the curtain to focus or meet

Some seats can be closed off with curtains for focused work, and can also be used for meetings (charged).

The meeting space can be hired for ¥550 an hour, for up to two hours and four people.

03 · How it works

IC card entry and a drink bar

Just tap your IC card key to enter and leave; your arrival and departure are recorded automatically.

There are chargers, Wi-Fi, power and a drink bar. Prices are ¥1,650 to register, ¥990 for the first hour and ¥495 per 30 minutes after that.

What is a drink bar?

A self-service station where you pour whatever drinks you like.

LOUNGE by m Shakujii-koen offers a drink bar as one of its workspace amenities.
