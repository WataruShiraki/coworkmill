Hibiya, Tokyo

Business-Airport Hibiya — a theatre-inspired workplace in harmony with greenery, on the 9th floor of the Toho Hibiya Building

Business-Airport Hibiya, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 9th floor of the Toho Hibiya Building in Yurakucho 1-chome. Surrounded by greenery and art, with a theatre as its motif and plants woven through the space, it is a workplace where people spark each other's creativity and keep sending out diverse culture. Introduction

A theatre motif, in harmony with greenery

The website describes it as “Business-Airport Hibiya, surrounded by greenery and art — a space with a theatre as its motif and in harmony with plants, a workplace where creativity sparks creativity and diverse culture keeps flowing out”.

Hibiya is a district of theatres and cinemas. On the 9th floor of the Toho Hibiya Building, a symbol of the area, is a workplace whose interior recalls a theatre.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on weekends and holidays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays.

Address membership is ¥82,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

Private rooms from one person

Private rooms for one start at ¥143,000 a month; rooms for several people from ¥272,800.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

One room for eight, two for four

Meeting room A seats eight at ¥3,300 an hour; rooms B and C seat four at ¥2,200 an hour.

The one-person online meeting room costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

What does a theatre motif mean?

Using the shape and atmosphere of a theatre as the starting point for the interior design. Business-Airport Hibiya is built around this idea.

In Hibiya, the district of theatres and cinemas, the interior takes in the memory of its neighbourhood.
