Matsudo, Chiba

M.A.D.center — a hub with murals and a coffee counter, two minutes from the West Exit of Matsudo Station

M.A.D.center is on the 7th floor of the Le Cinéma Building, a two-minute walk from the West Exit of Matsudo Station on the JR and Shin-Keisei lines. A community facility renamed from the former Matsudo shareBASE, it reopened on 25 February 2026, bringing together a coworking space, three meeting rooms and a 3D-printer studio on one floor. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A station-front shared space connected to the town's makers

M.A.D.center is a shared space run by MAD City, which works on community building around Matsudo Station. It reopened on 25 February 2026, renewed from the former Matsudo shareBASE, as a community facility with coworking, meeting rooms and a 3D-printer studio.

The official website also highlights collaboration with local creators and businesses. Mahamel Coffee, Sakura Parti and STUDIO Canvas were involved, adding green benches, colourful murals and a drinks counter from a local coffee roaster.

01 · Lounge

Work seats with green benches and murals

Monthly members can use the coworking space without time limits. Drop-in runs Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00–22:00, from ¥220 per 30 minutes or ¥1,320 a day.

Green benches and colourful murals bring colour to this one floor of a station-front building.

02 · Counter

A coffee counter from a local roaster

There is a drinks counter from a local coffee roaster.

Private booths are also available, so calls and online meetings can be kept separate.

03 · Meeting rooms

Three rooms for 14 and 6

The three meeting rooms are Large (14 people) and Middle and Window (6 people each). They cost ¥480–¥980 per 30 minutes, and monthly members pay half the drop-in rate.

Middle can be booked as a drop-in on Sundays and Mondays. On other days, the official website advises against confidential meetings there, as sound carries.

04 · How to use it

Two minutes from the station, 24 hours for members

Monthly members pay ¥9,900 for 24-hour access every day. The 3D printers are shown on the official website as being renewed.

Two minutes from the West Exit of Matsudo Station, it is easy to drop in from anywhere along the Joban Line.

What is MAD City?

A project working on community building around Matsudo Station. Its official website features several places around town, such as a studio in an old house and an event space.

M.A.D.center is one of MAD City's station-front bases, handling coworking and meeting rooms.
