Hachioji, Tokyo

BasisPoint Hachioji — a coworking space with 13 private booths and company registration, on the ground floor of the Matsuzaka Building

BasisPoint Hachioji is on the ground floor of the Matsuzaka Building, a three-minute walk from JR Hachioji Station and six minutes from Keio Hachioji Station. The official website calls it “a coworking space you can drop into right now — and register your company at too”. It has 13 single-person private booths, an open space, BOX seats and two meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A station-front ground floor with many single rooms

What sets Hachioji apart is its 13 single-person private booths for calls and focused work. There are also two BOX seats with monitors for up to 6.

There are two meeting rooms for 8, each 9.56 m². The official website notes that monthly members can also use the address for company registration.

01 · Open space

Wi-Fi and power at every seat

Open seats cost ¥330 for the first 30 minutes, with a daily cap of ¥3,300.

Hot coffee, tea and blankets are free.

02 · Private booths

Thirteen single booths

There are 13 single-person private booths, at ¥600 per 30 minutes with a daily cap of ¥4,290.

That is enough to secure a booth even on days full of web meetings.

03 · BOX seats and meeting rooms

From small groups up to 8

BOX seats hold up to 6 and have monitors, at ¥1,980 per hour. The two meeting rooms, Room A and Room B, cost ¥1,485 per 30 minutes.

Monitors, laptops and projectors can be rented for a fee.

What is company registration?

Registering a company's address and other details with the Legal Affairs Bureau. Some coworking spaces offer plans that let you use their address for this.

The official website describes BasisPoint Hachioji as “a coworking space where you can also register your company”.
