CROSSCOOP Shibuya Nextsite — a planted lounge and 19 phone booths, on the 5th and 6th floors of the Nextsite Shibuya Building

CROSSCOOP Shibuya Nextsite is on the 5th and 6th floors of the Nextsite Shibuya Building, seven minutes' walk from Shibuya Station and nine from Omotesando Station. Its motto is “+ PERFORMANCE”. It has a lounge with plants brought indoors, 7 meeting rooms, 19 phone booths and private rooms for 2 to 34 people. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

+ PERFORMANCE

CROSSCOOP Shibuya Nextsite's motto is “+ PERFORMANCE” — the idea that making people comfortable adds to the results of their work.

The website says it “creates a comfortable lounge space by placing plants inside the building”. It also has high security with face recognition.

01 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Seven meeting rooms and 19 booths

There are seven meeting rooms for 4 to 12 people and 19 phone booths.

The large number of booths suits a way of working with many online meetings.

02 · Private rooms

For 2 to 34 people

Private rooms hold 2 to 34 people; the room for 22 is 54.85 m².

Desks and chairs in the offices are provided free, and company registration is possible.

What is a phone booth?

A small private booth you enter alone for calls and online meetings. CROSSCOOP Shibuya Nextsite has 19 of them.

Even when the lounge is full of conversation, you can step into a booth and talk quietly.
