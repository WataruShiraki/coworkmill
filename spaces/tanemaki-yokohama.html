Yokohama, Kanagawa

TANEMAKI — somewhere between a library and a café, eight minutes from Yokohama Station

TANEMAKI is a coworking space on the ground floor of Sunny Court Yokohama in Okano, Nishi Ward, an eight-minute walk from Yokohama Station. It has been running near Yokohama Station since 2011, and the official website describes it as a space “halfway between a library and a café”. There are 18 seats plus sofas, available 24 hours. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Making a place where people get things done

TANEMAKI's idea is “making a place where people go and do something”. It aims to increase the number of people who turn ideas into action, and to create a lively place.

It is designed for long stays — for work, studying for qualifications or reading. Coming and going is free, talking is fine and you can bring things in.

01 · Seats

Four kinds of table, three kinds of chair

There are 18 seats plus sofas, with four kinds of table and three kinds of chair.

Every seat has a 60 W USB-C power supply, mobile cables and outlets.

02 · Equipment

13 monitors and free drinks

There are 13 monitors, first come, first served. As well as Wi-Fi, three seats have wired LAN.

Free drinks such as tea and coffee are available, and all seats are non-smoking.

03 · Location

A ground floor eight minutes from Yokohama Station

It is on the ground floor of Sunny Court Yokohama, with a TANEMAKI sign at the entrance.

Drop-in costs ¥375–¥1,480 per person per hour, monthly use starts at ¥5,480, and there is no joining fee.

What is a coworking space?

A new kind of workplace where different people work in the same place and solve problems through exchange and cooperation.

TANEMAKI is described as valuing study sessions and events, and putting effort into building community.
