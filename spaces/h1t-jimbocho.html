Jimbocho, Tokyo

H¹T Jimbocho — a corporate shared office on the 5th floor of the Ichigo Jimbocho Building, one minute from Jimbocho Station

H¹T Jimbocho, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 5th floor of the Ichigo Jimbocho Building, a one-minute walk from Exit A7 of Jimbocho Station on the Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line and Toei Shinjuku and Mita lines. The official website describes it as “a corporate shared office one minute from Jimbocho Station, with no initial or base fee”. It is a weekday-only site, open from 8:00 to 21:00. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A small site with a roomier open space

H¹T Jimbocho puts the emphasis on shared seating, with an open space for 14 compared with six single-person rooms.

There are four meeting rooms in all: two for 2, and one each for 4 and 8. Small as it is, you can still pick a room by headcount.

Seven single-person spots

There is one single-person box and six single-person rooms.

The meeting rooms are two for 2, one for 4 and one for 8.

From interviews to small meetings, everything can be handled on the same floor.

What is Jimbocho?

A neighbourhood centred on Kanda-Jimbocho in Chiyoda City, known for its many second-hand bookshops and publishers.
