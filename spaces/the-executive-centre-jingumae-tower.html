Harajuku, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Jingumae Tower — British-meets-Japanese interiors on floors 12–14 in Harajuku

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 12th to 14th floors of the Jingumae Tower Building, on the corner of Takeshita-dori in Harajuku. The website describes “an interior where Britain meets Japan”: polished oak floors, navy patterned wallpaper and vintage furniture. There are nine meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Where Britain meets Japan

According to the website, the centre adds artistic details reflecting trendy Harajuku to The Executive Centre's signature design.

Traditional polished oak floors are paired with navy patterned wallpaper and vintage furniture by Timothy Oulton. From its three floors, Yoyogi Park and Mt Fuji are visible, the website says.

01 · Reception and lounge

An entrance on oak floors

Reception leads into the coworking lounge.

The lounge has the vintage furniture the website describes.

02 · Coworking and private offices

Hot desks and rooms for teams

Hot-desk seating and private offices priced on request. Private offices have height-adjustable desks and Herman Miller chairs.

Hot desks and dedicated desks start at ¥54,000 a month; day passes from ¥5,000 a day.

03 · Meeting rooms and views

Nine meeting rooms

There are nine meeting rooms, presented by the website as well suited to meetings and discussions.

The windows look out over both city and greenery.

What is vintage furniture?

Furniture with the character that comes with age — a worn-in texture new pieces don't have.

The website says the Jingumae Tower Building centre is furnished with vintage pieces by Timothy Oulton.
