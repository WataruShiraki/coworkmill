Ueno, Tokyo

H¹T Ueno — a 3rd floor in Ueno Marui with shoes-off beanbag seats

H¹T Ueno is on the 3rd floor of Ueno Marui, connected to exit 5a of Ueno Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hibiya lines, one minute's walk. It is a satellite shared office run by Nomura Real Estate, with “Koagari” seats where you take off your shoes. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Shoes-off seats inside a workplace

H¹T Ueno has two “Koagari” seats: a Yogibo beanbag sofa with a Traybo lap table, used with your shoes off.

You can rest a laptop or book on the Traybo. There are no monitors here — these are seats for changing posture while you work.

01 · Corridor

A row of room doors

There are 16 single rooms, with doors lining both sides of the corridor.

The floor is finished in checkerboard tiles.

02 · Koagari

Shoes off, on a beanbag sofa

There are two Koagari seats for one, each a Yogibo beanbag sofa with a Traybo lap table.

Use them with your shoes off. There are no monitors.

03 · Boxes

Six for one and two for six

There are six single boxes and two boxes for six.

A row of enclosures finished in wood and black.

What is Koagari?

The name H¹T gives to shoes-off seats at some of its sites, combining a Yogibo beanbag sofa with a Traybo lap table. The word recalls the small raised tatami platform found in Japanese homes and restaurants.

You can put a laptop or book on the Traybo; there are no monitors. H¹T Ueno has two.
