Marunouchi, Tokyo

The Executive Centre JP Tower — serviced offices and coworking on the 11th and 14th floors, directly connected to Tokyo Station

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 11th and 14th floors of JP Tower, directly connected to Tokyo Station. JP Tower is the former Tokyo Central Post Office, reborn as an office building in 2013. It has a bar with a resident barista, a members' lounge and five meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace overlooking Tokyo Station

The website lists the centre's features as “unrivalled views of Tokyo Station”, “direct connection to Tokyo Station” and “abundant natural light”.

Art Deco design, a large refreshment area and switchable “magic glass” windows for privacy are also features. An international serviced office now operates inside a building with a postal history.

01 · Private offices and meeting rooms

Rooms for teams and five meeting rooms

Private offices come with height-adjustable desks, and there are five meeting rooms.

Outside the windows are Tokyo Station's red-brick building and the streets of Marunouchi.

What is magic glass?

Glass that switches electrically between clear and frosted. The website highlights magic-glass windows for privacy.

One pane gives you both openness and screening during meetings.
