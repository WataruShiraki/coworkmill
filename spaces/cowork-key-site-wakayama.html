Wakayama, Wakayama

Key Site (Wakayama) — an open space open on weekdays 10:00–17:30, eight minutes from Wakayama Station

The Key Site space is on the 5th floor of the Key Site building in Kuroda, Wakayama, an eight-minute walk from JR Wakayama Station. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An open space you can sit in without booking

It is eight minutes on foot from JR Wakayama Station.

The open space needs no booking — just use any free seat. There is Wi-Fi, and chargers and stationery to borrow.
