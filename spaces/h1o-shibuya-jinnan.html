Shibuya, Tokyo

H¹O Shibuya Jinnan — two lounges and a rooftop terrace supervised by parkERs

H¹O Shibuya Jinnan, a service office run by Nomura Real Estate, is in Jinnan, Shibuya, a seven-minute walk from Exit B1 of Shibuya Station on the Tokyo Metro. As well as private offices, it has shared lounges for tenants on the 2nd and 8th floors and a terrace on the roof. Interior design of the lounges and rooftop terrace was supervised by parkERs, the spatial design brand of Park Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Another place to be, outside your private office

H¹O is a service office run by Nomura Real Estate. You can rent a private office with utilities included, and it is designed for start-ups, projects and branch offices.

Shibuya Jinnan offers plenty of shared space outside the private offices: lounges for a change of scene or quick meetings on the 2nd and 8th floors, and a terrace on the roof.

01 · Lounges

Two lounges, on the 2nd and 8th floors

Shared lounges for a change of scene or quick meetings are on the 2nd and 8th floors.

The interior design of the lounges on floors 1, 2 and 8 and the rooftop terrace was supervised by parkERs.

02 · HUMAN FIRST SALON

A salon for many ways of working

The official website presents a “HUMAN FIRST SALON for a wide range of work styles”. There is also a PHONE BOOTH for calls.

03 · Rooftop terrace

A terrace overlooking Shibuya

On the roof is a green rooftop terrace, described on the official website as “an open space with a panoramic view of Shibuya's streets”.

04 · Entrance and hours

Open 24 hours

You can come and go 24 hours a day, with no closing days (except statutory inspection days). The building was completed in August 2020.

What is a service office?

An office where you can rent a private room by the month, with furniture, equipment and reception services included. Shared lounges and meeting rooms are often available.

The lounges at H¹O Shibuya Jinnan are shared facilities for tenants; there is no information on public drop-in use.
