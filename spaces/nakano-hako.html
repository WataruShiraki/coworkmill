Nakano, Tokyo

NAKANO HAKO — coworking and a private community hall for the neighbourhood, two minutes from Nakano Station

NAKANO HAKO is on the 2nd floor of the Nakano Southerrace Residence tower, about a two-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Nakano Station and directly connected by a deck. It is made up of PRIVATE BOX, a coworking space for work or study; COMMUNITY BOX, a private community hall for the neighbourhood; and OPEN BOX, where artworks can be displayed. There is a quiet focus lounge, an open lounge where you can talk, and a Refresh Box where you take off your shoes and relax. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Connect × expand × weave together

Under the motto “connect × expand × weave together”, NAKANO HAKO offers a learning space for study, work and hobbies. The name HAKO (box) stands for “a place for the individual, a place of your own”.

It aims to be a crossroads where users meet and a core for solving problems. Through its private community hall, it also sets out to support local problem-solving as a place where generations gather.

01 · PRIVATE BOX

Coworking for working alone or together

PRIVATE BOX is a coworking space for both people who want to focus alone and people who want to relax as in a café.

It costs ¥1,200 an hour or up to ¥3,400 a day, and monthly membership is ¥33,000 for 30 days.

02 · Focus lounge and open lounge

Seats for silence, seats for talking

SHU CHU LOUNGE has wide table seats and private or semi-private seats divided by partitions. Talking and video calls are not allowed there.

OPEN LOUNGE is a space where you can relax as in a café, talk and hold video calls. The Refresh Box is a shoes-off place to take a break.

03 · COMMUNITY BOX

A private community hall for the neighbourhood

COMMUNITY BOX is a private community hall where people can share hobbies and interests and meet one another. Group registration is required to use it.

There are four rooms; the largest, COMMUNITY BOX-C, is 72 m² for 20 people. There are also A (11 m², 6 people), B (18 m², 8 people) and D (16 m², 6 people).

What is a community hall (kominkan)?

A facility where local people gather for learning, hobbies and social activities. Most are run by local governments.

NAKANO HAKO, as a private facility, runs its own “private community hall”, opening a place for local gatherings on the same floor as its coworking space.
