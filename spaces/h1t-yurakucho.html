Yurakucho, Tokyo

H¹T Yurakucho — a corporate shared office with boxes, booths and rooms on the 9th floor of the Tokyo Kotsu Kaikan

H¹T Yurakucho, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 9th floor of the Tokyo Kotsu Kaikan, a one-minute walk from JR Yurakucho Station. With an open space for 19, 10 single boxes, 11 single booths, 14 single rooms and 6 meeting rooms, it is one of the H¹T sites with the widest range and number of seats. It is open 8:15–22:00 on weekends and holidays, just as on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A station-front site where you choose your seat type

H¹T Yurakucho has three kinds of single seat alone — boxes, booths and rooms — 35 in total. Depending on the day's work, you can pick anything from an open seat to a private room with a door.

There are six meeting rooms: two for 4 and four for 8. With four rooms for 8, internal meetings and training can be handled here too.

01 · Open space

Nineteen shared seats

The open space seats 19.

It is inside the Tokyo Kotsu Kaikan, one minute from Yurakucho Station, so it is easy to reach even on rainy days.

02 · Boxes and booths

21 single-person seats

There are 10 single-person boxes and 11 single-person booths.

These screened seats suit focused work.

03 · Rooms

14 single-person rooms

There are 14 single-person rooms.

Calls and online meetings can be taken calmly in a room with a door.

04 · Meeting rooms

Two for 4 and four for 8

There are two meeting rooms for 4 and four for 8.

You can choose a room by headcount.

What is the Tokyo Kotsu Kaikan?

A building right next to JR Yurakucho Station; H¹T Yurakucho is on its 9th floor.

The official website gives it as one minute on foot from JR Yurakucho Station, and Exit D8 for Yurakucho Station on the Yurakucho Line.
