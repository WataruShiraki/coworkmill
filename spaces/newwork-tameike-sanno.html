Tameike-sanno, Tokyo

NewWork Tameike-sanno — a corporate-membership satellite office with plenty of private booths, on the 8th floor of the Watanabe Shoji Akasaka Building

NewWork Tameike-sanno is on the 8th floor of the Watanabe Shoji Akasaka Building, a three-minute walk from Tameike-sanno Station and four minutes from Akasaka Station. Although it is a small site with 21 seats, it has a high share of private booths: nine single booths and one wide booth for 4. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A booth-rich site between the government district and Akasaka

Within walking distance of both Tameike-sanno and Akasaka stations, ten of its 21 seats are private booths.

There is one meeting room for 8. Both the meeting room and the private booths are booked in advance, and have locks and monitors.

01 · Workspace

A small 21-seat floor

There are 21 seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Nine single booths and one wide booth for 4

There are nine single private booths and one wide private booth for 4, all booked in advance.

It is easy to secure a booth even on days full of online meetings.

03 · Meeting room

One room for 8

There is one meeting room for 8, booked in advance.

It suits meetings before or after visiting clients in Akasaka or Tameike-sanno.

What is Tameike-sanno?

The name of a station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Namboku lines, close to Akasaka and Nagatacho.

NewWork Tameike-sanno is on the 8th floor of the Watanabe Shoji Akasaka Building, three minutes on foot from the station.
