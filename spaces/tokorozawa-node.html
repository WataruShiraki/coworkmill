Tokorozawa, Saitama

Tokorozawa Node — a shared space aiming to be a hub for people and business, two minutes from the West Exit of Tokorozawa Station

Tokorozawa Node is on the 3rd floor of Tokorozawa Sun Plaza, halfway along Prope-dori, a two-minute walk from the West Exit of Tokorozawa Station on the Seibu Ikebukuro and Shinjuku lines. It is a shared space combining rental meeting rooms, a shared office, a coworking workspace and multipurpose booths, with over 330 m² of floor space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A “node” where people and businesses cross paths

Tokorozawa Node is positioned as a facility for people who want to energise their business. It aims to be a “node (hub)” where many kinds of businesses gather and exchanges and new ideas are born.

With over 330 m² of floor space, it is presented as large for the Tokorozawa area.

01 · Workspace

A semi-private workspace with a sliding door and small windows

The coworking workspace has 6 seats and holds up to 10. It is a semi-private room enclosed by walls with a sliding door and small windows.

The official website describes a new design balancing a sense of privacy with a calm environment.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms from 4 to 30 people

There is a large meeting room (20–30 people, max 30), a medium room (max 8) and a small room (max 4). The large room comes with a big screen as standard.

Members get 20% off the standard meeting-room rate (booking required).

03 · Shared facilities

Café corner and shared office

There is a café corner and a vending machine for anyone to use, plus fast internet, a paid multifunction printer and lockers with lockable mailboxes.

The shared office comes with a dedicated desk (110 cm × 70 cm) and business chair for ¥38,500 a month (incl. tax).

What is a node?

A word for a “knot” or “junction point” in something connected like a net.

Tokorozawa Node takes the name because it aims to be a hub where diverse businesses gather and interact.
