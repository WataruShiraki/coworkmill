Chitose, Hokkaido

WORK STYLING New Chitose Airport — on the 3rd floor of the domestic terminal, open 8:00–21:00 including weekends and holidays

WORK STYLING New Chitose Airport is on the 3rd floor of the New Chitose Airport Domestic Terminal Building, a five-minute walk from JR New Chitose Airport Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.
