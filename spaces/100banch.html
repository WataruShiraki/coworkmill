100BANCH — a three-storey lab in Shibuya where 100 projects take shape

About a five-minute walk from Shibuya Station stands 100BANCH, a three-storey building opened on 7 July 2017 to mark Panasonic's 100th anniversary. It calls itself a lab where 100 projects are always on the move: a test kitchen on the ground floor, a garage for projects led by people under 35 on the second, and an event space for about 100 people on the third. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Young energy, free of convention, for a richer world 100 years from now

100BANCH was born to celebrate Panasonic's 100th anniversary. Its idea is that a gathering of young energy, unbound by convention, will enrich the future a hundred years ahead. It is run by the general incorporated association 100BANCH, and the food space on the ground floor is operated by Cafe Company.

At its centre is the GARAGE Program. Every month, projects led by someone under 35 are selected through a review, and the chosen teams use the second-floor GARAGE free of charge. Around 10 to 20 project teams work on the same floor at any time, and the layout can be rearranged freely.

01 · 3F Loft

An event space for 100 people, with a 200-inch screen

The third-floor LOFT is an event space meant to spark encounters with new knowledge. It holds about 100 people and has a 200-inch screen and a wooden stage.

Workshops, screenings, concerts and even plays are held here. Because the stage sits right above the work floor, projects that grow on the second floor can step straight out in front of an audience upstairs.

02 · 1F Kitchen

A test kitchen for the food of the future

The ground floor is a test kitchen run by Cafe Company. It explores future food experiences and works with 100BANCH projects as a place to create new value.

With food on the entrance floor, passers-by can feel the energy inside the building.

What is the GARAGE Program?

100BANCH's accelerator for projects led by people under 35. Teams selected in the monthly review can use the second-floor GARAGE for free.

Instead of selling desks, it chooses projects and gives them space for free — the opposite entrance to an ordinary coworking space.
