Nishi-Shinjuku, Tokyo

HAPON Shinjuku — a shared office themed on “Japan”, with an archipelago-shaped table and a tatami room, on the 5th floor of the Musashi Building

HAPON Shinjuku is on the 5th floor of the Musashi Building in Nishi-Shinjuku 7-chome. Its theme is “Japan”. A shared table shaped like the Japanese archipelago, a tatami meeting space called the “Fuji Room”, a café space and bookshelves fill a shared office covering the whole 419.41 m² floor. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Hap on!

HAPON's concept is “Hap on!” — the name carries the idea that work is all about chance encounters.

The official website describes it as a shared office themed on “Japan”, creating a place where people from many industries meet. The archipelago-shaped table turns that idea directly into furniture.

01 · The archipelago table

Sit on the map of Japan

At the centre of the open space is a shared table shaped like the Japanese archipelago.

It is a playful piece of furniture where conversation might start with which region of Japan you happen to be sitting on.

02 · The Fuji Room and café

Meetings on tatami

There is a tatami meeting space called the “Fuji Room”, where you take your shoes off to talk.

A café space and bookshelves are also part of the open space.

03 · Meeting rooms and reception room

Two meeting rooms for about 8

The South and North meeting rooms each hold about 8 people, and the reception room about 5.

Shared areas and closed rooms sit together on the same floor.

What does “Hap on!” mean?

The concept phrase of HAPON Shinjuku. The official website explains that it carries the meaning “work is all about chance encounters”.

Creating encounters between people from many industries is what the space sets out to do.
