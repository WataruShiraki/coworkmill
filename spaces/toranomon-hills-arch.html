Toranomon, Tokyo

ARCH, Toranomon Hills — 3,800 m² on the 4th floor of Toranomon Hills Business Tower, built only for the new-business teams of large companies

On the 4th floor of Toranomon Hills Business Tower, Mori Building runs ARCH, which it calls the world's first incubation center designed only for teams that create new businesses or transform existing ones inside large corporations. One 3,800 m² floor holds coworking areas, private offices, project rooms, a glass-walled event room and a café lounge. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Bringing the “outposts” of large companies under one roof

ARCH is for the teams inside large companies that build new businesses or drive change. Most incubators serve startups; ARCH focuses only on corporate venture teams, which its website describes as a world first. It is planned and run by Mori Building, with interiors by Wonderwall®.

The service rests on four pillars: training in the skills of building a business, mentoring by experienced mentors, networking across industries, and the 3,800 m² workplace itself. Two Chief Incubation Officers give advice and connect members with the right people.

01 · Co-work

Many kinds of seats, chosen to suit the task

CO-WORK offers a wide range of workspaces so members can pick where to work for each task. Every seat has power and network access.

PREMIUM CO-WORK is an executive area with its own lounge and private rooms.

02 · Small office and project room

A second office, and rooms for short projects

SMALL OFFICE is a secure second office, also suited to new businesses run jointly by several companies.

PROJECT ROOM is a room for small teams that can be used for short-term projects — rent it only for the weeks you need to focus.

03 · Magnet room and courtyard

A glass-walled event room and the courtyard beside it

MAGNET ROOM is an open, glass-walled space for seminars, events and workshops. Anyone outside can see what is happening inside.

Next to it, the COURTYARD is an open area with a pantry, used for events, seminars and lunch meetings.

04 · Café & lounge

An airy café and lounge

The café lounge is the place for a light meal and a quick meeting. Weekly mixers, small round-table talks and pitch events are held inside the center.

The members' community platform has personal profiles, a company list, project records and a discussion board.

What is a corporate “dejima”?

In Japanese business, a dejima is a team set up a little apart from the parent company to build new businesses or drive change. The name comes from Dejima, the small artificial island in Nagasaki that was Japan's only window to the West for two centuries. ARCH gathers only these teams in one place.

When people facing the same challenges share a floor, finding someone to talk to becomes easy. Choosing carefully who to bring together is what gives the place its value.
