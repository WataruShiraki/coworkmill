Nihonbashi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower — the “WORKSTYLING LAB” with nine themed meeting rooms, directly connected to Mitsukoshimae Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower is on the 6th floor of Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower, directly connected to Mitsukoshimae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hanzomon lines. Its concept is “WORKSTYLING LAB”, with nine meeting rooms, each with a different theme. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

WORKSTYLING LAB

The concept of WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower is “WORKSTYLING LAB”. With nine meeting rooms, each with its own theme, it responds to the many ways people work today, the website says.

It is directly connected to Mitsukoshimae Station and to Shin-Nihombashi Station on the JR Sobu Line, and is an eight-minute walk from JR Kanda and Tokyo stations.

01 · Entrance and open space

From reception to shared seating

Reception crew greet you at the 6th-floor entrance.

The open space has many kinds of seating, so you can choose where to sit according to the day's work.

02 · Themed meeting rooms

Nine meeting rooms

Each meeting room has a different theme. Besides rooms for up to 12, there are rooms for 13–80 people.

Choose a room to suit the meeting — or your mood.

What is WORKSTYLING LAB?

The concept of WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower. As the word “lab” suggests, it has nine meeting rooms, each with a different theme.

Different meetings suit different rooms. Giving each room its own character makes this a place to try out many ways of working.
