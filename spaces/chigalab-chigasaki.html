Chigasaki, Kanagawa

Chigalab — a place where new “schemes” are born from people's connections, three minutes from Chigasaki Station

Chigalab is a coworking space on the 5th floor of the Warashina Building in Shin'eicho, a three-minute walk from Chigasaki Station on the JR Tokaido Line. It describes itself as “a community in Shonan and Chigasaki where new ‘schemes’ are born from people's connections”. Its free-seating workspace comes with bookshelves, sofa seats, focus booths, a kitchen and a meeting room. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

New “schemes” born from people's connections

Chigalab is a coworking space run as a community for Shonan and Chigasaki. Its official website says it will “build a community that values long-term relationships”.

The founder's greeting includes the line “Life is made of happy accidents and chance encounters.” It is presented less as a place to rent a seat and more as one that values chance meetings.

01 · Workspace

Free seating — pick the spot you like

The coworking area is free seating. Besides power and Wi-Fi, there is a printer, external monitors, a projector, chargers, book stands and stationery.

Drinks include drip coffee and Shonan mandarin and tomato juices.

02 · Bookshelves and sofas

“My Bookshelf” and sofa seats

The facility guide shows a bookshelf named “My Bookshelf”. Alongside the work seats, there are sofa seats.

The bookshelves and sofas add a place to unwind to a place for work.

03 · Focus booths and meeting room

For when you want to focus, and when you want to talk

There are focus booths for working alone, and a bookable meeting room at ¥700 per 30 minutes for members and ¥1,000 for others.

04 · Kitchen

Coworking with a kitchen

There is a kitchen on site. The price list also includes a “Challenge Kitchen” plan for fixed use at lunch or dinner on 4–5 days a month.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space by the hour or the day without registering as a member.

At Chigalab, you can use it without membership for ¥800 for two hours, ¥1,500 for four hours or ¥2,200 a day.
