Sannomiya, Hyogo

WORK STYLING Sannomiya — partitioned seats on a blue carpet, one minute from Sannomiya Station

WORK STYLING Sannomiya is in the Nissay Sannomiya Building, a one-minute walk from JR Sannomiya Station and Kobe-Sannomiya Station on the Hankyu and Hanshin lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

In front of Sannomiya Station

It is one minute on foot from JR Sannomiya Station, Kobe-Sannomiya Station on the Hankyu and Hanshin lines, and the subway's Sannomiya Station.

According to the official website, after 19:00 you enter through the side entrance at the back of the car park on the right of the building.

01 · Open space

Seats on a blue carpet

Seats with black partitions line a blue carpet.

The chairs come in yellow, green and blue.

02 · Sofa seats

Brown sofas

By the windows are seats with brown sofas and white tables.

These facing seats also work for meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

A meeting room with green chairs

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with green chairs and a monitor.

04 · Private rooms

Private rooms with black doors

There are private rooms you can use with the door closed.

Pictured: private rooms with black-framed doors.

What is a side entrance?

An entrance separate from the building's main front door.

At WORK STYLING Sannomiya, after 19:00 you enter through the side entrance at the back of the car park.
