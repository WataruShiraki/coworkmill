Motoyawata, Chiba

WORK STYLING SOLO Motoyawata — a café space and single rooms, one minute from Motoyawata Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Motoyawata is on the 4th floor of the Motoyawata Eki Minami Building, a one-minute walk from JR Motoyawata Station, four minutes from Motoyawata Station on the Toei line and six from Keisei Yawata Station on the Keisei Main Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A one-minute walk from JR Motoyawata Station, four minutes from Motoyawata Station on the Toei line and six from Keisei Yawata Station on the Keisei Main Line.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
