Aoyama, Tokyo

fabbit Aoyama-itchome — private offices with a coworking lounge on the 8th floor of Aoyama Tower Place, 2 minutes from Aoyama-itchome Station

fabbit Aoyama-itchome is on the 8th floor of Aoyama Tower Place, a two-minute walk from exit 4 North of Aoyama-itchome Station. It is listed on the official website of the rental-office brand CROSSCOOP as one of its sites. It has fully private rooms for 2 to about 20 people, a coworking lounge, meeting rooms and phone booths. Introduction

On the 8th floor of an Aoyama tower

fabbit Aoyama-itchome is on the 8th floor of Aoyama Tower Place. The website calls it “a high-grade office designed for ease of work”.

Rooms range from 2 to about 20 people, fitting anything from a small team to a whole department. The shared facilities aim to balance working and refreshing.

01 · Coworking lounge

A workplace outside your room

The coworking lounge is a shared workspace for private-room tenants.

There are many kinds of seating to suit the day's tasks.

02 · Meeting room and phone booths

Places for meetings and calls

There is a meeting room and phone booths.

Use them for client meetings and online calls.

03 · Private rooms

From 2 to about 20 people

Private rooms hold 2 to about 20 people, all fully enclosed, and company registration is possible.

They are rooms inside a tower two minutes from Aoyama-itchome Station.

What is a fully private room?

A room enclosed by walls and a door, closed all the way to the ceiling. Every room at fabbit Aoyama-itchome is fully private.

Other tenants cannot hear your conversations or see your screens.
