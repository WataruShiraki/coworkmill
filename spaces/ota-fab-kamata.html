Kamata, Tokyo

Ota fab — coworking with a maker workshop, two minutes from Kamata Station

Ota fab is on the 6th floor of the Koyama No. 2 Building in Nishi-Kamata 7-chome, a two-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Kamata Station. Its roughly 200 m² floor brings together a coworking space, a fab lab with 3D printers and more, and rental space for meetings and lessons. In Ota City, known for its small factories, it is a place where work and making things share one space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A digital community workshop next to where you work

Ota fab is a shared space run by Smile Link Co., Ltd., offering three uses: coworking, making things and rental space.

The fab lab is a community workshop with 3D printers, a laser cutter and a UV printer. Its users range from elementary-school children to people in their seventies, and it runs beginner workshops and programming classes for children.

01 · Coworking

From drop-in to fixed desks

Choose from hourly drop-in to monthly fixed or free seats. Monthly plans are ¥30,800 for a fixed desk, ¥14,300 for a free desk and ¥5,500 for a weekend-and-holiday free desk.

There is free Wi-Fi, power at every seat and free drinks, and you can bring food in. There is a 34-inch ultra-wide monitor, and children are welcome.

02 · Lounge and meetings

A lounge for business talks, and meeting space

In the lounge space, you can invite clients for business talks, with free coffee and other drinks.

It also works as a venue for workshops and meetings, with a meeting area and whiteboards. Company registration and a dedicated mailbox are available, making it a base for starting a business or a side business.

03 · Fab lab

15 3D printers and a laser cutter

There are 15 3D printers of four types, plus a laser cutter and a UV printer. Coworking members can use the Fab space as a development base.

Machine fees per hour are ¥600 for a 3D printer, ¥2,000 for the laser cutter and ¥2,700 for the UV printer.

What is a fab lab?

A workshop that opens digital machine tools such as 3D printers and laser cutters to the public.

At Ota fab, the fab lab is on the same floor as the coworking space, and members who have taken the training can use the machines.
