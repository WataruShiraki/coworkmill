Marunouchi, Tokyo

EGG — Mitsubishi Estate's incubation office on the 10th and 9th floors of the Shin-Marunouchi Building

Right in front of Tokyo Station, EGG is on the 10th and 9th floors of the Shin-Marunouchi Building, run by Mitsubishi Estate. This incubation office in Marunouchi has private offices, an event space, the Sundial Lounge and an all-day dining room. Its motto is “360° Innovation Place”. Introduction

360° Innovation Place

EGG calls itself a “360° Innovation Place” and is run as a community hub where entrepreneurs in Marunouchi move forward together. The website describes it as a combination of refined workspace and an active business network.

It is on the upper floors of the Shin-Marunouchi Building, facing the Marunouchi exit of Tokyo Station. Reception is on the 10th floor. Private offices, shared spaces and a place to eat are all in one facility.

01 · Sundial Lounge

The shared lounge where members gather

The Sundial Lounge is the members' shared lounge, for work, meetings and breaks.

From the upper floors of the Shin-Marunouchi Building, it looks out over Marunouchi.

02 · Private offices

Rooms for teams

Private offices are rooms for teams. Entrepreneurs who want a base in Marunouchi and companies arriving from overseas move in here.

Members move between the shared lounge and their own rooms as they work.

03 · Event space and dining

A place to gather, and lunch

The event space hosts gatherings for members and people from outside.

The all-day dining room is open 12:00–17:00, and mealtimes double as time for members to meet.

What is an incubation office?

An office for growing newly founded companies and new businesses. Besides renting rooms, it provides connections and support programmes.

EGG plays this role in the middle of Marunouchi, one of Japan's leading business districts. Having large corporations in the building next door is a value that sites in other areas cannot offer.
