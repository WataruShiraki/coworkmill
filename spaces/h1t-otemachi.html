Otemachi, Tokyo

H¹T Otemachi — a corporate shared office with 45 single-person rooms on the 20th and 21st floors of the Taisei Otemachi Building

H¹T Otemachi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 20th and 21st floors of the Taisei Otemachi Building (formerly the Otemachi Nomura Building), a one-minute walk from Exit B2a of Otemachi Station on the Tokyo Metro Tozai Line and Toei Mita Line. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fee. Spread over two floors, this site has 45 single-person rooms, making it one of the larger H¹T locations. Introduction

A large site across two high floors

H¹T Otemachi spans the 20th and 21st floors of the Taisei Otemachi Building. With 45 single-person rooms and eight single-person boxes, it has a lot of seats for working alone.

There is one box for 4, and two meeting rooms each for 6 and for 10. The official website says it can be used for many purposes: remote work, a base for sales staff, seminars, training and interviews.

What is a satellite shared office?

A workplace away from head office that several companies use together.

H¹T is run by Nomura Real Estate: companies become members, and their employees use sites while out or near home.
