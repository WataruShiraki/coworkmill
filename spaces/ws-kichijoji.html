Kichijoji, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Kichijoji — a shared office by the station of a residential town, one minute from Kichijoji Station

WORK STYLING Kichijoji is on the 4th floor of the Kichijoji Subaru Building, a one-minute walk from Kichijoji Station (JR and Keio lines). It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

A place to work by the station where you live

It is on the 4th floor of the Kichijoji Subaru Building, one minute from Kichijoji Station. Unlike the city-centre sites, it sits by the station of a largely residential town.

It lets people who want to work near home do so without travelling into central Tokyo, with single rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space.

01 · Entrance

From reception to the work areas

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

It is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays.

02 · Open space

A mix of seat shapes

The open space has sofa seats, desks and other seat shapes.

Use it for solo work or short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms can be chosen by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

With a desk, chair and monitor, they are for when you need to focus.

What is a pay-as-you-go plan?

A contract where you pay only for the time you use, with no fixed monthly cost.

WORK STYLING offers one in 10-minute units; meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.
