Yotsuya, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Yotsuya — on the 6th floor of the No. 7 Akiyama Building, 4 minutes from both Kojimachi and Yotsuya stations

WORK STYLING Yotsuya is on the 6th floor of the No. 7 Akiyama Building, four minutes on foot from Kojimachi Station on the Tokyo Metro and four from Yotsuya Station on JR and Tokyo Metro lines. It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

Right between two stations

WORK STYLING Yotsuya is four minutes from both Kojimachi and Yotsuya stations — equally close whichever you use.

Between the government district and the residential streets of Bancho, it is easy to drop into between client visits.

01 · Entrance

Through reception to the 6th floor

There is a reception at the entrance with crew to help.

The work floor continues right from the entrance.

02 · Open space

Solo work and short meetings in one place

The open space has seats for solo work and for short meetings.

A mix of desk heights and shapes gives you choice.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms chosen by headcount

Meeting rooms can be chosen by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they work for meetings where you speak up.

04 · Private rooms

Close the door and focus

There are single-person rooms.

Phone booths, a multifunction printer and a drinks and café corner are also available.

What is a satellite office?

A small workplace away from head office, usable on the way to or between appointments.

WORK STYLING has about 600 sites across Japan, and a contract lets you move between them.
