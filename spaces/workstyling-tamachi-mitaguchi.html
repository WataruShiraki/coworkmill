Tamachi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Tamachi Mita Exit — a shared office on the 13th floor of Tamachi Center Building, one minute from Tamachi and Mita

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Tamachi Mita Exit is on the 13th floor of the Tamachi Center Building in Shiba 5-chome, a one-minute walk from both JR Tamachi Station and Mita Station on the Toei Subway. It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, available 8:00–21:00 on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The 13th floor, one minute from two stations

WORK STYLING Tamachi Mita Exit is on the 13th floor of the Tamachi Center Building, one minute on foot from JR Tamachi and Toei Mita stations — convenient whichever line you take.

The official site publishes seven photos of meeting rooms; this site has many of them. The open space at your home site needs no booking.

01 · Entrance and open space

Shared seating on the 13th floor

From the 13th-floor entrance you step into the open space.

The open space at your home site needs no booking, and there is a drinks and café corner.

02 · Meeting rooms

A row of rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people, and the photos show many rooms of different sizes.

Choose a room to suit the number of attendees.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for focus

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused work.

There are also phone booths you can use without booking.

How do WORK STYLING meeting rooms work?

WORK STYLING meeting rooms hold up to 12 people and are used by booking.
