Hachioji, Tokyo

Coworking Space Hachioji 8Beat — a neighbourhood coworking space from ¥500 for two hours, on the 5th floor of the Tonen Building

Coworking Space Hachioji 8Beat is on the 5th floor of the Tonen Building in Misakicho, near the North Exit of Hachioji Station. At ¥500 for two hours or ¥1,000 a day, it is a relaxed neighbourhood coworking space. Everything below is based on the official website.

4 photos · © 8Beat (official website) · Tap any photo to view it full size

UsesBesides work, it is used for events, study sessions and students' self-study

Introduction

A neighbourhood coworking space near Hachioji Station

8Beat is a coworking space near Hachioji Station offering seats with Wi-Fi and power.

The official website says it is used not only by people working but also by event organisers, classes and students studying on their own.

01 · Workspace

Seats from ¥500 for two hours

Drop-in costs ¥500 for two hours or ¥1,000 a day.

Seats come with Wi-Fi and power.

02 · Bookshelves

Books to read freely

There are bookshelves on the floor, and you can read freely while at 8Beat.

Books to pick up between tasks give the place its character.

What is coworking?

People from different companies and jobs working side by side in the same place.

A small neighbourhood coworking space like 8Beat also becomes a venue for study sessions and events.
