Ebisu, Tokyo

NewWork Ebisu — a 39-seat satellite office on the 3rd floor of the Ebisu ST Building, 3 minutes from the West Exit

NewWork Ebisu is on the 3rd floor of the Ebisu ST Building, a three-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Ebisu Station. It has 39 open and booth seats, meeting rooms for six and four, and four enclosed booths for one. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Another desk outside the office

NewWork Ebisu is a corporate-membership satellite shared office: a company joins, and its staff use it as a base away from head office.

Three minutes from the West Exit of Ebisu Station, with two meeting rooms of different sizes, it handles everything from solo work to small team meetings.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 39-seat workspace

There are 39 seats of two types, open and booth.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for six and four

One meeting room for six and one for four, by booking.

Choose a room by headcount.

03 · Enclosed seated booths

Four booths for one

There are four enclosed seated booths for one.

Take calls and online meetings without worrying about others.

What is corporate membership?

A system where a company, not an individual, signs up as a member and its employees use the facilities.

Because NewWork is corporate-membership only, drop-in use is not available.
