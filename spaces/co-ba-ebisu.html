Ebisu, Tokyo

co-ba ebisu — a shared workplace across two floors and about 1,600 m² in Ebisu-nishi, built as a “liberated zone for work”

co-ba ebisu is on the first and second floors of JP noie Ebisu-nishi, three minutes from Daikanyama Station and five from Ebisu Station. Opened in November 2019, it covers 1,573.42 m²: coworking on the first floor and private offices on the second. Its theme is “a liberated zone for work”, and its design theme is “WORK ASSEMBLE”. It is run by Ba & Co. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A liberated zone for work, for adults with ambition

co-ba is “a shared workplace for every kind of challenge”: a network with directly run sites in Ebisu (Tokyo) and Kannai (Yokohama) and more than 18 partner sites from Iwate to Miyazaki. Ebisu is one of the directly run sites, presented as “a place where adults with ambition gather”.

The design theme, WORK ASSEMBLE, encourages people to assemble their own way of working and spending time. The first-floor coworking is open 24 hours, and the second floor has private offices on two-year fixed-term contracts.

01 · Park and café

PARK for 70, CAFE for 40

The first floor has two event spaces, PARK and CAFE. PARK holds up to 70 people and CAFE up to 40; members can rent them at half price.

Day to day they are shared spaces, booked out only for events — keeping work and connection on the same floor.

02 · 2F private offices

Rooms for 8 to 20 people

The second floor is for private offices. Single and twin rooms are about 31–34 m² for around 8 people; suites are about 48–72 m² for 10–20.

Contracts are two-year fixed terms, management fee included. Second-floor tenants can add first-floor coworking for ¥30,000 a month.

What does WORK ASSEMBLE mean?

co-ba ebisu's design theme. It means that people put together their own way of working and spending time.

Early mornings only, nights and weekends only, two days a month — a price list split by time of day turns this idea directly into contract options.
