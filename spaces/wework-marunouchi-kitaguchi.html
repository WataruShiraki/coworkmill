Marunouchi, Tokyo

WeWork Marunouchi Kitaguchi — a flexible office on floors 7–11 of the Marunouchi Kitaguchi Building, directly connected to Tokyo Station

WeWork Marunouchi Kitaguchi is on the 7th to 11th floors of the Marunouchi Kitaguchi Building, directly connected to Tokyo and Otemachi stations. Built around the concept of a relaxing space, it has a lounge with a different concept on each floor and lots of sofa seating. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A different lounge on every floor

WeWork Marunouchi Kitaguchi's concept is “a space to relax”. Each floor has a lounge with its own concept, with lots of sofa seating, the website says.

The shared areas use a lively colour palette, creating spaces that feel bright yet calm.

01 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

35 meeting rooms

There are 35 meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

Ceilings are 2,800 mm high.

02 · Private offices

From one person to hundreds

The website says there are many private offices for anything from one person to several hundred, and it can accommodate whole floors and custom requests.

The ground floor of the building has shops, restaurants and a supermarket.

What is a flexible office?

An office where the size and contract can change flexibly with headcount and timeframe. WeWork describes its sites this way.

Contracts are shorter than ordinary office leases, and shared lounges and meeting rooms come with them.
