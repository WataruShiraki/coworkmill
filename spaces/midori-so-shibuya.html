Shibuya, Tokyo

MIDORI.so Shibuya — coworking with a lounge and library on floors 3–6 in Sakuragaokacho, Shibuya

MIDORI.so Shibuya is in Sakuragaokacho, a four-minute walk from Shibuya Station. Across four floors, from the 3rd to the 6th, there are workspaces, a lounge, a kitchen, a library, meeting rooms, private booths and private rooms. It is run by MIRAI INSTITUTE, which also operates the Nakameguro and Nagatacho sites. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Four floors to choose from, in Shibuya's Sakuragaoka

MIDORI.so is a coworking community exploring “the future of work in a society that values the individual”. The Shibuya site is in a corner of Sakuragaoka, an area being redeveloped, with workspaces and lounges over four floors.

The 3rd floor has the lounge and kitchen where people gather, the 4th and 5th have workspaces and booths for focus, and the 6th has private rooms. The higher you go, the quieter it gets — so you can choose where to be according to the day's work.

01 · Library

Seats surrounded by books

The library lets you work surrounded by bookshelves.

A free desk is ¥44,000 a month, a fixed desk ¥60,500 a month and a private booth from ¥71,500 a month (all with an ¥11,000 sign-up fee), available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

02 · 4F–5F workspaces and meeting rooms

Floors for focus

The 4th floor has a workspace, meeting rooms and private booths; the 5th has a workspace, private booths and a lounge.

Pop-in is ¥3,300 a day or ¥33,000 for an 11-day pack, usable 10:00–18:00.

What is a private booth?

A semi-private seat inside the coworking space, for one person or a small team. At MIDORI.so Shibuya they are on the 4th and 5th floors.

It sits between a free desk and a private room: you keep your own spot while still feeling the atmosphere of the shared space.
