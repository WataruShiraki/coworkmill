Hamamatsucho, Tokyo

NewWork Daimon / Hamamatsucho — a satellite office on the 4th floor of the RBM Hamamatsucho Building, 1 minute from Daimon and 4 from Hamamatsucho

NewWork Daimon / Hamamatsucho is on the 4th floor of the RBM Hamamatsucho Building, one minute from exit A2 of Daimon Station and four from the North Exit of JR Hamamatsucho Station. It has 36 open and booth seats, two meeting rooms for four and six enclosed booths for one. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site named after two stations

As its name suggests, NewWork Daimon / Hamamatsucho is within walking distance of both stations: one minute from exit A2 of Daimon and four from the North Exit of JR Hamamatsucho.

With 36 seats, two meeting rooms for four and six booths for one, it supports both solo work and small meetings.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 36-seat workspace

There are 36 seats of two types, open and booth.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for four

Two meeting rooms for four are available by booking.

Sized for small meetings.

03 · Enclosed seated booths

Six booths for one

There are six enclosed seated booths for one.

Use them for online meetings and calls.

What is a phone booth?

A small partitioned spot for making calls, so people working at open seats can talk without disturbing others.

Every NewWork site has phone booths.
