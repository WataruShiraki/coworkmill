Ikebukuro, Tokyo

WeWork Hareza Ikebukuro — interiors inspired by Ikebukuro's art and film, on the upper floors of Hareza Tower

WeWork Hareza Ikebukuro is on the upper floors of Hareza Tower, Ikebukuro's new landmark. One of the largest sites in Japan, it features interiors inspired by the art, Japanese painting and film rooted in Ikebukuro. Everything below is based on the official website.

BuildingCompleted May 2020 / 33 floors above ground, 2 belowFloorsCeiling height 2,900 mm / 22 meeting rooms / phone booths, parents' room, honesty market and more

Introduction

Where business and culture intersect

The website presents Ikebukuro as a district “where business and culture intersect”. The interiors draw on the art, Japanese painting and film rooted in Ikebukuro.

Members range from startups to long-established manufacturers, and the website says new collaborations come out of their exchanges.

01 · Shared areas

Inspired by art and Japanese painting

The shared areas are full of interiors inspired by Ikebukuro's art, Japanese painting and film.

Ceilings are 2,900 mm high.

02 · Meeting rooms

22 meeting rooms

There are 22 meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

It is open 8:30–20:00 on weekdays.

03 · Building resilience

Vibration control and triple power feeds

The building uses new-generation vibration-control oil dampers and a spot-network system that receives power on three lines simultaneously, as a safeguard against outages.

Each floor's elevator hall has security gates, and a disaster-prevention centre is staffed 24 hours.

What is an honesty market?

A shop with no staff, where you pay for what you take yourself — “honesty” being the key.

The WeWork website lists it among the facilities at its sites.
