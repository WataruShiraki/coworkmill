Machida, Tokyo

BUSO AGORA — a community-based coworking and shared office on the 4th floor of AETA Machida, three minutes from Machida Station

BUSO AGORA is on the 4th floor of AETA Machida, a three-minute walk from Machida Station on the Odakyu Line and JR Yokohama Line. With a free space, booth seats, single booths, private rooms for 2–3 and meeting rooms, it is a community-based coworking space and shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Supporting local start-ups and businesses

The official website presents BUSO AGORA as “a community-based coworking space and shared office supporting start-ups and businesses”.

It is registered with INCU Tokyo, the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's incubation-facility scheme, and offers start-up support programmes plus company registration and mailbox services. Lunchtime talks and other events give members chances to talk with one another.

01 · Free space

Spacious seating three minutes from the station

The free space costs ¥500 per hour, ¥1,100 for three hours and ¥1,700 per day.

Monthly free-space memberships range from ¥8,000 to ¥15,500.

02 · Booth seats and private rooms

Seats for one, rooms for small groups

Booth seats cost ¥2,800 per day. There are also single booths and private rooms for 2–3.

Private rooms range from ¥28,000 to ¥154,000 a month.

03 · Meeting space and meeting room

Places for meetings

There is a business meeting space and a meeting room, which members can book.

Meetings with visitors can be held three minutes from the station.

What is incubation?

Nurturing newly founded companies and businesses with space and advice. The word originally means hatching eggs.

BUSO AGORA is registered with the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's INCU Tokyo scheme and runs a start-up support programme.
