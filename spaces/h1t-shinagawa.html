Shinagawa, Tokyo

H¹T Shinagawa — a corporate shared office with 10 meeting rooms on the 3rd floor of A-PLACE Shinagawa

H¹T Shinagawa, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 3rd floor of A-PLACE Shinagawa, a four-minute walk from the Konan Exit of Shinagawa Station on the JR lines and Tokaido Shinkansen. It is a large site, with an open space for 33, 25 single-person rooms and ten meeting rooms for 4 to 12. It opens on weekdays only, from 7:00 to 20:00. Introduction

A site near the Shinkansen that makes meetings easy

H¹T Shinagawa has ten meeting rooms, including one for 12, plus four boxes for 4 — a layout suited to teams getting together.

For solo work there is an open space for 33 and 25 single-person rooms. Close to the Tokaido Shinkansen at Shinagawa, it is easy to drop in before or after a business trip.

01 · Open space

Thirty-three shared seats

The open space seats 33, on the larger side for H¹T.

It is on the 3rd floor of A-PLACE Shinagawa, four minutes from the Konan Exit of Shinagawa Station.

02 · Boxes

Four boxes for four

There are four boxes that seat 4.

They are handy for short discussions that do not need a meeting room.

03 · Rooms

25 single-person rooms

There are 25 single-person rooms.

Online meetings and focused work can be done in a room with a door.

04 · Meeting rooms

Ten rooms for 4 to 12

The meeting rooms are five for 4, two for 6, two for 8 and one for 12.

They can also handle training and internal meetings.

What is the Konan Exit?

The east-side exit of Shinagawa Station, leading out towards Konan in Minato City.

H¹T Shinagawa is on the 3rd floor of A-PLACE Shinagawa, a four-minute walk from this exit.
