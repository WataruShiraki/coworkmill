Iidabashi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Iidabashi Grand Bloom — a shared office across two floors, the 2nd and the 9th

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Iidabashi Grand Bloom is on the 2nd and 9th floors of Iidabashi Grand Bloom, a one-minute walk from the West Exit of JR Iidabashi Station. Its feature is a single site split across two floors. It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12, single-person rooms and rental offices. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

One site, two floors

WORK STYLING Iidabashi Grand Bloom spans the 2nd and 9th floors of the same building, and the official photo gallery presents them separately.

It is one minute from the West Exit of JR Iidabashi Station and two from exit B2a of the Tokyo Metro station — easy to drop into between transfers at Iidabashi, where several lines meet.

01 · Entrance and open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space needs no booking; use any free seat.

Each of the two floors has its own entrance and open space.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 2–12 people

Meeting rooms hold 2 to 12 people, by booking, in 10-minute units.

Each floor has meeting rooms, and the official gallery shows many of them.

03 · Private rooms and rental offices

Rooms for one and rooms for teams

Single-person rooms are booked in 10-minute units.

The 9th floor also has rental offices for teams.

What is a rental office?

An office where you rent a furnished, networked private room by the month.

At WORK STYLING these come under the dedicated-space plan (FLEX); at Iidabashi Grand Bloom they are on the 9th floor.
