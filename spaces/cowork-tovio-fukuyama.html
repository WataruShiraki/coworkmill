Fukuyama, Hiroshima

Coworking Space tovio (Fukuyama) — open 10:00–18:30 every day, seven minutes from Fukuyama Station

Coworking Space tovio is inside iti SETOUCHI on the ground floor of FP Corporation RiM in Nishimachi, Fukuyama, a seven-minute walk from Fukuyama Station on the JR San'yo Main Line. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.
