Takanawa, Tokyo

THE HUB Takanawa — a shared office in Takanawa where progress meets calm, on the 8th and 9th floors of the Grace Takanawa Building

THE HUB Takanawa is on the 8th and 9th floors of the Grace Takanawa Building, four minutes from exit A2 of Sengakuji Station on the Toei Asakusa Line and six from Takanawa Gateway Station. The website calls it “a shared office in Takanawa where progress and calm come together”. It has private offices for 2–5, a meeting room for 4, and private and semi-private booth seats. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Progress and calm

The website introduces it as “a shared office in Takanawa where progress and calm come together”.

Four minutes from Sengakuji, six from Takanawa Gateway and nine from Shinagawa, it sits between the growing new district of Takanawa Gateway and Takanawa's quiet residential streets.

01 · Reception terminal and waiting space

An entrance to welcome visitors

There is a reception terminal, and visitors can wait in the waiting space.

Free drinks, a copier, dedicated mailboxes and parcel lockers, and lockers are available.

02 · Meeting room and booth seats

A meeting room and seats to retreat to

There is a meeting room for four.

Booth seats come in private and semi-private types, for calls, online meetings or focused time.

03 · Private offices

For 2 to 5 people

Private offices are for 2 to 5 people (currently shown as full on the official site).

Coworking is available from ¥7,150 a month on the single-site plan and ¥2,200 on the 10-hour plan.

What is a semi-private booth?

A seat surrounded by partitions but not fully closed. THE HUB Takanawa has both private and semi-private booth seats.

You can focus without feeling watched, and it is easier to use than a private room.
