Marunouchi, Tokyo

NEXs Tokyo Community Space — a free members' base in Marunouchi connecting start-ups from across Japan and Tokyo

The NEXs Tokyo Community Space is on the 4th floor of the Shin-Tokyo Building in Marunouchi. It is a base where start-ups from regions across Japan and from Tokyo connect with players at home and abroad. There is LIVE PARK seating 80, Xs BAR for 8, WORK STUDIO for 20 and a RADIO STATION for broadcasting, all free for registered members. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Connecting Japan's regions and Tokyo, at home and abroad

The official website describes it as a base where start-ups from across Japan's regions and from Tokyo connect organically with players at home and abroad, offering fast-moving information sharing and a flexible work environment.

One floor takes on four roles: a place to share information, to work, to do business and to hold events.

01 · LIVE PARK

An event space seating 80

LIVE PARK seats 80 when used in full, or 40 in half.

When no event is on, it serves as a place to gather and share information.

02 · WORK STUDIO

A workspace for 20

WORK STUDIO seats 20, with free-address seats, Wi-Fi and power.

A multifunction printer is available (charged separately).

03 · Xs BAR

A business space for 8

Xs BAR seats 8 and suits business talks and small meetings.

There are also meeting rooms and workshop space.

04 · RADIO STATION

Broadcast on the spot

RADIO STATION is a broadcasting space for 2–4 people. For public broadcasts, around 20 people can gather around it.

Start-ups can share what they are doing with the outside world, right there.

What is a mentor?

Someone who supports another person's growth with advice drawn from their own experience and knowledge.

As well as start-up members, partner and mentor members can also use the NEXs Tokyo Community Space free of charge.
