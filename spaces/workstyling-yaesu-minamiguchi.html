Yaesu, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit — a shared office open at weekends, on the 2nd floor of Pacific Century Place Marunouchi

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit is on the 2nd floor of Pacific Century Place Marunouchi, near the Yaesu South exit of Tokyo Station. It is open from 8:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and weekends alike, with an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Open late, even at weekends

WORK STYLING Yaesu South Exit is open 8:00–22:00 on weekdays and weekends. The website highlights weekend and holiday opening as a feature of the site.

It is on the 2nd floor of Pacific Century Place Marunouchi, three minutes' walk from JR Tokyo Station and two from the Keiyo Line platforms. With many photos of its open space, it is a site built around shared seating.

01 · Entrance

The 2nd-floor entrance

Reception crew greet you at the 2nd-floor entrance.

It is just a short walk from the Yaesu South side of Tokyo Station.

02 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space can be used without booking.

There is a drinks corner, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

03 · Meeting rooms and private rooms

Rooms for up to 12, and rooms for one

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Private rooms can be used in 10-minute units, ideal for calls and online meetings.

What is pay-as-you-go?

Paying only for the time you use. At WORK STYLING this is the SHARE plan; at Yaesu South Exit it starts at ¥340 per 10 minutes.

No monthly contract — ideal when you only need a few hours on a business trip or at the weekend.
