Kyobashi, Tokyo

Business-Airport Kyobashi — a workplace expressing a district of makers in Japanese style, at FPG links KYOBASHI

Business-Airport Kyobashi, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 2nd and 4th–8th floors of FPG links KYOBASHI in Kyobashi 2-chome. Kyobashi was a “town of making” where Edo-period creators honed their craft side by side, and the website describes a space that expresses the charm of that craftsmen's district with a gentle Japanese touch. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Opening up a new era in Kyobashi, the town of making

The slogan is “Opening up a new era in Kyobashi, the town of making”. Kyobashi is where Edo-period creators competed and improved, and today it is a major business district.

The website describes a space that expresses the charm of that craftsmen's town in a gentle Japanese style — a workplace that stirs the creativity of business people and creators alike.

01 · Coworking

7:00–22:00 on weekdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00).

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

Private rooms from one person

Private rooms for one start at ¥165,000 a month; rooms for several people from ¥253,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Three meeting rooms

Room A seats eight at ¥3,300 an hour, room B four at ¥2,200 and room C six at ¥2,750.

The one-person online meeting room costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

What does “Japanese style” mean here?

A design atmosphere that draws on traditional Japanese materials, forms and colours. Business-Airport Kyobashi uses it gently to express the charm of a craftsmen's district.

Bringing Japanese elements into an office building softens its hard edges.
