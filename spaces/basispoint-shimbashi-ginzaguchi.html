Shimbashi, Tokyo

BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginzaguchi — a coworking space with large rooms for up to 56, one minute from the Ginza Exit of Shimbashi Station

BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginzaguchi is on the 2nd floor of the Kashikei Building, a one-minute walk from the Ginza Exit of JR Shimbashi Station. Along with an open space, sofa seats, private booths and BOX seats, it has three meeting rooms and two large “Lab.” rooms that each hold 56 people. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

From solo work to seminars

Shimbashi Ginzaguchi is another BasisPoint location built around “a refined space like a hotel lounge”. Fast Wi-Fi and power at every seat support remote work and web meetings.

What sets it apart is the range of room sizes: from a single-person booth to a combined meeting room for 25 and large rooms for 56, all in one place.

01 · Open space

A lounge one minute from the station

Open seats cost ¥330 for the first 30 minutes, with a daily cap of ¥3,300. Monthly plans range from ¥12,100 to ¥27,500.

There are also sofa seats for four.

02 · BOX seats and private booths

Seats for 1 to 4

BOX seats with monitors are for 1–4 people and cost ¥1,980 per hour. There are three private booths: A, B and C.

Seats for focusing alone and seats for talking in small groups are kept separate.

03 · Meeting rooms

From 6 to 25 people

There are three meeting rooms: Room.A (6), Room.B (10) and Room.C (12). B and C can be joined for 25.

From sales meetings to internal meetings, you can choose a room by headcount.

04 · Large rooms “Lab.”

Two rooms for 56

There are two large rooms, Lab.A and Lab.B, each holding 56 people.

They can host seminars, study sessions and other large events.

What is a combinable meeting room?

Two neighbouring meeting rooms whose partition can be opened so they work as one large room. At BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginzaguchi, Room.B and Room.C can be joined for 25 people.

They stay as small rooms day to day and become one big room only when needed.
