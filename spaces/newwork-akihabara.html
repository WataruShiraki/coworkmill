Akihabara, Tokyo

NewWork Akihabara — a 31-seat satellite office on the 10th floor of the Shin-Akihabara Building, one minute from the station, open weekends

NewWork Akihabara is on the 10th floor of the Shin-Akihabara Building, a one-minute walk from Akihabara Station. It has 31 booth seats, a meeting room for eight and nine enclosed booths for one, and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 10th floor by the station, open at weekends

NewWork Akihabara is on the 10th floor of the Shin-Akihabara Building, a minute from Akihabara Station. While many sites close on weekends and holidays, this one stays open.

Seating is mostly booths, with nine enclosed booths for one — a layout suited to people who want to focus on solo work.

01 · Booth seats

A 31-seat workspace

There are 31 booth seats.

A multifunction printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths are available.

02 · Enclosed seated booths

Nine booths for one

There are nine enclosed seated booths for one, used by booking.

Use them for online meetings and calls.

03 · Meeting room

One room for eight

One meeting room for eight is available by booking.

It works for weekend meetings too.

Which NewWork sites open at weekends?

Some NewWork sites open on weekdays only, others on weekends and holidays too.

NewWork Akihabara is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.
