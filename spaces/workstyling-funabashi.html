Funabashi, Chiba

WORK STYLING Funabashi — a 10th floor with yellow walls, directly linked to Keisei Funabashi Station

WORK STYLING Funabashi is on the 10th floor of the Funabashi Face Building, a one-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Funabashi Station and directly linked to Keisei Funabashi Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 10th floor linked to the Keisei station

It is one minute on foot from the South Exit of JR Funabashi Station and directly linked to Keisei Funabashi Station. The South Exit of Funabashi Station on the Tobu Noda Line is two minutes away.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see table seats enclosed by white curtains and seats at a long table with black chairs.

What is a curtain partition?

Dividing a space with curtains instead of walls. Opening or closing them changes how much room you have.

At WORK STYLING Funabashi, white curtains enclose the table seats.
