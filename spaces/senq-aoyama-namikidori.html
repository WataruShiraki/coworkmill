Aoyama, Tokyo

SENQ Aoyama Namiki-dori — a “BUILD NEXT CULTURES” workplace for pioneers, on the 3rd floor of the Daiichi Hoki headquarters

SENQ Aoyama Namiki-dori is on the 3rd floor of the Daiichi Hoki headquarters building, a four-minute walk from Aoyama-itchome and Gaiemmae stations. Its theme is “BUILD NEXT CULTURES” — a place where pioneers gather to create new values and cultures that will shape the next generation of innovation, the website says. It has a lounge, a café corner, 18 rooms, 20 solo booths and 3 meeting booths. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

BUILD NEXT CULTURES

SENQ Aoyama Namiki-dori's theme is “BUILD NEXT CULTURES” — a place where pioneers gather to create new values and cultures that influence the next generation of innovation, the website says.

While SENQ Aoyama nearby accepts room members only, Aoyama Namiki-dori also takes coworking members and drop-ins.

01 · Reception and lounge

The 3rd-floor entrance and shared space

From the 3rd-floor reception, the lounge opens up.

Coworking is ¥22,000 a month and drop-in ¥550 an hour, available 9:00–22:00 on weekdays.

02 · Rooms

18 rooms from 6.71 m² to 98.52 m²

There are 18 rooms ranging from 6.71 m² to 98.52 m², from ¥137,500 a month.

There is also a refresh area for tenants only.

03 · Solo booths and meeting booths

20 solo booths and 3 meeting booths

There are 20 solo booths from ¥82,500 a month; drop-in users can book one for ¥1,100 an hour.

Three meeting booths for 4 have wall-mounted monitors.

What does BUILD NEXT CULTURES mean?

“Build the next cultures” — SENQ Aoyama Namiki-dori takes these words as its theme.

In Aoyama, a source of trends and culture, it aims to be a place where people who create new values gather.
