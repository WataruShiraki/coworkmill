Yaesu, Tokyo

The Executive Centre TOFROM YAESU TOWER — 220 seats with Japanese design touches, on the 11th floor connected underground to Tokyo Station

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 11th floor of TOFROM YAESU TOWER, linked to Tokyo Station through the Yaesu underground mall. It has 220 workstations. The website describes an interior with Japanese design elements: a wall of washi-paper lighting, stone paving inspired by the Nakasendo highway, a large pine tree and a barista bar inspired by roof tiles. Introduction

An interior with Japanese design touches

The website lists the Japanese design elements in the interior: a wall of washi-paper lighting, stone paving inspired by the Nakasendo highway, a large pine tree and a barista bar inspired by roof tiles.

The building stands at the heart of Yaesu's redevelopment, connected underground to Tokyo Station and three minutes from Nihombashi Station. With 220 workstations, it is a large centre.

01 · Barista bar and lounge

A counter inspired by roof tiles

The barista bar, inspired by traditional roof tiles, is the face of the centre.

The members' lounge looks out over Yaesu.

02 · Coworking, private offices and meeting rooms

Seats to work and rooms for teams

There are coworking seats and a library, with height-adjustable desks.

Private offices start from one person, and meeting rooms are filled with natural light.

What is the Nakasendo?

An Edo-period highway that ran inland from Nihonbashi in Edo to Sanjo Ohashi bridge in Kyoto. Its starting point, Nihonbashi, is right next to Yaesu.

According to the website, the centre has stone paving inspired by the Nakasendo, bringing the area's history into the interior.
