Roppongi, Tokyo

H¹T Roppongi — a corporate shared office on the 7th floor of the Seishido Building, one minute from the station

Nomura Real Estate's H¹T Roppongi is on the 7th floor of the Seishido Building, a one-minute walk from exit 4a of Roppongi Station (Tokyo Metro Hibiya and Toei Oedo lines). H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fees. This site stands out for its many kinds of solo seating — open space, booths, boxes and single-person rooms — and is open 7:30–22:00 on weekends and holidays too. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Four ways to sit alone

H¹T Roppongi has 10 open-space seats, 4 single booths, 4 single boxes and 10 single rooms — four kinds of solo seating, from open seats to rooms with doors.

H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base between sales visits, and for seminars, training and interviews, the website says. The Roppongi site also opens on weekends and holidays.

01 · Open space

Ten shared seats

The open space seats 10.

Use it in the gaps between appointments to check email or tidy up documents.

02 · Booths and boxes

Semi-private seats for one

There are four single booths and four single boxes.

Seats for when you want to block other people's view a little.

03 · Rooms and meeting rooms

Ten private rooms and two meeting rooms

There are ten single rooms, with doors, for online meetings.

Two meeting rooms seat 6 and 8.

What is an H¹T booth?

One of H¹T's seat types: a single seat separated by partitions.

H¹T Roppongi offers solo seats in steps — booths, boxes and rooms.
