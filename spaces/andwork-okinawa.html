Naha, Okinawa

.andwork Naha — a lounge with power at every seat and a green terrace at ESTINATE HOTEL Naha

A five-minute walk from Miebashi Station on the Yui Rail monorail, .andwork Naha is inside ESTINATE HOTEL Naha in Matsuyama. The lounge is one with the hotel restaurant, and every seat — from booth sofas to tables and armchairs — has its own power outlet. It is open from 7:00 to 22:00 and costs ¥500 an hour or ¥2,000 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace for living the travel-and-work lifestyle in Okinawa

Step in from the bustle of Naha into a refined space with an Okinawan resort feel.

Every seat has its own power outlet. Complimentary drinks include an original coffee blend from a local roaster and a blended tea made with Okinawan leaves.

01 · Lounge

A workspace that is one with the restaurant

Because the lounge is part of the restaurant, breakfast, lunch and dinner are right there — no need to move.

Every seat has power for long sessions. The hourly plan includes one free drink and the day plan three.

02 · Terrace

A leafy outdoor terrace

The lounge opens onto a green outdoor terrace — a relaxed, open-air place to work.

03 · Bar counter

Evening happy hour

At the evening happy hour, draught beer is free.

04 · The building

A hotel on Route 58

The hotel is on Route 58, 10 minutes by car from Naha Airport and a 5-minute walk from Miebashi Station.

There is no hotel car park; use paid car parks nearby.

What is a workation?

A blend of “work” and “vacation”: working while you enjoy time away.

ESTINATE HOTEL Naha says it regularly runs one-week workation tours where participants work and get to know each other.
