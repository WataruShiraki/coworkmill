Toranomon, Tokyo

BIRTH WORK Toranomon — a wood-toned second office divided into study-like spaces, in Nishi-Shimbashi 1-chome

BIRTH WORK Toranomon is in Nishi-Shimbashi 1-chome. Billed as “a second office just like a study at home”, it divides its floors into a focus zone on the 2nd floor and a meeting and relaxation zone on the 3rd. The 2nd floor is a working office in warm wood, split into small sections to create a space where you can focus for four hours straight. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A second office, just like a study at home

BIRTH WORK Toranomon calls itself “a second office just like a study at home” — a second workplace, neither company office nor home, for calm thinking.

The 2nd floor is for focus and the 3rd for meetings and relaxing. You move between the two floors, each with its own role, by the stairs.

01 · 2F focus zone

A wood-toned office divided into small sections

The 2nd floor is “a working office in warm wood, divided into small sections to create a space where you can concentrate for four hours”.

Dividing a large room into small ones lets each person work as if shut away in their own study.

02 · 3F meeting and relaxation zone

A private meeting room for up to six

The 3rd floor has a private meeting room for up to six people and a space for face-to-face discussions.

After focused work, you move upstairs to talk or rest.

03 · Plans

A virtual plan; free working currently closed to new members

The virtual plan costs ¥5,500 a month (sign-up fee ¥16,500). The official website says free working is not currently accepting new members.

It can be entered 24 hours a day and is open every day (except for building inspections).

What is a second office?

A second place to work, separate from the company office and from home. BIRTH WORK Toranomon offers one “like a study at home”.

Finding the quiet that neither office nor home can give, in a second place — the word “study” captures its character well.
