Tanashi, Tokyo

Tanashi Coworking Space OK Nishitokyo — a start-up support hub three minutes from the South Exit of Tanashi Station

Tanashi Coworking Space OK Nishitokyo is in an area lined with the library and city offices, a three-minute walk from the South Exit of Tanashi Station on the Seibu Shinjuku Line. A facility supporting women's first steps into starting a business, it combines coworking, a virtual office and a tourist information point for Nishitokyo City. Besides the open space, there are shared desks and two meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A neighbourhood coworking space supporting women's first steps in business

OK Nishitokyo is presented as a facility supporting women's first steps in starting a business. It has three roles: coworking, a virtual office and a tourist information point for Nishitokyo City.

The entrance has a smartphone-operated auto-lock, and the lights and air conditioning can be controlled remotely. Welcome drinks such as coffee, tea and mineral water are provided.

01 · Entrance

Three minutes from the South Exit of Tanashi Station

It is in room 201 of South Town, near Nishitokyo City's Tanashi office, in an area lined with the library and city offices.

02 · Shared desks

Shared desks by the month

Shared desks are monthly plans at ¥14,800 for one seat and ¥19,800 for two (excl. tax), with a minimum of three months.

Plans combined with address use come with a locker with a dedicated mailbox.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two meeting rooms for different uses

Meeting room B suits workshops, business meetings and web meetings. Meeting room A can be used for treatments, yoga, personal training and web meetings.

Both can be rented from one hour, at ¥1,000 per hour on weekdays and ¥1,300 on weekends and holidays.

What is a virtual office?

A service that lets you use a business address and mail handling without renting an actual desk.

At OK Nishitokyo, you can use the facility's address on business cards and post for ¥9,800 a month (excl. tax).
