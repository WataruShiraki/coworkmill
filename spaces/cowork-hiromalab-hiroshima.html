Hiroshima, Hiroshima

Hiromalab (Hiroshima) — an open space on the 17th floor of a building, one minute from Kanayamacho Station

Hiromalab is on the 17th floor of Hiroshima High Building 21 in Kanayamacho, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, a one-minute walk from Kanayamacho Station on the Hiroden tram. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An open space on a 17th floor

It is on the 17th floor of Hiroshima High Building 21, one minute on foot from Kanayamacho Station on the Hiroden tram.

The open space needs no booking — just use any free seat. There is Wi-Fi, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

Open space

What is an open space?

A shared workspace without partitions.

At Hiromalab's open space, you can use any free seat without booking.
