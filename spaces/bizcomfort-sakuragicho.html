Sakuragicho, Yokohama

BIZcomfort Sakuragicho — 24-hour coworking near Minato Mirai, Yokohama

Six minutes' walk from Takashimacho Station on the Yokohama Blue Line and ten minutes from JR Sakuragicho Station, BIZcomfort Sakuragicho is on the 8th floor of NEXGATE Yokohama Sakuragicho in Nishi Ward. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day. Everything below is based on the official website.

5 photos · © BIZcomfort (BIZcomfort Sakuragicho) · Tap any photo to view it full size

Seats18 free-seating desks, 23 private offices

Introduction

Open 24 hours, built for focus

BIZcomfort says the coworking area for solo work has partitioned desks, suited to focused work and remote work.

Drinks, Wi-Fi and printers are free, and every seat has a power outlet. There is a 6-person meeting room and a booth for online meetings (paid, booking required).

01 · Free seating

Partitioned desks

There are 18 free-seating desks.

Every seat has a power outlet.

02 · Meeting room

A 6-person meeting room

The meeting room and online-meeting room are paid and must be booked.

03 · Private offices

Rental offices

What is the weekends & holidays plan?

A monthly plan for using one location's coworking space on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays only.

At BIZcomfort Sakuragicho it costs ¥5,500 a month.
