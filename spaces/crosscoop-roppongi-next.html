Roppongi, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Roppongi NEXT — two floors of about 1,620 m² and a 60-seat coworking lounge, on the 4th and 5th floors of Round Cross Roppongi

CROSSCOOP Roppongi NEXT is on the 4th and 5th floors of Round Cross Roppongi, a two-minute walk from exit 7 of Roppongi Station. Two floors total about 490 tsubo (about 1,620 m²). It has a coworking lounge of about 60 seats with high counters, a refresh lounge, four meeting rooms, phone booths and windowed private rooms for 1 up to 40 people. Introduction

Two floors, about 1,620 m²

CROSSCOOP Roppongi NEXT uses two floors of Round Cross Roppongi, the 4th and 5th, about 490 tsubo in all.

The website highlights the location two minutes from Roppongi Station, a design for small teams and companies of 1 to 20, rich shared facilities, and security for meeting rooms, lounges and offices.

01 · Reception

The front desk

Staff are at reception 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.

It is a two-minute walk from exit 7 of Roppongi Station.

02 · Coworking lounge

About 60 seats, with high counters

The coworking lounge has about 60 seats, including high-counter seats.

There is also a refresh lounge, so places to work and rest are kept separate.

03 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Four meeting rooms and booths for calls

There are four meeting rooms: one for 4, two for 6 and one for 8.

Phone booths are available for online meetings.

04 · Private rooms

Fully private rooms with windows

Private rooms range from 1 to 25 people, with a unit for up to 40. All are fully enclosed, with windows, desks and chairs.

Company registration is possible.

What is a high counter?

Counter seating at standing height, used standing up or on tall stools. The coworking lounge at CROSSCOOP Roppongi NEXT has it.

With a higher eye line, it suits short tasks and quick chats.
