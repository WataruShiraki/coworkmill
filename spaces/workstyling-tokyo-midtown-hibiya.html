Hibiya, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya — a “ZEN MODERN” shared office with traditional patterns, on the 12th floor of Hibiya Mitsui Tower

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya is on the 12th floor of Hibiya Mitsui Tower. Its design concept is “ZEN MODERN”: a space decorated with traditional Japanese patterns, with an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12, single-person private rooms and rental offices. Introduction

ZEN MODERN

The design concept of WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya is “ZEN MODERN” — a space decorated with traditional Japanese patterns, according to the website.

WORK STYLING is Mitsui Fudosan's office service. The Hibiya site is on the 12th floor of Hibiya Mitsui Tower at Tokyo Midtown Hibiya; SHARE (shared office) is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays, and FLEX members can enter 24 hours.

01 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people, and each is a different size so you can choose by the number of attendees.

In the heart of Hibiya, they are handy for meetings with visitors.

02 · Single-person rooms and rental offices

Rooms to shut the door and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls, online meetings and focused work.

There are also rental offices you can take as a team base.

What is ZEN MODERN?

The design concept of WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya. Traditional Japanese patterns overlay the calm of Zen on today's way of working.

In Hibiya, a district with many visitors from overseas, it is a choice to convey Japaneseness through the interior.
