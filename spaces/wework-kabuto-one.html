Nihonbashi Kabutocho, Tokyo

WeWork KABUTO ONE — a site at the heart of Kabutocho's redevelopment, directly connected to Kayabacho Station

WeWork KABUTO ONE is on the 9th and 10th floors of KABUTO ONE, at the centre of Nihonbashi Kabutocho, which is being redeveloped as a National Strategic Special Zone project for the Tokyo area. It is directly connected to Kayabacho Station on the subway, and an underground passage leads to Nihombashi Station. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Fit for Kabutocho, the international financial district

With meeting rooms of all sizes and phone booths, the site was designed for practical office use and the functionality befitting Kabutocho as an international financial district, the website says.

The building also has a hall, library and lounge where local companies can mix — a site where you feel both the energy of the redevelopment and the energy of the WeWork community.

01 · 9th-floor entrance

Reception and community bar

You enter the site on the 9th floor.

Straight ahead is the community bar, which doubles as reception and a place to mingle.

02 · Lounges

By the windows on the 9th and 10th floors

Both the 9th and 10th floors have window-side lounges.

Ceilings are 2,800 mm high.

03 · Pantry and meeting rooms

15 meeting rooms

The 9th floor has a pantry.

There are 15 meeting rooms, and the website says it can accommodate private offices and custom requests.

What is a National Strategic Special Zone?

A system in which the national government designates areas and eases regulations to boost economic growth and international competitiveness.

The Tokyo area is one, and the redevelopment of Kabutocho is proceeding as one of its projects.
