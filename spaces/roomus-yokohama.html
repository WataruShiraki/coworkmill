Yokohama, Kanagawa

RoomUs Yokohama — Keikyu's work and study rooms, two minutes from the East Exit of Yokohama Station

RoomUs Yokohama, a coworking space run by Keikyu Corporation, is on the 7th floor of a building in Takashima 2-chome, a two-minute walk from the East Exit of Yokohama Station. It opened on 6 January 2025. There are four kinds of seat — private seats, semi-private seats, a café area and soundproof booths — and it is open 7:00–23:00 every day of the year. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A safe, comfortable third place close to home

RoomUs is Keikyu Corporation's coworking space. Under the idea of “a safe, comfortable third place close to home”, it offers quality work and study rooms at affordable prices.

It is unstaffed, and you enter with a members-only smart lock. Advance member registration is required.

01 · Private seats

Private seats for one

According to Keikyu's news release, there are eight private seats.

02 · Semi-private seats

Twelve semi-private seats

There are 12 semi-private seats. Together with the private seats, the layout centres on seats for working alone.

03 · Café area and soundproof booths

Somewhere to rest, somewhere to talk

The café area has three seats. There are two soundproof booths, bookable at ¥100 per 20 minutes.

04 · Prices

Monthly plans and pay-by-time

Full-time membership is ¥18,500 a month. There are morning, day and night plans for both weekdays and holidays.

Pay-by-time use is ¥100 per 20 minutes, with a minimum charge of ¥300 a month. Lockers are ¥1,000 a month.

What is a smart lock?

A system that locks and unlocks a door using a smartphone or member credentials.

RoomUs is unstaffed and keeps entry and exit secure with a members-only smart lock.
