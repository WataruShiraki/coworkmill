Roppongi, Tokyo

SENQ Roppongi — a co-creation space breaking conventions through design and technology, on floors 4–9 of the Shin-Roppongi Building

SENQ Roppongi occupies the 4th to 9th floors of the Shin-Roppongi Building. Its theme is “CHANGE THE THEORY”: a place of co-creation that breaks with conventional thinking through design and technology. The 9th floor has about 70 coworking seats, a café corner, a sky terrace and solo desks. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

CHANGE THE THEORY

SENQ Roppongi's theme is “CHANGE THE THEORY” — a place of co-creation that breaks conventions through the power of design and technology, the website says.

It uses the 4th to 9th floors of the Shin-Roppongi Building, with reception on the 9th. There are 38 private rooms on floors 4–8, and the coworking area and terrace are on the 9th.

01 · 9F coworking and café corner

A workspace of about 70 seats

The 9th-floor coworking area has about 70 seats, with reception, a café corner and solo desks on the same floor.

Coworking is ¥22,000 a month, available 9:00–22:00 on weekdays.

02 · Sky terrace

A terrace that changes between day and night

The 9th floor has a sky terrace: bright outdoor seats by day, a place to look down on the city lights by night.

You can step straight from the indoor coworking area into the open air.

03 · Private rooms and meeting rooms

38 rooms on floors 4–8

There are 2 private rooms on the 4th floor, 4 on the 5th–6th and 32 on the 7th–8th, from ¥169,400 a month, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

The 9th floor has three meeting rooms.

What is a sky terrace?

An outdoor terrace on an upper floor of a building. SENQ Roppongi has one on its 9th floor.

Having a breezy outdoor space just outside where you work makes it easier to switch moods.
