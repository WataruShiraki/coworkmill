Otemachi, Tokyo

LIFORK Otemachi — a refined 24-hour shared office on the first two floors of Otemachi First Square, connected to the station

LIFORK Otemachi, run by NTT Urban Development, is on the first and second floors of Otemachi First Square West Tower, directly connected to Otemachi Station. It has 13 types of shared office for 4 to 30 people, Style Rooms rented by the hour and a 199.1 m² rental lounge; the shared offices are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. The official website describes “an open space with refined, calm interiors”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A place to work for a better life

LIFORK is NTT Urban Development's brand of shared offices, rental spaces and coworking, under the banner “a place to work for a better life”. It has sites in Akihabara, Harajuku, Minami-Aoyama and Hisaya-odori in Nagoya; Otemachi is the one designed most for focused work.

The building, Otemachi First Square West Tower, connects directly to Otemachi Station (Tokyo Metro Tozai, Marunouchi, Chiyoda and Hanzomon lines, and Toei Mita line) through exits C8, C11 and C12. It is also a five-minute walk from the Marunouchi North Exit of JR Tokyo Station.

01 · Shared facilities

Lockers, smart locks and drinks

Members get lockers, smart-lock entry, self-service drinks, a pantry with a microwave, a multifunction printer and a business-card scanner.

With no physical key to carry, members can come and go on their own late at night and on holidays.

02 · Rental lounge

199.1 m², 50 seated or 60 standing

The rental lounge is 199.1 m² and holds up to 50 people seated or 60 standing. It has a 130-inch screen, a 65-inch monitor, wireless microphones and a projector.

It costs ¥44,000 per 60 minutes (¥40,000 before tax), with a 60-minute minimum and 15-minute increments after that. Hours are 9:00–21:00; other times on request. It is used for seminars, mixers, award ceremonies, photo shoots and screenings.

03 · Style rooms

Ten rooms rented in 30-minute blocks

There are ten Style Rooms, B to L. Rooms for 4 cost ¥1,650 per 30 minutes, rooms for 8 cost ¥2,200 (¥1,925 for room C), and room G for 16 costs ¥4,400.

They are available 9:00–21:00, with a 30-minute minimum and 15-minute increments after that. Non-members can rent them by the room.

What is a smart lock?

An electronic lock opened with a smartphone or card instead of a physical key. LIFORK Otemachi uses one for member entry.

For a space open around the clock, managing who comes and goes when no one is at reception is the key question. A system that needs no keys handed out greatly reduces the work of running it.
