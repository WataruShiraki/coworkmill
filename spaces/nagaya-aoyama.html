Aoyama, Tokyo

NAGAYA Aoyama — a shared office with a 70 m² wooden terrace and 29 private rooms on the 2nd floor of Grancasa Minami-Aoyama

NAGAYA Aoyama is on the 2nd floor of Grancasa Minami-Aoyama, an eight-minute walk from Omotesando Station. Its concept is “a quiet working environment, achievable even in the city centre”. It has an 18-seat open space, 29 private rooms, four meeting rooms and an outdoor wooden terrace of about 70 m². Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Quiet in the city centre

NAGAYA Aoyama's concept is “a quiet working environment, achievable even in the city centre”. It is in Minami-Aoyama, eight minutes on foot from Omotesando Station.

Twenty-nine private rooms, an 18-seat open space, four meeting rooms and an outdoor wooden terrace are all gathered on the same 2nd floor.

01 · Reception and open space

A shared area with 18 seats

Past reception is the 18-seat open space.

The open-space “Full-time A” membership is ¥19,800 a month.

02 · Private rooms

29 private rooms

There are 29 private rooms; dedicated private-room memberships range from ¥55,000 to ¥154,000 a month.

There are also private booths for working quietly alone.

03 · Wooden terrace

About 70 m² outdoors

There is an outdoor wooden terrace of about 70 m².

It is a place to eat lunch outside or work somewhere different for a change.

04 · Meeting rooms and phone room

Four rooms for meetings

There are four meeting rooms, including ones for 6 and 4.

A phone room lets members without a private room take calls too.

What is a nagaya?

A traditional Japanese row house: several homes lined up side by side under one roof. Residents lived on either side of shared walls and met in the alleys and around the well.

NAGAYA Aoyama is likewise made up of rows of private rooms and shared areas. The origin of the name is being confirmed with the operator.
