Jiyugaoka, Tokyo

tefu jiyugaoka — a Jiyugaoka base with a café, offices, a studio and a lounge dotted with vintage furniture

tefu jiyugaoka is a two-minute walk from the South Exit of Jiyugaoka Station on the Tokyu Toyoko and Oimachi lines. It is made up of a café, lounge, offices and a studio, and the official website invites you to enjoy “rich time where work and life blend together through a stay in the lounge”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Time where work and life blend

The official website describes the lounge as a place to “find your favourite seat and your own way of spending time in a spacious room dotted with vintage furniture”.

The site is split into three buildings: Building C has reception and the café, Building A is for lounge users, and Building B for office users.

01 · Café and lounge

A spacious room of vintage furniture

The café and lounge are open 9:00–21:00 (café last order 20:30, last lounge entry 20:00).

Every plan includes free café coffee, with Wi-Fi and power.

02 · Meeting rooms and meeting booths

Places for meetings

Meeting rooms hold up to 6.

There are also meeting booths that handle online meetings.

03 · Offices

Desks and booths

Offices are available 24 hours. Up to 3 people can be registered for a desk and up to 4 for a booth.

The official website says that having your own special, private place within the lounge lets you enjoy the community more deeply.

What is vintage furniture?

Furniture from earlier eras that has gained character over many years. tefu runs a vintage-furniture sharing business.

The lounge at tefu jiyugaoka is dotted with vintage pieces, so each seat offers a different kind of comfort.
