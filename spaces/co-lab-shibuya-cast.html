Shibuya, Tokyo

co-lab Shibuya Cast — a 24-hour shared office for makers on the first two floors of Shibuya Cast

co-lab Shibuya Cast occupies the first and second floors of Shibuya Cast at Shibuya 1-23-21. co-lab is a “shared office for creators that sparks collaboration”, run by Harumaki Project since 2003. Here there are 16 booths for 1–2 people, 7 studios for 3–5, 8 ateliers for 4–12 and 4 rooms for 12–22, all open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Share of the Creative Life — when makers gather, the city changes

co-lab's motto is “when makers gather, the city changes”. It rests on three pillars: shared studios, a creative think tank it calls a “Do Tank”, and facilities that support making. It describes itself as a place that solves problems with collective intelligence gathered across organisations.

Shibuya Cast is a 17-storey building designed around the idea of “Echoes of Uniqueness”. co-lab Shibuya Cast, on its first two floors, is a creative platform that embodies “Share of the Creative Life” and supports the growth of the creative industries.

01 · 2F kitchen and shared areas

Window-side lounge and a kitchen

The second floor has a shared area along the windows and a kitchen — a place to work or rest outside your room.

Mail handling, high-speed internet, lockers and bicycle parking are available.

02 · 1F meeting rooms and workshop

Four meeting rooms and a place to make things

The ground floor has four meeting rooms, including the MR, which also works for workshops. There is also co-factory, equipment for making things, and an event space.

The eight ateliers for 4–12 people start at ¥160,000 a month, and the four rooms for 12–22 people at ¥380,000 a month (excluding tax).

03 · The building

The lower floors of Shibuya Cast

Shibuya Cast is a 17-storey building at Shibuya 1-23-21, close to Shibuya Station.

Next door is the creative café “Are”, so the inside and outside of the building flow loosely together.

What is a Do Tank?

co-lab's word for a “creative think tank” that values actually making and running things, as opposed to a think tank that only thinks.

Bring outside problems into a shared office full of creators and solve them there. It does more than rent space — it turns the people gathered into work.
