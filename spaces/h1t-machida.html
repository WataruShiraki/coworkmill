Machida, Tokyo

H¹T Machida — a shared office with single booths and private rooms, on the 6th floor of Machida Modi

H¹T Machida is on the 6th floor of Machida Modi, a one- to two-minute walk from Machida Station on the JR Yokohama and Odakyu lines. With six single booths, six single rooms and one single box, it is a base for people who want to focus on solo work. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Booths and rooms for focused solo work

Every seat at H¹T Machida is for one person: six booths, six rooms and one box. It is a place to concentrate on your own work.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.

01 · Booths

Six single booths

There are six single booths.

Partitioned seats let you work without minding the people around you.

02 · Rooms

Six private rooms with doors

There are six single rooms.

Close the door and use them for online meetings too.

03 · Entrance

Open from 8:00

It is open every day from 8:00 to 20:00.

Between 8:00 and 10:30, before Machida Modi opens, use the ground-floor entrance on the east side of the building.

What is a booth?

A single seat separated by partitions.

H¹T Machida has six single booths.
