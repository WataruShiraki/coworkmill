Aoto, Tokyo

NewWork Aoto — a small 13-seat corporate-membership satellite office on the 4th floor of the Asahi Life Katsushika Building

NewWork Aoto is on the 4th floor of the Asahi Life Katsushika Building, a five-minute walk from Aoto Station. It is a small site with 13 open and booth seats, a meeting room for 6 and three single-person seated booths. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A small workplace close to where people live

NewWork Aoto is a small site with 13 seats. It is made up of open and booth seats, one meeting room for 6 and three single-person seated booths.

The official website explains, with a diagram, that the way you enter and leave the building changes after 18:00. It is worth checking before using it in the evening.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 13-seat workspace

There are 13 seats of two kinds: open and booth.

The printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Seated private booths

Three single-person booths

Three single-person seated booths are available by booking.

To keep using one beyond 120 minutes, you make a new booking.

03 · Meeting room

One room for 6

There is one meeting room for 6, booked in advance.

After 18:00, follow the official website's instructions for entering and leaving the building.

What is drop-in?

Using a space for a single day and paying just for that, without becoming a member.

Because NewWork works on corporate membership, the official website states that drop-in use is not available.
