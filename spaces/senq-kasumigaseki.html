Kasumigaseki, Tokyo

SENQ Kasumigaseki — a “LEAD JAPAN” workplace with a sunken kotatsu, on the 2nd floor of the Nittochi Building

SENQ Kasumigaseki is on the 2nd floor of the Nittochi Building, a one-minute walk from Toranomon Station and three minutes from Kasumigaseki Station. Its theme is “LEAD JAPAN”. It has a lounge for 80, a salon for 40, a dining room, sunken kotatsu seating, 18 rooms and 17 booths. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

LEAD JAPAN

SENQ Kasumigaseki's theme is “LEAD JAPAN”. A one-minute walk from Toranomon Station and three from Kasumigaseki Station, it is easy to reach from government offices, the website says.

True to its name, it has many places for conversation: a lounge, a salon, a dining room and even sunken kotatsu seating.

01 · Lounge and salon

A lounge for 80 and a salon for 40

The lounge holds 80 people and the salon 40.

Coworking is ¥22,000 a month and drop-in ¥550 an hour, available 9:00–22:00 on weekdays.

02 · Dining and sunken kotatsu

A place to eat, and a place to sit and talk

There is a dining room and sunken kotatsu seating.

A sunken kotatsu is rare inside an office building. Take off your shoes and sit down, and the conversation changes from what happens in a meeting room.

03 · Rooms and booths

18 rooms and 17 booths

There are 18 rooms from 10.60 m² to 39.60 m², from ¥290,400 a month, and 17 booths from ¥96,800 a month.

Meeting rooms are also available.

What is a sunken kotatsu?

A traditional Japanese seating arrangement where the floor is dug out under a low table so you can sit with your legs hanging down. SENQ Kasumigaseki has one.

Neither a chair nor sitting on the floor, it is a relaxed posture for conversation.
