Yaesu, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit — a shared office on the 17th floor of GranTokyo North Tower, directly connected to Tokyo Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit is on the 17th floor of GranTokyo North Tower, on the Yaesu North side of Tokyo Station. Directly connected to Tokyo Station (JR lines and the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line), it has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms. Introduction

Seventeen floors above Tokyo Station

WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit is on the 17th floor of GranTokyo North Tower, directly connected to Tokyo Station. From the ticket gates to a high-floor seat without going outside.

It is a seven-minute walk from Nihombashi Station (Ginza and Tozai lines) and ten from Kyobashi Station (Ginza Line). The layout is focused: meeting rooms, single-person rooms and an open space you can use without booking.

01 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space can be used without booking.

There is a drinks corner, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Welcome visitors arriving at Tokyo Station in a building connected to the station.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for calls and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls and online meetings.

Separate phone booths are also available.

What does “directly connected to the station” mean?

You can enter the building from the station's gates or underground passages without going outside.

WORK STYLING Yaesu North Exit is directly connected to Tokyo Station on the JR lines and the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line — a big plus on rainy days and on business trips with luggage.
