Azabu-Juban, Tokyo

.andwork Azabu-Juban — a 2nd-floor lounge and a rooftop bar at THE LIVELY Tokyo Azabujuban

.andwork Azabu-Juban is inside THE LIVELY Tokyo Azabujuban hotel in Azabu-Juban 1-chome. Work in the 2nd-floor lounge and end the day at the bar on the top floor: this hotel-hybrid coworking space calls itself “a grown-up hideaway office for both work and play”. It is open from 8:00 to 22:00. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A grown-up hideaway office for both work and play

.andwork Azabu-Juban calls itself “a grown-up hideaway office for both work and play”. Work in the 2nd-floor lounge of THE LIVELY Tokyo Azabujuban and relax at the top-floor bar — moving between work and play inside the same hotel.

The .andwork brand aims for workplaces that boost creativity through striking interiors, good music and encounters with guests from around the world. The Azabu-Juban site carries the calm, grown-up air of its neighbourhood.

01 · 2F lounge

Work in a hotel lounge

The 2nd-floor lounge is the workspace, shared by hotel guests and people working in Azabu-Juban.

It costs ¥800 an hour or ¥2,500 a day (no sign-up fee). A 10-visit ticket is ¥17,500.

02 · Top-floor bar

A place to unwind after work

The hotel's top floor has a bar — a place to spend the evening after a day's work.

Having a place to work and a place to play in the same hotel is what the word “hideaway” means here.

03 · Plans

Unlimited for ¥34,000 a month

The monthly Unlimited plan costs ¥34,000 a month (sign-up fee ¥10,000).

Open from 8:00 to 22:00, it gives you a work base in the heart of Azabu-Juban.

What is a hideaway office?

The phrase .andwork Azabu-Juban uses for itself: a place where you can move calmly between work and play, away from prying eyes.

Being tucked inside a hotel, hard to see from the street, is what creates the hideaway feeling.
