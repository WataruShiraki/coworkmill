PORTAL POINT SHIBUYA — a creative office in Jinnan with 24-hour free desks on the 8th floor and a rooftop terrace

PORTAL POINT SHIBUYA is in Jinnan 1-chome, a three-minute walk from Exit B1 of Shibuya Station. A creative office run by Real Gate, it has free desks on the 8th floor, small offices on the 3rd and 5th floors, and whole-floor offices on the 2nd, 4th, 6th and 7th floors. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A place for self-realisation that treats the city as a playground

The official website says a new creative space has been born “in this city, where diverse values mix regardless of generation or culture, to stir the urge to create”.

The idea is a creative office where people gather as a “place for self-realisation”, treating ever-changing Shibuya as their playground while trying to reinvent themselves.

01 · 8F free desks

A workspace open 24 hours

The free desks are on the 8th floor and, according to the official website, available 24 hours.

The building also has a shared lounge and phone booths for online meetings.

02 · Meeting rooms

Hourly meeting rooms

Hourly meeting rooms are available for meetings.

Even on days with visitors, you can wrap things up inside the building.

03 · Offices and terrace

From small offices to whole floors

Floors 3 and 5 have small offices from 9.83 m², and floors 2, 4, 6 and 7 have whole-floor offices of 158.63–218.27 m².

On the roof, a rooftop terrace lets you reset above the streets of Jinnan.

What is Jinnan?

A district of Shibuya City spreading north of Shibuya Station around Koen-dori.

PORTAL POINT SHIBUYA is in Jinnan 1-chome; the official website gives it as three minutes on foot from Exit B1 of Shibuya Station.
