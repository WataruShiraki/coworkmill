Ikebukuro, Tokyo

H¹T Ikebukuro Nishiguchi — a corporate shared office on the 8th floor of Esola Ikebukuro, all but linked to the station

H¹T Ikebukuro Nishiguchi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 8th floor of Esola Ikebukuro, 15 seconds from the Central Passage West Exit of Ikebukuro Station on the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line and one minute from the South Exit of JR Ikebukuro Station. It has 16 single-person seats across boxes, booths and rooms, plus a meeting room for 10, and is open 8:00–22:00 on weekends and holidays too. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Inside a shopping building, 15 seconds from the station exit

H¹T Ikebukuro Nishiguchi is on the 8th floor of Esola Ikebukuro, a shopping building on the west side of Ikebukuro Station. The official website gives it as 15 seconds on foot from the Central Passage West Exit of the Marunouchi Line station.

There are three kinds of single seat: four boxes, three booths and nine rooms. A meeting room for 10 also works for training and meetings.

01 · Open space

Six shared seats

The open space seats 6.

Right by the station, you can drop in even between train changes.

02 · Boxes and booths

Seven single-person seats

There are four single-person boxes and three single-person booths.

These screened seats suit short tasks.

03 · Rooms and meeting room

Nine private rooms and a room for 10

There are nine single-person rooms and one meeting room for 10.

From solo work to a slightly larger meeting, everything can be done on one floor.
