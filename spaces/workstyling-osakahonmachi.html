Honmachi, Osaka

WORK STYLING Osaka Honmachi — a black-and-red interior and a room for seminars, one minute from Honmachi Station

WORK STYLING Osaka Honmachi is in the Midosuji Mitsui Building, a one-minute walk from Honmachi Station on the Midosuji and Chuo lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

One minute from Honmachi Station

It is one minute on foot from Honmachi Station on the Midosuji and Chuo lines.

The interior pairs black walls with accents of red.

01 · Corridor

Black walls and yellow benches

Yellow benches and plants line a corridor with black walls.

At the end of the corridor, a large room comes into view.

02 · Open space

Seats with black partitions

Under a red-framed ceiling are seats divided by black panels.

The open space can be used without booking.

03 · Meeting rooms

A white meeting room

Meeting rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with white walls.

04 · Large room

A big room lined with desks

There is also a large room set out with desks and chairs.

The layout suits seminar-style use by larger groups.

What is an accent colour?

Adding a small amount of a bold colour to a calm palette.

At WORK STYLING Osaka Honmachi, red is set against black walls.
