Azabu-Juban, Tokyo

BIRTH WORK Azabu-Juban — a shared office one minute from the station, with a 5th floor for focus and 8th–9th floors to relax

BIRTH WORK Azabu-Juban is in the Azabu-Juban Takagi Building, a one-minute walk from Azabu-Juban Station. The 5th floor is for focus and the 8th and 9th floors are for relaxing, and it is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. On the ground and basement floors of the same building is BIRTH LAB, known for its wall of timber shelves. It is run by Takagi Building Co., Ltd. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Where the seeds of an idea sprout and grow

BIRTH WORK describes itself as “the place where the seeds of an idea sprout and begin to grow”. BIRTH is a brand of Takagi Building, with sites in Azabu-Juban, Kanda and Toranomon, a community space called BIRTH LAB and a membership called BIRTHERs.

In Azabu-Juban, each floor has a role: the 5th is for focus, the 8th and 9th for relaxing. BIRTH LAB, a community space open to the neighbourhood, sits on the lower floors — stacking a place to work and a place to gather in the same building.

01 · 5F focus floor

A floor for heads-down work

The 5th floor is for focus, and the private desks are here too.

Non-members can drop in for ¥660 an hour or ¥2,420 for a day pass (both 10:00–18:00).

02 · 8F–9F relax floors

Floors for working at an easier pace

The 8th and 9th floors are for relaxing, and private offices are on these two floors.

BIRTHERs monthly membership is ¥38,500 a month (sign-up fee ¥16,500), with access 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

03 · 1F BIRTH LAB

A wall of timber shelves, one space from B1 to 2F

The website describes BIRTH LAB as “featuring timber shelves that project across an entire wall, with high ceilings, B1 to 2F forming a single space”. It has a studio on the ground floor, street-side terrace seats, a lounge on the 2nd floor and a kitchen half a floor down.

Each floor can be rented by the hour, or the whole building booked out. It is where neighbours and people working in the building meet.

What are BIRTHERs?

The name for BIRTH's monthly members. In Azabu-Juban it costs ¥38,500 a month for 24/7 access to the workspace.

Members are called companions who grow the place together, not people who rent a desk — the name says a lot about how it is run.
