Meguro, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Meguro — a shared office on the 5th floor of Meguro Hilltop Walk, one minute from Meguro Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Meguro is on the 5th floor of Meguro Hilltop Walk, a one-minute walk from Meguro Station (JR, Tokyo Metro, Toei and Tokyu lines). It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, available 8:00–21:00 on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Right in front of Meguro Station

WORK STYLING Meguro is on the 5th floor of Meguro Hilltop Walk, a minute from Meguro Station. Its address is Kami-Osaki, Shinagawa-ku, right next to the station.

With four railway companies' lines meeting at Meguro, it is easy to meet people coming from any direction. It is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays, with last entry at 20:00.

01 · Entrance

The 5th-floor entrance

The entrance is on the 5th floor, where reception crew help you get started.

From the entrance, the open space and meeting rooms follow.

02 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space at your home site needs no booking.

There is a drinks and café corner, so you can work over a coffee.

03 · Meeting rooms and private rooms

Meeting rooms for up to 12, and rooms for one

Meeting rooms hold 2 to 12 people and are booked in 10-minute units.

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused work.

What does “last entry” mean?

The latest time you can enter on a given day. WORK STYLING Meguro is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays, and the website lists last entry as 20:00.

If you drop by in the evening, make sure to arrive before 20:00.
