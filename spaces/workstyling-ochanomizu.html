Ochanomizu, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Ochanomizu — a shared office lined with arched walls, two minutes from JR Ochanomizu Station

WORK STYLING Ochanomizu is on the 4th floor of the New Surugadai Building, a two-minute walk from JR Ochanomizu Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 4th floor in Surugadai, walkable from four stations

WORK STYLING Ochanomizu is two minutes on foot from JR Ochanomizu Station and three from Ochanomizu and Shin-Ochanomizu stations on the Tokyo Metro. Jimbocho Station is eight minutes away.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, sofa seats line a wall cut with arches, and the wooden floor runs all the way to the back.

What is an open space?

A shared work area without dividing walls.

The open space at WORK STYLING Ochanomizu can be used without booking.
