WORK STYLING Ikebukuro IT tower TOKYO — a shared office on the 14th floor of IT tower TOKYO, directly linked to Ikebukuro Station

WORK STYLING Ikebukuro IT tower TOKYO is on the 14th floor of IT tower TOKYO, directly linked to Ikebukuro Station on the Tokyo Metro Fukutoshin and Marunouchi lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12, an open space and rental offices. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Green rings on the 14th floor

WORK STYLING Ikebukuro IT tower TOKYO is on the 14th floor of a building directly linked to Ikebukuro Station on the Tokyo Metro. JR Ikebukuro Station is four minutes on foot.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, rings of plants hang from the ceiling above circles of artificial turf and seating. Window seats look down over Ikebukuro.

01 · Open space

Seats under rings of plants

The open space can be used without booking.

Tables and sofas sit beneath rings of plants hung from the ceiling.

02 · Window seats

A counter overlooking the city

There are counter seats facing the windows.

From the 14th floor, you can see the Ikebukuro streets.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms in different colours

Meeting rooms hold up to 12.

Wall colours change from room to room: green, blue and yellow.

04 · Private rooms and rental offices

Rooms for one, and rooms for teams

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

There are also rental offices for teams, with the city spreading out beyond the windows.

What is a rental office?

A room equipped with desks, chairs and connectivity that you rent by the month or similar period.

At WORK STYLING Ikebukuro IT tower TOKYO, rental offices share the floor with the open space and meeting rooms.
