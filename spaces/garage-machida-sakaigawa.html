Machida, Tokyo

GARAGE MACHIDA Sakaigawa — a “second place” with a café, eight minutes from Kobuchi Station

GARAGE MACHIDA Sakaigawa is in Kiso-Higashi, Machida, an eight-minute walk from Kobuchi Station on the JR Yokohama Line. At the entrance is a serious café, GARAGE COFFEE; beyond it are a slab-wood table, wall-facing focus seats, L-shaped seats, private rooms and shared offices with lofts. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A “second place” where anyone can be themselves

The official website describes it as “a ‘second place’ where anyone can be themselves” — the idea of creating somewhere close to home where you can focus on work.

The flow is to order a hand-drip specialty coffee at the café by the entrance, then work in the space at the back. Members can enjoy more than 40 kinds of drinks and snacks free of charge.

01 · Entrance and café

A serious café at the door

At the entrance is the café GARAGE COFFEE, serving hand-drip specialty coffee and hot sandwiches.

Order a drink, then head through to the workspace at the back.

02 · Table and focus seats

A slab-wood desk and wall-facing seats

The prized single-slab wooden table is a wide, solid desk.

There are also wall-facing focus seats with side screens, and L-shaped seats for several people.

03 · Private room and lofts

A room for web meetings, shared offices with lofts

The private room suits web meetings or work you want to keep away from prying eyes.

The shared offices with lofts are just three exclusive rooms, from ¥89,800 a month (incl. tax).

What is a loft?

A mezzanine-like space built into the upper part of a room, reached by ladder or stairs.

The official website notes that the shared offices at GARAGE MACHIDA Sakaigawa have lofts that can also be used for storage.
