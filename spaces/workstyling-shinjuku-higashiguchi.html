Shinjuku, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit — a station-connected shared office open on weekends and holidays, on the 8th floor of NEWNO GS Shinjuku

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit is on the 8th floor of NEWNO GS Shinjuku, directly connected to exit B12 of Shinjuku Station on the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line. Its feature is that it opens on weekends and holidays as well as weekdays. It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12, single-person rooms and phone booths. Introduction

A station-connected Shinjuku base, open at weekends

WORK STYLING Shinjuku East Exit is on the 8th floor of NEWNO GS Shinjuku, which you can enter from exit B12 of Shinjuku Station on the Marunouchi Line without going outside — no hassle even on rainy days.

Most WORK STYLING sites open on weekdays only, but Shinjuku East Exit is also open 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays — useful for days when you want to get a little work done at the weekend.

01 · Entrance and open space

Seats you can take without booking

Reception crew greet you at the entrance.

The open space needs no booking; pick any free seat on the spot. There are several kinds of seating.

02 · Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms booked in 10-minute units

Meeting rooms hold 2 to 12 people, by booking, in 10-minute units.

Directly connected to Shinjuku Station, they suit meetings with visitors.

03 · Private rooms and phone booths

Places to shut yourself away

Single-person rooms are booked in 10-minute units — good for online meetings and focused work.

For a quick call, use the phone booths, no booking needed.

What is SHARE (pay-as-you-go)?

One of WORK STYLING's plans: pay only for the time you use, in 10-minute units.

At Shinjuku East Exit, SHARE is available on weekends and holidays as well as weekdays.
