Toranomon, Tokyo

H¹T Toranomon — a corporate shared office with 27 private rooms, directly linked to the station on the 9th floor of the Toranomon Jitsugyo Kaikan

H¹T Toranomon, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 9th floor of the Toranomon Jitsugyo Kaikan main building, directly linked to Exit 10 of Toranomon Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fee. This site has as many as 27 single-person rooms and is open 7:00–22:30 on weekdays and 7:00–17:30 on Saturdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A station-linked base for people in sales

The official website recommends this site for “sales staff who rush around the central business district”.

There is no open space: it has five single-person boxes, 27 single-person rooms and four meeting rooms — a layout built around seats where you can shut yourself away and work.

01 · Boxes

Five single-person seats

There are five single-person boxes.

They suit short tasks or waiting for your next appointment.

02 · Rooms

27 single-person rooms

There are 27 single-person rooms.

With a door to close, you can take online meetings and calls without worrying about those around you.

03 · Meeting rooms

Four rooms for 2 to 8

There is one meeting room each for 2 and 3, and two for 8.

They also work for meetings with clients in Kasumigaseki or Uchisaiwaicho.

What is an H¹T “room”?

One of H¹T's seat types: a single-person private room with a door, suited to online meetings and phone calls.

H¹T Toranomon has 27 of them.
