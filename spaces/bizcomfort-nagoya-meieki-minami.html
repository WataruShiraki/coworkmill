BIZcomfort Nagoya Meieki Minami — 24-hour coworking near Nagoya Station

Nine minutes' walk from underground Exit S6 of Nagoya Station, BIZcomfort Nagoya Meieki Minami is on the ground floor of the Kinjiro Meieki Minami Building in Nakamura Ward. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 you can drop in without registering. Everything below is based on the official website.

Seats48 free-seating desks, 10 semi-private booths, 20 private offices

Introduction

A flat monthly fee, walking distance from Nagoya Station

BIZcomfort describes this as a cost-conscious shared office for opening a branch, within walking distance of Nagoya Station.

The coworking area has places where online meetings and food are allowed, and because it is open 24 hours a day, every day, the website suggests it for early mornings, before heading home, or weekends only.
