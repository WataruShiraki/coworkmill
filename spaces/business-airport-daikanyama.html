Daikanyama, Tokyo

Business-Airport Daikanyama — a base among the trees where buzz and calm coexist, on the 3rd floor of Forestgate Daikanyama MAIN

Business-Airport Daikanyama, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 3rd floor of the MAIN building of Forestgate Daikanyama in Daikanyamacho. The website describes a place where buzz and calm coexist seamlessly and where encounters between all kinds of people take root. Surrounded by greenery, it supports original ideas and the first shoots of new businesses. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Where buzz and calm coexist seamlessly

The website describes Business-Airport Daikanyama as a place where “liveliness and calm coexist seamlessly, and encounters with all kinds of people take root”. A space surrounded by greenery, it says, inspires distinctive ideas and supports new businesses as they sprout.

Forestgate Daikanyama is a mixed-use complex in Daikanyamacho. On the 3rd floor of its MAIN building are private rooms, booths, fixed desks and coworking.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥82,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex membership ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM, BOOTH and DESK

Private ROOMs start at ¥165,000 a month, BOOTHs at ¥220,000 and fixed DESKs at ¥110,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Shared spaces among the trees

In the middle of Daikanyama

Being surrounded by greenery is what defines this site.

The calm of Daikanyama's streets and the buzz of the building mix on the same floor.

What is Flex membership?

A membership where you pay only for the time you use. At Business-Airport Daikanyama it costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

It is a way to find out how often you will use the space before committing to a monthly plan.
