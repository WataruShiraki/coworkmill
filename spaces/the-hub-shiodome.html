Shiodome, Tokyo

THE HUB Shiodome — rental offices and coworking across a whole building in the Italian Quarter, Showa Astec Building No. 1

THE HUB Shiodome is in Showa Astec Building No. 1 in Shiodome's “Italian Quarter” business area, a seven-minute walk from exit A2 of Daimon Station on the Toei Oedo Line and eight from the Karasumori exit of Shimbashi Station. The website describes “a wide range of sizes only a whole building can offer, plus generous shared spaces”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The breadth of a whole building

The website introduces it as “a rental office and coworking space in Shiodome's Italian Quarter business area”.

Using an entire seven-storey building, it offers many room sizes and generous shared spaces such as a lounge and reception room. It is seven minutes from Daimon, eight from Shimbashi and nine from the North Exit of Hamamatsucho.

01 · The building and waiting space

A whole building in the Italian Quarter

Showa Astec Building No. 1 is a seven-storey building renovated in March 2017.

Visitors can wait in the waiting space.

02 · Lounge

A shared space for members

The lounge is open to tenants and coworking members: the all-site plan is ¥16,500 a month, the single-site plan ¥8,800 and the 10-hour plan ¥2,200.

Drop-in is charged by usage, from ¥275 per 30 minutes.

03 · Meeting room, reception room and booths

Rooms for meetings and visitors

Besides a meeting room there is a reception room, so meetings and receiving guests can be kept separate.

Booth seats suit calls, online meetings and focused time.

What is the Italian Quarter?

The name for the blocks around Higashi-Shimbashi 2-chome on the west side of Shiodome. The website describes THE HUB Shiodome as being in “Shiodome's Italian Quarter business area”.

Tucked just behind Shiodome's towers, it is within walking distance of Shimbashi, Hamamatsucho and Daimon stations.
