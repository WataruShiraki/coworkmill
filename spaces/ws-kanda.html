Kanda, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Kanda — one minute from Kanda Station, with meeting rooms for 13 to 80

WORK STYLING Kanda is on the 6th floor of the Ueno Building, a one-minute walk from Kanda Station (JR and Tokyo Metro). It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

8 photos · © Mitsui Fudosan (WORK STYLING “Kanda”) · Tap any photo to view it full size

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

Small rooms to large ones, on a 6th floor by the station

It is a minute from Kanda Station and about six minutes' walk from Shin-Nihombashi and Mitsukoshimae stations.

Besides single-person rooms and meeting rooms for up to 12, it has meeting rooms for 13 to 80 — handling everything from solo work to large gatherings on one floor.

01 · Entrance

From reception to the work areas

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

It is open 8:00–21:00 on weekdays.

02 · Open space

A mix of seat shapes

The open space has sofa seats, desks and other seat shapes.

Use it for solo work or short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms can be chosen by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

With a desk, chair and monitor, they are for when you need to focus.

What is a pay-as-you-go plan?

A contract where you pay only for the time you use, with no fixed monthly cost.

WORK STYLING offers one in 10-minute units; meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.
