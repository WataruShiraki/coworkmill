Ikebukuro, Tokyo

Sauna & Hotel Karumaru Ikebukuro — coworking in a sauna's rest area, 30 seconds from the West Exit of Ikebukuro Station

Sauna & Hotel Karumaru Ikebukuro, in Ikebukuro 2-chome, is a 30-second walk from Exit C6 on the west side of Ikebukuro Station. A facility for saunas and overnight stays, it has a coworking space — desks free to use for work — inside its rest area. There are also recliners, capsule-style relaxation pods and a library of more than 10,000 manga and magazines. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A café-like workplace after the sauna

The official website describes the coworking space as a functional space where you can focus on work in a café-like atmosphere.

The rest area offers many kinds of space to choose from — recliners, relaxation pods, a library and coworking — so you can decide how to spend time after the sauna.

01 · Recliners and relaxation pods

Seats for watching and reading

The recliner space has purpose-designed recliners, with unlimited video on demand, reading lights and unlimited manga and magazines.

The relaxation pods are capsule-style rest spaces you enter between bookshelves, with mattresses so you can relax undisturbed. Neither can be reserved.

02 · Library space

More than 10,000 manga and magazines

In the library space, you can read more than 10,000 manga and magazines freely at no charge, with new titles added regularly.

03 · Mamejicafe

House-roasted coffee

On the 7th floor, coffee from a house-roasting brand run by a well-known chef is on sale, made from rare Guatemalan beans roasted on site.

Guests are asked to keep quiet in the building, and some areas are designated no-call zones.

What is a sauna & hotel?

A bathhouse centred on saunas, combined with rooms or capsules for overnight stays.

Karumaru Ikebukuro offers overnight and day use, with a coworking space inside its rest area.
