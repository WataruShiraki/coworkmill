Marunouchi, Tokyo

point 0 marunouchi — a “future office” on the 4th floor of Marunouchi 2-chome Building, with interiors by Klein Dytham architecture

A few minutes' walk from Tokyo Station, next to the Marunouchi Building, point 0 marunouchi is on the 4th floor of the Marunouchi 2-chome Building. Opened in July 2019, this members' coworking space is also a testbed for “the office of the future”. Companies such as Daikin, Okamura and Panasonic take part, and the interior was designed by Klein Dytham architecture. In January 2021 it became the first coworking office in Japan to earn WELL certification (WELL v2 pilot) at Gold level. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Towards the future of the office

point 0 is a place to keep asking what the office of the future should be. In an age when the lines between office and home, company and company, work and play are fading, it wants to redefine why people still gather in an office. Nineteen companies take part, including Daikin, Okamura, Panasonic, Lion, TOTO, Kajima and Mitsubishi Estate, collecting data on how people move and on the environment here to improve spaces and create new services.

The aim was “a green space that seems to bring Marunouchi's lush trees straight indoors”, with air, light, sound and scent that make the most of that greenery; uncompromising office furniture; elements of daily life such as showers and sleep; and food and drink that support good conversation. Interiors are by Klein Dytham architecture, planting by parkERs, and branding by Insitu and artless.

01 · Workspace and greenery

Seats surrounded by plants

Plants of many kinds are placed so people can relax as they work. There are 14 dedicated seats; free seating is available to monthly members or pay-as-you-go in 15-minute units. Both are open 24 hours, 365 days a year.

The official site lists a dedicated seat at ¥115,500 a month (12-month contract), a monthly free-seat membership at ¥82,500 a month (12-month contract), and drop-in use at ¥275 per 15 minutes (all including tax).

02 · Café and community

Specialty coffee and an event space for 80

There is a café serving specialty coffee, and the website describes a rich community and events for shaping the future of work together.

The event space holds up to 80 people. There are various meeting rooms for different purposes: ¥3,300 an hour, or ¥6,600 an hour for the large room (including tax).

03 · Rooms to focus and rest

Meditation room, focus booths, nap rooms and showers

The meditation room is for meditation, yoga, or thinking in a different setting. There are also private focus booths that block sound and sightlines.

Nap booths for better productivity, free showers open 24 hours and a free low-oxygen room complete the floor. Work and rest happen side by side.

04 · IoT and experiments

Experience the future office

IoT devices are installed throughout, so you can experience the office of the future. Partner companies bring their latest technology, data and know-how to the open space, meeting rooms and nap booths.

Data on movement, vital signs and equipment operation is collected and analysed to improve the space and create new services. Simply by sitting here, you take part in the experiment.

What is WELL Certification?

A rating system for buildings that adds human health to the design, construction and operation of a space. Started by the US-based IWBI in 2014, it assesses ten concepts: air, water, nourishment, light, movement, thermal comfort, sound, materials, mind and community.

point 0 marunouchi earned WELL v2 pilot Gold in January 2021 — a first for a coworking office in Japan — achieved, the website says, with technology from Daikin (air and space), Okamura (furniture) and Panasonic (lighting).
