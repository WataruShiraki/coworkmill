Shimbashi, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Shimbashi — a 96-seat lounge and high-grade private rooms with company registration, in the Urbannet Uchisaiwaicho Building

CROSSCOOP Shimbashi is in the Urbannet Uchisaiwaicho Building, two minutes from exit A5 of Uchisaiwaicho Station and four from Shimbashi Station. Across the 3rd to 5th floors are a lounge of about 96 seats, eight meeting rooms, web booths, a coworking space, a seminar room for 72 and private rooms for 10 to 100 people. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

High-grade private offices with company registration

The website describes it as “a high-grade private office with company registration, meeting rooms and full facilities”, highlighting face-recognition security and a building prepared for business continuity (BCP).

Private rooms for 10 to 100 people are combined with a 96-seat lounge and a coworking space. Contracts start from one month.

01 · Meeting rooms and seminar room

Eight meeting rooms and a room for 72

There is one meeting room for 8 and seven for 10. Private-room contracts include free meeting-room hours.

There is also a seminar room for 72.

02 · Private rooms

From 10 to 100 people

Private rooms range from 10 to 100 people. A room for 10 is ¥1,010,000 a month and a room for 45 is ¥4,500,000.

Even a large company's base fits on a single floor.

What is BCP?

Short for Business Continuity Plan: preparations to keep working even when disasters strike. The website says CROSSCOOP Shimbashi is in a building prepared for BCP.

When large companies choose a base, how well the building is prepared is an important condition.
