Sangenjaya, Tokyo

WORK STYLING SOLO Sangenjaya — single rooms and a café space, two minutes from Sangenjaya Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Sangenjaya is on the 3rd floor of the Kurubiru building, a two-minute walk from Sangenjaya Station on the Tokyu lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

OperatorMitsui Fudosan

Introduction

Private rooms near home

It is two minutes on foot from Sangenjaya Station, with last entry at 20:00.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.

01 · Café space

Drinks and café space

There is a drinks and café space where you can get something to drink.

02 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Private rooms for one line the floor.

03 · Entrance

Entrance

The entrance is on the 3rd floor of the building.

What is last entry?

The last time you can enter the facility that day.

WORK STYLING SOLO Sangenjaya is open until 21:00, with last entry at 20:00.
