Chitose-Funabashi, Tokyo

H¹T Chitose-Funabashi — a shared office open 7:00–22:00, 3 minutes from Chitose-Funabashi Station's South Exit

H¹T Chitose-Funabashi is on the 2nd floor of the TOM Building, a three-minute walk from the South Exit of Chitose-Funabashi Station on the Odakyu Line. A small base of eight single rooms and three single boxes, it is open every day from 7:00 to 22:00. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies.
