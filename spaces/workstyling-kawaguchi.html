Kawaguchi, Saitama

WORK STYLING Kawaguchi — an 11th floor looking out over the city, three minutes from Kawaguchi Station

WORK STYLING Kawaguchi is on the 11th floor of LaLa Terrace Kawaguchi, a three-minute walk from Kawaguchi Station on the JR Keihin-Tohoku Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The 11th floor of LaLa Terrace Kawaguchi

It is three minutes on foot from Kawaguchi Station on the JR Keihin-Tohoku Line.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see sofa and round-table seats by the window and round tables lined up along the windows.

Many ways to sit

The open space can be used without booking.

Seating includes sofa and round-table seats by the window and round tables lined up along the windows.

02 · Other seats

Partitioned desks with monitors

You will also find partitioned desks with monitors.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with a wooden table.

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is a lounge?

A shared area with sofas and round tables where you can relax as you work.

At WORK STYLING Kawaguchi, sofas and round tables line the windows.
