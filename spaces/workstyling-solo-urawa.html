Urawa, Saitama

WORK STYLING SOLO Urawa — single rooms usable at weekends, four minutes from Urawa Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Urawa is on the 5th floor of the Urawa Daini Daiei Building in Takasago, Urawa Ward, Saitama, a four-minute walk from JR Urawa Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

It is four minutes on foot from JR Urawa Station.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also an open space.
