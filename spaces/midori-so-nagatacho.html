Nagatacho, Tokyo

MIDORI.so NAGATACHO — MIDORI.so's seven-floor flagship in Nagatacho, for teams of 1 to 150

MIDORI.so NAGATACHO is at Hirakawacho 2-5-3, a two-minute walk from Nagatacho Station. MIDORI.so's flagship uses an entire building — seven floors from B1 to 6F, about 23,000 sq ft — and can host teams from 1 to 150 people. The second floor has a 212 m² engagement space for team meetings, meetups, pop-up offsite offices and mixers. Its operator, MIRAI INSTITUTE, runs eight sites in Tokyo. Introduction

FUTURE DIVERSITY

MIDORI.so is a coworking community exploring “the future of work in a society that values the individual”. Beyond freelancers and startups, it is for people who try to stay independent inside large organisations and people who want to create new value, the website says.

The building was originally run as Nagatacho GRiD. MIRAI INSTITUTE took over the whole building in May 2023 and reopened it, expanded, as MIDORI.so NAGATACHO on 1 November 2023 under the concept “FUTURE DIVERSITY”. It is the flagship alongside the Nakameguro, Bakuro-yokoyama, Shibuya, Aoyama, Ikejiri, Kichijoji and Nihonbashi sites.

01 · 5F workspace

A shared floor with a kitchen, lounge, beds and a library

The fifth floor is shared. Along with the workspace, meeting rooms, kitchen and lounge, there are beds and a library. Window seats, sofas and bookshelves let you change how you work on a single floor.

A free desk is ¥42,350 a month and a fixed desk ¥60,500 a month (both with an ¥11,000 sign-up fee, including tax), available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Ten hours of meeting rooms a month are included, and you can register your company here.

02 · 5F library

Working surrounded by books

The fifth-floor library is surrounded by bookshelves — a place to read and to work.

Pop-in costs ¥3,300 a day or ¥33,000 for an 11-day pack, using any free seat on weekdays from 10:00 to 18:00. It is a way to try the floor before becoming a member.

03 · 2F engagement space

212 m², 80 seated or 100 standing

The second-floor engagement space is for deepening ties with the community: team meetings, meetups, offsite offices for a few days, mixers and programmes that raise engagement in organisations.

It is 212.0 m² and holds up to 80 seated or 100 standing. With a bar counter, fridge, projector, screen, sound system and Wi-Fi, it can be rented 9:00–22:00 for ¥385,000 a day or ¥55,000 an hour (¥38,500 an hour for 6 hours or more, all including tax).

What is the engagement space?

A bookable room on the second floor of MIDORI.so NAGATACHO, made for deepening ties with the community. It is used for team meetings, meetups and offsite offices for a limited number of days.

Placing a room for outside teams inside the members' workplace increases how often members and outsiders meet in the same building.
