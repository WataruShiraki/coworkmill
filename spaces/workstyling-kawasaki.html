Kawasaki, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING Kawasaki — a 12th floor of hexagonal booths, one minute from Kawasaki Station

WORK STYLING Kawasaki is on the 12th floor of Pacific Marks Kawasaki, a one-minute walk from JR Kawasaki Station and two minutes from Keikyu Kawasaki Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

One minute on foot from JR Kawasaki Station

It is one minute on foot from JR Kawasaki Station and two from Keikyu Kawasaki Station.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see seats divided by curtains and hexagonal booths enclosed by plywood.

01 · Open space

Many ways to sit

The open space can be used without booking.

Seating includes seats divided by curtains and hexagonal booths enclosed by plywood.

02 · Other seats

Oil-drum tables and a high counter

You will also find oil-drum tables and a high counter.

You can choose a seat to suit your task.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

Meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.

Pictured: a meeting room with green chairs and a round table.

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is a booth seat?

A single-person seat enclosed by panels or walls.

WORK STYLING Kawasaki has rows of hexagonal booths enclosed by plywood.
