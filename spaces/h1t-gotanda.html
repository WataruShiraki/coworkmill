Gotanda, Tokyo

H¹T Gotanda — a corporate shared office in front of Gotanda Station, on the 7th floor of the 5 Central Building

H¹T Gotanda, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 7th floor of the 5 Central Building, a two-minute walk from the East Exit of JR Gotanda Station and one minute from Exit A4 of Gotanda Station on the Toei Asakusa Line. The official website calls it “a satellite shared office right in front of Gotanda Station, with excellent transport links”. It is open 8:00–22:00 every day and has four meeting rooms for 2 to 6. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site in front of a three-line station

H¹T Gotanda is right in front of Gotanda Station, served by the JR, Toei Asakusa and Tokyu Ikegami lines. The official website names its convenient transport as a key feature.

Seating is an open space for 8 and ten single-person rooms. Meeting rooms are two for 2 and one each for 4 and 6, a layout well suited to small meetings.

01 · Open space

Eight shared seats

The open space seats 8.

One minute from Exit A4 of the Asakusa Line, you can stop by between train changes.

02 · Rooms

Ten single-person rooms

There are ten single-person rooms.

With a door to close, they also work for online meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Four rooms for 2 to 6

The meeting rooms are two for 2, one for 4 and one for 6.

They handle anything from a two-person interview to a small meeting.

What is a satellite shared office?

A workplace away from head office that several companies use together.

The official website describes H¹T Gotanda as a “satellite shared office”.
