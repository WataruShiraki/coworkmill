THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho — a rental office where every room is fully private, with a rooftop terrace, in the Kabutocho Heiwa Dia Building

THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho is in the Kabutocho Heiwa Dia Building in Nihonbashi Kabutocho, a one-minute walk from exit 11 of Kayabacho Station. A SOHO building boldly renovated so that every room is fully private, it offers offices with a high degree of independence, plus three meeting rooms for four, a lounge and a rooftop terrace. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A bold renovation of a SOHO building

The website's headline reads: “A bold renovation of a SOHO building — offices with high independence, every room fully private.” The 10-storey building was renovated in July 2022.

Every room is fully private, from 2 to 10 people. A building of small live-work units has been reborn as a cluster of independent offices.

01 · Fully private offices

Independent rooms for 2–10

Every room is fully private, for 2 to 10 people, with furniture, internet, lighting and air-conditioning in place, from ¥75,350 a month.

The website names independence as a key feature.

What is SOHO?

Short for Small Office / Home Office: a small office or a workplace that doubles as a home. THE HUB Nihonbashi Kabutocho is a renovated SOHO building, the website says.

SOHO buildings, with many small units, have the right bones for private rental offices.
