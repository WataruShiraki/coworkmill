Lounge ASO (Aso Kumamoto Airport) — in the domestic departure area, open 6:30–20:00 every day

Lounge ASO is inside the domestic departure waiting area of the passenger terminal at Aso Kumamoto Airport. It is listed on the WORK STYLING official website as a site that Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING members can also use. Everything below is based on the official website.

LocationAso Kumamoto Airport passenger terminal, inside the domestic departure waiting area

Introduction

A lounge to use before boarding

It is inside the domestic departure waiting area and can be used by departing passengers.

According to the official website, a private-room booking is required, and you complete entry at the Lounge ASO reception.

What is a departure waiting area?
