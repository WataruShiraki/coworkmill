Ginza, Tokyo

fabbit Ginza — private offices with a coworking lounge, on the 7th floor of the Hulic Ginza 1-chome Showa-dori Building

fabbit Ginza is on the 7th floor of the Hulic Ginza 1-chome Showa-dori Building, a three-minute walk from Ginza-itchome Station on the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line. It is listed on the official website of the rental-office brand CROSSCOOP as one of its sites. It has fully private rooms for 2 to 6 people, a coworking lounge, meeting rooms and phone booths. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Balancing work and refreshment

The website describes the shared facilities as “diverse shared spaces that balance working and refreshing”.

Ginza-itchome, Takaracho and Kyobashi stations are all within walking distance, and the site is on the 7th floor of a building on Showa-dori. Private rooms for 2 to 6 people are sized for small teams.

01 · Reception and coworking lounge

A place to work outside your room

Past reception is the coworking lounge.

It is a shared space where private-room tenants can change scene while working.

02 · Meeting room and phone booths

Places for meetings and calls

There is a meeting room and phone booths.

Client meetings and online calls can both be handled on the same floor.

03 · Private rooms

Fully private rooms for 2–6

Private rooms hold 2 to 6 people, all fully enclosed, and company registration is possible.

The website calls it “a high-grade office designed for ease of work”.

What is a coworking lounge?

A shared workspace that rental-office tenants use outside their private rooms. fabbit Ginza has one.

For a team in a small room, it means a second place to work beyond their own door.
