Kasukabe, Saitama

Kasukabe Yumoto Onsen — a space for desk work on the 2nd floor of a hot-spring facility

Kasukabe Yumoto Onsen is a bathhouse in Shimo-Omasu-Shinden, Kasukabe. On the 2nd floor is a workspace for reading or a little desk work, alongside a rest zone and relaxation corner. It is open 10:00–23:00, and the restaurants inside can be used without paying the entry fee. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A little time at a desk after the bath

The official website presents the 2nd-floor workspace as a place for reading or a little desk work.

The same floor has a rest zone, a relaxation corner and chargers, so you can bathe, rest, do a bit of work and rest again.

01 · Workspace

A 2nd floor for reading and desk work

The 2nd-floor workspace is for reading or a little desk work. Chargers are also available on this floor.

02 · Rest zone

A 2nd-floor rest zone for after the bath

There is a rest zone on the same floor as the workspace.

03 · Relaxation corner and meeting zone

Comfortable seats, and a ground-floor meeting point

The 2nd floor has a relaxation corner, and the ground floor a meeting zone.

The restaurants inside can be used without paying the entry fee.

What is the night service?

A discounted entry fee for people arriving late in the evening.

At Kasukabe Yumoto Onsen, adults who check in after 20:00 pay ¥1,000 (last entry 22:00).
