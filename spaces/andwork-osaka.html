Sakaisuji-Hommachi, Osaka

.andwork Osaka — a hotel lounge at THE LIVELY Osaka Honmachi, 1 min from Sakaisuji-Hommachi

A one-minute walk from Sakaisuji-Hommachi Station on the Chuo and Sakaisuji lines, .andwork Osaka is inside THE LIVELY Osaka Honmachi hotel in Chuo Ward. There is a lobby lounge with a pool table and a coworking space with fast Wi-Fi and power. Drop-in runs from 8:00 to 22:00 and costs ¥2,000 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Public spaces that set your creativity free

A relaxed lobby lounge and a coworking space with fast Wi-Fi and power.

The lounge has a pool table, so between tasks you can play a game with the guests around you, drink in hand. There is a restaurant on the ground floor and a bar on the top floor.

01 · Lounge

A lobby lounge with a pool table

The lobby lounge doubles as coworking space, with fast Wi-Fi and power.

Between tasks you can play pool with hotel guests.

02 · Food and drink

A ground-floor restaurant and a top-floor bar

With a restaurant on the ground floor, food is never far away on a long working day.

After work, the top-floor bar serves signature cocktails with a night view.

03 · The building

A hotel 1 minute from the station

It is a one-minute walk from Exit 5 of Sakaisuji-Hommachi Station. The official site also notes it is 5 minutes by taxi from Dotonbori.

The hotel describes itself as being at the centre of a triangle linking Kita, Minami and Osaka Castle.

What is happy hour?

A set time when drinks are cheaper or free.

.andwork says its evening happy hour serves free draught beer.
