Fuchu, Tokyo

WORK STYLING SOLO Fuchu — single rooms and a café space, five minutes from Fuchu Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Fuchu is on the 3rd floor of the Fuchu Dai-ichi Seimei Building, a five-minute walk from Fuchu Station on the Keio Line and six minutes from JR Fuchuhonmachi Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms in Fuchu

It is five minutes on foot from Fuchu Station on the Keio Line and six from JR Fuchuhonmachi Station.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
