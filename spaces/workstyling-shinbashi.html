Shimbashi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shimbashi — a shared office on the 2nd floor of Shimbashi M-SQUARE, one minute from Shimbashi Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Shimbashi is on the 2nd floor of Shimbashi M-SQUARE, a one-minute walk from Shimbashi Station on the Tokyo Metro. Most of its photos show the open space — this is a site built around shared seating — and it also has meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

On the 2nd floor, in front of Shimbashi Station

WORK STYLING Shimbashi is on the 2nd floor of Shimbashi M-SQUARE, one minute from the Tokyo Metro station and two from the JR, Toei and Yurikamome stations.

Most of the photos on the official site show the open space, which can be used without booking.

01 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space can be used without booking.

There are many kinds of seating, so you can choose where to sit according to the day's work.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

One minute from Shimbashi Station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for calls and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls and online meetings.

There is also a drinks corner, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

What is an open space?

Shared work seating without partitions. At WORK STYLING, the open space can be used without booking.

With sofas, counters and large tables, you can sit differently in the same place depending on the work at hand.
