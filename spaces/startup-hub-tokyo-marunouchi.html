Marunouchi, Tokyo

Startup Hub Tokyo Marunouchi — the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's free start-up support centre for anyone wondering “should I start a business?”

Startup Hub Tokyo Marunouchi is on the ground floor of the Meiji Yasuda Life Building in Marunouchi. Part of TOKYO Startup Station, it supports people who have started to wonder “should I start a business?”. A lounge for working and meetings, start-up advice from concierges who are entrepreneurs themselves, events almost every day, and a kids' room staffed by childcare workers — every service is free. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

When you start wondering “should I start a business?”

The official website presents this place as a start-up support centre for people who have begun to wonder whether to start a business. It handles a wide range of questions, from first interest through to preparation.

All it takes is an online member application, and there is no charge. It is designed as an entrance anyone can walk into, even before deciding to start a business.

01 · Events

Events almost every day

Events useful for starting a business are held almost every day.

Resident community managers help members share information with one another.

02 · Kids' room

Bring your children to consultations

There is a kids' room where qualified childcare workers look after children (by reservation).

It makes it easier for parents to join consultations and events.

What is a start-up support centre?

A facility that supports people about to start a business or who have just started one. Many offer advice and seminars as well as space.

Startup Hub Tokyo Marunouchi offers consultations, events, its lounge, childcare and books, all free.
