Susukino, Sapporo

BIZcomfort Sapporo Susukino — 24-hour coworking 1 minute from Hosui-Susukino Station

One minute's walk from Exit 1 of Hosui-Susukino Station on the Toho Subway Line, BIZcomfort Sapporo Susukino is on the 4th floor of NBF CONNECT SAPPORO in Chuo Ward. It is BIZcomfort's second location in Sapporo. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 you can drop in without registering. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Meet, talk, spark ideas

BIZcomfort says Cross Lounge, at the centre of the space, is the hub for meeting people and moving around the office.

The floor is zoned by purpose, so you can use it for remote work, meetings or quiet study. There is a staffed reception and meeting rooms.
