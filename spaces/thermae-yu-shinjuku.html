Kabukicho, Tokyo

Shinjuku Natural Hot Spring Thermae-Yu — coworking and lounges in a Kabukicho hot spring

Shinjuku Natural Hot Spring Thermae-Yu, in Kabukicho 1-chome, is a 24-hour bathhouse about a two-minute walk from Exit E1 of Shinjuku-sanchome Station on the Tokyo Metro and Toei Shinjuku Line. On the ground floor, inside the locker-room area, is a coworking space with individually partitioned seats, and a Relax & Coworking Lounge opened on the basement floor in July 2025. Every seat has power, and free Wi-Fi is available. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Sauna and work, under one roof

Thermae-Yu describes its ground-floor coworking space as making possible “the luxurious lifestyle of sauna × workation”.

It suggests refreshing yourself in the baths, then working or reading in a gentle focus mode. There are several lounges for resting too.

01 · 1F coworking space

Individually partitioned desks

The ground-floor coworking space is set up separately for men and women inside the locker-room area.

Each desk is partitioned, every seat has USB and power outlets, and free Wi-Fi is available.

02 · B1F lounge

The Relax & Coworking Lounge, opened July 2025

The basement Relax & Coworking Lounge opened on 1 July 2025 and is free to use.

There are 24 relaxation beds and 11 coworking seats, all with power outlets.

03 · Lounges

A women-only lounge and a waiting lounge

The 3rd-floor Venus Lounge is a space where women can relax on their own with peace of mind, at no extra charge. Every seat has a TV and USB and power outlets, with free Wi-Fi.

On the second basement level there is a double-height relaxation lounge, and on the ground floor a lounge room (waiting room).

What is a workation?

A blend of “work” and “vacation”: working while travelling or taking a break somewhere restful.

Thermae-Yu calls using the sauna and coworking under one roof “sauna × workation”.
