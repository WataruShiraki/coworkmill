Ebisu, Tokyo

PORTAL POINT Ebisu — a creative office in Yebisu Garden Place, with a light-filled lounge and a nap room

PORTAL POINT Ebisu is inside Glass Square at Yebisu Garden Place. It is a five-minute walk from the East Exit of JR Ebisu Station, and the Ebisu Skywalk moving walkway links the station directly to its basement level. The building, completed in 2002, was renovated in 2019. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Light, coffee and a nap room

The official website describes “a community lounge flooded with light through a glass atrium — where you turn over new ideas with coffee in hand, and restore body and mind in the nap room”.

It covers 2,549.84 m² and combines offices with a shop, showroom and event space.

01 · Community lounge

Beneath a glass atrium

The lounge is lit by light pouring through a glass atrium.

The official website presents it as a place to think up new ideas with a coffee in hand. Free desks are also available as a plan.

02 · Nap room and phone booths

Recharge, then talk

The official website suggests using the nap room to “restore body and mind”.

Phone booths are available for online meetings.

03 · Showroom and events

A place to show your work too

With showroom and event-space functions, it is a place to share your work as well as to do it.

Offices come in units from one person (15.25–121.87 m²).

What is the Ebisu Skywalk?

A moving walkway linking JR Ebisu Station with Yebisu Garden Place.

PORTAL POINT Ebisu's official website says that, using this walkway, you reach its basement level directly from the station.
