Kanda, Tokyo

WeWork KANDA SQUARE — a large site themed on Akihabara's Electric Town and retro video games

With reception on the 11th floor of KANDA SQUARE in Kanda-Nishikicho, WeWork KANDA SQUARE is a large site with many private offices for anything from 2 to several hundred people. Its interior theme is Akihabara's Electric Town, with art inspired by retro video games scattered throughout. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Akihabara's Electric Town and retro games

The interior theme is Akihabara's Electric Town, with art inspired by old video games scattered throughout the space.

The website describes a site at the centre of culture and trends: Kanda Myojin shrine and the Kanda Festival, Akihabara's Electric Town and Jimbocho's second-hand book fairs.

01 · Shared areas

Art inspired by video games

Art inspired by retro video games is scattered through the shared areas.

Ceilings are 2,900 mm high.

02 · Private offices and resilience

From 2 to several hundred, with backup power

There are many private offices for 2 to several hundred people.

The building has seismic isolation and dampers, and in a power cut its emergency generator can supply air-conditioning, lighting and outlets in tenant areas for up to about 15 days.

What are BCP measures?

BCP stands for Business Continuity Plan: preparations to keep working through disasters and power cuts.

KANDA SQUARE has, among other things, an emergency generator that can supply power for about 15 days continuously when running on city gas.
