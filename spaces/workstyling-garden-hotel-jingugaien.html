Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier Lounge — a green lounge and meeting rooms, one minute from Kokuritsu-Kyogijo Station

The 2nd-floor lounge of Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier, a one-minute walk from Kokuritsu-Kyogijo Station on the Toei Oedo Line, can be used as a WORK STYLING site. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Working in a hotel by Jingu Gaien

It is one minute on foot from Kokuritsu-Kyogijo Station, five from Sendagaya Station and six from Shinanomachi Station.

In the photos you can see a lounge with plants hanging from the ceiling, plus meeting rooms and private rooms.
