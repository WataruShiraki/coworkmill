Tsukiji, Tokyo

H¹T Tsukiji — a shared office of 10 single rooms, 30 seconds from Tsukiji Station

H¹T Tsukiji is in the Emita Ginza East Building, a 30-second walk from exit 4 of Tsukiji Station on the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line. Its seats are ten single rooms, and it is open every day from 7:00 to 22:00. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private rooms only, 30 seconds from the station

H¹T Tsukiji consists of ten single rooms (there is no multifunction printer) — a place to concentrate on your own work.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.
