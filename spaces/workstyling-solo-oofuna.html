Ofuna, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING SOLO Ofuna — single rooms and a café space, three minutes from Ofuna Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Ofuna is on the 3rd floor of Ofuna Center Place (formerly Ichigo Ofuna Building), a three-minute walk from the North Gate (Kasama Exit) of JR Ofuna Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A three-minute walk from the North Gate (Kasama Exit) of JR Ofuna Station.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
