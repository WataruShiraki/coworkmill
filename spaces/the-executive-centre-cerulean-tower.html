Shibuya, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Cerulean Tower — a tea-room-inspired reception and views of Mt Fuji, on the 15th floor in Shibuya

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 15th floor of Cerulean Tower, a five-minute walk from Shibuya Station. Completed in 2001, Cerulean Tower was the tallest building around Shibuya Station until 2019. The website describes a reception inspired by the Japanese tea room, and views over Tokyo to Mt Fuji. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A reception inspired by the tea room

The website's signature feature is a reception inspired by the Japanese tea room: on the 15th floor of a Shibuya skyscraper, visitors are first greeted by a Japanese setting.

Spacious coworking and collaboration areas and views of Tokyo and Mt Fuji are also listed among the centre's features.

01 · Coworking and pantry

Spacious shared seating

Coworking seats the website calls “spacious”, and a pantry with drinks.

Hot desks and dedicated desks start at ¥54,000 a month, day passes from ¥5,000 a day and virtual offices from ¥16,200 a month.

02 · Private offices and meeting rooms

Rooms looking down on Shibuya

Private offices are priced on request, and there are two meeting rooms.

The windows look out over Shibuya, and on clear days Mt Fuji is visible, the website says.

What is a Japanese tea room?

A small room designed for the tea ceremony — a space shaped by simple materials and the etiquette of welcoming guests.

The centre at Cerulean Tower has a reception inspired by it.
