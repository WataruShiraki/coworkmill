JustCo GranTokyo South Tower — premium coworking on the 11th floor, directly connected to Tokyo Station

JustCo GranTokyo South Tower is on the 11th floor of GranTokyo South Tower, a commercial complex directly connected to Tokyo Station, about 150 m from the Yaesu South exit. The website highlights excellent access to the shinkansen, airport express trains, highway buses, JR and Tokyo Metro. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Right above the shinkansen station

The website presents the centre as premium coworking and shared offices in a commercial complex directly connected to Tokyo Station.

About 150 m from the Yaesu South exit, with connections to the shinkansen, airport express trains and highway buses, it can be the Tokyo front door for companies that travel a lot or have offices across Japan.

01 · Main lounge

Shared space on the 11th floor

The main lounge is where members gather, supported by resident community staff.

Phone booths, lockers and printers are available.

02 · Private offices

From small rooms to large ones

Choose a private office size: Small for 1–3, Mid-size for 4–49 and Enterprise for 50 or more.

Hot desks and dedicated desks are also available.

03 · Meeting rooms

Set up for online meetings

Meeting rooms are equipped with online meeting systems.

You can hold a meeting as soon as you arrive at Tokyo Station.

What is a dedicated desk?

A fixed seat in a coworking space that only you use. Unlike a hot desk, where you look for a free seat each time, you can leave your things there.

JustCo GranTokyo South Tower offers both hot desks and dedicated desks.
