Shijo Karasuma, Kyoto

BIZcomfort Kyoto Shijo Karasuma — Japanese-style coworking near Nishiki Market

Four minutes' walk from Exit 22 of Shijo Station (Karasuma Subway Line) and Karasuma Station (Hankyu Line), BIZcomfort Kyoto Shijo Karasuma is on the 4th and 5th floors of the Ishikan Building on Nishikikoji Street in Nakagyo Ward. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 you can drop in without registering. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Where tradition meets the present

BIZcomfort describes Shijo Karasuma as an attractive area where tradition and the modern city blend together.

The interior uses wooden pillars, noren curtains and round paper-style lamps. Drinks, printers and lockers are available, and there are meeting rooms and online-meeting rooms.

What is a drop-in?

Using the space for just one day without becoming a member.

At BIZcomfort Kyoto Shijo Karasuma you can drop in on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 for ¥550 per hour or ¥1,650 per day. Reservations are not taken.
