BIZcomfort Sanjo Kiyamachi — top-floor 24-hour coworking 3 min from Sanjo, Kyoto

Three minutes' walk from Sanjo Station on the Keihan Line, BIZcomfort Sanjo Kiyamachi is on the 5th, 9th and 10th floors of aune Kyoto Sanjo in Nakagyo Ward, beside the Kamo River and Sanjo Ohashi bridge. The coworking floors sit at the top of the building with panoramic views over the city, and members can use them 24 hours a day, every day. There is no hourly drop-in; the pay-as-you-go 1-minute plan costs ¥6.6 a minute after a ¥5,500 registration. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Views over Kyoto from the top floor

The official site says the space is on the top floor of the building, with a panoramic view across the city.

Coworking is spread over two floors, with a meeting room fitted with a large screen and private rooms for online meetings.

01 · Free seating

Free desks by the window

There are 61 free-seating desks.

Café booths allow food, calls and online meetings; work booths allow calls of up to 10 minutes.

02 · Private offices

Rental offices

There are 32 lockable private offices for one to six people.

03 · Booths

Booth seats

High-backed booth seats.

What is the 1-minute plan?

A plan where you pay only for the time you use, by the minute.

After registering, BIZcomfort's 1-minute plan works at any BIZcomfort coworking space in Japan.
