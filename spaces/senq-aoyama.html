Aoyama, Tokyo

SENQ Aoyama — a “CREATOR'S VILLAGE” of 22 rooms on the 2nd floor of Lattice Aoyama Square

SENQ Aoyama is on the 2nd floor of Lattice Aoyama Square, a one-minute walk from exit 4 of Aoyama-itchome Station. Its theme is “CREATOR'S VILLAGE”. It has 22 rooms from 7.39 m² to 33.74 m², a lounge, an open space and three meeting rooms, and it accepts room members only. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

CREATOR'S VILLAGE

SENQ Aoyama's theme is “CREATOR'S VILLAGE” — a village of creators in Aoyama.

With 22 rooms and recruitment of room members only, it works like a village where room holders share the lounge and open space.

01 · Lounge and open space

Shared by room members

The lounge and open space are shared by the room members.

You can move between time shut away in your room and time meeting people in the shared areas.

02 · Rooms

22 rooms from 7.39 m² to 33.74 m²

There are 22 rooms ranging from 7.39 m² to 33.74 m², from ¥183,920 a month with utilities included in the rent.

Choose anything from a room for one to a room for a small team.

03 · Meeting rooms

Three meeting rooms

There are three meeting rooms.

Reception is open 9:00–18:00 on weekdays. It is a one-minute walk from exit 4 of Aoyama-itchome Station.

What is room membership?

A membership where you rent a private room. SENQ Aoyama accepts room members only.

With no seat-only members, the shared spaces are used only by people with rooms — so the faces stay familiar, like a village.
