Sannomiya, Kobe, Hyogo

BIZcomfort Kobe Sannomiya — coworking with window seats, 5 minutes from Sannomiya

Five minutes' walk from the West Exit of Sannomiya Station on the Kobe Municipal Subway, BIZcomfort Kobe Sannomiya is on the 8th and 9th floors of the Kenso Building on Shimoyamate-dori in Chuo Ward. Members can use it 24 hours a day, every day, and on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 you can drop in without registering. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Beside Kobe's biggest station

BIZcomfort describes the location as next to Sannomiya, Kobe's largest terminal, with several train lines nearby.

There is a concierge, you can drop in, and there are meeting rooms with monitors.

What is the Kansai plan?

A monthly plan for using BIZcomfort coworking spaces across the Kansai region, including Osaka, Kyoto and Hyogo.
