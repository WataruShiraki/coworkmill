Nishi-Funabashi, Chiba

WORK STYLING SOLO Nishi-Funabashi — single rooms at a transfer station, one minute from Nishi-Funabashi Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Nishi-Funabashi is on the 4th floor of NST Building No. 2, a one-minute walk from Nishi-Funabashi Station on the JR, Tokyo Metro and Toyo Rapid lines. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Single rooms near the station

A one-minute walk from Nishi-Funabashi Station on the JR, Tokyo Metro and Toyo Rapid lines.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.
