Hakata, Fukuoka

Zero-Ten Park Canal City Hakata-mae — 900 tsubo of coworking by Kushida-jinja-mae

Zero-Ten Park (formerly The Company) Canal City Hakata-mae is on the ground and 2nd floors of the Daiichi Prince Building in Gion-machi, Hakata — right outside Kushida-jinja-mae Station on the Nanakuma Line and a 10-minute walk from Hakata Station. With about 900 tsubo of floor space, it has more seats than any other location of the brand. Drop-in needs no booking: ¥660 an hour or ¥2,200 a day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A space that accelerates creativity

This is the first Zero-Ten Park (formerly The Company) in Japan, opened as one of the largest shared offices and coworking spaces in western Japan. With about 900 tsubo of floor space, it has the most office floors and coworking seats of all its locations.

The interior concept is “a space that accelerates creativity”, with care taken over the art and furniture. It also has the most members of any location, and the operator says networking and business matching among members are lively.

01 · Coworking space

No booking, ¥2,200 a day

Drop-in needs no advance booking — register at reception on the day and start straight away.

It costs ¥660 an hour; three hours or more is capped at the ¥2,200 day rate. An All Day Pass (¥3,300) lets you stay until 9:00 the next morning.

02 · Meeting room and offices

A meeting room and semi-private offices

An 8-person meeting room has a monitor and a high-quality camera. Members pay ¥550 an hour.

There are also semi-private offices for 1–4 people, open to the ceiling.

03 · Reception and drinks

Reception staff and a drinks corner

Reception staff handle guests and receive post.

There are free coffee and tea and an office snack shop. Phone booths are free, even for drop-in users.

04 · Entrance

Right outside Kushida-jinja-mae Station

Kushida-jinja-mae Station on the Nanakuma Line is right in front, one stop from both Hakata and Tenjin.

What is drop-in?

Using a coworking space for just a day or a few hours, without becoming a member.

Zero-Ten Park's drop-in needs no booking and is cashless only (credit card, transit IC card and other e-money). First-time users show photo ID and register.
