Ichigaya, Tokyo

H¹T Ichigaya — a shared office with three meeting rooms, one minute from Ichigaya Station on four lines

H¹T Ichigaya is on the 3rd floor of the MG Ichigaya Building, a one-minute walk from Ichigaya Station, served by four lines: JR, the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho and Namboku lines and the Toei Shinjuku Line. Along with 14 open-space seats and 12 single rooms, it has meeting rooms for 2, 6 and 8. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Introduction

Three meeting rooms, one minute from a four-line station

H¹T Ichigaya has 14 open-space seats, 12 single rooms and three meeting rooms for 2, 6 and 8 — from focused solo seats to places for meetings, chosen by purpose.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.
