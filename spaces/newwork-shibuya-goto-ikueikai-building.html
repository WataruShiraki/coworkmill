Shibuya, Tokyo

NewWork Shibuya Goto Ikueikai Building The Flagship — 93 seats, 6 premium meeting rooms and massage chairs

NewWork Shibuya Goto Ikueikai Building The Flagship is on the 7th floor of the Goto Ikueikai Building on Dogenzaka, a five-minute walk from Shibuya Station. Among NewWork — the corporate-membership satellite shared offices run by Tokyu Corporation — it is the one named “The Flagship”. It has 93 seats, 3 standard and 6 premium meeting rooms, 28 enclosed seated booths, and even massage chairs and a stretching area. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The Flagship

NewWork is a satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation on a corporate-membership basis. Its aims include supporting new ways of working, promoting work-life balance, raising productivity and reducing commuting stress, the website says.

This site carries the name “The Flagship”. Its 93 seats and premium meeting rooms for 10 to 16 people show flagship scale.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 93-seat workspace

There are 93 seats of two types, open and booth.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Enclosed seated booths

Wide booths for four and booths for one

There are four wide enclosed booths for 4 people and 24 for one.

Focused solo time and small-group talks can be separated on the same floor.

What is a satellite shared office?

A small workplace away from head office (a satellite office) shared by multiple companies.

With NewWork, the company becomes a corporate member and its staff use sites in various locations.
