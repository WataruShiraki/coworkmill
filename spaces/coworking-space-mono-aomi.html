Aomi, Tokyo

Coworking Space MONO — a makers' coworking space directly linked to Telecom Center Station

Coworking Space MONO is on the 14th floor of the Telecom Center Building East Wing, directly linked to Telecom Center Station. It is a coworking space for making things, where engineers, designers and start-ups gather, with a workshop equipped with a laser cutter, 3D printers and a CNC machine. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An open-innovation platform for making things

MONO is presented as an “open innovation platform” where engineers, designers and start-ups gather.

With a workshop right next to where you work, you can build prototypes as you go. It also supports business development and hosts events for anywhere from a few people to about 300.

01 · Working space

Working on the 14th floor

The working space has power, Wi-Fi and free drinks. Drop-in is available in two-hour or full-day units during opening hours, 10:00–18:00 on weekdays.

The monthly coworking plan for weekdays is ¥6,000 (excl. tax).

02 · Workshop

A laser cutter and 3D printers

The workshop has a laser cutter, 3D printers and a CNC machine. A news post also announced the addition of a Bambu Lab H2S 3D printer.

03 · Meeting rooms and tenant plans

Meeting rooms and private booths

As well as meeting rooms, there is a nap room and a shower room. Tenant plans include private booths, private desks, free desks and private offices.

Tenants can use the facility 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and rent meeting rooms for ¥700 per hour (excl. tax).

What is a makerspace?

A place for making things, where machine tools such as 3D printers and laser cutters are shared.

MONO combines a coworking space with a workshop, bringing the places for working and prototyping together.
