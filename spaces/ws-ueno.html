Ueno, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Ueno — an open space you can use without booking, one minute from JR Ueno Station

WORK STYLING Ueno is on the 2nd floor of the TT Ueno Ekimae Building, a one-minute walk from JR Ueno Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A complete workplace on the 2nd floor, a minute from the station

WORK STYLING Ueno is on the 2nd floor of a building one minute from JR Ueno Station and five from the Tokyo Metro station. Places to work are gathered just above the ticket gates.

The open space here can be used without booking, so you can drop in and sit down straight away — or book a meeting room or private room in advance.

01 · Entrance

Through reception to the 2nd-floor workspace

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

Being so close to the station, it is easy to drop in on the way somewhere.

02 · Open space

Shared seats, no booking needed

The open space can be used without booking.

Seats of different shapes suit both solo work and short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 2 to 12

There are several meeting rooms, so you can choose by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms are there for when you need to focus.

With a desk, chair and power, you can close the door and work.

What is drop-in?

Using a space on the day without a membership contract, paying by the hour.

WORK STYLING has a pay-as-you-go plan in 10-minute units, and the open space at this site can be used without booking.
