Hamamatsucho, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Hamamatsucho — a shared office on the 3rd floor of ORIX Hamamatsucho Building, 2 minutes from Hamamatsucho and Daimon

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Hamamatsucho is on the 3rd floor of the ORIX Hamamatsucho Building, a two-minute walk from both Hamamatsucho Station (JR lines and Tokyo Monorail) and Daimon Station (Toei Asakusa and Oedo lines). It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Two minutes from two stations

WORK STYLING Hamamatsucho is a two-minute walk from both Hamamatsucho and Daimon stations — four lines in all: JR, Tokyo Monorail, Toei Asakusa and Toei Oedo.

Register it as your home site and you can use the open space without booking. Single-person rooms and meeting rooms are booked in 10-minute units.

01 · Entrance and open space

Shared space you can use without booking

Through the 3rd-floor entrance, the open space spreads out.

The open space at your home site needs no booking, and there is a drinks corner and equipment to borrow.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people and can be booked in 10-minute units.

Choose a room to suit the size of your meeting.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for calls and focus

Single-person rooms are for online meetings and focused work.

There are also phone booths you can use without booking.

What is a home site?

At WORK STYLING, the site a member registers as their usual base. The website says: “You can use the open space at your home site without booking.”

Make Hamamatsucho your home site and you have a seat two minutes from the station, no booking needed.
