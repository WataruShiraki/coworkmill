Higashi-Nihonbashi, Tokyo

THE HUB Higashi-Nihonbashi — a rental office of private rooms for 1–4, on floors 5–7 of the Sanko Nihonbashi Plaza Building

THE HUB Higashi-Nihonbashi is on the 5th to 7th floors of the Sanko Nihonbashi Plaza Building, a three-minute walk from Higashi-Nihonbashi Station on the Toei Asakusa Line. It has private offices for 1–4 people, a meeting space, a lounge and dedicated mailboxes. Four stations — Higashi-Nihonbashi, Bakuro-yokoyama, Bakurocho and Ningyocho — are within reach. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Private offices for small teams

The website advertises “private offices from the ¥30,000s” and describes it as a base for founders, licensed professionals and small companies.

Rooms for 1 to 4 people line the 5th to 7th floors, all furnished and with internet. Access to several lines is another feature.

01 · Meeting space and lounge

A place for short meetings

The meeting space can be used for up to 30 minutes at a time, and there is a lounge.

It is three minutes from Higashi-Nihonbashi and Bakuro-yokoyama stations.

02 · Private offices on floors 5–7

Rooms for 1–4 people

Private rooms for 1 to 4 people are on the 5th, 6th and 7th floors, all furnished and with internet.

Availability changes over time, so check the official website.

03 · 7th-floor rooms and mailboxes

An office that can receive mail

There are private rooms on the 7th floor too.

Tenants get a dedicated mailbox, so the office can receive business mail.

What is the meeting space?

A place for short meetings. At THE HUB Higashi-Nihonbashi it can be used for up to 30 minutes at a time.

Limiting the time lets tenants share a meeting place even in a small site.
