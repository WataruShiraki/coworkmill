Shinjuku, Tokyo

CROSSCOOP Shinjuku SOUTH — a rental office with 15 meeting rooms on the 3rd, 5th and 6th floors of the Hulic Shinjuku 4-chome Building

CROSSCOOP Shinjuku SOUTH is on the 3rd, 5th and 6th floors of the Hulic Shinjuku 4-chome Building (formerly FORECAST Shinjuku SOUTH), a one-minute walk from Shinjuku-sanchome Station on the Tokyo Metro and four from the South Exit of Shinjuku Station. Billed as “a rental office for flexible working with Shinjuku as your base”, it has high-grade private rooms where you can register your company and 15 meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Flexible working in Shinjuku

The website describes it as “a rental office for flexible working with Shinjuku as your base”, with high-grade private rooms that allow company registration, 15 meeting rooms and a staffed reception.

It uses three floors — the 3rd, 5th and 6th — with private rooms from 3 to 57 people. Shared facilities are open 24 hours.

01 · 5th-floor entrance and lounges

Staffed reception and shared spaces

The office entrance on the 5th floor has a reception desk staffed 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.

Lounges are on the 3rd and 6th floors.

02 · Meeting rooms and web booths

15 meeting rooms

There are 15 meeting rooms across the three floors, for 4 to 10 people.

TELECUBE web-meeting booths are on the 3rd and 6th floors.

03 · Private rooms

From 3 to 57 people

Private rooms range from 3 to 57 people. A room for 5 is ¥425,000 a month and a room for 10 ¥850,000 (excluding tax).

Some rooms have windows, and larger rooms come with their own phone booths.

04 · Seminar room

A room for 54

The building has a seminar room for 54.

Training sessions and briefings can be held in the same building.

What is a TELECUBE?

A box-shaped private booth for online meetings and calls. CROSSCOOP Shinjuku SOUTH has TELECUBEs on its 3rd and 6th floors.

You can focus on web meetings without worrying about noise from the lounge or offices.
