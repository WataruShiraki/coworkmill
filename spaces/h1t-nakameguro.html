Nakameguro, Tokyo

H¹T Nakameguro — a corporate shared office one minute from the station, on the 7th floor of the Asahi Denki Asahi Life Nakameguro Building

H¹T Nakameguro, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 7th floor of the Asahi Denki Asahi Life Nakameguro Building, a one-minute walk from Nakameguro Station on the Tokyu Toyoko Line and Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line. It has an open space for 15, 18 single-person rooms, four single-person boxes and one meeting room each for 6 and 8. It is open 8:30–20:00 every day. Introduction

A station-front site close to where people live

H¹T Nakameguro is one minute from Nakameguro Station, where the Toyoko and Hibiya lines meet. It has 18 single-person rooms and a roomy open space for 15.

There are two meeting rooms, for 6 and 8. People who work for companies in central Tokyo can also use it as a place to work near home.

01 · Open space

Fifteen shared seats

The open space seats 15.

One minute from Nakameguro Station, you can stop by before heading out or on the way home.

02 · Boxes and rooms

22 single-person seats

There are four single-person boxes and 18 single-person rooms.

Take online meetings in a room and short tasks in a box.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 6 and 8

There is one meeting room each for 6 and 8.

They can be used for small meetings and interviews.

What does “working near home” mean?

The idea of bringing where you live and where you work closer together (shokuju kinsetsu in Japanese).

H¹T places sites not only in central Tokyo but also near residential stations such as Nakameguro.
