Seijogakuen-mae, Tokyo

NewWork Seijogakuen-mae — a corporate-membership satellite office on the 4th floor of the SMBC Seijo Building, one minute from Seijogakuen-mae Station

NewWork Seijogakuen-mae is on the 4th floor of the SMBC Seijo Building, a one-minute walk from the South Exit of Seijogakuen-mae Station on the Odakyu Line. It has 28 booth seats, meeting rooms for 6 and 4, and seven single private booths. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A station-front site with two meeting rooms of different sizes

One minute from the South Exit of Seijogakuen-mae Station, this site has 28 seats.

The two meeting rooms, for 6 and 4, let you choose by headcount, and there are also seven single private booths.

01 · Booth seats

A 28-seat workspace

There are 28 booth-style seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Seven single booths

There are seven single private booths.

They are handy on days full of online meetings or calls.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for 6 and 4

There is one meeting room each for 6 and 4.

Use the room for 4 for small meetings and the room for 6 for slightly larger ones.

What is a seated private booth?

A small private room with a door, where you sit to work. It is used for calls and online meetings.

NewWork Seijogakuen-mae has seven single seated private booths.
