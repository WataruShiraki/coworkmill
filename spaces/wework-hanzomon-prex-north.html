Hanzomon, Tokyo

WeWork Hanzomon PREX North — an elegant space inspired by a “gate”, one minute from Hanzomon Station

WeWork Hanzomon PREX North is a one-minute walk from Hanzomon subway station, in the business heart of Chiyoda City. Kojimachi Station is four minutes away and Nagatacho Station is also within walking distance, giving access to the Hanzomon, Yurakucho and Namboku lines. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A space inspired by a “gate”

The official website describes an elegant interior combining metal and wood, inspired by the “mon” (gate) in the name Hanzomon — design drawn from the place itself to spark creativity.

A rooftop garden offers somewhere to recharge and meet people between tasks. The building has floors from the 1st to the 13th, and whole-floor use by a single company and customization are also available.

01 · Workspace

Meeting rooms and phone booths

There are eight meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

The ceiling height is 2,700 mm.

02 · Private floor

A whole floor for one company

The official website shows a furnished private floor on the 12th floor with its own pantry.

The private office on the 1st floor has direct access from outside the building, so it is described as suitable for uses such as a showroom.

What is a rooftop garden?

A garden planted on a building's roof where people can spend time.

Here it is presented as a place to recharge and meet people between tasks.
