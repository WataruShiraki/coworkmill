MIDORI.so Nakameguro — a shared office in two buildings, restored from an ivy-covered ruin

MIDORI.so Nakameguro is in Aobadai, Nakameguro. Its operator, Mirai Institute, was founded on 5 January 2011 and runs eight sites of its own in Tokyo. Nakameguro is “a shared office converted from an abandoned, ivy-covered house”, made up of two buildings: the main house and the old residence. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

When you're enjoying yourself, ideas come naturally

MIDORI.so is a coworking community exploring “the future of work in a society that values the individual”. Freelancers, small companies and employees of large organisations — of different professions, nationalities and hobbies — work side by side. Its motto: “When you're enjoying yourself, ideas come naturally.”

The Nakameguro site was born from the renovation of an abandoned, ivy-covered house. The third floor of the main house holds the workspace, lounge, meeting room and gallery; the old residence holds private offices. It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with a kitchen, lockers and free drinks.

01 · Main house

Workspace, lounge, meeting room and gallery

The main house uses its second and third floors. The workspace, lounge, meeting room and gallery are gathered on the third.

Because the old building's frame was kept, it has textures and shadows that new buildings lack.

02 · Old residence

The private-office building

The old residence has private offices on its first and second floors. Members move across the site between the shared spaces of the main house and the closed rooms of the residence.

With two buildings, switching between focused time and time with others is as simple as walking from one to the other.

03 · Plans

Free desk, fixed desk and pop-in

A free desk is ¥42,350 a month and a fixed desk ¥60,500 a month, both with a ¥11,000 sign-up fee. Pop-in costs ¥3,300 a day, or ¥33,000 for an 11-day pack.

It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with a kitchen, lockers and free drinks.

What is pop-in?

MIDORI.so's day-use option: ¥3,300 a day, or ¥33,000 for an 11-day pack.

It is a way to feel the place before signing a monthly contract. The 11-day pack also suits people who come only two or three days a week.
