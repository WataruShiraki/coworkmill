Fujisawa, Kanagawa

STAYUP Shonan Fujisawa — a designer space with greenery, five minutes from Fujisawa Station

STAYUP Shonan Fujisawa, a coworking space and shared office, is on the 6th floor of the Mega SanS Building in Kugenuma-Ishigami 1-chome, about a five-minute walk from the South Exit of Fujisawa Station on the JR and Odakyu lines. A designer space with greenery, its floor is described on the official website as one of the largest in Fujisawa. Web meetings are possible at every seat, and there are soundproof booths, meeting rooms and a café space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A Shonan designer space with greenery

STAYUP Shonan Fujisawa is a designer coworking space with greenery. Web meetings are possible at every seat, with fast Wi-Fi.

STAYUP has sites in Yokohama, Shonan Fujisawa, Saitama Omiya and Hatchobori. Shonan Fujisawa offers a monthly plan that includes use of the Yokohama site.

01 · Open space

Roomy seats where you can hold web meetings

Web meetings are possible at every seat, making it well suited to people working alone. Seats are generously spaced, with plenty of high-performance chairs.

Window seats have monitors and monitor arms.

02 · Soundproof booths

Booths where you can talk freely

There are soundproof booths for people who want better sound isolation or need to focus. Standing desks are also available.

03 · Café and meeting rooms

About 30 drinks and rooms for up to 8

The café space has about 30 kinds of free drinks, plus sofa seats screened from view.

Meeting rooms cost ¥1,650 an hour for 6 people and ¥2,200 an hour for 8. They are reservation-only, and monthly members get ¥550 an hour off.

What is a soundproof booth?

A small enclosed room that keeps your voice in and outside noise out, used for calls and online meetings.
