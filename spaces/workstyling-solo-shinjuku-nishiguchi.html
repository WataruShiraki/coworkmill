Shinjuku, Tokyo

WORK STYLING SOLO Shinjuku West Exit B1F — single rooms close to the gates, on the basement floor of Shinjuku L Tower

WORK STYLING SOLO Shinjuku West Exit B1F is on the basement floor of Shinjuku L Tower, zero minutes on foot from gate A17 of Shinjuku Station on the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line and two minutes from the West Exit (towards exit 20) gate of JR Shinjuku Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Right by the ticket gates

It is zero minutes on foot from gate A17 of the Marunouchi Line's Shinjuku Station, and two minutes from the West Exit (towards A18) gate of the Odakyu and Keio lines.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also a drinks and café space.

Private rooms for one line the floor.
