Tamachi, Tokyo

co-ba Re-SOHKO Tamachi — a bay-area skeleton space with a rooftop meeting area, on the 8th floor of Daisan Toun Building in Shibaura

co-ba Re-SOHKO Tamachi is on the 8th floor of the Daisan Toun Building in Shibaura, a seven-minute walk from Tamachi Station on the JR Yamanote Line. It turns a bright bay-area skeleton space into customisable private rooms and an open coworking area, promising “an ideal work environment for startups and growing teams”. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A skeleton space in the bay area

The website presents it as “an ideal work environment for startups and growing teams”.

A bright bay-area skeleton space is laid out as customisable private-room plans and an open coworking area. It is run by e-Sohko.com Co., Ltd.

01 · Coworking and shared lounge

An open shared space

Free-address membership starts at ¥13,200 a month, and there is also a lounge membership.

Drop-in is ¥2,750 a day.

02 · Private rooms

Rooms you can customise

Private rooms are plans that let you customise the skeleton space.

You can shape the room to your team's size and work.

What is a skeleton space?

A space with ceiling and wall finishes stripped away, leaving the building's structure exposed. co-ba Re-SOHKO Tamachi is built from such a space in the bay area.

Ceilings feel higher, and it is easy for users to make changes themselves.
