Nihonbashi, Tokyo

X-NIHONBASHI TOWER — Mitsui Fudosan's hub for the space business, on the 7th floor of Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower

X-NIHONBASHI TOWER is on the 7th floor of Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower, directly connected to Mitsukoshimae Station. It is one of the sites of X-NIHONBASHI (pronounced “Cross Nihonbashi”), Mitsui Fudosan's hub for collaboration in the space business, with coworking, conference and meeting space in 307 m². To use it, you need to join the general incorporated association Cross U. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Towards new innovation, in space and on Earth

X-NIHONBASHI is a stage where people gather and connect to grow the space business and create new innovation in space and on the ground. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it has two facilities: TOWER in Nihonbashi Mitsui Tower and BASE in the Nihonbashi IT Building.

TOWER uses a single 307 m² room for coworking, conferences and meetings: about 120 seats theatre-style, about 40 as coworking, or about 150 standing. The same room changes shape to fit the use.

01 · Coworking

About 40 seats, from 8:00 on weekdays

As a coworking space it has about 40 seats, open 8:00–19:00 on weekdays.

Membership of Cross U is required — a focused place where people in the space business gather.

02 · Conferences

About 120 theatre-style, about 150 standing

For conferences it seats about 120 theatre-style or holds about 150 standing.

Space-business presentations and networking events take place in the same room as the coworking — a place to work and a place to present, in one.

03 · The other site, BASE

Floors 3–4 of the Nihonbashi IT Building

X-NIHONBASHI BASE is on the 3rd and 4th floors of the Nihonbashi IT Building, a one-minute walk from Mitsukoshimae Station. It has coworking, a conference room, a bar lounge, meeting rooms, an exhibition area, web-meeting booths and solo work booths.

Members can use TOWER and BASE as two bases within Nihonbashi.

What is Cross U?

The general incorporated association you must join to use the X-NIHONBASHI facilities — a body bringing together companies and people involved in the space business.

Limiting use to members means the person at the next seat works in the same field. That is the strength of a coworking space with a clear focus.
