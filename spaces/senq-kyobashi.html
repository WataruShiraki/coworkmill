Kyobashi, Tokyo

SENQ Kyobashi — a “FOOD INNOVATION” workplace on floors 3–4 of Kyobashi Edogrand, directly connected to Kyobashi Station

SENQ Kyobashi is on the 3rd and 4th floors of Kyobashi Edogrand, directly connected to Kyobashi Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line. Its theme is “FOOD INNOVATION”. Around a lounge of about 60 seats are private rooms, booths and meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

FOOD INNOVATION

SENQ Kyobashi's theme is “FOOD INNOVATION”. Each SENQ site has its own theme, and Kyobashi focuses on food.

It is on the 3rd and 4th floors of Kyobashi Edogrand, directly connected to exits 7 and 8 of Kyobashi Station, with private rooms, booths and meeting rooms around a lounge of about 60 seats.

01 · Lounge

A shared space of about 60 seats

The lounge has about 60 seats. Coworking members can use it 9:00–22:00 on weekdays.

Coworking is ¥22,000 a month, and drop-in ¥550 an hour.

02 · Rooms and booths

Rooms for teams and seats for one

Rooms start at ¥653,400 a month and booths at ¥163,350.

Closed rooms and semi-private seats share the floor with the lounge.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for meetings

There are meeting rooms for discussions.

Directly connected to Kyobashi Station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

What is FOOD INNOVATION?

Creating new value in the field of food. SENQ Kyobashi takes it as the theme of the site.

When each site has a field as its theme, the conversations among the people who gather tend to line up.
