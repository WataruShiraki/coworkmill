Akasaka, Tokyo

H¹T Akasaka-mitsuke — a corporate shared office on the 7th floor of No.R Akasaka-mitsuke, one minute from the station and open Sundays and holidays

H¹T Akasaka-mitsuke, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 7th floor of No.R Akasaka-mitsuke, a one-minute walk from Exit 10 of Akasaka-mitsuke Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Marunouchi lines. H¹T is a shared office for companies with no initial or base fee. This site is open 7:30–20:30 on weekdays and Saturdays, and 7:30–17:30 on Sundays and holidays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

In Akasaka, convenient and well known

The official website presents this site as a satellite shared office in the Akasaka area that “combines convenience and name recognition”.

It has 12 open-space seats, five single-person boxes, eight single-person rooms and three meeting rooms, and stays open until 17:30 on Sundays and holidays.

01 · Open space

Twelve shared seats

The open space seats 12.

One minute from the station exit, it is easy to drop in between appointments.

02 · Boxes and rooms

13 single-person spots

There are five single-person boxes and eight single-person rooms.

A box suits times when you want some screening from view; a room suits calls and online meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for 2 and one for 6

The meeting rooms are two for 2 and one for 6.

They work for meetings with clients in Akasaka and small-group discussions.

What is an H¹T “box”?

One of H¹T's seat types: a single-person seat with surrounding panels. It sits between a “room”, a private room with a door, and the “open space”.

H¹T Akasaka-mitsuke has five single-person boxes.
