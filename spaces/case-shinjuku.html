Takadanobaba, Tokyo

CASE Shinjuku — a shared office connecting creators, engineers and entrepreneurs, one minute from Takadanobaba Station

CASE Shinjuku is on the 4th floor of the Sankei Building, a one-minute walk from Takadanobaba Station. Under the motto “broaden, deepen and change your world in an office with loose connections”, it creates a place where creators, engineers, entrepreneurs and business owners connect. There is coworking with an open interior, a members-only shared office, private rooms of about 10 m² and an event space, and you can also consult certified SME advisers on financing and more. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An office with loose connections

The official website carries the motto “broaden, deepen and change your world in an office with loose connections”.

It positions itself as “a place where creators, engineers, entrepreneurs and business owners connect”, aiming for an environment where people in different lines of work naturally get to know each other.

01 · Coworking space

An open space that also hosts study sessions

The coworking space has an open, airy interior.

Study sessions and events can be held here too.

02 · Shared office

From booths to teams

The members-only shared office can be used as individual booths or shared by a small team.

The shared office is available 24 hours a day.

03 · Private office

A private room of about 10 m²

There are self-contained private offices of about 10 m².

Company registration (address use) is also available.

What is a shared office?

A workplace where several companies or individuals share one office.

CASE Shinjuku's shared office is for members only and can be used as individual booths or shared by a team.
