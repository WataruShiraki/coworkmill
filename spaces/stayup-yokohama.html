Yokohama, Kanagawa

STAYUP Yokohama — a designer office overlooking Minato Mirai, six minutes from Yokohama Station

STAYUP Yokohama, a coworking space and shared office, is on the 14th floor of Yokohama Creation Square in Sakaecho, Kanagawa Ward, about a six-minute walk from the Kita-Higashi (north-east) Exit of Yokohama Station. On a floor with windows looking out to Minato Mirai and the Bay Bridge are an open space, focus booths, meeting rooms and private rooms. Staff are on hand during the day on weekdays and weekends; at other times it runs unstaffed. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Stylish × Urban × BusinessLife

STAYUP Yokohama is a designer-office coworking space under the banner “Stylish × Urban × BusinessLife”. Besides Yokohama, STAYUP has sites in Shonan Fujisawa, Saitama Omiya and Hatchobori.

The official website describes it as a “service office” whose mission is to accelerate members' businesses and create synergy between them, and says that in five years since the Yokohama site opened, 26 business connections have been made.

01 · Open space

High-performance chairs and a bay view

The open space is furnished with high-performance chairs, and electric sit-stand desks let you work standing up.

From the 14th-floor windows you can see Minato Mirai and the Bay Bridge. Drop-in is ¥880 an hour or ¥2,750 a day.

02 · Focus rooms and private rooms

Focus rooms for online meetings, and dedicated offices

There are focus rooms for online meetings and private booths.

Semi-private personal booths start at ¥66,000 a month, and dedicated private offices at ¥132,000 a month.

03 · Sofa seats and services

Sofa seats for breaks and lunch, and about 30 drinks

Sofa seats screened from view are used for breaks and lunch. There are about 30 kinds of free drinks.

A smart lock lets you enter with Suica or face recognition, and monthly members can print up to 1,000 pages a month free.

04 · Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms with monitors

The meeting room for 4 (40-inch monitor) is ¥2,200 an hour; the room for 6 (60-inch monitor and whiteboard) is ¥2,750 an hour. Monthly members get ¥550 an hour off.

There is also an event space for seminars of up to 40 people.

What is a service office?

An office with furniture, internet, reception and other services in place, so you can start working as soon as you sign up.

STAYUP describes itself as a service office that aims to create synergy between members.
