Ogikubo, Tokyo

NewWork Ogikubo — a small corporate-membership satellite office on the 7th floor of the Daiwa Ogikubo Building, one minute from Ogikubo Station

NewWork Ogikubo is on the 7th floor of the Daiwa Ogikubo Building, a one-minute walk from Ogikubo Station. It is a small site with 13 booth seats, a meeting room for 6, a wide private booth for 4 and four single private booths, in front of a station close to Suginami's residential neighbourhoods. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A small site for working near home

A small 13-seat site built around booth seats and private booths, it lets you get work done at the station without travelling to head office in central Tokyo.

The official website notes that after 20:00 you leave by the side exit on the opposite side, not the main entrance. Booths have locks and monitors.

01 · Booth seats

A 13-seat workspace

There are 13 booth-style seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

A wide booth for 4 and single booths

There is one wide private booth for 4 and four single private booths.

Online meetings that are hard to hold calmly at home can be done here with focus.

03 · Meeting room

One room for 6

There is one meeting room for 6.

It is handy for short meetings with colleagues who live nearby.

What does “working near home” mean?

Having your home and workplace close together (shokuju kinsetsu in Japanese) — cutting commuting time to give more room to both life and work.

The official website describes NewWork as a satellite shared office aiming to reduce commuting stress.
