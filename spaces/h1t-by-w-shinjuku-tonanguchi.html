Shinjuku, Tokyo

H¹T by W Shinjuku Tonanguchi — a corporate shared office built around single seats, on the 4th floor of the Ichijima Building

H¹T by W Shinjuku Tonanguchi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 4th floor of the Ichijima Building, a two-minute walk from the Southeast Exit of JR Shinjuku Station and five minutes from the South Exit of Odakyu Shinjuku Station. With 12 single-person rooms and 6 single-person boxes, almost every seat is for one person, and it is open 7:00–22:00 on weekends and holidays, just as on weekdays. Introduction

A Shinjuku site focused on seats for working alone

H¹T by W Shinjuku Tonanguchi narrows its seating to solo use: 12 single-person rooms, 6 single-person boxes and an open space for 5. No meeting rooms appear in the official seat list.

The official website describes it as a corporate shared office with no initial or base fee, usable for remote work or as a base for sales staff on the move.

01 · Open space

Five shared seats

The open space seats 5.
