Nihonbashi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building — a shared office and private rooms on the 9th floor, directly connected to Nihombashi Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building is on the 9th floor of the Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building, directly connected to Nihombashi Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Tozai lines and the Toei Asakusa Line. It has an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, and supports company registration. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Nine floors above Nihombashi Station

WORK STYLING Nihonbashi Takashimaya Mitsui Building is on the 9th floor of a building directly connected to Nihombashi Station on three lines, and a five-minute walk from JR Tokyo Station.

The website highlights its direct station access and the option to register a company here. Companies on a dedicated-space plan can register this as their head-office address.

01 · Open space

Seats you can take without booking

The open space can be used without booking.

There is a drinks corner, and chargers and stationery to borrow.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Directly connected to Nihombashi Station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

03 · Single-person rooms

Rooms for calls and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls and online meetings.

Separate phone booths are also available.

What is company registration?

Registering details such as the location of a company's head office with the Legal Affairs Bureau. Some shared offices allow it, some do not.

At WORK STYLING, companies on the dedicated-space plan (FLEX) or the single-site unlimited plan (BASE) can register.
