Where are coworking spaces linked directly to a station?

There is LIFORK Otemachi, linked to Otemachi Station, and WeWork and SHIBUYA QWS in Shibuya Scramble Square, linked to Shibuya Station. LIFORK Otemachi connects directly to Exits C8, C11 and C12 of Otemachi Station, and is five minutes on foot from JR Tokyo Station.

LIFORK Otemachi →

Linked to Exits C8, C11 and C12 of Otemachi Station

LIFORK Otemachi is on the 1st and 2nd floors of Otemachi First Square West Tower, linked via Exits C8, C11 and C12 to Otemachi Station on the Tozai, Marunouchi, Chiyoda, Hanzomon and Toei Mita lines.

It is also five minutes on foot from the Marunouchi North Exit of JR Tokyo Station — a base focused on an environment for concentrated work.

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square →

High floors linked to Shibuya Station, with five railways

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square occupies floors 37–42 and 45 of the East Tower of Shibuya Scramble Square, linked directly to Shibuya Station.

The station is served by the JR Yamanote Line, Tokyu Toyoko and Den-en-toshi lines, Tokyo Metro Hanzomon and Fukutoshin lines, and Keio Inokashira Line.

SHIBUYA QWS →

The 15th floor of the same Shibuya Scramble Square

SHIBUYA QWS is on the 15th floor of the East Tower of Shibuya Scramble Square. Its floor of about 2,600 m² holds PROJECT BASE and the 200-person SCRAMBLE HALL.

Hours are 9:00–22:00 (last entry 21:30). Above the station, one building holds two coworking spaces: WeWork and QWS.

Business-Airport Aoyama →

Not linked, but three minutes from Gaienmae

Business Airport Aoyama is a three-minute walk from Exit 1a of Gaienmae Station on the Ginza Line, on the 4th and 6th floors of the Spline Aoyama Tokyu Building.

The coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays, so you can spend the hour before work in Aoyama.

What does “linked directly to the station” mean?

You can walk from the station gates or underground passages into the building without going outside — a real difference on rainy days or with luggage.

Many spaces that are not linked are still only 3–5 minutes from the station. Weigh how often you will go and what the walk from the station is like.
