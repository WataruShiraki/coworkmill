Which coworking spaces are on high floors with a view?

Some occupy the upper floors of skyscrapers. WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square is on floors 37–42 and 45, WORK STYLING Kasumigaseki Building on the 36th, and Business Airport Shinagawa on the 28th.

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square →

Floors 37–42 and 45, linked to Shibuya Station

WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square uses floors 37–42 and 45 of a 47-storey tower.

There are 70 meeting rooms, with ceilings of 3,000–3,200 mm.

WORK STYLING Kasumigaseki Building →

The 36th floor of the Kasumigaseki Building

WORK STYLING Kasumigaseki Building is on the 36th floor.

It is two minutes on foot from Toranomon Station on the Ginza Line.

Business-Airport Shinagawa →

The 28th floor of the Taiyo Life Shinagawa Building

Business Airport Shinagawa is on the 28th floor of the Taiyo Life Shinagawa Building.

It has rooms for 1–6, desks, coworking and three meeting rooms.
