Which coworking spaces have themed interiors?

Some bring local history or a story into their interiors. WORK STYLING's Hibiya site is “ZEN MODERN” and its Shinjuku Mitsui Building site “RETRO FUTURE”; Business Airport uses a theatre motif in Hibiya and the railway in Shimbashi.

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya →

“ZEN MODERN” with traditional Japanese patterns

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown Hibiya is on the 12th floor of Hibiya Mitsui Tower.

Its design theme is “ZEN MODERN”, decorated with traditional Japanese patterns.

WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building →

“RETRO FUTURE”, spanning past and future

WORK STYLING Shinjuku Mitsui Building is on the 11th floor. Its design theme is “RETRO FUTURE”.

FLEX members have 24-hour access.

Business-Airport Hibiya →

A theatre motif, surrounded by greenery and art

Business Airport Hibiya is on the 9th floor of the Toho Hibiya Building, a workplace with a theatre motif.

The coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays.

Business-Airport Shimbashi →

Motifs recalling the birthplace of Japan's railways

Business Airport Shimbashi is on floors 6–8 of Shimbashi Place, full of motifs recalling the birthplace of Japan's railways.

There are four meeting rooms.
