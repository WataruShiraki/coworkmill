Tachikawa, Tokyo

H¹T Tachikawa — a shared office in the Tama area with a full range of seats, 2 minutes from Tachikawa Station's South Exit

H¹T Tachikawa is on the 6th floor of ALEALEA 2, a two-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Tachikawa Station. With 10 open-space seats, 4 single booths, 5 boxes for 1–4 people, 15 single rooms and 2 meeting rooms, it offers many kinds of seats. H¹T is Nomura Real Estate's satellite shared office for companies. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Many seat types, 2 minutes from Tachikawa Station

H¹T Tachikawa has open seats, booths, boxes for 1–4, single rooms and meeting rooms for 4 and 6 — from focused solo seats to places for meetings, chosen by purpose.

H¹T is a satellite shared office for companies, usable for remote work, as a base while out on business and for meetings, the website says.

What is a box?

One of H¹T's seat types: a semi-private seat enclosed by walls.

H¹T Tachikawa has boxes for 1, 2 and 4 people.
