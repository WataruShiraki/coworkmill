Roppongi, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Roppongi Hills North Tower — floors 16–17 with shoji-style meeting rooms, directly connected to Roppongi Station

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 16th and 17th floors of Roppongi Hills North Tower, directly connected to Roppongi Station. The website describes light oak finishes inspired by nature and meeting rooms like shoji screens. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

An interior inspired by nature

According to the website, the design takes its inspiration from nature, using delicate light-oak surfaces.

The meeting rooms recall Japanese shoji screens. It sits inside Roppongi Hills, surrounded by restaurants, hotels and homes.

01 · Reception and corridor

An entrance inside the Hills

A corridor runs from reception to a waiting area.

The centre spans two floors, the 16th and 17th.

02 · Refreshment area and coworking

Shared spaces in oak

The refreshment area and coworking seats are open to members.

Hot desks and dedicated desks start at ¥54,000 a month; day passes from ¥5,000 a day.

03 · Private offices and meeting rooms

Shoji-style meeting rooms and city views

Private offices are priced on request and have height-adjustable desks. There are three meeting rooms, and the website highlights their city views.

One meeting room holds 10.

What is shoji?

A traditional Japanese sliding screen of washi paper on a wooden lattice. It lets soft light through while blocking sightlines.

The website describes the centre's meeting rooms as shoji-style — bringing Japanese light into a glass high-rise.
