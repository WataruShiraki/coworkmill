Otsuka, Tokyo

RYOZAN PARK Otsuka GREEN — offices and a lab designed by Akihisa Hirata, like a green mountain in the city

RYOZAN PARK Otsuka GREEN is in Urban Green in Minami-Otsuka. The official website calls it “an office that supports sustainable activities” and notes that the office was designed by the architect Akihisa Hirata. The 2nd floor has private offices and fixed desks; the 3rd floor has private offices and a lab. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A lush green mountain in the city

The official website describes “a bold, green-filled design that connects with the park in front, like a lush green mountain in the middle of the city”.

Beyond offices, it has a meeting room that doubles as a laboratory and shared spaces indoors and out, supporting challenges to “give shape to your aspirations”.

01 · Shared space

Surrounded by greenery

The shared space holds up to 5 and is free to use.

There are also outdoor desks and meeting spots surrounded by greenery, and members can use the terrace and rooftop.

02 · Fixed desks and private offices

Workspaces on the 2nd and 3rd floors

Fixed desks are available 24 hours, 365 days, for ¥38,500 a month.

Fixed-desk members can also use the coworking space on the 6th floor of OTSUKA, five minutes' walk away.

03 · Laboratory

A meeting room with a sink and workbench

There are facilities specialised for environmental and agricultural work, including a lab with a sink and workbench.

Another feature is the chance to build connections with the park in front, local government and people in primary industries.

Who is Akihisa Hirata?

The architect who designed the office at RYOZAN PARK Otsuka GREEN. The official website describes it as “office design by architect Akihisa Hirata”.

According to the official website, the building connects with the park in front and is designed like “a lush green mountain in the middle of the city”.
