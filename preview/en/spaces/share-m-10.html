Azabu-Juban, Tokyo

SHARE M-10 — a forest-like coworking space by Kengo Kuma, one minute from Azabu-Juban Station

SHARE M-10 is on the 2nd and 3rd floors of MAXPLAN AZABU10, which stands at the Ichinohashi intersection a one-minute walk from Azabu-Juban Station. Both the building and the interior were designed by architect Kengo Kuma. The second floor is a 16-seat coworking space and the third a meeting room for up to 10 people. Billed as “a place for coexistence and creation”, it lets you work in a café-like atmosphere. Everything below is based on the official website and the architect's project page.

Introduction

A free, soft space — the opposite of a closed office

According to the architect's project page, SHARE M-10 is a shared office inside MAXPLAN AZABU10 (2020), a building by the same firm. The Ichinohashi intersection in Azabu-Juban is where working, living and shopping meet; there, the firm created a free, soft space that is the opposite of a closed office.

The room is surrounded by planters made of stacked timber rings, which make the shared office feel like a forest. The official site describes it as “a place for coexistence and creation” and “coworking and meeting rooms that feel like a café”.

01 · 2F coworking

16 seats surrounded by timber-ring planters

The second floor is a 16-seat coworking space. Planters of stacked timber rings surround the seats, so you work among the greenery.

Carefully chosen coffee and other drinks are provided, and entry is linked to the Akerun smart lock.

02 · Air and windows

HEPA-filtered ventilation and Low-E double glazing

The official website says the space has a 24-hour ventilation system with HEPA filters.

The windows use Low-E double glazing to reduce energy use. In a room full of plants, the quality of the air is also looked after.

03 · 3F meeting room

A meeting room for 10, and whole-floor hire

The third floor is a meeting room for up to 10 people.

The floor can also be hired exclusively, for 30 people with 21 seats (up to 25). Exclusive bookings are accepted up to one week in advance.

What is Low-E double glazing?

Window glass made of two panes with a layer of air between them and a special metal coating on the surface. It keeps out the heat of summer sun and keeps indoor warmth from escaping in winter.

The bigger the windows, the more the glass decides how comfortable a room feels. SHARE M-10 pairs its green interior with good windows and ventilation.
