Aoyama, Tokyo

WeWork the ARGYLE aoyama — a site inside Kita-Aoyama's new landmark, 2 minutes from Gaiemmae Station

WeWork the ARGYLE aoyama is in “the ARGYLE aoyama”, a mixed-use building on the site of Aoyama Bell Commons, long loved as a local landmark. It is a two-minute walk from Gaiemmae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, and the building also has cafés, restaurants and a hotel. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A bright space in pale wood and oak

The shared areas are bright and open, built around pale wood, with warm oak furniture, the website says. The interior is based on white.

The building also has shops that bring life to the street and an open event space. Companies of all sizes, from startups to large corporations, use the site.

01 · Shared areas

A lounge in pale wood

The shared areas are bright and open, with pale wood as the main material.

Oak furniture makes the space comfortable.

02 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Nine meeting rooms

There are nine meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

Ceilings are 2,800 mm high.

03 · A base for projects

A launchpad for new businesses

Beyond head-office use, the website recommends it as a project room or a base for launching new businesses.

With cafés, restaurants and a hotel in the building, it suits work involving many visitors.

What is an honesty market?

An unstaffed shop at WeWork sites. “Honesty” means you settle the bill yourself.

You can get snacks and drinks without going far from your seat.
