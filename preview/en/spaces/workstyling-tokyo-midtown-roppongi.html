Roppongi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi) — a shared office with an artistic touch, on the 18th floor of the Tower

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi) is on the 18th floor of Tokyo Midtown Tower, directly connected to Roppongi Station on the Toei Oedo and Tokyo Metro Hibiya lines. The website presents it as a refined space that adds an element of art to a place for “design thinking”. It has an open space, meeting rooms, single-person rooms and rental offices. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Design thinking and art

The website says of WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi): “We have created a refined space that adds an element of art to a place where you can always work with design thinking.”

It also has areas to refresh in — a base that builds a place to change your mood into the place you work.

01 · Entrance and open space

Shared space with an artistic touch

From the entrance, a space with an artistic touch begins.

The open space can be used without booking, and there are areas to refresh.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12, and larger rooms

There are meeting rooms for up to 12 and larger rooms for 13 or more.

Use them to welcome outside guests or for larger gatherings.

03 · Private rooms and rental offices

Rooms to shut yourself away and focus

Single-person rooms are for calls, online meetings and focused work.

There are rental offices too, and dedicated spaces (FLEX) can be entered 24 hours.

What is design thinking?

An approach that finds problems from the user's point of view and searches for answers by making and testing things.

WORK STYLING Tokyo Midtown (Roppongi) adds an element of art to a place for working this way.
