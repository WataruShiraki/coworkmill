Narimasu, Tokyo

SHARE LOUNGE Olive LOUNGE Narimasu — a lounge with bookshelves and partitioned seats, one minute from Chikatetsu-Narimasu Station

SHARE LOUNGE Olive LOUNGE Narimasu is on the 2nd floor of a building a one-minute walk from Chikatetsu-Narimasu Station on the Tokyo Metro Fukutoshin Line and two minutes from Narimasu Station on the Tobu Tojo Line. It can be used as a WORK STYLING site. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A lounge by the station

It is one minute on foot from Chikatetsu-Narimasu Station, and open 10:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays too.

In the photos you can see seats by bookshelves and partitioned seats.

Partitioned seats

What is a share lounge?

A large room that members share for work and relaxation.
