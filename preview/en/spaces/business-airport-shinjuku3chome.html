Shinjuku, Tokyo

Business-Airport Shinjuku 3-chome — a workplace expressing the city's diversity, on floors 4–6 of Q Plaza Shinjuku 3-chome

Business-Airport Shinjuku 3-chome, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 4th to 6th floors of Q Plaza Shinjuku 3-chome. From Shinjuku 3-chome, where all kinds of energy intersect, to the next stage: the website describes a space that expresses the diversity of the people on its streets and generates inspiration and creativity. There are four meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

From Shinjuku 3-chome, where every kind of energy meets

The website writes: “From Shinjuku 3-chome, where diverse energies intersect, to the next stage. Shinjuku embraces every kind of diversity and radiates enormous energy. Business-Airport Shinjuku 3-chome offers a space that expresses the diversity of the people on its streets and creates inspiration and creativity.”

It uses three floors of Q Plaza Shinjuku 3-chome, a commercial building in the district.

01 · Shared workspace

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

The shared workspace is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays (reception 9:00–18:00) and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥71,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

ROOM and DESK

Private ROOMs start at ¥165,000 a month and fixed DESKs at ¥104,500.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

One room for eight and three for six

Meeting room A seats eight at ¥3,300 an hour; rooms B to D seat six at ¥2,750.

The one-person online meeting room costs ¥550 per 30 minutes.

Why diversity?

People and things of many differences existing together. Business-Airport Shinjuku 3-chome makes the diversity of the people on its streets the theme of its space.

It turns the character of Shinjuku — a city where every kind of person passes through — into the subject of its interior.
