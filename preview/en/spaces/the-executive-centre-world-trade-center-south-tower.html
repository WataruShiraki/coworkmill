Hamamatsucho, Tokyo

The Executive Centre World Trade Center South Tower — a lounge overlooking Tokyo Bay on the 17th floor, connected to Hamamatsucho Station

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 17th floor of the World Trade Center Building South Tower, directly connected to JR Hamamatsucho Station — a symbol of Hamamatsucho's redevelopment. Floor-to-ceiling windows look out over Tokyo Bay, and the lounge has stone floors, a black-marble barista bar and vintage furniture by Timothy Oulton. Introduction

A 17th floor overlooking Tokyo Bay

The website presents the building as a symbol of Hamamatsucho's redevelopment, part of a plan to create “an iconic international business hub for Tokyo”.

The centre is on the 17th floor overlooking Tokyo Bay, with floor-to-ceiling windows, stone floors, a black-marble barista bar and vintage furniture by Timothy Oulton.

01 · Reception and lounge

A place to welcome visitors and a home for members

A business concierge is at reception, which leads into a spacious lounge with a black-marble barista bar.

The lounge also hosts events.

02 · Coworking

Seats by the windows

Coworking passes start at ¥54,000 a month and day passes at ¥5,000 a day.

There are height-adjustable desks and a coworking library.

03 · Private offices and meeting rooms

Rooms for teams and five meeting rooms

Private offices include sea-facing rooms, and day offices start at ¥45,000 per person per month.

There are five meeting rooms.

What is the Asian Headquarters Special Zone?

An area designated by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government to attract foreign companies' Asian headquarters and R&D centres to Tokyo.

The website notes that the centre is inside this zone — a location chosen with overseas companies setting up in Japan in mind.
