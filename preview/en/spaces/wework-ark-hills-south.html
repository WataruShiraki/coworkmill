WeWork Ark Hills South — American style meets glamour, directly connected to Roppongi-itchome Station

WeWork Ark Hills South is on the 16th floor of Ark Hills South Tower, directly connected to Roppongi-itchome Station on the subway. The website highlights its international setting and a design that blends an American feel with glamour. Everything below is based on the official website.

BuildingCompleted August 2013 / 20 floors above ground, 3 belowFloorsCeiling height 2,850 mm / 12 meeting rooms / phone booths, parents' room, honesty market and moreHoursWeekdays 8:30–18:00

Introduction

American style and glamour

Its design blends an American atmosphere with glamour. In this cosmopolitan area, many companies, global firms among them, use the site, the website says.

The surroundings have green plazas, tree-lined paths and street trees, so you can feel Japan's four seasons in the middle of the city.

01 · Front desk and shared areas

A glamorous entrance

A glamorous space leads from the front desk into the shared areas.

Ceilings are 2,850 mm high.

02 · Meeting rooms

12 meeting rooms

There are 12 meeting rooms, plus phone booths, a parents' room and an honesty market.

The building has a vibration-control structure and can supply power to common areas for up to 72 hours in an emergency.

What is a vibration-control structure?

A structure with dampers and other devices built into the building to absorb and reduce earthquake shaking.

Ark Hills South Tower has one, plus backup power for common areas for up to 72 hours in an emergency.
