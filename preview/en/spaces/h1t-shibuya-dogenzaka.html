Shibuya, Tokyo

H¹T Shibuya Dogenzaka — a private-rooms-only corporate shared office on the 3rd floor of the ACN Shibuya Dogenzaka Building

H¹T Shibuya Dogenzaka, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 3rd floor of the ACN Shibuya Dogenzaka Building, a two-minute walk from the Dogenzaka exit of Shibuya Mark City and seven minutes from Hachiko Square. Its only seats are ten single-person rooms — a site focused entirely on private rooms. It is open 7:00–22:00 on weekends and holidays, just as on weekdays. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A small site with nothing but single rooms

The only seats at H¹T Shibuya Dogenzaka are ten single-person rooms. No open space or meeting rooms appear in the official seat list.

Because every seat is a private room with a door, it works as a place for online meetings or work you want to do without distractions. It is a separate site from H¹T Shibuya Miyamasuzaka (listed separately on this site), on Dogenzaka on the opposite side of the station.

02 · Rooms

There are ten single-person rooms.

With a door to close, they work for calls and online meetings.

03 · Hours

It is open from 7:00 to 22:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

It suits early-morning and late-evening work.

What is Dogenzaka?

The name of the slope rising south-west from the west side of Shibuya Station, and of the area around it.

H¹T Shibuya Dogenzaka is on the 3rd floor of the ACN Shibuya Dogenzaka Building in Dogenzaka 1-chome.
