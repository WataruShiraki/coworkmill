Minatomirai, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING Minatomirai — wooden booths and sofas by a red wall, directly linked to Minatomirai Station

WORK STYLING Minatomirai is in Queen's Tower B, directly linked to Minatomirai Station on the Minatomirai Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a shared office that, according to the official website, operates without on-site staff. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Directly linked to Minatomirai Station

It is directly linked to Minatomirai Station on the Minatomirai Line, and ten minutes on foot from Sakuragicho Station on the JR and municipal subway lines.

According to the official website, it is an unstaffed site with no crew. The open space can be used without booking.

What is an unstaffed site?

A site run without reception staff (crew) on hand.

The official website describes WORK STYLING Minatomirai as an unstaffed site with no crew.
