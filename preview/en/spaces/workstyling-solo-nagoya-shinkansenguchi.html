WORK STYLING SOLO Nagoya Shinkansen Exit — single rooms and an open space, one minute from Nagoya Station

WORK STYLING SOLO Nagoya Shinkansen Exit is on the 6th floor of the Meiji Yasuda Life Nagoya Nishiguchi Building, a one-minute walk from Nagoya Station on the JR lines and Tokaido Shinkansen, and five minutes from Meitetsu Nagoya Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, it is a site built around private rooms for one. Everything below is based on the official website.

Single rooms near the station

A one-minute walk from Nagoya Station on the JR lines and Tokaido Shinkansen, and five minutes from Meitetsu Nagoya Station.

Single-person rooms are booked in advance and can be used in 10-minute units. There is also an open space.
