Ebisu, Tokyo

Business-Airport Ebisu — a workplace expressing Ebisu in soft curves, on the 2nd and 10th floors of Ebisu Business Tower

Business-Airport Ebisu, run by Tokyu Land, is on the 2nd and 10th floors of Ebisu Business Tower in Ebisu 1-chome. The space expresses Ebisu — where many kinds of business cross paths — in soft curves. From a place that feels like your own private base, free thinking beyond boundaries is born, the website says. Introduction

Ebisu, expressed in soft curves

The website says it expressed Ebisu, a district where many businesses intersect, in soft curves. From a space like your own private base, free ideas beyond boundaries emerge and help businesses take off.

Creating curved spaces inside a building full of straight lines: at Ebisu, the shape itself is the theme of the design.

01 · Coworking

From 7:00 on weekdays, and open on Saturdays

Coworking is open 7:00–22:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on Saturdays.

Address membership is ¥82,500 a month, Master ¥38,500, Private ¥14,300, and Flex ¥550 per 30 minutes.

02 · Serviced offices

Private rooms from one person

A single ROOM costs ¥176,000 a month; multi-person rooms start at ¥308,000.

Serviced offices can be entered 24 hours a day.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for six and one for eight

Meeting rooms A and B seat six at ¥2,750 an hour; room C seats eight at ¥3,300.

Meet inside soft, curving spaces.

Why curves?

Design that shapes spaces and furniture with smooth curves rather than angular straight lines. Business-Airport Ebisu expresses its neighbourhood this way.

Curves guide people's movement naturally and soften the hardness of a space.
