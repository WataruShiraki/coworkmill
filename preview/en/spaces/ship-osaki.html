Osaki, Tokyo

Shinagawa Industry Support and Exchange Facility SHIP — a members' lounge and a workshop with 3D printers in Osaki

SHIP, the Shinagawa Industry Support and Exchange Facility, is on the 3rd and 4th floors of Osaki Bright Core, an eight-minute walk from Osaki Station. In the Shinagawa, Osaki and Gotanda area, where manufacturing companies cluster, it is a base where engineers, designers and entrepreneurs meet companies large and small. It has a monthly-membership open lounge, a workshop with 3D printers and laser cutters, rental meeting rooms and offices, and marked its 10th anniversary on 1 June 2025. Introduction

Helping bring next-generation devices and services to life

The official website explains that, in an area where major manufacturers and their suppliers gather, SHIP serves as a hub where start-ups, small and medium firms and large companies meet, helping realise next-generation devices and services.

As of May 2025 it had 208 member companies, with information and communications the largest group at 38.1%, followed by academic research and professional services, and manufacturing.

01 · Open lounge

Choose any seat

The 4th-floor open lounge is a coworking space with monthly membership. You can sit anywhere in the facility.

Seats at the back of the lounge are set aside for web meetings.

02 · Café area and sofas

A space with greenery and light

The café area is where you can eat and drink.

The sofa area, filled with greenery and light, works for meetings or a change of pace.

03 · Focus seats and booths

Bookable booths for four

There are focus seats you can use for up to four hours a day.

Bookable four-person booths suit business talks and meetings.

04 · Workshop

3D printers and laser cutters

The workshop has several 3D printers for different uses and a laser cutter.

There is a range of tools, and advice on making things is free.

What is a designated manager?

An organisation entrusted by a city or ward to manage and run a public facility on its behalf.

SHIP is run by a designated manager.
