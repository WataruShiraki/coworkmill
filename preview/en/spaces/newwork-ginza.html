Ginza, Tokyo

NewWork Ginza — a corporate-membership satellite office on the 2nd floor of the K-18 Building, 5 minutes from Shimbashi Station

NewWork Ginza is on the 2nd floor of the K-18 Building in Ginza 8-chome, five minutes' walk from JR Shimbashi Station and six from Ginza Station on the Ginza Line. It has 40 open and booth seats, two meeting rooms for six and eight enclosed booths for one. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Introduction

Between Ginza and Shimbashi

NewWork Ginza is in Ginza 8-chome, five minutes from JR Shimbashi and six from Ginza Station — right between the two districts.

It is a corporate-membership satellite office designed for dropping in between client visits to answer email and join online meetings.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 40-seat workspace

There are 40 seats of two types, open and booth.

A multifunction printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths are available.

02 · Enclosed seated booths

Eight booths for one

There are eight enclosed seated booths for one, used by booking.

To keep using one beyond 120 minutes, you book again.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for six

Two meeting rooms for six are available by booking.

Meet clients in Ginza or Shimbashi close by.

What is an enclosed seated booth?

A small single-person room with a door, used sitting down — ideal for online meetings and calls.

NewWork Ginza has eight of them.
