Shibuya, Tokyo

NewWork Tokyu Shibuya 1-chome Building — a corporate-membership satellite office one minute from Shibuya Station, open weekends and holidays

NewWork Tokyu Shibuya 1-chome Building is on the 4th floor of the Tokyu Shibuya 1-chome Building, a one-minute walk from Exit B2 of Shibuya Station. It has 24 booth seats, two meeting rooms for 8, two wide private booths for 4 and six single private booths, and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A site by Shibuya Station that opens at weekends

Right by Exit B2 of Shibuya Station, this site keeps the same 8:00–21:00 hours on weekends and holidays as on weekdays.

There are 24 booth seats, two meeting rooms for 8 and two wide private booths that seat 4, so it handles small meetings too.

01 · Booth seats

A 24-seat workspace

There are 24 booth-style seats.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for 8

There are two meeting rooms for 8, booked in advance.

Right in front of Shibuya Station, they also suit meetings with outside guests.

03 · Private booths

Wide booths for 4 and single booths

There are two wide private booths for 4 and six single private booths.

With locks and monitors, they suit online meetings.

What is a satellite shared office?

A small workplace away from head office that several companies use together.

NewWork is run by Tokyu Corporation, and its official website describes it as a “shared-ride” satellite shared office for corporate members.
