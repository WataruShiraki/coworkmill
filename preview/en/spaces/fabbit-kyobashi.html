Kyobashi, Tokyo

fabbit Kyobashi — private offices with a coworking lounge on the 2nd floor of Central Building, 4 minutes from Tokyo Station

fabbit Kyobashi is on the 2nd floor of the Central Building in Kyobashi, right by exit 24 of the Yaesu underground mall at JR Tokyo Station. It is listed on the official website of the rental-office brand CROSSCOOP as one of its sites. Four minutes on foot from Tokyo Station, it has fully private rooms for 2 to 6, a coworking lounge and meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Ready to work from day one

The website describes “an environment fully equipped with what you need for business, such as a coworking lounge and meeting rooms, so you can start working as soon as you move in”.

Right by exit 24 of the Yaesu underground mall, it suits companies that travel often by shinkansen or to the airport.

01 · Entrance and coworking lounge

Shared space on the 2nd floor

The entrance is on the 2nd floor of the Central Building.

The coworking lounge is a shared place to work outside your room.

02 · Meeting room

A secure meeting room

There is a meeting room for discussions.

The website says the meeting room and offices have security systems.

03 · Private rooms

Fully private rooms for 2–6

Private rooms hold 2 to 6 people, all fully enclosed, and company registration is possible.

Each room has a different shape, so you can choose by headcount and use.

What is the Yaesu underground mall?

The shopping arcade that spreads underground on the Yaesu side of Tokyo Station. fabbit Kyobashi is right by its exit 24.

Even on rainy days, you can walk most of the way from Tokyo Station underground.
