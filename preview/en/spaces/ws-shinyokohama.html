Shin-Yokohama, Kanagawa

WORK STYLING Shin-Yokohama — on the 8th floor of the SD18 Building, three minutes from Shin-Yokohama Station

WORK STYLING Shin-Yokohama is on the 8th floor of the SD18 Building (formerly Nisso No. 18 Building), a three-minute walk from JR Shin-Yokohama Station and one minute from Exit 7 of the Yokohama Municipal Subway station. It is a shared office run by Mitsui Fudosan. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Work between journeys, next to the Shinkansen

Shin-Yokohama is a stop on the Tokaido Shinkansen. This site is on the 8th floor of a building three minutes from JR Shin-Yokohama Station and one minute from subway Exit 7.

The time before or after a Shinkansen ride can be spent working in a meeting room or private room.

01 · Entrance

Through reception to the workspace

Inside the entrance is reception, which leads to the work areas.

It is open on weekdays from 8:00 to 21:00.

02 · Open space

Seats of different shapes

The open space mixes sofa seats, desks and other kinds of seating.

It suits both solo work and short meetings.

03 · Meeting rooms

Enclosed rooms for meetings

There are meeting rooms you can choose by headcount.

Enclosed by walls, they suit meetings where you speak up and online calls.

04 · Private rooms

Single-person rooms

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

Each has a desk, chair and monitor for when you need to focus.

What is a pay-as-you-go plan?

A contract where you pay only for the time you use, with no fixed monthly fee.

WORK STYLING has a pay-as-you-go plan in 10-minute units; meeting rooms and private rooms are charged separately.
