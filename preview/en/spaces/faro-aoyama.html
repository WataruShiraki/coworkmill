Aoyama, Tokyo

FARO Aoyama — a design-led shared office of Japanese wood and greenery, five minutes from Gaienmae and Aoyama-itchome

FARO Aoyama is in Minami-Aoyama 2-chome, a five-minute walk from Gaienmae and Aoyama-itchome stations. It opened in 2013. The official website calls it “a shared office that combines design and comfort”, describing an office space built with 100% Japanese timber and wrapped in greenery. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Japanese wood and greenery

FARO Aoyama uses 100% Japanese timber. The official website describes an office wrapped in greenery, where you can feel the warmth of natural materials and a sense of living alongside nature.

It opened in 2013 as “a shared office that combines design and comfort”, and has underfloor heating.

01 · Members' lounge

Free-address shared seats

The free-address shared seats double as the members' lounge, at ¥22,000 a month.

There is a kitchenette and kitchen, three multifunction printers and phone booths.

02 · Small offices

Fully private rooms for about 1–10

Small offices are fully private rooms for about 1–10 people.

The shared lounge and team rooms are in the same building.

03 · Meeting rooms and rooftop

A rooftop terrace

The rooftop terrace was renovated in 2024.

On the basement floor is a 36 m² gallery space.

What is free address?

A way of working with no assigned seat: you use whichever seat is free. At FARO Aoyama, the free-address seats double as the members' lounge.

You can change where you sit to suit your mood or work that day.
