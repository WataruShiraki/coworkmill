Kichijoji, Tokyo

MIDORI.so Kichijoji — coworking on the 8th floor of Kichijoji PARCO, where a small community meets the open air of a department store

MIDORI.so Kichijoji is on the 8th floor of Kichijoji PARCO, a two-minute walk from Kichijoji Station. It is MIDORI.so's first site outside Tokyo's 23 wards. Workspace, meeting rooms, a kitchen, a lounge and booths are all on one floor, open every day from 8:00 to 23:00. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A close-knit community and the open air of a shopping building

The website describes MIDORI.so Kichijoji as “a place where a community where everyone knows each other mixes with the open atmosphere of a commercial building”. It is on the 8th floor of Kichijoji PARCO, a building shoppers pass through too.

It is MIDORI.so's first site outside the 23 wards — a place for people who would rather work by the station in their own neighbourhood than commute into central Tokyo.

01 · Workspace

Shared and fixed desks

The workspace has shared desks and fixed desks.

A free desk is ¥33,000 a month, a fixed desk ¥47,300 a month and a private booth from ¥66,000 a month (all with an ¥11,000 sign-up fee).

02 · Kitchen and meeting rooms

A place to eat and a place to talk

The kitchen is a shared space for members.

Meeting rooms are available for discussions, so work and conversation happen on the same floor.

03 · Plans

Evening-and-weekend membership, and pop-in from two hours

An evening-and-weekend free desk, usable weekdays 17:00–23:00 and on weekends and holidays, is ¥22,000 a month.

Pop-in is ¥1,100 for two hours, ¥2,200 a day or ¥22,000 for an 11-day pack, usable 10:00–18:00. Kichijoji Station is a two-minute walk.

What is evening-and-weekend membership?

A plan you can use only on weekday evenings and on weekends and holidays. At MIDORI.so Kichijoji it costs ¥22,000 a month.

It is for people who keep a day job and work on their own projects at night and at weekends — a plan that makes sense precisely because the space is by the station where they live.
