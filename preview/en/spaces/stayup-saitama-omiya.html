Omiya, Saitama

STAYUP Saitama Omiya — a shared office with a café space, ten minutes from Omiya Station

STAYUP Saitama Omiya, a coworking space and shared office, is on the 8th floor of the OS Building in Sakuragicho 4-chome, about a ten-minute walk from the West Exit of Omiya Station. STAYUP's fourth site, its open, airy space includes sit-stand desks, window seats, sofa seats, partitioned desks and focus booths. On clear days, you can see Mt Fuji from the window seats. Introduction

Choose your seat to suit how you work

STAYUP Saitama Omiya is STAYUP's fourth site. Its open workspace has ergonomic chairs and quality tables.

Besides the open space, there are sit-stand desks, window seats, sofa seats screened from view and desks enclosed by side panels, so you can choose a seat to suit your work style.

01 · Open space

Airy seating with high-performance chairs

The open space is furnished with high-performance chairs. Drop-in comes in three tiers — ¥880, ¥1,650 and ¥2,750 — from one hour to a full day.

On clear days, Mt Fuji is visible from the window seats.

02 · Sofa seats and focus booths

Sofas for meetings, booths for web sales calls

Sofa seats screened from view are used for meetings and lunch.

Focus booths work for concentrated work, online meetings and web sales calls.

03 · Café space and meeting rooms

About 30 drinks, and rooms for 6 and 8

The café space has about 30 kinds of free drinks, plus vending machines and light snacks.

Meeting rooms cost ¥2,200 an hour for 6 people and ¥2,750 for 8, with large monitors and whiteboards. Monthly members get ¥550 an hour off.

What is a sit-stand desk?

A desk whose top can be raised and lowered, so you can work sitting or standing.

STAYUP Saitama Omiya offers sit-stand desks as one of its seat types.
