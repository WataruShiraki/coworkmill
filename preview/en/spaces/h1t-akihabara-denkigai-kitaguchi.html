Akihabara, Tokyo

H¹T Akihabara Denkigai Kitaguchi — a corporate shared office on the 5th floor of the Shin-Akihabara Building, handy on the way back from a business trip

H¹T Akihabara Denkigai Kitaguchi, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 5th floor of the Shin-Akihabara Building, a one-minute walk from the Electric Town North Exit of JR Akihabara Station. The official website presents it as a satellite shared office where you can quickly clear paperwork, for example on the way back from a business trip. It has 20 single-person rooms and five meeting rooms for 2 to 10, and is open 7:30–22:00 every day. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A satellite office to stop at on business trips

H¹T Akihabara Denkigai Kitaguchi is built around private rooms, with 20 single-person rooms. The open space is small, for 4.

There are five meeting rooms: two for 2, and one each for 4, 8 and 10. It is three minutes from the Tsukuba Express station too, making it easy to stop at on days when you travel between the suburbs and central Tokyo.

02 · Rooms

20 single-person rooms

There are 20 single-person rooms.

03 · Meeting rooms

The meeting rooms are two for 2 and one each for 4, 8 and 10.

They cover everything from interviews to slightly larger meetings.

What is a satellite office?

A small workplace set up somewhere other than head office or a branch.

H¹T is a satellite-type shared office: companies become members, and employees use it while out or on the way to and from business trips.
