Otemachi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Otemachi — a shared office with meeting rooms inspired by works of art, on the 6th floor of Otemachi One Tower

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Otemachi is on the 6th floor of Otemachi One Tower, directly connected to Otemachi Station. It is a modern, refined space in white, and its meeting rooms take artworks from around the world as their motifs. Besides meeting rooms for up to 12, there are rooms for 13–80 people. Introduction

Meeting rooms inspired by works of art

WORK STYLING Otemachi is a modern, refined space based on white. Its meeting rooms have interiors that take artworks from around the world as motifs, the website says.

It is on the 6th floor of Otemachi One Tower, directly connected to Otemachi Station, reachable on foot from the Toei Mita Line and the Tokyo Metro Chiyoda, Marunouchi, Tozai and Hanzomon lines.

01 · Entrance and open space

A shared space in white

From the entrance, a space based on white unfolds.

The open space can be used without booking.

02 · Meeting rooms

A different artwork motif in every room

The meeting rooms for up to 12 have interiors inspired by artworks from around the world.

Rooms for 13–80 people are also available for large meetings and briefings.

03 · Rental offices

A base for your team

Rental offices can be taken as a team base.

Directly connected to Otemachi Station, they make it easy to come and go with clients.

What is a motif?

The subject a design is based on. WORK STYLING Otemachi uses artworks from around the world as motifs for its meeting rooms.

Rooms of the same size become easier to remember and tell apart when each has its own subject.
