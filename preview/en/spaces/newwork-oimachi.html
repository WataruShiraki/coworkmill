Oimachi, Tokyo

NewWork Oimachi — a satellite office with 11 private booths, open weekends, on the 5th floor of the K-3 Building

NewWork Oimachi is on the 5th floor of the K-3 Building, a three-minute walk from Oimachi Station. It has 27 booth seats, two meeting rooms for eight and 11 enclosed booths for one, and is open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Places to retreat and places to talk

NewWork Oimachi is on the 5th floor of the K-3 Building, three minutes from Oimachi Station, and open 8:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.

For 27 seats, it has 11 booths for one and two meeting rooms for eight — plenty of places to retreat and to talk for its size.

01 · Booth seats

A 27-seat workspace

There are 27 booth seats.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Enclosed seated booths

11 booths for one

There are 11 enclosed seated booths for one, used by booking.

To keep using one beyond 120 minutes, you book again.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for eight

Two meeting rooms for eight are available by booking.

Open on weekends and holidays, they work for weekend meetings too.

What is rebooking?

Booking again to continue using a room after your booked time runs out.

At NewWork, using a meeting room or booth for more than 120 minutes straight requires rebooking — a rule so more people can use them.
