Nihonbashi, Tokyo

Soil work Nihonbashi 1st — coworking and a cooperative office with park-facing windows in Nihonbashi Kobunacho

Soil work Nihonbashi 1st is on the 6th floor of a building in Nihonbashi Kobunacho. One floor is coworking, and the floor above is a cooperative office shared by a variety of tenants. The official website says its park-facing windows let you sense the passing of the day and the seasons, even in the city centre. On the ground floor is the café-bakery PARKLET. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Windows facing a park

Soil work Nihonbashi 1st has one floor of coworking, with a cooperative office above it shared by a variety of tenants.

The official website highlights the park-facing windows, through which you can sense the passing of the day and the seasons. On the ground floor is the café-bakery PARKLET.

01 · Coworking floor

Lounge and fixed desks

Membership types are lounge, group and fixed-desk.

It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and company registration is available.

02 · Meeting rooms and phone booths

Meetings and calls

There are meeting rooms and phone booths.

A shared kitchen also lets you prepare meals.

03 · The park and the neighbourhood

Seasons outside the window

A park is just a short walk away.

Together with PARKLET, the café-bakery on the ground floor, the whole building connects with the neighbourhood.

What is a cooperative office?

An office where several companies or tenants gather in one building and use it together. At Soil work Nihonbashi 1st, the floor above the coworking space is a cooperative office.

It is a format that makes it easy for people in the same building to connect.
