Kashiwa, Chiba

Noblesse Oblige (NOB) — coworking with raised seating and sofas, near the East Exit of Kashiwa Station

Noblesse Oblige — NOB for short — is on the 3rd floor of the Daiichi Mitoya Building, an eight-minute walk along the station-front street from the East Exit of Kashiwa Station on the JR Joban Line and Tobu Noda Line. It combines a shared office and coworking space with rental meeting rooms and rental space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A workplace for “local living where you know your neighbours”

Under the keyword “local living where you know your neighbours”, NOB proposes working and living close together rather than separating home and work. It aims to be a base for creators rooted in the area and for would-be entrepreneurs who want to energise their town.

Creators, entrepreneurs and remote workers use it as a workplace, and it is also used for studying for qualifications. The official website describes it as “a spacious place where you can focus on your work”.

01 · Focus desks

The most popular seats

The focus desks are named on the official website as the “number one most popular” seats, for when you want to work quietly on your own.

Open 8:50–22:00 on weekdays, it is handy before work or on the way home.

02 · Raised seating

Popular raised seats, sofas and high tables

The raised seating area (koagari) is also presented on the official website as popular.

There are sofa and high-table seats too, so you can choose to suit your mood or task.

03 · Prices

From ¥400 an hour, plus monthly plans

Drop-in is ¥400 per hour or ¥1,500 per day (excl. tax). Show a student ID for a youth discount of ¥1,000 a day.

Monthly membership has five plans — G, S, W, W+ and N — from ¥11,000 to ¥35,000 a month (excl. tax; joining fee ¥5,000).

What does “working near home” mean?

Keeping your home and workplace close together (shokuju kinsetsu in Japanese) — the opposite of separating them and commuting into the city centre.

NOB champions this as a base for people living in Kashiwa to work locally.
