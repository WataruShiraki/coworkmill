Tamachi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Tamachi Shibaura Exit — a shared office on the 5th floor of the Shibaura SEC Building, four minutes from JR Tamachi Station

WORK STYLING Tamachi Shibaura Exit is on the 5th floor of the Shibaura SEC Building, a four-minute walk from JR Tamachi Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The other site, on the Shibaura side of Tamachi Station

WORK STYLING also has a site on the Mita Exit side of Tamachi Station; this one is on the Shibaura side. It is four minutes on foot from JR Tamachi Station and six from Mita Station on the Toei subway.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, booths accented with blue and patterned tile walls catch the eye.

01 · Open space

Booths with blue accents

The open space can be used without booking.

There are booths with blue backrests and tables under pendant lights.

02 · Partitioned seats

Desks for focusing alone

There are desks divided by partitions.

By the windows there are also desks set among plants.

03 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12.

Blue chairs and white walls give them a calm palette.

04 · Private rooms

Single rooms with white tiles

Single-person rooms let you close the door and work.

They have white-tiled walls and desk lamps.

What is an accent colour?

A bold colour used in just a few places within an otherwise calm colour scheme.

WORK STYLING Tamachi Shibaura Exit uses blue as an accent in its white and grey interior.
