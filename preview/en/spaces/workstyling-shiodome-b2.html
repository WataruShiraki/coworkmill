Shiodome, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Shiodome B2F — a shared office on the 2nd basement floor of Shiodome City Center, one minute from Shiodome Station

WORK STYLING Shiodome B2F is on the 2nd basement floor of Shiodome City Center, a one-minute walk from Shiodome Station on the Toei Oedo Line. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has single-person rooms, meeting rooms for up to 12 and an open space. It is a separate site from the one on the 5th floor of the same building. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Reached from underground in Shimbashi and Shiodome

WORK STYLING Shiodome B2F is one minute on foot from Exit 4 of Shiodome Station on the Oedo Line and one minute from Shimbashi Station on the Yurikamome. JR Shimbashi Station's Shiodome Exit is three minutes away.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos, wooden counter seats and seats around the columns stretch across a wide basement floor.

What is pay-as-you-go?

A system where you pay only for the time you use.

WORK STYLING's pay-as-you-go plan (SHARE) works in 10-minute units.
