Yaesu, Tokyo

POTLUCK YAESU — an innovation field where regional dreams gather, on the 5th floor of Tokyo Midtown Yaesu

Innovation Field Yaesu is on the 5th floor of Tokyo Midtown Yaesu, in front of the Yaesu exit of Tokyo Station. It is the base of POTLUCK YAESU, a project for regional economies planned and run jointly by Mitsui Fudosan and NewsPicks Re:gion, with a café, a lounge, a studio for about 140 people and an exhibition area. Introduction

Where Japan's dreams gather — and grow into the world's

POTLUCK YAESU calls itself “Japan Presentation Field — where Japan's dreams gather and grow into the world's”. True to the name, a potluck meal, it is where businesses and people brought from regions all over Japan meet in front of Tokyo Station.

It is planned and run jointly by Mitsui Fudosan and NewsPicks Re:gion. Eating, talking, showing and presenting: four places on one floor create a flow where people from the regions and from Tokyo mix.

01 · Lounge

Open until 22:00 on weekdays

The lounge is open 11:00–22:00 on weekdays and 11:00–19:00 on weekends and holidays.

Right in front of Tokyo Station, it is easy to drop by between business trips and trains.

02 · Café & kitchen

Chikaba Kitchen Tokyo Yaesu

The café and kitchen is Chikaba Kitchen Tokyo Yaesu, open 11:00–20:00 on weekdays and 11:00–18:00 on weekends and holidays.

Regional ingredients and dishes become the first point of contact with people in Tokyo.

03 · Studio and exhibition

A stage for 140, and a place to show

The studio and event space holds about 140 people for presentations of regional businesses and networking events.

The exhibition area shows regional products and initiatives. Hear the story, see the product, taste it — all on one floor.

What is a potluck?

A meal where every guest brings a dish to share. POTLUCK YAESU takes its name from the word.

Regions across Japan bring their businesses and people and lay them on the table in front of Tokyo Station — on the 5th floor of Tokyo Midtown Yaesu, the place lives up to its name.
