Otemachi, Tokyo

Global Business Hub Tokyo — a shared office linking Otemachi and the world, on the 3rd floor of Otemachi Financial City Grand Cube

Global Business Hub Tokyo, run by Mitsubishi Estate, is on the 3rd floor of Otemachi Financial City Grand Cube. It has 50 furnished offices for 2–20 people, 14 meeting rooms for 4–20 and an event space for up to 200, all available 24 hours. It is built for overseas companies entering Japan and for Japanese companies aiming at the world. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A collaborative shared office linking Otemachi and the world

Global Business Hub Tokyo calls itself “a collaborative shared office linking Otemachi and the world”. Run by Mitsubishi Estate, it brings together companies entering Japan from overseas and companies growing towards global markets.

Offices come furnished, with electricity and internet included. Being able to move in in as little as two weeks is a big advantage for companies arriving from abroad, the website says.

01 · Offices

50 furnished offices for 2–20 people

There are 50 furnished offices for 2–20 people, with electricity and internet included.

You can start in as little as two weeks — ideal for companies that need a base in Japan quickly.

02 · Meeting rooms

14 rooms for 4–20 people

There are 14 meeting rooms for 4–20 people.

With many meeting rooms for the number of tenants, finding a room for a meeting is rarely a problem.

03 · Event space

Seminars and launches for up to 200

The event space holds up to 200 people for seminars and product launches.

It sits on the same floor as a place for tenants to connect with each other and with people from outside.

What is a furnished office?

An office with desks, chairs and storage already in place, ready to use as soon as the contract is signed. At Global Business Hub Tokyo, electricity and internet are included too.

No choosing furniture, running cables or signing extra contracts. For a company arriving from abroad, this decides whether the first few weeks go into setup or into work.
