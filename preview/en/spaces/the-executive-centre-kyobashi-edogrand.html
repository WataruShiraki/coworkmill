Kyobashi, Tokyo

The Executive Centre Kyobashi Edogrand — a stone-topped barista bar and lounge on the 26th floor, connected to Kyobashi Station

The Executive Centre has a centre on the 26th floor of Kyobashi Edogrand, connected underground to Kyobashi Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line. Tokyo Station is also within walking distance. There is a barista bar with a stone counter, a business lounge furnished by Timothy Oulton, and four meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A 26th floor overlooking Tokyo Station

The website calls Kyobashi Edogrand “an impressive building connected underground to Kyobashi Station” and highlights the views over Tokyo Station.

It is close to Marunouchi, Ginza and Nihonbashi alike. A stone-topped barista bar, a lounge furnished by Timothy Oulton, Herman Miller chairs and height-adjustable desks are all here.

01 · Coworking and call rooms

Seats to work and rooms to call

Coworking passes start at ¥54,000 a month and day passes at ¥5,000 a day.

Phone booths along the corridor are for calls and online meetings.

02 · Private offices and meeting rooms

Rooms for teams and four meeting rooms

Private offices are priced on request. The four meeting rooms have pull-down presentation screens and audiovisual equipment.

Outside the windows, the streets around Tokyo Station spread out.

What is a day office?

A plan at The Executive Centre for using a private office during the daytime. At Kyobashi Edogrand it starts at ¥45,000 per person per month.

It suits people who want a private office for set hours before committing to a permanent one.
