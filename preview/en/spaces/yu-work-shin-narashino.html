Shin-Narashino, Chiba

Yu~Work (Natural Hot Spring Yu~neru) — a workspace inside a natural hot-spring facility, two minutes from Shin-Narashino Station

The natural hot spring Yu~neru is on the 2nd floor of the Mr Max Shin-Narashino Shopping Center, a two-minute walk from Shin-Narashino Station on the JR Keiyo Line. Yu~Work is the coworking space inside it. You can do remote work and meetings in the facility's loungewear, and switch gears in the hot spring or sauna between tasks. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Remote work and meetings in loungewear

Yu~Work is a coworking space inside the natural hot spring Yu~neru. Presenting it as “a new way of working”, the official website highlights that you can work remotely and hold meetings in the facility's loungewear.

It is a workspace for people who want to get work done, also usable for remote meetings and discussions. When you tire of work, the hot spring and sauna are in the same building.

01 · Workspace

8 fixed seats and 6 open-space seats

There are 8 fixed seats and 6 seats in an open space. Drop-in is ¥500 for five hours, with extensions at ¥100 per hour (incl. tax).

Bathhouse entry is charged on top: ¥1,050 on weekdays and ¥1,150 on weekends and holidays.

02 · Private hire

Hire the whole room for up to 12

With advance booking, you can hire the whole coworking room. Private hire is for up to 12 people, at ¥5,000 for three hours or ¥8,000 for five (incl. tax; book by the day before).

The official website says you can hold meetings in loungewear.

03 · Equipment

Power and USB, whiteboard, free drinks

Equipment includes a 55-inch TV, power and USB, a whiteboard, internet and lockers, and drinks are free.

That the baths themselves count among the facilities is something no other coworking space has.

What is bathhouse loungewear?

Comfortable clothing provided by a bathhouse for wearing inside the facility before and after bathing.

At Yu~Work, you can work remotely and hold meetings in this loungewear, moving between the hot spring and work without changing.
