Kuramae, Tokyo

MOV KURAMAE — a Kuramae shared office drawing on KOKUYO's expertise, with a lounge and a tatami meeting room

MOV KURAMAE occupies an eight-storey building in the Kuramae–Asakusabashi area. The official website describes it as “a shared office combining flexible private spaces and a variety of common spaces, drawing on KOKUYO's expertise in creating workspaces”. Shared spaces are spread across the floors, from the ground-floor café and the 2nd-floor lounge to the 8th-floor meeting rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Drawing on workspace expertise

The official website presents it as “a shared office in the Kuramae–Asakusabashi area combining flexible private spaces and a variety of common spaces, drawing on KOKUYO's expertise in creating workspaces”.

Members have 24-hour access with their own key card. Wi-Fi and utilities are included in the fee, and furniture is provided.

01 · Ground-floor café and entrance

An entrance open to the street

On the ground floor is the café “REFRESH STAND”.

The building is an eight-storey steel-reinforced concrete structure completed in 1998, with a monument at the ground-floor entrance.

02 · 2F MOV LOUNGE

A lounge for tenant companies

MOV LOUNGE is a spacious lounge and dining area that tenant companies can use freely.

With sofa seating, it works for meetings and breaks.

03 · Booths and high booths

Seats for one or two

The 3rd floor has single-person booths, and the 4th and 5th floors have high booths for 1–2 people.

Booths start at ¥43,000 a month and high booths at ¥78,000 (excl. tax).

04 · 8F KURA-MA and TATAMI

Two meeting rooms with different moods

The 8th floor has “KURA-MA”, a meeting room with a counter, and “TATAMI”, a tatami meeting room.

There are also meeting rooms for up to 10 and a room for solo web meetings.

What is KURA-MA?

The name of a meeting room on the 8th floor of MOV KURAMAE. The official website describes it as a room with a counter and sink, a balcony and a TV.

The same floor also has the tatami meeting room “TATAMI”, so you can choose the mood of your meeting.
