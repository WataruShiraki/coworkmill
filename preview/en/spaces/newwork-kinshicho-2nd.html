Kinshicho, Tokyo

NewWork Kinshicho 2nd — a satellite office open weekends, on the 2nd floor of the Kinshicho Square Building, 2 minutes from the South Exit

NewWork Kinshicho 2nd is on the 2nd floor of the Kinshicho Square Building, a two-minute walk from the South Exit of Kinshicho Station — the second NewWork in Kinshicho. It has 27 open and booth seats, two meeting rooms for four and four enclosed booths for one, and opens on weekends and holidays too. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Kinshicho's second site

As its name says, NewWork Kinshicho 2nd is the second NewWork in Kinshicho — a sign of how many people use NewWork in this district.

It is on the 2nd floor, two minutes from the South Exit, and open 8:00–20:00 on weekends and holidays.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 27-seat workspace

There are 27 seats of two types, open and booth.

The multifunction printer is free, and there is Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for four

Two meeting rooms for four are available by booking.

Sized for small meetings.

03 · Enclosed seated booths

Four booths for one

There are four enclosed seated booths for one.

Use them for online meetings and calls.

What is commuting stress?

The burden of commuting — packed trains and long journeys.

NewWork lists reducing commuting stress among its aims. Working at a station near home cuts down travel.
