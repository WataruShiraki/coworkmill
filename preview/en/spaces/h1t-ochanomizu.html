Ochanomizu, Tokyo

H¹T Ochanomizu — a corporate shared office with 10 single-person rooms on the 5th floor of VORT Ochanomizu

H¹T Ochanomizu, run by Nomura Real Estate, is on the 5th floor of VORT Ochanomizu, a two-minute walk from Shin-Ochanomizu Station (Hijiribashi Exit) on the Tokyo Metro Chiyoda Line and three minutes from the Hijiribashi Exit of JR Ochanomizu Station. It has ten single-person rooms and is open 7:00–21:00 every day. It is a separate site from H¹T Ochanomizu THE GARDEN, listed separately on this site. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A small private-room site in Surugadai

H¹T Ochanomizu is a site of just ten single-person rooms on the 5th floor of VORT Ochanomizu in Kanda-Surugadai. No open space or meeting rooms appear in the official seat list.

The official website describes it as a corporate shared office usable for many purposes: remote work, a base for sales staff, seminars, training and interviews.

01 · Entrance

The 5th floor of VORT Ochanomizu

It is on the 5th floor of VORT Ochanomizu.

It is within three minutes of the Hijiribashi exits of both the JR and Chiyoda Line stations.

02 · Rooms

Ten single-person rooms

There are ten single-person rooms.

With a door to close, they also work for online meetings.

03 · Hours

7:00 to 21:00 every day

It is open from 7:00 to 21:00 on weekdays, weekends and holidays.

It is also handy for days when you want to get a little work done at the weekend.

What is the Hijiribashi Exit?

The ticket gate on the east side of JR Ochanomizu Station, at the foot of the Hijiribashi bridge over the Kanda River.

H¹T Ochanomizu is a three-minute walk from this exit.
