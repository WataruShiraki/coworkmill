Nihonbashi, Tokyo

WORK STYLING Nihonbashi 1-chome — a shared office on the 5th floor of Nihonbashi 1-chome Mitsui Building, directly connected to Nihombashi Station

Mitsui Fudosan's WORK STYLING Nihonbashi 1-chome is on the 5th floor of Nihonbashi 1-chome Mitsui Building, directly connected to Nihombashi Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Tozai lines. Along with an open space, meeting rooms for up to 12 and single-person rooms, it has rental offices. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

On the 5th floor, by the Nihonbashi bridge

WORK STYLING Nihonbashi 1-chome is on the 5th floor of a building directly connected to Nihombashi Station. It is three minutes from Nihombashi Station on the Toei Asakusa Line and six from JR Tokyo Station.

Hourly open space and private rooms share the floor with rental offices taken on the dedicated-space plan (FLEX).

01 · Entrance and open space

Seats you can take without booking

Reception crew greet you at the 5th-floor entrance.

The open space can be used without booking.

02 · Meeting rooms

Rooms for up to 12

Meeting rooms hold up to 12 people.

Directly connected to Nihombashi Station, it is easy to direct visitors here.

03 · Private rooms and rental offices

Rooms for one, rooms for teams

Single-person rooms are for calls and online meetings.

Rental offices can be taken as a team base.

What is a rental office?

A room with furniture and connectivity ready, rented by the month. At WORK STYLING this is the dedicated-space plan (FLEX).

Being on the same floor as the shared open space and meeting rooms, you can use facilities outside your room on busy days with visitors and meetings.
