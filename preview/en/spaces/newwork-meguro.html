Meguro, Tokyo

NewWork Meguro — a 51-seat corporate-membership satellite office on the 17th floor of the JR Tokyu Meguro Building, one minute from Meguro Station

NewWork Meguro is on the 17th floor of the JR Tokyu Meguro Building, a one-minute walk from Meguro Station. With 51 open and booth seats, two meeting rooms for 8 and 16 single private booths, it is one of the larger NewWork sites. It is a corporate-membership satellite shared office run by Tokyu Corporation. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

A larger site on the 17th floor of a station building

On the 17th floor of the JR Tokyu Meguro Building, which is directly linked to Meguro Station, it has a large 51 seats.

There are 16 single private booths and two meeting rooms for 8, so everything from solo work to small meetings can be done on one floor.

01 · Open and booth seats

A 51-seat workspace

There are 51 seats of two kinds: open and booth.

There is a free printer, Wi-Fi, power and phone booths.

02 · Private booths

Sixteen single booths

There are 16 single private booths.

That is enough to find a booth even when online meetings overlap.

03 · Meeting rooms

Two rooms for 8

There are two meeting rooms for 8.

Above Meguro Station, they also suit meetings with outside guests.

What is the JR Tokyu Meguro Building?

A building above Meguro Station housing offices and shops.

NewWork Meguro is on its 17th floor.
