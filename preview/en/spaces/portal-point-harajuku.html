Harajuku, Tokyo

PORTAL POINT HARAJUKU — more than “just a place to work” — 8th-floor coworking and a sky terrace overlooking the National Stadium

PORTAL POINT HARAJUKU is in Sendagaya, a four-minute walk from Kita-sando Station on the Tokyo Metro Fukutoshin Line and seven minutes from JR Harajuku Station. Real Gate has turned an entire building — one basement level and eight floors above ground — into offices under the concept “New Standard”. The 8th floor is a coworking space, and there is a sky terrace on the roof. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

For grown-up creative workers who know how to play

The official website presents the building, under the concept “New Standard”, as a new kind of office that is not “just a place to work”, aimed at grown-up creative workers who know how to play.

Real Gate says it has been creating offices under the PORTAL POINT brand since 2012, with design that sets them apart from other coworking brands. Harajuku is the brand's flagship building.

01 · 8F coworking

A workspace divided into zones

The 8th-floor coworking space has a conference zone, a relax zone, a mix zone and a booth zone.

Free desks come with or without company registration, and dedicated desks, meeting rooms and phone booths are on the same floor.

02 · Sky terrace

A rooftop overlooking the National Stadium and Meiji Jingu

The official website says the rooftop sky terrace looks out over the National Stadium and Meiji Jingu.

Somewhere to step outside for fresh air between tasks is right in the same building.

03 · The whole building

From a shop to small offices

The ground floor is a shop unit, floors 2–3 are whole-floor offices, floors 4–7 are small offices and the basement holds storage rooms.

You can start in coworking and move up to a private office or a whole floor in the same building.

What is PORTAL POINT?

An office brand that Real Gate has run since 2012. Its official website gives the theme as “New Modern”, offering distinctive design and service experiences.

There are sites in Shibuya, Yoyogi-Koen, Ebisu, Harajuku and elsewhere, each building with its own concept.
