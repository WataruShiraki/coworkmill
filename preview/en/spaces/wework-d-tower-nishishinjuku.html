Nishi-Shinjuku, Tokyo

WeWork D Tower Nishi-Shinjuku — high floors of Modernism and wood tones, 4 minutes from Nishi-Shinjuku Station

WeWork D Tower Nishi-Shinjuku is on the 16th floor of D Tower Nishi-Shinjuku, a four-minute walk from Nishi-Shinjuku Station on the Marunouchi Line. Its interior takes Modernism as its concept, using light and dark wood to create a calm space. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

Modernism and shades of wood

The interior concept is Modernism: a calm space design inspired by great architects, making use of light and dark wood, the website says.

On a high floor, it also has views from the windows. Close to Shinjuku's top hotels and sightseeing areas, it is a convenient place to welcome guests from overseas.

01 · Views and meeting rooms

By the windows on a high floor

On a high floor, it offers views from the windows.

There are 20 meeting rooms, plus phone booths and a parents' room.

02 · 200+ seats on one floor

From projects to head-office moves

More than 200 seats can be used on a single floor, from project teams up to a full head-office move.

The building has shops and a childcare facility.

What is Modernism?

A 20th-century movement in architecture and design that restrains decoration and shows function and materials honestly.

WeWork D Tower Nishi-Shinjuku takes Modernism as its interior concept and ties the space together with shades of wood.
