Kaihin-Makuhari, Chiba

WORK STYLING Kaihin-Makuhari — a high-ceilinged 2nd floor, five minutes from Kaihin-Makuhari Station

WORK STYLING Kaihin-Makuhari is on the 2nd floor of World Business Garden Malibu East, a five-minute walk from the South Exit of JR Kaihin-Makuhari Station. Run by Mitsui Fudosan, this shared office has an open space, meeting rooms and private rooms. Everything below is based on the official website.

Introduction

The 2nd floor of World Business Garden

It is five minutes on foot from the South Exit of JR Kaihin-Makuhari Station.

The open space can be used without booking. In the photos you can see numbered seats with partitions and long tables under a high ceiling.

Sofa seats by the window

Rooms for meetings

04 · Private rooms

Rooms for one

There are private rooms where you can close the door and work.

They can also be used for online meetings.

What is ceiling height?

The height from floor to ceiling. The higher it is, the roomier the same floor area feels.
